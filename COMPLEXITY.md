# Complexity: current topic comparison

The active comparison is organized by topics and complete application
papers. For topic A the denominator is the full cost

```text
c(U_A) + c(A₁) + c(A₂) + …,
```

against the matched historical manuscripts LA₁, LA₂, …. Required proof
appendices and mathematical programs belong in those costs. Topic B pays
its full U_B independently, even when its mathematical content overlaps U_A.
No cross-topic deduction applies to this statistic.

The [topic complexity protocol](TOPIC_COMPLEXITY.md) defines the accounting.
The [dependency pilot](DEPENDENCY_PILOT.md) compares a specific source proof
with its replacement and retained inputs. A dependency graph is evidence
about reuse; its number of vertices is not a proof-length measurement.

An explicitly separate chronological GKM account may discount proof nodes
already acquired in that account, with the conditions and full application
costs recorded. Acquisition spikes, later reuse and decreases after
refactoring must be observed rather than built into the choice of graph.

**The earlier measurements are not current granular topic results.** The
existing A–D drafts still need to be reworked into the requested topic
families, and their matched costs need to be measured under the current
protocol. This page assigns no new compression rate to that work.

The [historical coarse checkpoint plot](archive/COMPLEXITY.md), its
[historical six-source measurement](archive/MEASUREMENT_A.md) and the
[older compilation records](archive/README.md) remain available for audit.
They concern their exact earlier packages and accounting conventions.
Their saved scientific artifacts and plotting script are unchanged.
