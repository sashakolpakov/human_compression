# Module B: Forest coordinates and primitive coset geometries

This application manuscript is in preparation. It brings together tree
cuts, compatible partitions, independent generating sets and Frattini
coordinates to study coset geometries and their automorphism groups.
It will cite [U: Marked groups, kernels and intersections](UMBRELLA.md)
for shared group-theoretic proofs and supply the complete application
arguments.

The planned source bundle is:

| ID | Source | Application arguments |
|---|---|---|
| N001 | Buekenhout–Cara, *Some properties of inductively minimal geometries* (1998) | Tree-cut descriptions and the stated primitivity and intersection criteria |
| N003 | Cameron–Cara, *Independent generating sets and geometries for symmetric groups* (2002) | Forest coordinates, the sign kernel, independent generation and all eight numbered targets in their recorded finite-group and degree ranges |
| N008 | Archer–Cara–Krempa, *Using the Frattini Subgroup and Independent Generating Sets to Study RWPRI Geometries* (2005) | Relative generation, Frattini quotients, residue tests and the principal geometric results |
| L030 | García-Colín–Leemans, *An infinite family of locally X graphs based on incidence geometries* (2021) | Disjoint-subset incidence as a weighted-star construction, followed by the required residue, diagram, distance and automorphism arguments |

The [source registry](PAPER_MAP.md) records the authors, exact source
links, existing derivations and retained inputs. Those proofs are being
reworked into this manuscript. In particular, foundational classification
and Whiston's results remain attributed inputs, and the finite-group
qualification in N008 remains explicit.

The organizing idea is that cutting a forest gives coordinates for
partitions and subgroup intersections. Weighted stars put the disjoint-subset
construction in the same language. Sign and Frattini quotients then provide
the additional group coordinates needed for the generation and primitivity
arguments. Each source still requires its complete application proof.

The intended deduction is:

    U + Forest coordinates and primitive coset geometries
        -> the declared principal results of N001, N003, N008 and L030.
