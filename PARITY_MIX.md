# Parity fibers and intersections of marked groups

The group construction in **[Equal-label polytopes and quadratic symmetries](EQUAL_LABEL_POLYTOPES.md)**
now has a reviewed Lean proof of its full intersection property. It is one
construction theorem supported by twelve formal modules. The module count
does not count new research results or additional source replacements.

Let `e_i` and `g_i` mark groups E and G. Suppose every element of E is
annihilated by some power of two; both markings consist of involutions,
satisfy the string relations and have the intersection property; and the
adjacent products in G have odd order. Suppose also that actual
homomorphisms from E and G to a common commutative group send all markers
to the same nonidentity element. These character hypotheses must be
proved in an application.

Then the subgroup generated by the pairs `(e_i, g_i)` has the intersection
property on every pair of marker subsets. The theorem proves this both in
E × G and intrinsically in the generated subgroup. Its paired markers
generate that subgroup, are nonidentity involutions and satisfy the string
relations. Neither ambient finiteness nor generation of all of E and G
is required by the final intersection statement.

The proof first uses coprime powers to identify the actual generated group
on an interval with its character fiber. It constructs genuine coordinates
for the consecutive components of every support. For two supports, group
membership then gives row and column character equations on their interval
overlaps. The previously proved interval-cancellation theorem forces every
overlap defect to vanish, giving the required intersection. These fibers,
coordinates and equations are proved; they are not premises replacing the
desired conclusion.
