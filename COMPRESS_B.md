**L032 + L035 + F013 + F016 → [Quotients, deletion maps and cyclic cores of marked two-groups](research/case_study/phase37_compress_twogroups_manuscript.md).**

The shared calculus transports subgroup intersections through kernels and
constructs compatible groups with deletion maps. One square-lattice seed
serves the all-order existence and prescribed-section results. Graded
support proves the canonical class-two quotient. A bounded cyclic-core
argument plus four finite relation patterns supplies the complete eight-family
classification. All those arguments and the finite algorithm appear inside
the replacement manuscript.

The [independent assembled review](research/case_study/phase37_compress_twogroups_recent_audit.md)
passes all eleven numbered target theorems, itemized in seventeen clause
rows in the [source/dependency ledger](research/case_study/phase37_compress_twogroups_ledger.md).
The reviewer read the complete replacement, checked the selected primary
statements and independently reran the order 256 census: 1,651 eligible groups,
92 anchored triples and pattern counts 64, 4, 16, 8. The all-parameter lifting
argument is a written proof; it is not inferred from a finite sample.
The [root review](research/case_study/phase37_compress_twogroups_root_audit.md)
records the final status over historical pending labels.

Substantial background remains explicit: the polytope/C-group correspondence,
Burnside and lower-central theory, the cyclic-core bound, the complete
SmallGroups catalogue, and the earlier alternating assembly/free-extension
geometry theorems. The bare universal local presentation's IP and existence
are proved by deletion maps, rather than retained as a target assumption.

Corrections to F013's auxiliary Frattini kernel and two F016 proof arguments
are separate from the recovered principal results. The source meaning of
an infinite two-group permits elements of infinite order; every finite-order
element must have two-power order. The exact rank and parameter restrictions
remain in the ledger. N009 and other excluded source material are not intake.
