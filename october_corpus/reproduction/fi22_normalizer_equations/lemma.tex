\section*{A supplied finite normalizer certificate}
Let $A$ be a group and let $a,c\in A$ be distinct commuting nonidentity
involutions. Put $K=\langle a,c\rangle=\{1,a,c,ac\}$.
Suppose that supplied elements $r,t\in A$ satisfy
\[
 a^r=a,\qquad c^r=ac,\qquad a^t=c,\qquad c^t=a,
 \qquad x^h=h^{-1}xh.
\]
Then $r$ and $t$ normalize $K$. Their conjugation actions on
$\{a,c,ac\}$ are the distinct transpositions $(c\;ac)$ and $(a\;c)$.
They generate all of $\operatorname{Aut}(K)\cong S_3$.
The conjugation homomorphism
$N_A(K)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Aut}(K)$ has kernel $C_A(K)$
and is therefore onto. Consequently
\[
  N_A(K)/C_A(K)\cong S_3.
\]
If, in addition, an accepted independent result identifies the
\emph{entire} centralizer $C_A(K)=H$ and gives $|H|=1024$, then
$|N_A(K)|=6\cdot1024=6144$.
The weaker premise $H\leq C_A(K)$, $|H|=1024$ alone does not imply
this order.
