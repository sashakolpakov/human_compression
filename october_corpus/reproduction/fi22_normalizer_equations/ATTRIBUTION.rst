Classical operations and the Fi22 application
=============================================

These operations belong to Wilson's classical theory of regular maps [W].
In the convention (a,b,c) = (rho0,rho1,rho2) of [LS, Section 2.1, Figure 1],
duality is D(a,b,c)=(c,b,a), Petrie duality is P(a,b,c)=(ac,b,c), and
the opposite operation is O=DPD, with O(a,b,c)=(a,b,ac). They generate S3
on the three nonidentity elements of K=<a,c>. This framework is already
used in the `L023 recovery
<../../papers/recoveries/L023/v1/PUBLICATION.rst>`_.

Every ordered basis (u,v) of K satisfies <u,b,v>=<K,b>. For a finite
nonabelian simple G, the stated involution and generation hypotheses then
allow the separate `intersection theorem in rank three
<../fi22_markings/intersection_theorem.tex>`_ to be applied to each triple.
This is a logical application; it does not change an earlier certificate's
intersection claim.

The embedding N_A(K)/C_A(K) into Aut(K)=S3 is also standard. Its image
need not be all of S3. The supplied certificate checks concrete r,t on all
3,510 points; they induce the required two transpositions of K. Their group
membership and the identification H=C_A(K), including its order, remain
separate stated premises. Sharing a classification between candidates
further requires verified words in H acting on their two triangles.

No historical novelty, sixfold division of class counts, triality of an
individual map, or coverage of all candidates is claimed here.

[W] S. E. Wilson, `Operators over regular maps
<https://doi.org/10.2140/pjm.1979.81.559>`_, Pacific J. Math. 81(2)
(1979), 559–568, especially p. 562.

[LS] D. Leemans and K. Stokes, `Incidence geometries with trialities coming
from maps with Wilson trialities <https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.08215v2>`_,
Section 2.1 and Figure 1.
