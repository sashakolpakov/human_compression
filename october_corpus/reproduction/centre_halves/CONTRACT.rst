Local reproduction of the ordinary H_b halves
=============================================

Scope and borrowed mathematics
------------------------------

This package enumerates the ordinary unrooted half domains H_b for b=1..4.
The domain has an endpoint, five endpoint rows, six side vertices and b
intrinsic degree-two ports. The endpoint meets all rows; a row meets an
independent four-subset of the side; a port meets an independent two-subset.
The side is triangle-free with exactly 5-b edges. Every side column obeys
alpha+beta+degree=5, including zero alpha. All twelve nonports have degree
five. The original problem is attributed to Cambie, Goedgebeur, Jooken and
Van den Eede, arXiv:2511.21144v3, Appendix B, Table 6. This half calculation
does not alone prove the complete 32-vertex cage count.

halves.py is the exact previously reviewed mathematical kernel 1129b778.
THEORY.tex is the exact 697f8edc proof; THEORY_PEER.json authenticates its
independent handoff. The complete full-cage centre/gluing dependency closure
belongs to the aggregate order32 assembly. Standard incidence margins,
finite permutation actions, witnesses and canonical minima are borrowed.
No novelty or new conjecture claim is made.

The fourteen side types are all retained, including infeasible types:
seven with four edges, four with three, two with two and one with one.
Their Source-literal schedule is in SHAPE_SCHEDULE.json. No feasibility
result or ordinary count is used to omit a type. Source domain dimensions
are computed by the worker, not copied from a reference result.

Enumeration and ordinary identification
--------------------------------------

The exact old dimensions, run_interval, cover, interval_family, code_lines
and collect functions are reused without a body change. The raw ordinal
is j*n_a+i in the complete rectangle of side-port multisets and five-row
multisets. All raw cells are covered before symmetry and margin filters.
The side automorphism and port-stabilizer reductions remain unchanged.
A stop retains only the completed half-open raw prefix, with its counter
partition and exact next_raw_cell. An interrupted cell remains unpaid.
Every zero-size raw domain requires its explicit [0,0] receipt.

Ordinary codes use every eligible unmarked root and every isomorphism of
its extracted side to a standard side. Ports are intrinsic by degree.
The original full-vertex permutation witnesses and canonical fixed-point
checks are unchanged. Zero endpoint columns are allowed at every site.

Collection requires exact raw coverage of every side type for its b,
without gaps, overlaps or a second empty interval. It joins every selected
record to its original raw ordinal, row tuple and port tuple, verifies
its full ordinary witness, and forms the sorted unique union. It does
not add marked counts or side counts. A full-stream collection returns
h_b; a proper record interval has complete_ordinary_union=false and
h_b=null. The package deliberately omits the unused old merge/seal modes.
If a full stream does not fit 2,048 selected records, this minimal package
cannot claim its complete h_b. No cap is raised to make it fit.

Local binding is not execution provenance
----------------------------------------

The old caller required independent Root gates. This distinct frontend
requires local file descriptors and emits HB_LOCAL_RECEIPT_V1. It never
creates Root SDK events, root='/root', positive Root reviews or adoption.
The local campaign name is a join label, not a secret or a signature.

The --bind operation reads a canonical template, snapshots the actual
control and raw-receipt files, hashes their complete bytes, validates their
local contexts, and declares their exact data descriptors. It authenticates
all declared files before and after binding, then writes one fresh job.
The --job operation independently checks that job, its Source/proof/control
context, all declared inputs and final whole-file identities. It performs
one enumeration or one collection, not a hidden loop of invocations.

The protocol detects stale hashes, context substitutions, old Root receipt
schemas, duplicate input versions and missing domain coverage. It cannot
prove from a plaintext receipt alone that another process really ran.
That limitation is explicit: local acquisition is not independent audit.
A recorded Root execution requires its genuine external SDK frames,
supervisor evidence and separate review. A clone's local receipt is not
such evidence merely because its numerical fields match a known result.

elapsed_seconds and run paths change receipt bytes. Bind the exact new
files after each run. Do not strip timing fields to reuse a historical
hash, or copy a historical Root review into a local job. Follow-up family
digests bind the new complete descriptors. Compare only stable scientific
code streams and complete domain/count fields after successful completion.

Unchanged bounds
----------------

Each worker retains 128 MiB RSS, 120 seconds science and 150 seconds outer
wall; RAM admission/floor 4/3 GiB and disk 10/8 GiB. The unchanged supervisor
pair supplies the outer process-tree sampling, lock, admission and floors.
Its optional dependency is psutil==7.2.2. The worker volume must be the
bundle directory, where all local outputs reside. The sampled supervisor
does not prove absence of unseen detached descendants.

One invocation has at most 4,096 compatible enumeration attempts, or 4,096
selected witness plus fixed-point units. A collection selects at most 2,048
marked records. There are at most 4,096 output records, 1,024 bytes per line
and 2 MiB worker output, with 64 KiB reserved for its receipt. A binding writes
only one job of at most 64 KiB. No per-case reset or enlarged role is used.

Unique declared input extent is at most 16 MiB, at most 1,024 paths, each
file at most 8 MiB. Bootstrap is at most 256 KiB. All read admissions reserve
the final whole-input authentication and 2 MiB+64KiB output readbacks within
the 64 MiB declared-artifact read allowance. The active Source union includes
the whole caller, controls, kernel and both supervisors, at most 65,536
bytes. Standard-library, psutil and loader accesses are environment costs,
not an exact whole-filesystem byte claim.

stable_read preserves the old ordinary-file tests: canonical parent/path,
regular single-link file, size admission before opening, matching initial
and final opened fstat signatures, final pathname signature and exact
length. read_whole additionally checks the complete expected descriptor.
acquire uses the same read and records each bootstrap occurrence before
any dependent use. Input authentication uses the complete unique input
set twice; consumption reads and bootstrap occurrences are also charged.
The receipt reports declared_artifact_read_bytes and a separate reserved
readback upper bound. It does not mislabel that as every environment read.

Controls and evidence boundary
------------------------------

The proposed controls retain five explicit small presentations spanning
all b values, including zero columns; independent expansion, exhaustive
tiny root/side brute comparison, complete adjacency under the carried
permutation, arbitrary reversal, fixed points, malformed graphs, typing,
path and JSON refusals, and interrupted-cell resumption. New tests cover
fresh elapsed-field rebinding, stale hashes, wrong contexts, old Root
schemas, conflicting file versions, attempted Root flags and missing
side coverage. Their actual verdict is outstanding.

The fourteen separate side-automorphism checks and old merge-only seam
are not repeated in this minimal controls file. The unchanged kernel's
earlier reviewed controls remain attributed provenance; the new five
independent brute fixtures still compare actual canonicalization against
a separate full trial. No old verdict is transferred to the new frontend.
Fresh full-domain replays and a different-author review remain required.
No Source, controls, graph calculation, Lean or TeX build was executed
by the author of this packet.
