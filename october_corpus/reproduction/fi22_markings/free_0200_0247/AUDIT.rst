Verification and attribution
============================

The certificates are literal copies of accepted mathematical records from
the three consecutive epochs for cases 200--215, 216--231 and 232--247.
They contain 36 generation witnesses and 12 proper invariant sets. Their
indices, owners and witness digests are unchanged. The separate intersection
theorem applies to precisely those 36 generating triples.

This package changes the verifier's fixed case range and framing literals.
Its mathematical functions, input checks and 25 controls are identical to
the preceding public edition. The controls and every one of the 48 cases
were run for this edition. No earlier execution verdict was transferred.

VALIDATION.json records the Source, manifest and certificate hashes,
individual outcomes, work counts and resource measurements. Its figures
come from actual runs. Performance on another machine may differ.

The standalone verifier imports the named group, original model, membership
and class data, transport interpretation and complete maximal-subgroup
cover. It checks the supplied permutation witnesses and their exact original
row identities. The generation argument and separate intersection proof
are retained in the parent directory, with attribution and hypotheses.
Internal tool transcripts and temporary working directories are unnecessary
for running these mathematical checks from a clone.

The additions are verified instances of the existing recovery. They carry
no new whole-manuscript, conjecture, Lean or compression-measurement credit.
