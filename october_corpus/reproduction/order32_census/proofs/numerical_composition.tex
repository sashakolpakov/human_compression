\begin{corollary}
Assume the complete fixed-geodesic reduction, intrinsic centre and
ordinary gluing theorems of the retained editions~\cite{reduction,halves}.
Assume also that the separately reviewed complete finite ordinary unions
have been accepted, with
\[
 H=1395,\qquad(h_1,h_2,h_3,h_4)=(2,5,36,115),
\]
and that the accepted bipartite enumeration has ordinary count $16467$.
Here $H$ is the enlarged nonnegative-column two-centre half domain, and
$h_b$ is the ordinary unrooted centre-three half domain of order $12+b$
with $b$ intrinsic degree-two ports. Then the ordinary nonbipartite
connected quintic graphs of order $32$, girth four and diameter eight
number $8941$. Including the bipartite graphs gives $25408$ ordinary
isomorphism classes.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Theorem \texttt{total} of~\cite{reduction} is an exhaustive ordinary
classification by the following nine intrinsic centre strata. Its
component-identification and gluing proofs have already removed choices
of endpoints, middle sets, port labels, component presentations and the
permitted simultaneous permutations of equal-weight centre classes.
They give the formulas in the second column, before any substitution.
\[
\begin{array}{lrr}
\toprule
\text{intrinsic centre and full-graph twin classes}&
 \text{ordinary count formula}&\text{value}\\
\midrule
\text{one class of size }2&6H&8370\\
\text{one class of size }3&h_1h_4+h_2h_3=2h_4+5h_3&410\\
\text{one class of size }4&6&6\\
\text{two independent classes of sizes }(1,2)&h_4&115\\
\text{two independent classes of sizes }(1,3)&6&6\\
\text{two independent classes of sizes }(2,2)&4&4\\
P_3&6&6\\
K_{2,2}&6&6\\
K_{2,3}&18&18\\
\bottomrule
\end{array}
\]
The centre is intrinsic. Its false-twin classes here are defined by equal
\emph{full-graph open neighborhoods}, not merely equal neighborhoods
inside the induced centre. Their cardinalities and their mutual
adjacencies form an isomorphism-invariant weighted centre quotient.
Thus an independent centre consisting of one twin class cannot be
identified with an independent centre consisting of two distinct twin
classes, and the different weight patterns are distinguished. The
nonempty induced centre graphs distinguish the last three rows. This
is exactly the ordinary disjointness proved in~\cite{reduction}; it
justifies adding the nine rows rather than adding geodesic descriptions
or the twenty-four scalar nonisolated-middle rows.

The complete half collections are ordinary unions across every allowed
side shape and every admissible root. They are neither sums of marked
records nor sums of side-shape counts. Substitution therefore yields
\[
\begin{split}
 N_{\rm nb}
 &=6\cdot1395+5\cdot36+3\cdot115+46\\
 &=8370+180+345+46=8941.
\end{split}
\]
Equivalently the centre-three product is
$2\cdot115+5\cdot36=410$, as displayed in the table.
The earlier strict positive-column half set of size $1209$ is an
externally checked subset of the enlarged set of size $1395$. Its
singleton-middle contribution $6\cdot1209=7254$ is consequently already
inside the first row's $8370$; it is never added a second time. A larger
middle-set presentation of the same graph cannot supply a new ordinary
class merely by receiving a different local description.

Bipartiteness is an ordinary isomorphism invariant. The separately
accepted exhaustive bipartite ordinary union~\cite{bipartite} is
disjoint from all nine nonbipartite strata. Hence the complete order-32
ordinary count, under the stated hypotheses, is
\[
 N_{32}=16467+8941=25408.
\]
The value $25408$ is the resulting composition, not an expected-count
input, termination test or acceptance predicate of any producer.
\end{proof}
