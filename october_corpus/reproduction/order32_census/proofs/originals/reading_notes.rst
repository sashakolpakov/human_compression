The two leading themes are extremal strip costs and finite graph enumeration.
The paper's local Moore-tree bounds, repeatable-strip construction and bridge

Before a recovery can be accepted, the asymptotic argument needs a complete
account of exact diameter. Constructing a sequence with increasing diameters
alone does not immediately prove a bound for every sufficiently large diameter
in the stated definition of f. The loop-removal proof must justify its bounded
strip selection and how the retained layer length relates to the resulting
graph diameter. These are requests for proof completion, not counterexamples
to Theorems1–2.

The literal Algorithm1 adds n-M(k;g,d) isolated vertices to a tree whose stated
order is M-prime(k;g,d). These bounds can differ: at (k,g,d)=(3,4,4), M-prime=10
and M=12. The printed instruction therefore does not give n vertices. A recovery
must add n-|V(T)|. The two retrieved original C files allocate a graph of the
requested order independently; the printed discrepancy alone says nothing
about their numerical outputs. The generator's README explicitly requires final
filtering to obtain exact girth and exact diameter. Complete implementation and
run audit remain pending.
