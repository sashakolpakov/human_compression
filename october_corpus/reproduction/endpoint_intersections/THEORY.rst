The finite certificate and the imported generation criterion
============================================================

Let G be the named inner G2(11) in its faithful natural Cayley action on
V=F11^7. The original four matrices a,b,c,e must belong to this acquired
model; a public digest does not establish that premise. Put L=<a,b,c>,
R=<b,c,e> and M=<b,c>. The public program verifies finite identities,
then states its conclusion conditional on the imported classification.

The commuting original involutions a,c give four rational simultaneous
sign spaces. The certificate supplies bases of lengths1,2,2,2. The
program checks both actual sign images on every nonzero row. Two rows
in a length-two block are independent because their normalized
projective lines differ. Distinct sign spaces are in direct sum, so
these seven independent eigenvectors fill V and prove completeness of
the four spaces without a Gaussian-elimination solver.

For every supplied cyclic certificate, six fresh earlier-parent images
under(a,b,c,c) give seven actual word images of its root. The equation
A*B=I proves those rows span V. All37 roots are checked afresh for their
a/c signs. Their normalized projective lines are distinct, with counts
1,12,12,12 in the four complete spaces. These are all the projective
lines because a two-dimensional F11 space has12. If a nonzero
L-invariant subspace existed, one of the polynomial joint-sign
projections would give a line in it. The corresponding cyclic root and
all its word images would then lie in that subspace and span V.
Consequently L is irreducible over F11. No absolute-irreducibility claim
is needed, and the public check has no enumeration-order dependency.

The point-prefix certificate independently reconstructs3025 distinct
actual L-word images of a nonzero seed fixed by a,c. The program has
checked that I,a,c,ac are four distinct matrices of a Klein four.
Orbit-stabilizer therefore gives |L|>=4*3025=12100, without requiring
closure of that point prefix. The literal original-label word(bc)^3
is replayed and has square different from I and fourth power I.
It gives an element of exact order4 in L and the relation(bc)^12=I.

The imported complete maximal-subgroup classification implies that an
irreducible subgroup of this natural G2(11), of order>12096 and with an
order-four element, is G. The parabolics have proper rational fixed
spaces of their normal11-radicals; the full A2 normalizers preserve
rational lines, and the quaternion row preserves a3+4decomposition.
The remaining small rows have order<=12096, and J1 has elementary
abelian Sylow2 subgroups and no order-four element. Thus L=G under
these attributed imports. Ordinary F11-irreducibility suffices.

The literal retained Schreier module checks the entire1320-point right
orbit, all3960closure edges, the closed derived stabilizer, and every
Schreier loop. It verifies stabilizer order1320 and |R|=1742400. Its
full reconstruction is run once. No central involution, group-type
recognition or supplier search is needed here. Since two involutions
b,c generate elements of the form(bc)^j or(bc)^j*b, their subgroup has
order<=24. Hence

              L intersection R = R != M,

which violates the rank-four intersection property. This is a
particular finite certificate, conditional on the named model and
classification imports, rather than a new classification theorem.
