Attribution and mathematical scope
==================================

The finite orbit/stabilizer decomposition and Schreier word argument are
standard group theory: H is the disjoint union of S*T_i because every full
edge T_i*g_j=S_e*T_target closes the union, all S elements are actual words
fixing the seed, and the orbit rows are distinct. The full matrix equations
and omitted-mark membership test are independently checked, not inferred
from a supplied order or hash. No novel abstract theorem is claimed.

The separate conditional generation application uses the complete odd-q
maximal-subgroup classification of Kleidman(J.Algebra117(1988),30-71), the
full pre-exclusion list in Nguyen(arXiv0910.4994v1, proofTheorem4.4), Wilson's
natural Cayley-module line-normalizer description(The Finite Simple Groups,
section4.2.6), and the ATLAS order of J1. The retained original faithful
G2(11) model and matrix membership are external assumptions. A prime19
matrix preserving a rational line fixes it because gcd(19,10)=1; fixed
dimension1 identifies that line. An orbit3080>10 rules out a line-preserving
overgroup. Any remaining proper maximal overgroup has order175560, equal
to the certified end; it is therefore that end. This proves maximality
without naming the end from its order. PROOF.tex gives the complete argument,
references and fourNG/threeGEN table. No generation conclusion alone pays
rank4 intersection properties or any whole-manuscript obligation.
