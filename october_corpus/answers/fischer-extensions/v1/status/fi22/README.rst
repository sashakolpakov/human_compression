Fi22 source status
==================

The precise source target is a possible exception in Piedade–Tranchida,
Theorem 5.3, arXiv:2605.04646v2, physical page 19. It asks for both a regular
rank-three polytope and a regular rank-three hypertope with nonlinear diagram
for the proper almost-simple extension of Fi22. This is Fi22:2 because the
outer group has order two. It is not phrased as a numbered conjecture.

The primary arXiv page checked on 11 October 2026 still lists version 2,
last revised 28 May 2026, as the current edition:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04646 . Its local PDF is exactly hash-bound in
evidence.json. No newer arXiv revision is displayed. That observation does
not rule out a separate paper, a catalogue entry or an unpublished solution.

The bounded queries in evidence.json did not locate a primary resolution
of the exact simultaneous target. Related primary abstracts concern other
ranks, other automorphism groups or other incidence diagrams. This is not
an exhaustive literature review or evidence of priority.
