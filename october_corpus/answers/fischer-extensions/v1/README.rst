Two Fischer-group existence cases
=================================

The possible Fi22 and Fi24-prime exceptions in Piedade--Tranchida,
The geometry of wreath and semi-direct products, arXiv:2605.04646v2,
Theorem5.3 are answered by explicit regular rank-three geometries.
These answers are separate from compression of the ten affirmative cases
in the source. They add no original to the corpus and no compression factor.

     - Triangular hypertope
     - Complete proof
   * - Fi22:2
     - {6,13}
     - (6,6,13)
     - `Three-page proof <fi22/fischer22.pdf>`_
   * - Fi24-prime:2
     - {6,35}
     - (6,6,35)

Both manuscripts include complete generation, incidence, residue and full
automorphism arguments. All programs and saved witnesses are published.
Distinct reviewers checked each proof and all actual finite intersections
and anchored flags using a separate coset method. The `case ledger
<cases.json>`_ states the exact source target, borrowed inputs, our contribution,
review, current literature limits and missing formalization for each case.

Fi22 generation is checked by equality to its named primitive permutation
group. Fi24 generation uses actual elements of orders23 and29 and the retained
complete maximal-subgroup classification. Its two external Atlas permutation
files total4,425,390bytes; exact primary URLs and hashes are in
`retained_inputs.json <fi24/retained_inputs.json>`_. These data and the
classification are not hidden or treated as new theorems.

The source has two possible exceptions to one theorem, not two numbered
conjectures. A bounded literature check located no separate resolution of
these exact targets. This does not establish priority. The assertions concern
the full extensions, not the simple Fischer socles. There is no Lean
verification of these complete proofs or the GAP algorithms yet.

Frozen packets retain their historical pending-review and other-case-untouched
wording. The later `root admission <admission.json>`_ records acceptance of
both cases. The original theorem and standard constructions retain their
authors' attribution. The saved witnesses and these complete certificates
are this project's contribution; their historical novelty is not asserted.

Each packet contains its replay instructions. Both published review checkers
pass; add their --programs option for fresh complete finite calculations.
The Fi24 replay also requires the two external Atlas files. Primary source
PDFs, Atlas files, search traces and catalogue files are not redistributed.
