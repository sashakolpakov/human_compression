1. Escape and the general commutation graph
--------------------------------------------

Commutation with two matrices is preserved under their product and,
for an invertible matrix, its inverse. Thus the commutant of X among
the representation images contains every product of the proposed marks.
If an actual g in the same G has an image not commuting with X, g is
outside their subgroup. Neither injectivity of rho nor invertibility
of X is required. The representation and the provenance of g are
essential; a convenient unrelated ambient matrix is not an escape.

For each unknown j, its far-commutations with the known marks put its
image in A(S_j)'. Requiring X in A(S_j)'' therefore makes that unknown
commute with X. Intersecting with the known matrices' commutant handles
the fixed marks. This proves the exclusion simultaneously for every
allowed choice of unknowns, without computing their full group
centralizers or enumerating them. A(S_j)<=A(S_j)'' suffices for the
smaller-space variant. Equality with the double commutant is never used.
For empty S_j the double commutant is the scalar algebra, by the
matrix-unit argument; no escaping scalar witness exists.

2. All ranks at least five and the four coefficients
----------------------------------------------------

For rank r>=5, position2 commutes with0 and r-1; position r-2 commutes
with0 and1. Every intervening position3 through r-3 commutes with all
three fixed positions. For r=5 this interval is empty, as required.
Putting R=F[A,B,E_0], inclusion P,Q<=R gives R'<=P',Q' and hence
P'',Q''<=R''. Consequently the displayed W_* centralizes those
intervening unknowns as well. There is no missing rank6 middle case.

A and E_0 commute and square to1, so all their words reduce to
I,A,E_0,AE_0. Their algebra is commutative of dimension at most4 in
every characteristic, including characteristic2; linear independence
is not asserted. For X in P intersect Q, the remaining explicit
condition XB=BX supplies the fixed-mark commutation not automatic from
P. The membership and commutation conditions are homogeneous linear
conditions on the four coefficients. They are not presumed true for
an arbitrary polynomial or inferred from a group-order label.

3. Word-algebra certificates and the half-turn
-----------------------------------------------

If a full K=C_G(a,e) is actually generated by the acquired K_i, checking
X against their images covers every c in K by products and inverses.
Here the term K-basis means a complete group generating list, not an
assertion that arbitrary endomorphisms are linear combinations of it.
Every d centralizing a,b commutes with every word in A,B and every
linear combination of those words. Thus an exact word-algebra proof
of X needs no full H acquisition. Explicit A,B,E_0 checks are retained.
A finite supplied linear combination proves membership in the algebra;
it need not certify that a producer's basis was complete.

For producer completeness, a span containing I and closed under right
multiplication by A and B contains every word by induction on length.
Its elements were themselves built from words, giving equality with
F[A,B]. At most n^2 independent endomorphisms can be retained. This
dimension bound proves termination of basis enlargement, not a runtime
fit promise for an implementation or its independence tests.

The identities A(AB+BA)=B+ABA=(AB+BA)A and the analogous B identity
hold because A^2=B^2=I. E_0 commutation is inherited from A and B.
K-generator commutations and an original escape remain separate checks.
For the group half-turn, a,b invert ab, so its even-order half-turn z
is nonidentity and fixed by both. E_0's group preimage commutes with it;
thus z belongs to abelian K and centralizes all K. All of C_G(a,b)
centralizes the word z. This proves the stated rank-five centreless
group obstruction, subject to acquired exact-order/full-K hypotheses.
No order for a currently pending prefix follows from this proof alone.

4. Complete commutant bases
----------------------------

The commutation map L has m=n^2 domain coordinates and s*m codomain
coordinates. LQ=0 places the columns of Q in its kernel. A nonzero
d-square minor of Q proves their independence, so nullity(L)>=d.
A nonzero (m-d)-square minor of L proves rank(L)>=m-d. Rank-nullity
then forces both bounds to equality. Hence all kernel elements are
linear combinations of the supplied columns, and commuting with each
column is sufficient for the double commutant. Neither a merely
independent incomplete kernel list nor LQ=0 alone would suffice.

The stipulated distinct in-range minor indices, fixed coordinate order,
typed field arithmetic and actual products with supplied inverse
matrices are necessary checker obligations. The empty minor convention
correctly handles d=0 and d=m; a malformed out-of-range d cannot pass
the stated minor dimensions. This is linear completeness and does not
prove equality with any named group centralizer.

5. Affine fibres and permutation orbitals
------------------------------------------

All defining constraints on X are homogeneous linear equations. A
nonzero escape entry ell(X) may be rescaled by its inverse to1. Once
one solution X_0 exists, subtraction identifies the entire solution set
with X_0+(W intersect ker ell). Additive translation by that kernel is
free and transitive, so the torsor terminology is literal. Checking all
entries for a finite acquired ambient generating list is complete for
finding an escape in the chosen W: if X commutes with their images it
commutes with the entire image group. It is not complete for every
possible subgroup obstruction outside W. Failure remains inconclusive.

For a permutation representation, conjugating a matrix by the action
simultaneously permutes its row and column labels. The invariant matrices
are precisely those constant on pair orbits. The orbit indicators have
disjoint nonempty supports; they are independent and span over every
field, including modular characteristic. No semisimplicity or division
by orbit sizes is needed. The resulting square-domain size may still
be impractical, as the note explicitly qualifies.
