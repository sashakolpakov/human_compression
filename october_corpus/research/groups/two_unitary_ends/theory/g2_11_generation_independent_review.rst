The unique-line argument is valid. The multiplicative order of 11 modulo
37 is six. Separability of X^37-1 and its nontrivial factors of degree six
force a nonidentity seven-dimensional representation of the cyclic group
of order 37 to have a one-dimensional fixed space and one nontrivial
six-dimensional constituent. A rational invariant line must be fixed
pointwise, because gcd(37,10)=1. Thus a nonzero fixed row and one generator
moving its span prove generation. If all generators preserve the span,
the subgroup lies in its proper unitary normalizer. The general
structure-stabilizer lemma correctly includes the extra proper-stabilizer
