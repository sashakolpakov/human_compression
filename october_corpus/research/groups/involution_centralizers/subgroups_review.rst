be divided out uniquely; equal determinants leave an actual SL2 kernel,

The main dichotomy is exhaustive. Restricted kernels are odd by Cauchy,
so the Klein four injects into both images. The joint map is injective
when z is avoided. A trivial restricted kernel gives the1320 bound;
otherwise the opposite kernel images are nontrivial odd normal subgroups,
and the joint embedding gives576. No hidden divisibility inference enters.

The marking corollary uses all the standing conditions correctly. The

makes a,c distinct; their commutation gives the needed Klein four. The

The rooted word-DAG description proves membership and its distinctness
