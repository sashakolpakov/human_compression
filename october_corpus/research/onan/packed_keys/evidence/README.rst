Historical evidence, preserved bytes
====================================

This directory contains unchanged historical documents, not new portable
acceptance gates. ``HistoricalManifest.json`` maps each relative copy to its
original path and exact size/digest. Absolute paths, original authorship,
Source-freeze wording and original tool identifiers inside those documents
are deliberately preserved.

``source/core/`` and ``source/portable/`` retain the respective Source packets,
arguments/interfaces, inverses, checks/readsets, author methods/preparation
captures and distinct Source reviews/executions. Their Source4 maps identify
the exact eight files in the parent module, while their old private paths
remain historical paths rather than public filesystem promises.

``actual/core18/`` and ``actual/portable_io10/`` retain the genuine whole Root
reports, terminal/capture/guard receipt and complete parent stdout/stderr.
They record successful unchanged harnesses with true exit0. The I/O harness
performs eleven native child calls: six positive and five expected refusals,
counted as ten existing controls. Its child buffers and temporary tapes were
not retained; only the complete parent report/logs and acquired aggregate
scope are asserted. Empty parent stderr does not assert empty negative-child
stderr.

``actual/metadata_review/`` retains the genuine independent metadata guard
capture and its physical clearance. The larger original distinct review,
execution, checks and Root adoption gate are referenced by exact historical
pins in the manifest rather than copied with their full input-cut tables.

The omitted material includes complete scientific/runtime/compiler input
cuts, SDK/header/library trees, compiler/linker binaries, workspaces, generated
objects/executables and bytecode caches. Some retained records refer to those
private files. Those unresolved references are explicit: this small evidence
bundle cannot independently replay or freshly authenticate the entire old
closed history. It provides provenance for the historical synthetic 28 checks
and Source identities; a new run through ``../check.py`` is separate evidence
and must use its own actual compiler/runtime/resource boundary.

Nothing here grants named ON partition, generation, Original completion or
whole Lean credit. The parent ``SourceManifest.json`` is the unchanged initial
preparation snapshot: its README pins and unrun status describe that earlier
stage. The later ``reproduction/manifest.json`` and its byte-exact records
document a real public-layout build and all eighteen plus ten synthetic
checks. They do not change the older records or extend their proof scope.
