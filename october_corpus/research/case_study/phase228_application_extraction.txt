Prescribed rank, type and two-power order
Abstract
For integers 𝑑 ≥ 3 , 𝑛 ≥ 5 and 𝑘1, … , 𝑘𝑑−1 ≥ 2 satisfying ∑𝑖 𝑘𝑖 ≤ 𝑛−1 , we construct a string C-group
of rank 𝑑, order 2𝑛 and type {2𝑘1, … , 2𝑘𝑑−1}. This recovers the existence theorem of Hou, Feng and
Leemans. The construction attaches dihedral groups to a product of elementary abelian factors and one
affine square group. We verify all hypotheses of the supported-extension theorem, the exact order and
every pair-product order. A standard correspondence then gives the regular polytope. We also include
the elementary order bound showing that the parameter inequality is necessary. The construction works
throughout the stated range without a separate small-order census.
1. Markings and the source theorem
Let 𝐺 = ⟨𝑟 0, … , 𝑟𝑑−1⟩, where the 𝑟𝑖 are nonidentity involutions. For 𝐽 ⊆ {0, … , 𝑑 − 1} , write
𝐺𝐽 = ⟨𝑟 𝑗 ∶ 𝑗 ∈ 𝐽 ⟩ . The marking has the intersection property if
𝐺𝐽 ∩ 𝐺𝐾 = 𝐺 𝐽∩𝐾 (𝐽 , 𝐾 ⊆ {0, … , 𝑑 − 1}).
It is a string C-group if it also satisfies [𝑟𝑖, 𝑟𝑗] = 1 whenever |𝑖 − 𝑗| > 1 . Its rank is 𝑑, and its type is
the ordered list of adjacent product orders (|𝑟0𝑟1|, … , |𝑟𝑑−2𝑟𝑑−1|). We use 𝐷2𝑚 for the dihedral group
of order 2𝑚.
We retain the regular-polytope/string-C-group correspondence in [MS02, Section 2E], in exactly the form
stated in Section 10 of the companion umbrella [UA]. A string C-group with its distinguished marking
is the full automorphism group of a regular abstract polytope of the same rank and type; the group acts
regularly on flags. Its order is therefore the number of flags. No geometric recognition stronger than this
correspondence is needed below.

For a group of order 2𝑛 and type (2), Proposition 2 gives ∑𝑖 𝑘𝑖 ≤ 𝑛 − 1 . Combined with Theorem 1,
the inequality is exactly the existence condition within the stated finite, nondegenerate two-power setting.
Since every 𝑘𝑖 ≥ 2, it also gives 2𝑑 − 1 ≤ 𝑛. This concerns possible types and orders, not classification
of all realizing marked groups.
5. Source coverage and retained work
The intake is one edition: [HFL20], the eight-page revised author manuscript dated 8 November 2019.
The principal source contract is Theorem 1.2. The proof covers every stated parameter, including 5 ≤
𝑛 ≤ 9 , and the regular-polytope interpretation. Proposition 2 recovers the recalled order bound with
its original attribution. We do not claim the source’s particular defining presentation or every auxiliary
proposition, and do not solve its recalled classification problem for all two-power orders.
The common proofs in [UA] are the affine normal form and square/diagonal seed, Boolean deletions,
supported-kernel generation, exact local recognition and order formula. The local work here is the choice
of base and maps, each proper-parabolic injection, the distant-marker calculation, and the parameter
3
calculation. These were present in the reviewed combined manuscript and have been reworked into a
closed source-specific argument. No new historical existence theorem is claimed.
The geometric correspondence in Section 1 is retained without proof. Standard elementary group and
integer arithmetic are also used. No GAP program, saved census, atlas table, small-group classification
or theorem recognizing all square maps is an input to this construction. Existing Lean certificates verify
their declared supported-kernel core; the full affine premise and complete source-to-conclusion chain
are not presently formalized in Lean. The accompanying scope distinguishes those declarations from
any newly checked arithmetic.
