\section*{The elementary transfer statement}

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:automorphism}
Let $\alpha:G\to G'$ be an isomorphism and let $(r_i)_{i\in E}$ be a
labelled family in $G$.  For every $A\subseteq E$,
\[
              \alpha\bigl(\gen{r_i:i\in A}\bigr)
                       =\gen{\alpha(r_i):i\in A}.
\]
For any subgroups $H,K\le G$,
\[
                       \alpha(H\cap K)=\alpha(H)\cap\alpha(K).
\]
Consequently generation, nonidentity involutions, specified commutations
and every labelled subset intersection are preserved by $\alpha$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
An element of a generated subgroup is a word in the indicated generators
and their inverses.  Applying $\alpha$ gives the same word in their
images, proving one inclusion in the first equality.  Applying the same
argument to $\alpha^{-1}$ proves the other inclusion.

The image of an element in $H\cap K$ belongs to both images.  Conversely,
if $x\in\alpha(H)\cap\alpha(K)$, write $x=\alpha(h)=\alpha(k)$ with
$h\in H$, $k\in K$.  Injectivity gives $h=k\in H\cap K$, proving the
second equality.  In particular, if the family satisfies
\[
 \gen{r_i:i\in A}\cap\gen{r_i:i\in B}
                      =\gen{r_i:i\in A\cap B},
\]
then its image satisfies the corresponding equality with the very same
labels.  Generation follows by taking $A=E$, and the remaining assertions
are preserved by an isomorphism directly.
\end{proof}

If the first generates $G$ and has the full rank-four intersection

\[
 (s^{-1}as,s^{-1}bs,s^{-1}cs,s^{-1}ds)=(a,b,c',d).

therefore applies simultaneously to all subsets of the four labels.

\end{proof}
