The eight programs below are retained in full. Five are required by the
finite classification proofs: the degree-six census and the four Hall--Janko
programs. The formula and two Suzuki programs are additional diagnostics,
conservatively included in the complete program boundary. Their finite tests
do not prove the formulas for arbitrary degrees or field exponents. The two
full execution drivers are separately inventoried; the selected modes run
only these three applications. No source classification table is used as a
filter on the census.
