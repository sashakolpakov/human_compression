\begin{abstract}
An ordered string marking of a Suzuki group can be normalized to two field
coordinates. We identify its generated group by the joint field of definition
and prove the intersection property directly. The common orbit theorem then
gives both counting formulas of Kiefer and Leemans, including their equality
in every allowed field, the prime-degree specialization and the absence of
self-dual examples. The finite diagnostics are separate from the uniform
written argument.
\end{abstract}
The source is A.~Kiefer and D.~Leemans, \emph{On the number of abstract regular
polytopes whose automorphism group is a Suzuki simple group Sz(q)},
Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series A \textbf{117} (2010), 1248--1257.
The exact consulted author manuscript is dated 27 September 2011 and has
twelve pages. The statements below preserve that edition's two principal
counting theorems and their stated geometric consequences.

The normalization and generation arguments retain the classical Suzuki-group
structure used in that source, including the work of Suzuki and L\"uneburg.
The earlier rank result of Leemans (2006) is credited at its restatement in
the source; it is not an additional source recovered by this application.
The shared counting principles are the classical orbit--stabilizer identity,
fixed-point counting and M\"obius inversion, stated with complete proofs in
[UD]. The contribution here is the explicit route from those principles to
the normalized data, with the local hypotheses discharged.

\section*{Verification and references}
The companion appendix preserves both complete finite diagnostics: the
finite-field enumeration and formula comparisons, and the matrix calculation
for all normalized triples at $q=8$. They do not prove a statement for
arbitrary odd field exponent. The written normalization, subfield-position,
intersection and counting proofs provide that conclusion.

[UD] \emph{Stabilizers, extensions and the enumeration of finite geometries},
common paper in this topic. Its full text is part of the proof dependencies.
The classical inputs used in Section 1 are H.~Lüneburg,
\emph{Translation planes}, Springer, 1980, and M.~Suzuki,
\emph{On a class of doubly transitive groups}, Annals of Mathematics
\textbf{75} (1962), 105--145. The earlier theorem restated as source
Theorem 3 is D.~Leemans, \emph{Almost simple groups of Suzuki type acting
on polytopes}, Proceedings of the American Mathematical Society
\textbf{134} (2006), 3649--3651. The counting result is Kiefer--Leemans;
these structural inputs keep their earlier attribution. Fermat's congruence and M\"obius
inversion are not results newly attributed to Kiefer and Leemans or to this
project. The standard string-C-group/polytope correspondence is retained
from the established theory of abstract regular polytopes.
