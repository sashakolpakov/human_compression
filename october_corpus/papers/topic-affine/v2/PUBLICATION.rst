================================================

The intake is Hou, Feng and Leemans, *Regular 3-polytopes of order 2^n p*,
arXiv:2001.02945v2, 14 July 2024. The original principal results retain that
attribution. One affine kernel and a uniform test for period intersections
supply the cubic families. The common paper also includes the complete
supported-kernel and square-group proofs used by the application. Those
proofs have their full cost in this topic even though another topic uses them.

construction, all stated cubic families and the exact existence range for
type {6,2^s}. One auxiliary period presentation needs the explicit sign
correction recorded against the bound source edition. This is not a claim
about a later journal edition or a newly solved conjecture. The equal-label
existence question was already answered by Montero-Weiss and Li-Zhang;
its group construction is proved locally and attributed.
