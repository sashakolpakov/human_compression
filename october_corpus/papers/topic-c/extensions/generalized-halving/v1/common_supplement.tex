\begin{abstract}
The component construction of geometric halving has a precise
residual-connectivity condition: intersecting nonbipartite shadows of
an incident flag must not create a bipartite shadow. We give the full
residue transport under this condition and compute every maximal
stabilizer without assuming residual connectivity of the output.
This proves the literal coset identification independently of the
stronger hypertope-preservation assertion. We also give an exact
criterion for a second leaf and an induced-neighbor coset criterion.
The accompanying application treats the regular counterexample and
all branches of cubic-toroid halving.
\end{abstract}
