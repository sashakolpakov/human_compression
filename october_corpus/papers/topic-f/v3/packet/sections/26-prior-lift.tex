\section{The previously handled small even range}
For even $6\leq m\leq p+1$, put $n=m-2$. The odd construction
above has $e=1$, $r=n$ and gives $G_0$ of order $2p^{m-1}$
with type $\{p^2,2p\}$. Residue modulo $\pi$ on the module,
with $\beta,\sigma$ sent to zero, defines a surjection to the
additive group $\mathbb F_p$. The carry has zero residue because
$r\geq4$; both $T$ and conjugation preserve residue. Thus all
presentation relations are respected. It sends $a_0=\beta s$ to
$1$ and $b_0=s^{-1}\sigma$ to $-1$.
The common cyclic-lift theorem gives order $2p^m$ and type
$\{p^2,2p^2\}$. Its type product is $2p^4<2p^m$.
Together with the two preceding constructions this covers every
$m\geq5$. The small-order theorem proves the complementary
nonexistence for non-tight chiral polyhedra at $m<5$.
