Odd kernels and cyclotomic constructions
=======================================

Two source manuscripts now use one common theory. The complete written
replacement consists of the seven-page `common paper <packet/umbrella.pdf>`_,
the five-page `regular application <packet/odd-kernel-polyhedra.pdf>`_ and
the six-page `chiral application <packet/chiral-odd-kernels.pdf>`_.

The common arguments combine normal odd kernels, character components,
cyclotomic coordinates and group extensions. The chiral application uses
class-three collection and exact presentation bounds to treat the small
orders uniformly. Its constructions prove the group order, generation,
intersection property and absence of a reversing automorphism for every
stated parameter. Finite examples are supplementary checks.

The recovered results retain their original attribution. Cyclotomic
arithmetic and the cited finite-group theorems are classical inputs.
The common construction, uniform small-order argument and repaired
quotient route are identified in the proofs and the complete
`source record <packet/scope.json>`_. The earlier central lift is reproduced
with its attribution; this edition adds no solved-conjecture or priority
credit. The source's literal use of “tight” in Section 7 is distinguished
from its intended non-tight question.

this topic and receives no discount for overlap with another topic.

that change of representation is not mathematical compression.
