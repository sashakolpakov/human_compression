L029: complete source disposition
================================

This volume addresses the final journal edition of D. Leemans and
P. Tranchida, On residual connectedness in chiral geometries, Algebraic
Combinatorics 4 (2021), 491--499, DOI 10.5802/alco.162. The retained PDF
has one archive cover and nine article pages. No claim is made about
later editions, errata, or historical priority of the corrections.

The principal theorem remains refuted by the separately reviewed
PSL(2,13) example. The present assembly supplies the surviving valid
arguments and the reviewed auxiliary dispositions. It proposes closing
the remaining whole-source obligation for this already registered
principal refutation. It is not an ordinary recovery of a false theorem,
a second original, a new open-question solution, or a measurement.

The mathematical volume
-----------------------

* Coset residues and chamber orbits: seven pages of complete proofs,
  source attribution, and a table covering every numbered assertion.
  Its new contribution is the assembly and explanation; the underlying
  arguments are traced to the original source and retained project work.
* A chiral coset geometry with disconnected residues: the accepted five
  pages, unchanged. This is the substantive principal refutation. Its
  full automorphism group is larger than the specified marked group.
* Panels in the coset construction for a C+-group: the accepted three
  pages, unchanged. The unrestricted rank statement is false in rank
  two. The exact rank-four panel criterion and four previously acquired
  PSL(2,7) checks do not prove general firmness in higher rank.
* Chirality without firmness: the accepted two pages, unchanged. The
  seven-object example and the source's rank-one residue boundary are
  auxiliary corrections, separate from the thin principal example.

The complete.pdf volume has one introduction page and these seventeen
mathematical pages. All four TeX sources and the complete-volume TeX
source are supplied. Page counts describe the artifacts; they do not
establish any mathematical compression factor.

Scope and attribution
---------------------

claims.json lists the exact source pages, assertion labels, proof
locations, and status of each claim. Source [1] is Buekenhout and Cohen's
Diagram geometry; source [6] is Fernandes, Leemans and Weiss, Highly
symmetric hypertopes. Those expressly cited background results retain
their attribution. The elementary map and connectivity arguments needed
here are proved in the synthesis, rather than acquired from an implicit
classification premise.

The old phase276 proof bank is a source of valid arguments, not a second
intake. Its earlier pending statements remain historical records. The
accepted principal and ancillary reviews supply the later dispositions.
The full type-preserving group is distinguished throughout from the
specified group of the coset construction. The source uses right cosets;
inversion gives the exact left-coset convention of the synthesis.

Unproved corrected variants
---------------------------

General firmness in rank at least four, with no added transitivity
hypothesis, remains unproved. It is a possible corrected replacement for
the source's false unrestricted assertion, not an additional result
claimed by this volume. The rank-two example does not refute that variant.
Nor does the rank-one example for Proposition 4.7 establish any
unrestricted theorem about residues of higher rank. The source's own
preceding rank-one exclusion is stated explicitly.
