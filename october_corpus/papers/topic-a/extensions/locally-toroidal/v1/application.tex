\begin{abstract}
We recover the two infinite families of Fernandes, Leemans, Piedade and
Ivi\'c Weiss by identifying fixed affine groups and their period lattices.
The common kernel and coset theorems then give every parameter at once.
The proof includes the exact ordered residues, universal presentations,
permutation graphs and full type-preserving automorphism groups.
We also verify the paper's two finite residue corrections and correct its
subsequent presentation claims. The original constructions and principal
results remain attributed to their authors.
\end{abstract}

The source is the twelve-page author edition of \cite{source}. Its
Sections 3 and 4 concern the cubic and square families, respectively;
Section 5 concerns two finite presentations. We use AL.1--AL.2,
AA.ParabolicPeriods, UA.K, UA.SK.Helly and UA.Geometry from the common
paper \cite{common}. Their proofs are acquired once and are not repeated.
The standard Euclidean triangle and affine Coxeter realizations, and
their toroidal parameter conventions, remain explicitly retained
classical inputs. The source's preliminary incidence facts retain the
attribution given there to Tits and the earlier literature.

All group products use functional composition. Parameters are introduced
only after the fixed affine group and its integral translation lattice
have been identified. Exact presentation and finite-set calculations are
stated where required; they are not replaced by sampling parameter values.

\section{Attribution and verification}
The source's two families, their intended residue types and their
permutation graphs are prior results. The affine generators and parameter lattices are also prior material.
Our proof identifies the abstract groups with these affine models through
AL.2, computes the complete integral parabolic lattices through AL.1,
and applies the common kernel and coset theorems. For the square family,
a single fixed linear quotient and a direct summand of the translation
lattice replace the parameter-dependent subgroup and coset arguments.
The cubic flag-transitivity argument retains the source's four-coset
decomposition; the affine model and its faithful action make its
intersection and orbit assertions explicit. The common theorems,
these applications and the corrections are distinguished in the source map. The degree 32 action at
parameter 15 refutes the source's unrestricted minimum-degree suggestion;
it is intransitive and says nothing about the minimum transitive degree.
The distinction between the full cubic toroid and its index-two subgroup
is already explicit in \cite{earlier}. The two finite replacement claims
are corrected for the literal words in the retained source edition.
None of these corrections is assigned historical priority or current
open-conjecture credit.

The complete required programs and literal data are retained in the
accompanying directories. GAP's exact presentation algorithms and the
integer matrix and permutation calculations remain declared computational
premises. The six Lean declarations in the finite case verify the shared
relators, the two modified relators and the failure of the asserted
consequence in an explicit permutation model. They do not formalize the
group orders, the infinite families or the geometry. The remaining formal
verification is separate from the written proof and computational evidence.
