\section{Program roles}
Four complete programs provide mathematical inputs: the exceptional
linear and small-unitary actions, the deleted-pencil residue at order
four, the native Witt designs and stabilizers, and the Mathieu chamber
fibers. The independent witness validator and the generator of the
literal Lean tables complete the computational bridge for the latter
models. Seven further programs test consequences and source corrections
proved in the written papers. They are retained in full and distinguished
from the required mathematical inputs. The operational driver
\texttt{check.py} verifies the input hashes, runs these files, rebuilds
the formal certificates and rejects specified altered inputs. It is
included separately, rather than counted as another mathematical lemma.

The finite programs do not replace the proofs for every prime power.
Classical Mathieu actions, the GAP permutation-group algorithms and the
DESIGN/GRAPE full automorphism calculation remain explicit computational
inputs. The exact roles and filenames are bound in the inventory.
