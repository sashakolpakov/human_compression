Complete Mathieu geometry census
================================

residue connectedness and exhaustive augmentation. The `application
<packet/mathieu-census.pdf>`_ recovers the corrected complete classification
of 81 rank-two and 431 rank-three geometries in Leemans--Rowley (2010),
register L073. Its whole application includes the complete
`class tables <science/class-tables.pdf>`_ and the dispositions of all 431
physical `source rows <science/source-rows.pdf>`_. Historical unavailable
geometry identifiers are not reconstructed or claimed.

All seven required programs appear in the `complete appendix
<science/required-programs.pdf>`_. Both full JSON inputs remain in science/data.
The predecessor supplies retained notation; its 48 pages are not counted as
another recovered original. Named-group identification, catalogue completeness
and exact algorithms remain declared premises. There is no Lean proof here.

0.113 with printed code and 0.114 with raw code. The conditional factor
is 0.383 after full stated acquisition. Each is an expansion. Corrections
and reproducibility do not turn these lengths into a compression gain.
