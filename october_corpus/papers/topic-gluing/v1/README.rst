Gluing: supported orbits and synchronized trees
==============================================

Two complete common papers and one application recover the principal
constructions and disproof in Leemans and Mulpas, arXiv:2504.17535v1.

* `Gluing through a common point <common-point/common_point_gluing.pdf>`_: five pages.
* `Two copies of a tree <parallel-trees/parallel_trees.pdf>`_: six pages.
* `Applications and a failed intersection <application/gluing_applications.pdf>`_: five pages.

The first theory decomposes each selected group into a supported symmetric
factor and its complementary action. The second describes selected groups
by forest partitions and agreement between two rows. Their intersections
then follow from unique paths. The first tail extension requires a separate
argument using nested terminal components. A character detects the source
counterexample uniformly.

Both common papers are charged in full to this topic. The application retains
the classical end-parabolic criterion and endpoint central extension theorem.
The source map records every principal clause and the ancillary numerical
sequence which is not recovered. The old disproof remains attributed to the
source; it receives no new conjecture credit.

The frozen author editions retain their original draft wording. The later
`admission <../../../audit/topic-gluing/v1/admission.json>`_ records complete
written-proof review, including separate review of the root-contributed example.
Ten Lean declarations verify specified supporting steps; the complete
construction proofs are not formalized. Three GAP programs supply bounded
validation, with no finite sample used as a universal proof premise.
Numerical comparison is pending.
