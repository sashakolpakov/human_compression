\title{Chamber fibers, reflections and regular covers}
\author{Human Compression project}
\begin{document}\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
Two elementary descriptions govern the results considered here. Normalizing part of a flag turns its remaining choices into a double-coset space. Normalizing the image of a group intersection turns its remaining obstruction into a kernel intersection. We prove these statements, give an explicit chamber model for a reflection interchanging two regular group orbits, and construct the smallest regular cover by intersecting a relation kernel with its mirror. The hypertope and polytope applications supply different geometric hypotheses; none of those hypotheses is inferred from a finite order calculation. The arguments rework previously reviewed proofs and retain the standard correspondence between string rotation groups and polytopes at its precise point of use.
\end{abstract}
