\title{A two-coset criterion for chiral hypertopes}
\author{Human Compression project}
\begin{document}\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
We derive the chirality criterion of Zhang and Leemans, Theorem 1.1 of their November 2025 author manuscript, from chamber fibers and an explicit reflection of a chamber graph. The finite cardinality test is written as a statement about two cosets, which is also meaningful for infinite groups. We prove residual connectedness directly, including the cyclic rank-two residues, and distinguish the designated group from the full type-preserving automorphism group until that distinction is resolved. The target is this criterion, not a replacement of the source's implementation or every ancillary assertion.
\end{abstract}
