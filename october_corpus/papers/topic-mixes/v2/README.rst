Mixes and faithful thin covers
=============================

This accepted edition contains two complete common papers and three
application papers. The first common paper proves simultaneous-action and
common-quotient intersection criteria; the second develops their use for cover
fibers, face incidences and double covers. Both common papers are charged once.
The two F005 applications include the exceptional two-orbit correction. The
L016 application proves the uniform faithful thin, nonpolytopal constructions
and records the precise failure and repair of the source thin-base lemma.

The original papers are F005 (21 pages) and L016 (12 pages), not the 54 detailed
principal/background dispositions. The three final F005 problems remain
unanswered. L016's declared ancillary exclusions are retained. No F007 result
or input appears here, and no new question or historical-priority claim is made.

Read fiber/fiber_intersections.pdf and covers/cover_fibers.pdf for the common
proofs; fiber/mix_applications.pdf, exceptional/exceptional_two_orbits.pdf and
covers/faithful_thin_covers.pdf give the complete accepted applications.
All corresponding TeX and exact frozen program/formal/evidence files are present.

current acceptance chain is recorded in audit/topic-mixes/v2/admission.json.
