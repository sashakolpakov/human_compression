Coset geometries under group operations
======================================

Three originals by Claudio Alexandre Piedade and Philippe Tranchida are
recovered through one common paper and complete applications. The proofs use
coset intersection criteria, actions on trees, orbit constructions and modules
for semidirect products. Original constructions and results remain attributed
to their authors; the common arguments, corrected statements and exact source
correspondences are identified in the papers and audit.

The `common paper <integrated/common-theory.pdf>`_ has 21 pages. It contains the
proofs used by all three applications, replacing the earlier common editions
for this comparison. Those editions remain archived; they are not additional
unpaid premises of these proofs.
