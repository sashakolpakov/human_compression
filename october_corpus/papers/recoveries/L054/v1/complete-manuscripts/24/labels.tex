\paragraph{New cyclic-four and dihedral-eight lift tests.}
Let $N\mathrel{\triangleleft}H$ be the actual checked normal subgroup.
Suppose that $x\in H$, $x^4\in N$, and the four cosets
$N,Nx,Nx^2,Nx^3$ are pairwise disjoint and cover $H$.
Then $H/N$ is cyclic of order four. A complement exists if and only if
some $a\in Nx$ satisfies $a^4=1$. Necessity follows by lifting the fixed
quotient generator through the complement. Conversely such an $a$ has
order exactly four, since its quotient image does, and
$\langle a\rangle\cap N=1$, so it is a complement.
Thus checking $a^4\ne1$ for every $a\in Nx$ certifies the printed dot.

For the dihedral test, take $x,y\in H$ satisfying
\[
 x^4,\ y^2,\ (xy)^2\in N,
 \qquad
 H=\coprod_{j=0}^{1}\coprod_{i=0}^{3}Nx^iy^j.
\]
The quotient is $D_8$: the presentation
$\langle r,s\mid r^4=s^2=(rs)^2=1\rangle$ reduces every word to
$r^is^j$, with $0\le i<4$, $0\le j<2$, while the eight disjoint
cosets show these forms distinct. A complement exists if and only if
there are $a\in Nx$, $b\in Ny$ with
\[
 a^4=b^2=(ab)^2=1.
\]
A complement necessarily supplies these lifts. Conversely, such lifts
define a homomorphism from the presented $D_8$ to $H$. Composing with
$H\to H/N$ is the isomorphism sending $r,s$ to $Nx,Ny$.
Consequently the lift homomorphism is injective, its image meets $N$
trivially, and its image maps onto $H/N$. It is the required complement.
The certificate tests all $|N|^2$ pairs in the two specified cosets and
requires each pair to fail at least one relation. It makes no inference
from failure to discover a complement.

The reader first establishes group closure, the normal subgroup and its
name, the quotient relations and every coset normal form. Thus neither
quotient identity nor completeness of the lift choices is assumed.
The three new cyclic-four and three new dihedral-eight certificates use
these arguments. Four new prime-index models, one Klein-four quotient,
one $S_3$ quotient and one $C_2\times C_4$ quotient use the previously
accepted complete lift tests, with their exact model data checked afresh.
