\section{The finite source obligations}
Leemans and Liebeck~\cite{LL} prove that every nonabelian finite simple
group except $A_7,\operatorname{PSL}_2(q),\operatorname{PSL}_3(q)$ and
$\operatorname{PSU}_3(q)$ admits a chiral polyhedron. We recover the
existence assertions for the thirty-five parameter entries in Table~\ref{table}.
These are existing results, not new existence theorems.

There are twenty-one simple entries in their list (4.1), physical page~7:
\[
\begin{gathered}
 L_4^\epsilon(2),L_4^\epsilon(3),L_4^\epsilon(4),L_5^\epsilon(2),L_6^\epsilon(2),\\
 \operatorname{PSp}_4(3),\operatorname{Sp}_4(4),\operatorname{Sp}_6(2),
 \operatorname{PSp}_6(3),\operatorname{Sp}_8(2),\\
 \Omega_7(3),\Omega_8^\epsilon(2),\operatorname{P}\Omega_8^+(3),\Omega_{10}^\epsilon(2).
\end{gathered}
\]
Both signs occur, with $L^+=\operatorname{PSL}$ and $L^-=\operatorname{PSU}$.
The additional printed $\operatorname{Sp}_4(2)\cong S_6$ is not simple;
it is not a positive simple-case obligation. The eleven remaining finite
exceptions in Section~4(2), physical page~8, are
\[
 L_5^\epsilon(3),L_6^\epsilon(3),L_8^\epsilon(2),
 \operatorname{Sp}_6(4),\operatorname{PSp}_8(3),\operatorname{Sp}_8(4),
 \Omega_{12}^\epsilon(2).
\]
The last three entries are $G_2(3),G_2(4),{}^2F_4(2)'$, treated at the end
of Section~3, physical page~6. We do not include the nonsimple
$G_2(2)$, nor turn its derived group $\operatorname{PSU}_3(3)$ or
${}^2G_2(3)'\cong\operatorname{PSL}_2(8)$ into positive exceptions.

\begin{lemma}\label{word}
Let $G=\langle x,t\rangle$, where $|t|=2$. If
$|w(x,t)|\ne|w(x^{-1},t)|$ for a group word $w$, no automorphism of
$G$ inverts $x$ and fixes $t$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Such an automorphism would carry the first word value to the second.
It preserves element order, which contradicts the inequality.
\end{proof}
This is the elementary word criterion already proved in the accepted
twelve-sporadic component~\cite{word}; it is restated to make the present
argument self-contained. In every row we take
$w(x,t)=xtx^2tx^3t$.

\begin{theorem}\label{main}
Each parameter entry in Table~\ref{table} has generators $x,t$ satisfying
conditions (i)--(iii) of \cite[Theorem~1.1]{LL}. It therefore admits a
chiral polyhedron of the displayed type $\{|x|,|x^{-1}t|\}$, with that
named simple group as full automorphism group.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The exact checks described below establish membership, $|t|=2$, full
generation, and the unequal word orders. Lemma~\ref{word} rules out even
noninvolutory reversing automorphisms. Put $y=x^{-1}t$. Then $xy=t$.
An element of $\langle x\rangle\cap\langle y\rangle$ commutes with both
$x,y$, so is central. The named groups are nonabelian simple, hence this
intersection is trivial. The first two orders exceed two. The retained
Schulte--Weiss reconstruction theorem~\cite{SW}, in the precise form used
in \cite[Section~1]{LL}, now gives the stated geometric conclusion.
\end{proof}

\section{Named models and exact verification}
Every \texttt{pair.g} is a literal permutation record containing the two
witnesses, the full ambient generating list, the parameters, the degree
and the expected orders. A fresh GAP process resets the same recorded
constructor seed and calls \texttt{SimpleGroup} with those parameters.
It requires exact equality of the saved and newly constructed generating
lists. There is no recognition of a group from its order.

The named-model identifications are retained GAP~4.15.1 construction and
catalogue premises~\cite{GAP}. The \texttt{L}, \texttt{U} and \texttt{S}
branches construct \texttt{PSL}, \texttt{PSU} and \texttt{PSp}. Their
projective matrix constructions and subsequent faithful smaller actions
are the documented named constructors. The odd and signed even
orthogonal branches take the derived subgroup of the appropriate
special orthogonal action on lines, followed when applicable by the
constructor's smaller action. Their identification and simplicity are
retained; neither the stored \texttt{Size} nor a simplicity flag proves
the identification here. The three exceptional calls are explicitly
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
G_2(3)&\texttt{PrimitiveGroup(351,7)}\\
G_2(4)&\texttt{PrimitiveGroup(416,7)}\\
{}^2F_4(2)'&\texttt{PrimitiveGroup(1600,20)}.
\end{array}
\]
The exact primitive catalogue records name these groups. The constructor,
classical projective interface, primitive loader and actual catalogue
files are pinned in the dependency ledger. Correctness of GAP's exact
permutation algorithms and the named library inputs is explicit
computational infrastructure, not a new classification proof.

For every row the checker reconstructs $H$ from the saved ambient
generators without supplying its order. It verifies $H=G$, computes
$|H|$, checks $x,t\in H$ and $|t|=2$, and forms $K=\langle x,t\rangle$
without supplying its order. It verifies $|K|=|H|$ and $K=H$.
Membership and equal finite order already imply generation; the latter
equality is a redundant explicit check. The four table entries are then
computed by exact permutation operations. The new groups are checked to
have no preassigned size before the relevant calculations.

A separate standard-library Python parser reads the original cycle
notation directly, checks disjoint cycles, and composes the corresponding
right-action permutations. Cycle lengths and integer least common
multiples reproduce all four orders and $|t|=2$, independently of GAP's
word calculations. It also parses every ambient generator, but it does
not purport to duplicate GAP's large subgroup computations.

The source roster is constructed independently as the explicit sets of
parameters above. Missing, duplicate, nonsimple, misrouted and displaced
entries are rejected. Seven GAP controls damage parameters, ambient
generators, the involution, membership, generation, an order, and the
word inequality. All must reject before producing a positive certificate.
Both Python entry points reject optimized execution before output work.
Every replay is finite; a timeout or nonclean terminal result grants no
certificate. The recorded searches merely supplied candidates and are
not run by verification.

\section{Attribution and limits}
The root agent supplied the bounded candidate searches and the acquired
word argument; this component's author supplies the deterministic
certificate, independent literal oracle, source routing and explanation.
All original candidate and execution bytes are retained. An initial
author setup used a directory ordinal instead of the actual recorded
seed for some constructors; that failed preliminary setup is preserved
outside the scientific replay. The final replay uses the recorded seed
and changes no candidate.

These are thirty-five parameter entries, not thirty-five newly classified
groups. In particular $L_4(2)\cong A_8$ overlaps the alternating family,
and $U_4(2)\cong\operatorname{PSp}_4(3)$ occurs twice under the source's
parameters. The group identities, geometric reconstruction and original
existence theorem retain their attribution. This component does not
settle the infinite Lie families, ancillary enumerations, or the whole
original source, and adds no measurement or conjecture claim.
