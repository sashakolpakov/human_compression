\paragraph{Complete certificate argument.}
We acquire the labelled-generator transport criterion. A bijection from
a finite model to an actual subgroup, fixing identity and preserving
right multiplication by every ordered generator, is a homomorphism by
induction on words. Hence it is an isomorphism. The reader enumerates
both actual finite groups by permutation multiplication, verifies the
entire bijection and checks every generator edge. References to certified
factors are processed in dependency order.

For a split description the certificate supplies $N,K\leq H$ with their
acquired or previously checked names. It checks normality of $N$ on the
generators of $H$, $N\cap K=1$, and $|N||K|=|H|$. Thus $H=NK$ and the
description follows. A noncommuting generator pair confirms the printed
nontrivial action. For a direct product the factor generators commute,
so the same generation and intersection checks give $H=N\times K$.

Three new nonsplit models have quotient $C_2$. Every element outside the
specified normal subgroup is checked to have square different from
identity. Any complement would have order two and contradict this check.
The other two have quotient $C_2^2$. Four literal cosets, with the quotient
relations, establish this quotient. All lifts of its two fixed basis
elements are tested: no pair consists of commuting involutions. A
complement would lift that basis to such a pair, so no complement exists.
The printed $Q_8\mathbin{\cdot}6$ description uses an earlier accepted

extraspecial. The source symbol $2^{1+4}$ makes no further sign choice.
