\begin{lemma}[Composite-quotient lift tests]
Let $N\triangleleft H$ be finite, with $|H:N|=6$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item If $H/N$ is cyclic, fix $xN$ generating it. The extension splits
if and only if some $a\in Nx$ satisfies $a^6=1$.
\item Suppose $xN,yN$ generate $H/N$ and satisfy
$(xN)^2=(yN)^3=(xyN)^2=N$. The extension splits if and only if some
$a\in Nx,b\in Ny$ satisfy $a^2=b^3=(ab)^2=1$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
In the cyclic case, a section supplies a lift of the fixed generator of
order six. Conversely any displayed lift with sixth power one has order
exactly six, since its image does, and generates a complement.

In the second case the presentation
$\langle u,v\mid u^2=v^3=(uv)^2=1\rangle$ is $S_3$:
$uv=v^{-1}u$ reduces every word to $v^i$ or $uv^i$, $0\leq i<3$,
and the usual transposition and 3-cycle give all six permutations.
Thus the specified quotient is $S_3$. A complement lifts its two fixed
quotient generators and gives the stated relations. Conversely these
relations define a homomorphism from $S_3$ into $H$. Its image maps onto
the quotient of order six, so it has exactly six elements and meets $N$
trivially. Hence it is a complement.
\end{proof}

The literal implementation checks normality first. For the cyclic case
it verifies that the six cosets $Nx^i$ cover $H$ and that $x^6\in N$.
For the symmetric case it checks the three quotient relations and that
$N,Ny,Ny^2,Nx,Nxy,Nxy^2$ are six disjoint cosets covering $H$.
It then tests all elements of the indicated coset, or all pairs in the
two indicated cosets. Each fails at least one required relation. This
proves nonsplitting without assuming completeness of a complement-search
routine. Eight new composite-quotient models use 39,312 literal lift tests.
For instance $(2^4:5:2)\mathbin{\cdot}S_3$ and
$D_8\mathbin{\cdot}6$ require different quotient presentations;
the verifier does not identify quotients merely from order six.

\paragraph{The source atom $Q_{12}$.}
The three occurrences are \texttt{pgammal216}, row 46;
\texttt{psigmal225}, row 59; and \texttt{pgammal225}, row 76.
We make the meaning precise as the dicyclic group of order twelve:
\begin{equation}\label{dic}
\langle a,b\mid a^6=1,\ b^2=a^3,\ b^{-1}ab=a^{-1}\rangle.
\end{equation}
Every word reduces to $a^i$ or $a^ib$, $0\leq i<6$, so this presentation
has at most twelve elements. The actual certificate gives generators
$a,b$ of a twelve-element group with $|a|=6$ satisfying these relations;
therefore it identifies the presented group exactly. The installed GAP
primary documentation explicitly defines the dicyclic presentation in
this way and reserves generalized quaternion for its 2-power-order case.
The source article does not define $Q_{12}$ separately. Our exact
presentation fixes the nomenclature qualification rather than claiming
that this is a generalized quaternion group of 2-power order.
