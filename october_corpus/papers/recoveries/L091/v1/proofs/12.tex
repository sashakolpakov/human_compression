This component concerns the actual distinguished parabolic subgroups in the
86 retained Higman--Sims models and the corresponding 312 node labels in the
fixed supplement. The source-to-model type permutation, exact subgroup
orders and actual generators are acquired from the accepted local-data and
source-coordinate components. The 398 Borel/kernel occurrence maps are also
acquired. The present task is the full parabolic and its extension over the
\emph{displayed} kernel. Neither a group order nor an unproved finite
presentation is used as a naming certificate.

\begin{theorem}
For all 312 source/model slots, the supplied literal certificates identify
the actual parabolic, its distinguished kernel and quotient, with the
splitting and direct-product assertions recorded in the supplied source ledger.
The 61 raw source label strings and all parentheses and kernel boxes are
retained. Exactly 26 slots require a correction, including five slots whose
kernel correction was already acquired. The other labels are verified or
made more precise. These are statements about the displayed subgroups,
not a classification of all parabolic conjugacy classes or geometries.
\end{theorem}

\section*{Literal transport}
\begin{lemma}
Let $M=\langle g_1,\ldots,g_s\rangle$ be a finite permutation model whose
complete right-generator Cayley graph is known. Suppose permutations
$h_1,\ldots,h_s$ define images $y_m$ along the discovery words, with $y_1=1$,
and every edge satisfies $y_{mg_i}=y_mh_i$. Then $m\mapsto y_m$ is a
homomorphism. If its values are distinct, contain every generator of an
actual subgroup $P$, and $|P|=|M|$, it is an isomorphism onto $P$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induction on a positive word gives the claimed image of every product.
Every element of a finite generated group has such a word, including
inverses. Thus all products are respected. Distinctness proves injectivity;
containment and the independently acquired order give equality with $P$.
\end{proof}
The enumerator closes from the identity under every generator and checks
its resulting size; the expected size never truncates an open traversal.
All non-discovery edges are checked. Hence a GAP isomorphism proposal is
only a way of choosing the $h_i$. The proof uses the literal permutations
and complete edges, with no group-recognition or presentation assumption.

For an extension let $K$ be the actual listed kernel, and let $M$ be the
named quotient model. Enumerate $K$, verify its order, and check that every
$h_i$ normalizes it. The discovery lifts $y_m$ must satisfy
\[
 y_mh_i y_{mg_i}^{-1}\in K.
\]
The checked normalization makes coset multiplication well defined, so the
same word argument gives a homomorphism $M\to\langle K,h_i\rangle/K$.
Requiring all $ky_m$ to be distinct proves injectivity and the exact order
$|K||M|$. Every actual generator of $P$ must occur among them. The acquired
order $|P|=|K||M|$ then proves equality with $P$, including the actual
kernel embedding. If all edges hold exactly, the $y_m$ form a section.
If the lifts also commute with every kernel generator, this is an internal
direct product. A quotient certificate alone asserts neither splitting
nor nonsplitting.

\section*{Complete section tests}
Once the quotient map and ordered generators of $M$ are fixed, a section
must send $g_i$ to one of $k_i h_i$, $k_i\in K$. Enumerating every ordered
lift tuple and testing every Cayley edge therefore covers every section.
An exact edge solution is automatically a right inverse of the quotient
map and hence injective. Conversely every section supplies such a solution.
The cap on the number of tuples rejects oversized tests; it never reports
nonsplitting after a partial enumeration.

There are 4096 lift tuples for each of the three $|K|=64$, $M=S_5$ cases,
and all fail. Thus the three printed colons in these cases are false.
The four extra central $C_2$ models used to identify unboxed full parabolics
have respectively $4,8,8,8$ lift tuples and $2,4,4,4$ sections. These central
subgroups are additional witnesses and are not substituted for the source's
boxed kernels. A separate inner $\E_2^4/S_5$ test has 256 tuples and 32
sections. Its purpose is a precise reassociation of one actual named model;
it does not identify arbitrary groups sharing the same extension notation.

\section*{Names and parenthesized factors}
The acquired named-model library gives 244 full-parabolic maps. Six further
full groups are identified as $A_5\times(5:4)$ by explicit disjoint actions;
four are $C_2\times M_{10}$ or $C_2\times\Aut(A_6)$ through literal central
sections. The remaining 58 are specified by their actual kernel/quotient
extension certificates and fixed permutation actions. All 108 nontrivial
boxed kernels have quotient certificates. There are 56 section certificates
for these exact source kernels and 29 centralizing sections in total,
including the four additional central witnesses. No failure of a discovery
procedure is used as a nonsplitting assertion.

The elementary naming models are explicit: $\operatorname{GL}(3,2)$ on the
seven nonzero binary vectors is $L_3(2)$; adjoining translations gives
$\E_2^3:L_3(2)$. Affine transformations of $\mathbb F_5$ give $5:4$.
Signed coordinate matrices on $\mathbb F_3^2$ give $3^2:D_8$.
The matching-sign subgroup of $S_3\times S_4$, over the normal Klein four
in the second factor, is $2^2:(3^2:2)$. The full product is
$2^2:(3^2:2^2)$. Finally $\mathbb F_9=\mathbb F_3[u]/(u^2+1)$ has a
primitive element $1+u$ of order8; Frobenius acts on it by cubing.
Translations, multiplication and Frobenius therefore give
$3^2:(8:2)=\operatorname{A\Gamma L}(1,9)$.
Every use of these names has its own complete literal transport.

Nested accepted trees are not reassociated by notation alone. The two
actual full models named $(2^4:A_5):2$ have separately checked normal
$\E_2^4$ and $S_5$ complements. Both actual $576$-order models needed for
$2^4:(3^2:2^2)$ have checked $\E_2^4$ and $S_3\times S_3$ complements.
One of these is additionally identified as $S_4\times S_4$. The quotient
of slot $66:P3$ has its own checked $\E_2^4:S_5$ section. Model IDs here
are local to their exact bound input files, never an order-based alias.
For $A_5:D_{12}$ the transported full model is $S_5\times S_3$:
$A_5\lhd S_5$ has a transposition complement and $C_2\times S_3=D_{12}$.
For $([2]\times A_6).2^2$, the stronger actual product
$C_2\times\Aut(A_6)$ supplies the normal intermediate $C_2\times A_6$.

\section*{Source corrections}
The full slot table records source label, physical page, source type,
model type, the untouched raw expression and box span, and the exact
certificate for every assertion. The following descriptions explain all
new correction patterns; the five acquired kernel corrections remain
explicitly attributed to their prior components.
\begin{itemize}
\item The three $[4.2^4]:S_5$ extensions do not split over the displayed
kernel, by complete lift exhaustion.
\item The ten quotients printed $2^3\times L_3(2)$ are
$2^3:L_3(2)$. The latter affine group has trivial centre: commuting with
translations forces a central element's linear part to be the identity,
and a translation fixed by every linear map is zero.
\item The quotient printed $2\times D_{10}$ is $5:4$; an element of
order4 distinguishes these groups. Quotients printed $2$ in source3.1
and3.20 have order4 and are $2^2$. Source3.4 type2 has quotient $D_{12}$,
not $S_3$, in addition to its already corrected kernel; its corrected
exact sequence makes no splitting assertion.
\item The two unboxed $2\times A_6$ labels have actual order1440,
with groups $C_2\times M_{10}$ and $\Aut(A_6)$ respectively. Source3.24
has full group $\Aut(A_6)$ with trivial centre. Even if its label means
$(C_2\times A_6).C_2$, the characteristic $C_2$ centre of that normal
index-two subgroup is fixed by every conjugation and remains central in
the whole group. Thus both conventional readings are excluded.
\item Source4.24 type1 is $U_3(5):2$ and source4.28 type1 is $U_3(5)$,
with orders252000 and126000 respectively. Literal named transport, not
just the corrected type sizes, establishes these identifications.
\end{itemize}
The unparenthesized source4.12 type3 expression is read sequentially as
$(K\times\E_2^2):(\E_3^2:\E_2^2)$. This is verified: its actual boxed
$K$ of order4 lies in a checked normal elementary group of order16,
has an internal elementary complement there, and the normal group has a
checked $S_3\times S_3$ complement. The full group is $S_4\times S_4$.
Noncentrality of $K$ in the whole group does not refute this intermediate
direct-product assertion. The earlier working interpretation is retained
as a reader correction, not a source error.

The generic dot is used only for an extension; it is not a nonsplitting
claim. The exact permutation model and the maps disambiguate extensions
when a conventional expression alone does not. No parabolic conjugacy,
residual primitivity, geometry enumeration, automorphism-group or complete
original recovery is inferred by this component.
