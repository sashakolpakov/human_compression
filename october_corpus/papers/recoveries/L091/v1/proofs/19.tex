\paragraph{Counting pairs from one point.}
Choose an anchor type $a$ of size $n$, base point $b$, and stabilizer $P_a$.
Let $O_k$ be its orbits on points, with $d_k=|O_k|$. For another type $j$,
its base object has point shadow $S_j=b^{P_j}$. For a non-diagonal orbit,
let $e_{jk}$ count ordered pairs in $S_j$ belonging to the $G$-orbit of
$(b,y)$, where $y\in O_k$. Then
\begin{equation}\label{fast}
 e_{jk}=|S_j|\,|S_j\cap O_k|,\qquad
 m_{jk}=\frac{[G:P_j]|S_j|\,|S_j\cap O_k|}{n d_k}.
\end{equation}
To prove the first equality, take any $x\in S_j$. Some element of $P_j$
sends $b$ to $x$. It preserves $S_j$ and the specified $G$-orbit on pairs,
so the number of possible second coordinates at $x$ equals that at $b$.
Summing this constant over $S_j$ proves the assertion. For the second,
count triples consisting of a type-$j$ object and a pair in its shadow.
There are $[G:P_j]e_{jk}$ triples and $nd_k$ pairs, and transitivity makes
the number of objects per pair constant. This counts distinct objects even
when their shadows coincide. The type-$a$ convention is equality within
that type; its off-diagonal entries are zero.

For each pair form the sorted multiset of $([G:P_j],m_{jk})$ over all types.
A correlation preserves this signature, including multiplicities. Partition
the distinct pairs by signature and order the signatures lexicographically.
For the valency $d$ in the table, choose the first relation with that valency.
This rule is intrinsic and is compatible with passage to another anchor of
the same size. It does not require the valency to determine a unique relation:
in representative 73 there are two relations of valency 105. Every selected
relation is verified to be symmetric and irreflexive.

\paragraph{Exact point coordinates and faithful restriction.}
For each anchor a literal ordered list of generator images defines a
bijective homomorphism $\phi:G\longrightarrow E\leq\operatorname{Sym}(n)$.
The saved elements $t_x\in G$ satisfy $b^{\phi(t_x)}=x$, and the stabilizer
of $b$ in $E$ is exactly $\phi(P_a)$. These checks prove the identification
of the right coset $P_ag$ with $b^{\phi(g)}$; no correctness assumption
about a producer's transversal is required. Orbits of $\phi(P_a)$ and
$\phi(P_j)$ give the sets in~\eqref{fast}. The formula constructs the base
neighbour set, and the $t_x$ transport it to every adjacency row. Every
specified generator is checked on every row.

At all twenty-four anchors, the stabilizer of $S_j$ in $E$ is exactly
$\phi(P_j)$ for every $j$. Thus the equivariant map from type-$j$ objects
to their shadows is injective: its fibres are the cosets of the shadow
stabilizer modulo the object stabilizer. Consequently a typed automorphism
fixing all anchor points fixes all objects. This proves faithfulness of
restriction to each anchor. No refinement by additional types is needed
for these fifteen applications.

\paragraph{Full graph groups and normality.}
A complete nauty calculation gives the order $q|G|$ in the table for each
of the twenty-four graphs. The integer-order extraction is exact in the
specified range, and every returned generator is checked against all
adjacency rows. Let $D$ be their literal permutation group. The verifier
checks its order, $E\leq D$, and the two conditions
\[
 e^z\in E\quad(e\in\mathcal E, z\in\mathcal D),\qquad
 [z,w]\in E\quad(z,w\in\mathcal D),
\]
where $\mathcal E$ and $\mathcal D$ are the displayed generating sets.
The first condition implies $E\lhd D$: conjugation gives inclusion, hence
equality by finiteness. The second makes $D/E$ abelian. The acquired $G$
is perfect, also checked in its original model, so
$E=E'\leq D'\leq E$. Therefore $D'=E$ and $E$ is characteristic in $D$.

A correlation carries each anchor graph to the graph of its image anchor,
hence carries their derived groups to each other. If $c$ is a correlation
and $g\in G$, the restriction of $cgc^{-1}$ to the image anchor equals
that of some $h\in G$. The typed automorphism $h^{-1}cgc^{-1}$ fixes the
anchor pointwise and is therefore trivial by faithfulness. Thus the
specified $G$ is normal in the full correlation group.

\paragraph{Completing the geometry groups.}
Put $B=\bigcap_jP_j$, and let $S,T$ be the ordered and setwise stabilizers
of the parabolic family in $A$. The acquired coset normalizer theorem now
applies to the full geometry groups and gives
\[
 |\Aut(\Gamma)|=|G:B||S|,\qquad
 |\Cor(\Gamma)|=|G:B||T|.
\]
The calculation considers every type permutation. Necessary order tests
only discard impossible cases. For each remaining permutation, successive
transporter calculations retain precisely the automorphisms carrying all
parabolics already placed to their targets. Positive maps are checked on
the whole family. The resulting orders, in the order of the theorem, are
\[
\begin{gathered}
(|B|,|S|,|T|)=
(660,660,1320),\ (1344,2688,2688),\\
(1536,3072,3072),\ (12,12,24),\ (12,12,12),\ (12,12,12),\\
(16,16,16),\ (256,512,512),\ (256,512,1024),\\
(4,4,8),\ (4,4,4),\ (4,4,8),\ (4,4,8),\\
(64,128,256),\ (3,3,6).
\end{gathered}
\]
In every row $S\cap G=B$, so the typed group acts faithfully by conjugation
on $G$ and is identified with $G$ or $A$ by its order. The full centralizer
of $G$ is the actual quotient $(T\cap G)/B$. It has order two precisely
for representatives 7,33,73 and order one otherwise. In those three cases
the centralizer is central, meets the typed group trivially, and together
with it has the full correlation-group order. This gives the asserted
direct products. In every other row conjugation is faithful on the full
correlation group and identifies it with $G$ or $A$.

\paragraph{Attribution and verification boundary.}
The classification, source coordinates, shadow criterion and normalizer
theorem are reused from the reviewed material. The contribution here is
the fifteen explicit applications, the evaluation of pair counts using
transitivity of a single shadow, and the use of a perfect subgroup with
abelian quotient to verify the required characteristic subgroup. These
are elementary deductions; no claim of historical novelty is made.
GAP's exact group algorithms and nauty's complete upper bounds remain
computational premises. Literal small coset models check~\eqref{fast}
against direct incidence counts, including repeated shadows. Source
pages 3,4,8,9,11,18,19,20,21 were inspected in the retained edition.
Abstract parabolic and Borel names, other geometries and the whole
manuscript remain outside this component. No conjecture resolution,
new Lean verification or compression measurement is asserted.
