\title{A bound from the group generated by all panels}
\author{Human Compression project}\date{}
\begin{document}\maketitle
Fix a finite group $G$ and a subgroup $B$. Suppose that an acquired
complete transport procedure supplies the actual list $\mathcal P_B$
of admissible panel subgroups above this fixed $B$. We consider only
families of these panels that generate $G$. The completeness of the
panel list, including its local action conditions, is an input to the
following argument, not a conclusion of it.
\begin{lemma}
Put $J_B=\langle B,P\mid P\in\mathcal P_B\rangle$.
If $J_B<G$, no family under consideration exists at $B$.
If every $P\in\mathcal P_B$ has index two over $B$, then
$J_B\leq N_G(B)$, so a proper normalizer suffices for the same conclusion.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every selected panel lies in $J_B$. The group generated by any selected
family therefore lies in $J_B$, and cannot equal $G$ when $J_B<G$.
In the second assertion, a subgroup of index two is normal. Thus each
$P$ normalizes $B$, and the same holds for the group they generate
 together with $B$.
\end{proof}
For the connected coset geometries in the acquired primitive
augmentation theorem, generation of $G$ by the panel subgroups is
part of the established correspondence. The list $\mathcal P_B$ is
obtained by the already proved transport of complete maximal
inclusions; for the Higman--Sims source it is restricted to the
inclusions with a two-transitive local action. The bound applies to
this exact list. It makes no assertion that proper generated families
are absent, and supplies no classification of them.

The implementation computes the same complete actual panel list as the
accepted enumeration, forms $J_B$, and tests properness. An exclusion
saves explicit generating permutations for $B$, every panel, and $J_B$.
It has its own terminal status. It is not an empty final level of the
old enumeration. If $J_B=G$, the wrapper invokes the unchanged old
procedure. This may repeat the inexpensive construction of its panel
list; it changes no recognition, equivalence or pruning rule inside
that procedure.

For example, the retained Higman--Sims case numbered 96 has
$|B|=16$ and 19 admissible panels, all of order 32. Their generated
group has order 512, strictly less than $|G|=44\,352\,000$.
The completed lower levels of an interrupted enumeration remain
valid, but are unnecessary for this exclusion. The unfinished rank
is not credited as exhausted. The exact subgroup classification and
finite-group software premises used to obtain the panel list remain
explicitly acquired.

This is an elementary bound used to avoid unnecessary enumeration.
No novelty, open-conjecture, whole-source or compression claim is
made by the argument alone.
