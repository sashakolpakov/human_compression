Linear groups and regular polytopes
===================================

This recovery covers all twelve principal theorems and corollaries and both
classification tables of Irina Markovskaya's 2025 thesis, *Linear groups over
finite fields and over the ring of integers as automorphism groups of regular
polytopes of rank 3*. The supplied edition has 80 pages; its supervisor is
Ya. N. Nuzhin. The original results and source constructions retain the
attributions given in the thesis.

Begin with the `three-page complete classification <classification/source_recovery.pdf>`_.
The `integral proof <integral-groups/integral_groups.pdf>`_ supplies all integral
dimensions. The `centre calculation <central-quotients/central_quotients.pdf>`_,
`determinant-sign constructions <determinant-sign/finite_H.pdf>`_ and
`remaining quotient proof <remaining-quotients/quotients_and_classification.pdf>`_
complete the finite classifications and their rank-three polytopes.
The already published `common root theory <../../N011/v1/root_parameters.pdf>`_
and `finite general-linear classification <../../N011/v1/linear_classification.pdf>`_
are acquired in full and reused byte for byte.

Root parameters and elementary matrix words supply uniform generation.
A single calculation on five coordinates controls the intersections for the
determinant-sign constructions and every scalar quotient. The centre calculation
separates the two quotient groups, and the perfect-group central lift supplies
markings when a central factor remains. The complete integral proof now supplies
the integral premise previously retained externally in the finite classification;
this dependency order is acyclic.

The source quotient Q is H/Z(SL), not H/Z(H). The structural clause of source
Corollary 4.2.1 needs correction: if e=(q-1)/gcd(n,q-1) is even, H/Z(H) is PSL;
if e is odd, it is the indicated index-two extension. The asserted polytope and
type [4p,n] hold in both cases. The source's defective four-dimensional witness
and omitted square in a seven-dimensional word are also identified and replaced.
Every negative exception is preserved, including the inadmissible Q_2(9), all
Q_3(q), and the rank-three exclusion of S3.

The seven immediate papers occupy 30 physical pages: five new papers or editions
occupy 21 pages, while two already public papers occupy nine. These are document
inventory counts, not a compression measurement. The original accepted finite-H
edition remains in the audit inventory; its readable copy only clarifies that
the product AC has order 4p and its dihedral subgroup has order 8p. The exact
sentence diff is retained.
