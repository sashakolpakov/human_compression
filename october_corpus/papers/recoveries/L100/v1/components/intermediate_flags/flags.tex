\begin{lemma}
The actual $H_{123}$ is $D_{10}$. The actual $H_{25}$ is a split
$2^4:3$ with fixed-point-free complement action on the nonidentity
elements of its normal elementary abelian subgroup.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
In $H_{123}$ the saved elements $a,b$ have orders five and two,
respectively, satisfy $a^b=a^{-1}$, and the ten words
$a^i b^j$ ($0\leq i<5$, $0\leq j<2$) are pairwise distinct and
exhaust the actual set. These relations and normal forms give mutually
inverse homomorphisms with the dihedral group of order ten.

In $H_{25}$ the elements of order at most two form a 16-element
subgroup $N$. The saved four generators are commuting involutions
and their sixteen binary coordinate words are distinct. Normality is
checked by conjugation with the saved generators of $H_{25}$.
The saved element $r$ has order three. Its conjugation action on the
four basis elements is recorded as an actual matrix over $\mathbb F_2$,
and it fixes no nonzero coordinate vector. All 48 words $nr^j$,
$n\in N$, $0\leq j<3$, are distinct and exhaust $H_{25}$.
Thus $N\cap\langle r\rangle=1$ and the extension splits with exactly
the recorded action. To identify this action abstractly, its minimal
polynomial divides $X^3-1=(X-1)(X^2+X+1)$. Absence of fixed vectors
eliminates the first factor, so $N$ is the direct sum of two copies
of the irreducible two-dimensional $\mathbb F_2 C_3$ module.
This specifies the semidirect product beyond a mere order comparison.
\end{proof}
