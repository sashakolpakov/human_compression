Chiral generating pairs in finite simple groups
===============================================

This package recovers the positive Theorem 1.1 of Leemans--Liebeck,
*Chiral polyhedra and finite simple groups*, arXiv:1603.07713v3, and its
chiral-polyhedron consequence. Every finite nonabelian simple group outside
the stated isomorphism families A7, PSL2(q), PSL3(q) and PSU3(q) has the
required generating pair, excluding reversers in its full automorphism group.

Start with the `seven-page synthesis <principal/principal.pdf>`_
(`TeX <principal/principal.tex>`_). The `complete reading volume
<principal/complete_proof.pdf>`_ appends all 51 pages of the fourteen
component manuscripts. The `manuscript index
<../../../../audit/recoveries/L045/v1/acquired_manuscripts.json>`_ locates
all fifteen exact TeX/PDF editions separately. These page totals describe
the documents; they are not a compression measurement.

Exact source boundary
----------------------

The theorem is positive. The source's PSL2 and A7 negative statements
remain attributed prior results. Its PSL3/PSU3 nonexistence statement is
announced as forthcoming in the fixed edition and is not promoted here
to a proved converse. Historical map counts and example types, the separate
all-types O'Nan total, and the almost-simple extension question are not
claimed recovered by this existence proof. Isomorphism of marked rotation
data is not a count of literal labelled pairs or unmarked maps.

The `source register
<../../../../audit/recoveries/L045/v1/scope_routes.json>`_ gives all 36
individual dispositions. The proof covers every alternating degree at least
eight, all 26 sporadic groups and all eligible Lie families. Its 35 explicit
finite Lie parameter certificates include duplicate abstract groups under
different parameter names; Sp4(2) is nonsimple and is excluded correctly.

Several supporting source estimates are repaired: the cyclic normalizer
is paid, reversers and fixed groups are counted in their actual cosets,
the missing symplectic unitary overgroup is included, and orthogonal scalar
losses and triality multiplicities are corrected. The false special PSU4
half-bound is replaced by an exact formula and a sufficient quarter bound.
These repairs preserve the principal positive theorem.
