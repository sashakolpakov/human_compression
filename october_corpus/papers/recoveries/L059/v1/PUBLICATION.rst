Faithful coset geometries and graph actions
=========================================

This recovery studies rank-two coset geometries through their faithful
actions on bipartite graphs. For a fixed connected graph with ordered
vertex parts, the geometries correspond exactly to conjugacy classes of
edge-transitive subgroups of its type-preserving automorphism group.
The common kernel explains unfaithful realizations, and an edge normalizer
determines dualities. A single descent through maximal subgroups enumerates
all the required actions. For cubic subdivisions, an index-two normalizer
construction reuses the classifications on the original graphs.

The source is Julie De Saedeleer, Dimitri Leemans, Mark Mixer and Tomaz
Pisanski, *Core-Free, Rank Two Coset Geometries from Edge-Transitive Bipartite
Graphs*, Mathematica Slovaca 64 (2014), 991--1006. The fixed primary PDF is
arXiv:1106.5704v2. The published version adds subdivisions of symmetric
cubic graphs; that additional obligation is included here.

Start with the `principal manuscript <principal/faithful_markings.pdf>`_
(10 pages). Its two complete acquired components are
`the historical quartic input set
<corpus106_L059_historical_quartic_v1/historical_quartic.pdf>`_ (3 pages)
and `the actual subdivision actions
<corpus106_L059_subdivision_actual_actions_v1/actual_actions.pdf>`_ (2 pages).
These are entry points to the full proof and evidence collection. Their
15 pages are not a compression denominator, and this publication assigns
no compression rate.

The classifications contain:

* 714 bipartite symmetric cubic graphs and 1082 faithful markings;
* 43 semisymmetric cubic graphs and 102 markings;
* subdivisions of all 796 symmetric cubic graphs in scope and 1427 markings;
* 397 historical quartic graphs in the corrected scope and 1643 markings.

Thus 1950 graph inputs give 4254 markings. All 39 printed table cells are
recovered. Each retained finite record includes the actual permutation
groups, their stabilizers and the four numerical columns.

The source qualifications are part of the result. Its printed quartic
vertex-stabilizer cutoff below 100 excludes K4,4, which appears in its own
table. The recovered scope has full vertex stabilizer below 200,
equivalently |Aut+(X)|/|X| below 100. One explicitly recorded reversal of
the ordered parts of current graph 128_055 is needed for the printed Max
table; its original witness is preserved. The 110-vertex exceptional
dihedral groups have orders 24 and 12. The manuscript also corrects the
unrestricted transitivity, disconnected-colour and marked-isomorphism
assertions, and gives the Mathieu example with an explicit automorphism
and conjugating element. The exact eight qualifications and their proofs
are retained in the source contract.

The historical quartic identification borrows the published catalogue
coverage and distinctness statements. The cubic lists borrow the primary
Conder--Potocnik/Foster classification. GAP, GRAPE and nauty supply their
declared finite group and graph algorithms. These inputs remain attributed;
the present census does not reprove their completeness. The large-stabilizer
classification problem, and the cyclic-configuration problem in the earlier
arXiv edition, are not claimed solved. No new Lean result is claimed.

Replaying the public package
----------------------------

The portable checker restores exact proof, review, program and execution
files from their public objects. Repeated bytes are stored once, and large
text files use lossless gzip. Original manifests and completed receipts
remain unchanged. The two historical timeouts are retained alongside
separate successful retries. No private source directory is consulted.

Full articles, webpages and full upstream source catalogues are represented
by 19 explicit documentary hashes. The bounded numerical records actually
needed by the public replay are supplied: all 839 required cubic input
records, their exact source-line hashes, and all 261 vertex permutations
used to reuse earlier classifications. The adapter reconstructs the graphs
and checks those maps. It preserves the original verifier rather than
rewriting it at runtime. Relative-path adaptations of the named-group
checker and small method controls are separately recorded as literal diffs.

The replay verifies all 1950 completed cases and 39 table cells, checks the
839 numerical inputs and 261 reuse maps, reruns the named-group and Mathieu
certificates, compares the enumeration methods on five small graphs, and
rebuilds the three manuscripts with identical page text. It does not repeat
the full subgroup census. Exact commands, hashes, software requirements and
the successful replay report are in the admission proposal and transport
record under ``audit/recoveries/L059/v1``.

Storage reduction is not mathematical compression. Mathematical coverage,
source attribution and computational provenance are recorded separately;
the later complexity audit must count all acquired proof and validation
material according to its declared comparison rule.
