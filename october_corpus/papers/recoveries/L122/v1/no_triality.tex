\begin{abstract}
We give an explicit rank-three coset geometry whose defining subgroups are
cyclically permuted by a group automorphism, although no correlation of
order three cycles its types. This refutes the bare existential assertion
of Lemma 1 of Leemans--Stokes in its printed generality. The proof uses a
small integer certificate, including a full correlation-group bound by
color refinement; no graph-automorphism search is a proof premise.
\end{abstract}

\section{The exact assertion and the construction}
The fixed six-page source~\cite{LS}, physical pages 2--3, calls a rank-three
pregeometry a geometry when every flag extends to a chamber. It defines
a triality to have order three. Lemma 1 assumes only that an automorphism
of the coset group cycles three defining subgroups; it does not require
firmness, residual connectedness, or order three for that automorphism.
We retain exactly this meaning. All group actions and permutation products
below are on the right.

Let $T$ be the subgroup of $S_{18}$ generated by
\begin{align*}
 u&=(3\ 17\ 15\ 13\ 11\ 9\ 7\ 5)(4\ 18\ 16\ 14\ 12\ 10\ 8\ 6),\\
 v&=(1\ 2\ 11)(3\ 13\ 9)(4\ 15\ 6)(5\ 8\ 12)
       (7\ 18\ 16)(10\ 14\ 17).
\end{align*}
The following two elements belong to $T$:
\begin{align*}
 a&=(1\ 2\ 3\ 5\ 16\ 15\ 14\ 9\ 11)
        (4\ 7\ 10\ 6\ 12\ 13\ 17\ 18\ 8),\\
 b&=(2\ 13)(3\ 14)(4\ 8)(5\ 11)(6\ 9)(7\ 16)(10\ 12)(17\ 18).
\end{align*}
The certificate reconstructs $T$ by closure under these literal generators:
$|T|=2448$, $|a|=9$, $|b|=2$, and $|\gen{a^3,b}|=12$.
The discovery model was $\operatorname{PSL}_2(17)$, but that abstract
identification is not needed anywhere in the proof.

Put $G=\gen c\times T$, where $|c|=3$, and set
\[
 H=\gen{ca^3,b},\qquad H_i=H^{a^i}\quad(0\leq i<3),
 \qquad \sigma(g)=a^{-1}ga.
\]
Here $T$ denotes its embedded factor in $G$. Direct closure gives
\[
 |G|=7344,\quad |H_i|=12,\quad
 H_i\cap H_j=B=\gen{ca^3}\ (i\ne j),\quad
 \gen{H_0,H_1,H_2}=G.                                      \tag{1}
\]
It also verifies that the three $H_i$ are distinct and $H^{a^3}=H$.
Consequently $\sigma$ cyclically permutes them, as required in the source.

Let $\Gamma$ have the 612 right cosets of $H_i$ as its objects of type $i$.
Cosets of different types are incident when they intersect. This is
indeed a geometry: an incident pair has a common element $g$ and extends
to $(H_0g,H_1g,H_2g)$; every singleton extends in the same way, and a
three-element flag already is a chamber. Its incidence graph is connected
by (1). The certificate constructs all coset partitions, hence every
incidence, rather than sampling them. There are 1836 objects, 7344 edges,
valency eight, and 2448 chambers. Each panel has one completion. Thus this
example is not firm or residually connected; neither property is a
hypothesis of the printed lemma. In fact the right action of $G$ is
chamber-transitive, since its chamber orbit has size $|G:B|=2448$.

\section{A central order-nine type cycle}
Write $R_g$ for right multiplication by $g$ on all cosets. This action is
faithful. Indeed its kernel lies in $B$, and the certificate checks that
neither of the two nonidentity elements of $B$ fixes all cosets.
The induced correlation
\[
 \tau(H_i g)=H_{i+1}a^{-1}ga
\]
cycles the types. Define $z=\tau R_{a^{-1}}$, so
\[
 z(H_i g)=H_{i+1}a^{-1}g.                                  \tag{2}
\]
It commutes with every $R_g$. Since $ca^3\in H_i$ for every $i$,
\[
 z^3(H_i g)=H_i a^{-3}g=H_i cg=H_i gc;
 \qquad z^3=R_c.                                          \tag{3}
\]
Thus $z$ has order nine. Further,
$\gen z\cap R_T=1$: an element of this intersection fixes types, so it
is $z^{3j}=R_{c^j}$, and faithfulness of $\gen c\times T$ gives $j=0$
modulo three. Therefore the correlation group contains
\[
 L=\gen{z,R_T}\cong C_9\times T,\qquad |L|=22032.           \tag{4}
\]
Every element of order three in $L$ preserves all types. To see this,
write it uniquely as $z^eR_t$. Its cube being the identity forces
$z^{3e}=R_{t^{-3}}$; the trivial intersection just proved gives
$3\mid e$, exactly the condition for preserving types.

\section{The full correlation bound}
Let $A$ be the full correlation group: incidence-preserving permutations
which send each type class to a type class. Form an auxiliary graph by
adding three marker vertices $m_0,m_1,m_2$, with $m_i$ adjacent to every
object of type $i$. Color the objects with one color and all three markers
with a second color. The markers are \emph{not} individually colored.
Automorphisms of this colored graph are exactly $A$: the unique marker
neighbor of an object records its type, and any permutation of the three
markers is allowed. Thus this construction imposes no extra order or
type-fixing requirement on a correlation.

\begin{lemma}\label{refine}
Refine a vertex coloring by replacing each color with the pair consisting
of its previous color and its vector of neighbor counts in the previous
color classes. Every color-preserving automorphism preserves every
refined class. If refinement after individualizing vertices is discrete,
their pointwise stabilizer is trivial.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
An automorphism preserves a vertex's previous color and bijects its
neighbors in every previous color class. It therefore preserves the
refined color. Induction proves the first assertion; singleton classes
prove the second.
\end{proof}

For reproducibility the certificate numbers objects from zero. Group
elements are sorted lexicographically as $(j,t)$ with $0\leq j<3$ and
$t$ its 18-entry permutation tuple; right cosets are numbered by the first
uncovered representative, separately for each type. The markers are
1836, 1837 and 1838. Vertices 0 and 612 are incident. After individualizing
these two objects, the numbers of color classes at successive refinements
are
\[
 9,21,60,187,579,630,633,633.
\]
The class containing vertex 1 is exactly $\{1,441,481\}$. After
individualizing 0, 612 and 1, the sequence is
\[
 11,28,126,786,1822,1839,1839;
\]
in particular it is discrete. All neighbor counts are recomputed from
the complete coset incidence relation.

There are at most 1836 images of vertex 0. Once it is fixed, vertex 612
has at most eight images, its object neighbors. Once both are fixed,
vertex 1 has at most three images by Lemma~\ref{refine}, and their
pointwise stabilizer is trivial. Hence
\[
 |A|\leq1836\cdot8\cdot3=44064.                            \tag{5}
\]
The data file additionally supplies one involution $r$, as a complete
permutation of the 1839 vertices. The certificate checks every adjacency
and both colors directly. On markers it fixes $m_0$ and interchanges
$m_1,m_2$. Thus $r\notin L$, since elements of $L$ induce only cyclic
type permutations. Equations (4)--(5) prove $|A|=44064$ and $[A:L]=2$.
They also show that the full type-preserving group is exactly $R_G$:
the type image is all $S_3$, and its kernel has order $44064/6=7344$.

\begin{theorem}
The geometry $\Gamma$ satisfies the hypotheses of source Lemma 1, but
has no correlation of order three cycling its types.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The construction and (1) verify the exact source hypotheses. Since $L$
has index two in $A$, every element of order three of $A$ lies in $L$.
The observation following (4) says that every such element fixes types.
This contradicts the defining property of a triality.
\end{proof}
There is also a complete Sylow certificate, independent of a conjugacy
class routine. The subgroup $P=\gen{z,R_a}$ is $C_9\times C_9$ of order
81, the full 3-part of 44064. Its 81 explicitly generated permutations
have order distribution $1:1$, $3:8$, $9:72$. All eight order-three
elements preserve types. By Sylow conjugacy, the same holds for every
order-three element of $A$, since the type-preserving kernel is normal.

\section{Finite boundary and consequences}
The whole \texttt{certificate.py} and \texttt{data.json} are required
finite premises. They use only exact integers and Python's standard
library. The former constructs the finite groups and all cosets, verifies
faithfulness and every adjacency, computes both refinement sequences,
checks the complete supplied reflection and enumerates the explicit
81-element Sylow subgroup. GAP and nauty were used to discover the model
and reflection, but neither is called by this certificate. The search
over other groups is not an exhaustiveness premise for this one example.

This resolves the previously isolated \emph{bare existential} obligation
of L122, not merely the order of the induced map. The valid repair of the
source construction is its already proved order qualification: requiring
$\sigma^3$ to act trivially on every defining coset, in particular
$\sigma^3=1$, supplies an actual triality. The six explicit source-family
examples already satisfy that stronger hypothesis and are unaffected.
No whole-original admission, new conjecture answer, formalization or
measurement is asserted by this bounded author packet.
