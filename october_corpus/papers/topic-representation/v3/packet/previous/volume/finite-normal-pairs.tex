\begin{abstract}
We give a complete reconstruction of the finite normal-pair representation
results of D\'iaz Ramos, Molinier and Viruel. An explicit ordered-pair graph
encodes a finite group and a normal subgroup, and the common chamber theorem
turns it into a connected incidence geometry. We also recover the authors'
smaller recursive $(S_n,A_n)$ family, including its color sizes and faithful
top action. A completed-hexagon example is calculated directly. Its corrected
type-preserving subgroup, the chamber degree correction and the degree-one
base of the recursive family are kept separate from the unchanged principal
representation theorem.
\end{abstract}
