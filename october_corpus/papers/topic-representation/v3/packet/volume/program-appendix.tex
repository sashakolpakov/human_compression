This appendix contains every declared mathematical program: one required
finite graph certificate and nine optional validation files. The complete
\texttt{certificate/graph.json} adjacency and generator data is also mandatory
for the required certificate; it is supplied as a whole machine-readable file,
not replaced by a digest or by this appendix. The uniform theorems have written
proofs; the bounded runs do not establish them. The orchestration driver and
scoped Lean files are inventoried separately. No executable listing is hidden
inside either scientific application.
