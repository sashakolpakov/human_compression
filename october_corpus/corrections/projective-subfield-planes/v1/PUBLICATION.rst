Projective subfield planes: one omission in two fixed sources
============================================================

This package publishes a complete three-page counterexample proof and its
literal finite certificates. It concerns two already separate catalogue
entries, L063 and L064, and one shared mathematical omission. It does not
give a corrected global classification or recover their other results.

Read proof/subfield_planes.pdf (or its exact TeX) first. For s=8 and a prime
cubic extension, the ambient group is PSL2(512). Its two parabolics have
orders504 and448, intersection56, and local degrees9 and8. The geometry
is connected, thick, flag-transitive, residually weakly primitive and
locally two-transitive. The second local action has kernel8; the actual
source definition permits that kernel. Nine additive planes are counted
as subspaces, not as nine geometry isomorphism classes.

The uniform argument works for every s=2^n>=4 and prime extension degree
m>=2. It retains classical projective group orders, simplicity and
natural prime-extension subfield maximality. The scalar-subspace lemma
is proved here. Its related L115 manuscript is retained only as provenance.
No finite program proves that classical classification or the uniform
argument. The original quadratic and analogous A5 families retain their
source authorship; no historical priority or absence-of-errata claim is made.

Exact source dispositions
-------------------------

L063 is the fixed24-page Ars Mathematica Contemporanea6(2013),365–388
edition, published15March2013, of Buekenhout, De Saedeleer and Leemans,
On the rank two geometries of the groups PSL(2,q): part II. The targets
are Theorem1.1/Table4 and Lemma5.11/Proposition5.12. The table restricts
the relevant affine partner to m=2, and its other rows do not contain
the s=8,m=3 example even after interchanging the two types.

L064 is the separately catalogued21-page appendix with placeholder
volume/date and older numbering. The contradiction is in the proof of
Proposition22, general case, subcase2, physical page12. Lemma21's proof
already lists the affine abstract group without that restriction; this
package does not misattribute the error to that lemma or infer an unseen
principal theorem from the appendix heading. The false restriction and
resulting exhaustion are the separately scoped principal disposition.

scope_routes.json preserves the exact source hashes, pages, targets and
remaining scope. admission_proposal.json requests two principal-refutation
dispositions only after public gates. There is one mathematical defect,
zero ordinary recoveries, no newly solved question, Lean formalization or
compression measurement. Both originals' other classifications remain
neither recovered nor refuted by this construction.
