The remaining extension step for L029
====================================

This is a working proof frontier, not an accepted theorem or an assertion
that the principal source result is false.

Established by the frozen component
-----------------------------------

The canonical gallery component Omega is GC union GD_0. It contains all
objects and incident pairs. Every proper face of a canonical chamber is
a G-translate of the corresponding base face: for a face of D_0 choose
an omitted type i, and use D_i=alpha_i^{-1}D_0, since the proper face of
D_i omitting i is the base face. This argument is intrinsic to the
canonical component and does not normalize arbitrary flags.

Consequently, in a thin geometry, a chamber outside Omega cannot have a
canonical panel. A canonical panel already has its two completions in
Omega. In rank four any extra chamber therefore has four noncanonical
triple faces, although every one of its pairs is canonical. Under full
chirality every gallery component is an automorphic image of Omega and
also spans the entire object set and incidence graph.

The source's remaining main obligation is exactly to rule out such
additional components under full-group chirality. The stronger assertion
that no noncanonical triple in any base-thin rank-four C+ system can
extend to a chamber would suffice in rank four, but it has not been proved.
The completed S6 diagnostic supports that assertion only for its literal
finite target: 456 C+ markings up to inner conjugacy, 384 base-thin, all
384 globally thin, and no noncanonical triple with a completion. It is
not a general argument, and no new larger census is planned.

The residue obstruction
-----------------------

At a fixed vertex the internal rank-three C+ coset model contains every
object of the actual residue, because the ambient specified group is
transitive on incident pairs. It may have fewer incidence edges. Its
rank-three residual connectedness therefore gives only a connected
spanning graph of that singleton residue. It does not establish that the
actual rank-two subresidues are connected, or that the internal triangles
are all actual triangles. The possible extra chamber above corresponds
to a triangle in the actual residue with all three edges missing from
the internal model. Replacing that model by the actual residue without
paying incidence surjectivity is exactly the invalid shortcut to avoid.

A possible algebraic approach, still unproved
--------------------------------------------

If an automorphism normalizes the specified group, its action on each
coset orbit is a group automorphism followed by a coset transport. In the
special case of an automorphism commuting with G, these transports lie
in N_G(H_i)/H_i and must preserve every product H_i H_j. Noncanonical
images of the base chamber would be compatible local transports without
a common global representative. This is a possible place to use a
compatibility or cohomological argument. No general exactness theorem,
normality of G in the full automorphism group, or reduction of all extra
components to this special case has been proved.

The explicit class-two diagnostic tested odd-characteristic central quotients
only. Its coordinate quotients can collapse one parabolic central line to
zero; thus one must not infer that every valid quotient has four nonzero
central lines in a plane. The characteristic-two version has not been tested
here and might behave differently. Since its displayed generators are
involutions, any finding would still need a separate full-group chirality
check. No characteristic-two counterexample or general quotient conclusion
is claimed.
