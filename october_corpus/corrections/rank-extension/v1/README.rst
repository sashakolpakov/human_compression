Two corrections to the specified rank-extension paper
====================================================

These complete proofs concern Cameron, Fernandes and Leemans,
*The number of string C-groups of high rank*, arXiv:2212.12723v2
(11 January 2023), catalogue original L019. They address the literal
statements in that edition. No historical-priority claim is made.

The `three-page rank-extension proof
<rank_extension/rank_extension_correction.pdf>`_ gives a C-marking on nine
points satisfying every stated hypothesis of Theorem 6.4. Its prescribed
extension fails the intersection property: two disjoint pairs of new
generators have cubes equal to the same nonidentity transposition. The
original C-property is proved through explicit subgroup formulas and the
standard attributed end-parabolic criterion. All fracture, split and
endpoint conditions are checked. The example is intransitive; it does not
disprove the transitive restriction or the main stability theorem.

The `two-page endpoint proof <endpoint/endpoint_converse.pdf>`_ addresses
the general converse in Proposition 2.4. The dependent marking (a,b,ab) of
C2 squared fails the intersection property, while twisting its first mark
by an outside central involution gives a basis marking of C2 cubed. The
forward extension theorem used in our L010 constructions is unaffected.

Distinct review read both complete proofs and the exact source hypotheses.
All five fresh proof pages were viewed. Independent vector-space and
permutation-tuple implementations agree with the complete GAP diagnostics.
The written proofs do not depend on these computations. The classical
end criterion is retained and attributed, rather than claimed as new.

These are corrections to published statements, not answers to numbered
open conjectures or recoveries of the whole L019 manuscript. L019 remains
on the programme awaiting complete recovery. There is no new compression
factor or full Lean claim. Frozen author-stage wording is superseded by
the `distinct admission <admission.json>`_.
