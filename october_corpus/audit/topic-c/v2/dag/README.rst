Topic C: dependencies and complete proof costs
==============================================

The paired dependencies, passage ownership and reader are independently
reviewed and accepted. Start with the `two-page explanation <costs/reader.pdf>`_
and the `acquisition plot <costs/acquisition.pdf>`_. Download the
`self-contained explorer <costs/explorer.html>`_ and open it locally to
inspect the historical and current proofs for four complete applications
or any of the forty recorded source clauses. A
`static graph <costs/default_graph.pdf>`_ illustrates the equal-parameter
application. The HTML works without a server or network connection.

The accepted comparisons
------------------------

The combined written comparison remains ``(x + 60,256) / 71,277``, where
``x`` is the unknown cost of L108's original PDF. Our entire common paper
costs 27,131 characters and the four entire applications cost 44,146.
The missing source leaves the combined numerical factor unknown. Its
10,071-character application is still fully charged.

The whole printed appendix contains both required programs. Including it
gives ``(x + 60,256) / 80,076``. Counting the two complete raw files instead
gives denominator 79,600. These are alternative program representations,
not two charges for the same programs. The execution driver is inventoried
separately in the scientific edition.

After acquiring the whole common paper and both complete programs, the
individual source/application factors are 1.118 for L088, 1.709 for N013
and 2.960 for F014; L108 remains unknown. Each denominator includes the
entire application. These use calibrated NFC characters without whitespace;
all thirty extraction, normalization and metric combinations are retained.
The `length admission <../measurement/admission.json>`_ supplies the
independently counted document costs. This graph does not change them.

What the graph adds
-------------------

Arrows run from prerequisites to their uses. The graph has 156 nodes and
249 typed arrows; historical attribution, formal evidence and validation
are distinguished from mathematical premises. Nodes have different levels
of detail, so there is no node-count compression factor.

Every measured passage belongs to one of 199 exclusive cost owners.
Statements can be shared by a named result and its proof branches. A route
first takes all its prerequisites, then the union of their passage owners,
and only then sums costs. Adding displayed box costs would count shared
statements twice. Context and references outside a selected route remain
charged in the complete-paper comparison.

The acquisition sequence pays for U_C, the complete program appendix,
and C1 through C4. It displays new local cost and previously acquired
passages used, without subtracting those passages again. Separate topics
each pay their own common theory. The sequence describes a reading order;
it does not measure research effort or a shortest possible proof.

Review and limits
-----------------

The `logical review <logical_review/review.json>`_ read the complete
sources and current proofs and corrected six dependency interfaces.
The `ownership and reader review <independent_review/review.json>`_
checked all 195 selected-text boundaries, 703 spans across extraction
variants, 5,970 owner metric cells and 270 complete-document metric cells.
Its independent implementation reproduced 85 output unions, 90 known
conditional fractions and 90 symbolic combined expressions. All fifteen
cost corruptions and a reader regression were rejected.

The root replayed those independent checks and the logical check, which
rejects sixteen deliberate corruptions. The offline reader was checked
in 180 selected DOM views, with all 249 fine arrows retained. Its static
figures and both PDF pages were visually inspected. No real-browser
layout test is claimed. Full reproduction uses the private original PDFs;
the public snapshot verifier checks frozen bindings, document conservation,
prerequisite unions, complete acquisition and the admitted arithmetic.

The original L108 PDF, expanded external proofs and unextracted graphics
remain unknown. The source corrections, two excluded auxiliary assertions,
and separate higher-rank question remain visible. Sixteen existing Lean
declarations verify specified components; the graph adds no formal theorem
or complete application formalization.

Frozen author packets retain their original prospective review wording.
`admission.json <admission.json>`_ and the subsequent reviews record their
acceptance. The original logical and reader packets are preserved under
``logical_previous`` and ``costs_previous``. The latter has a corrected
Lean count and selected-route display; all its costs and mathematical graph
data are unchanged. This publication adds no source or solved-question credit.
