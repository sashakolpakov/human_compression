Further leaves and elementary halving criteria
============================================

Read remaining_clauses.pdf and its complete dependencies. This root-authored
four-page continuation is frozen for distinct review. It covers the generic
second-leaf criterion, stabilizer tests and elementary examples, together with
the girth and duality clauses. It does not alone recover N014.

The optional second_leaf_example.py enumerates the literal finite incidence
example and its parity-component construction. Its generated output checks
all flags, residues and both leaf conditions. The mathematical proof does
not rely on that code. No new Lean or measurement is included.
