The active catalogue: 106 accessible originals
==============================================

The programme now contains **106 original manuscripts**, each with a usable
complete source. The `catalogue <catalogue.json>`_ records their titles,
authors, source editions, proof targets and current principal recovery status;
`catalogue.csv <catalogue.csv>`_ is the concise complete list.

The user narrowed the former 161-original programme to accessible sources.
The `55 unavailable originals <archived_unavailable.json>`_ are archived
outside the active catalogue, with individual reasons and historical access
evidence. Retrieval is no longer scheduled. Their earlier bibliography and
all published numerical reports remain preserved; this scope change removes
no accepted proof and changes no measured compression factor.

Within the active catalogue, 42 originals have admitted measured recoveries
and 64 await complete recovery. Recovery means the specified principal
results, with corrections and retained inputs declared in each admission.
It does not mean that every conjecture or cited theorem has been resolved.

The proposal to exclude topics that ultimately contain only one paper is
recorded in `singleton_topics.json <singleton_topics.json>`_. First assess
whether those papers share a useful common theory with other accessible
originals. No singleton has yet been removed. If exclusions are later made,
the excluded originals and their previous factors will remain visible, and
comparisons for the reduced set will be labeled as such.
