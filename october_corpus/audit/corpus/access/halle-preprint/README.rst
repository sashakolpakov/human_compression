A complete source for L100
=========================

Gottschalk and Leemans, Geometries for the group PSL(3,4),
`Halle report 99-30 <https://www2.mathematik.uni-halle.de/reports/shadows/99-30report.html>`_.
The institutional PostScript source and its local PDF derivative contain 22
complete pages, all read and visually checked. This is the 1999 preprint;
the 25-page 2003 journal edition has not been obtained or compared.

The paper classifies 29 geometries across ranks 2--5, supplies their diagrams,
automorphism and correlation data, and gives geometric constructions. These
are `pending recovery targets <recovery_targets.json>`_, not admitted proofs.
The existing enumeration and projective constructions may supply common theory.

The `access outcomes <outcomes.json>`_ retain the seven unsuccessful attempts
in the same batch. The fixed catalogue is unchanged. The source files and
rendered pages remain private; exact hashes and the edition distinction are
recorded in the `root review <root_review.json>`_. There is no numerical factor
or conjecture credit for retrieving a paper.
