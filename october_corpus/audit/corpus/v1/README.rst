All 161 originals remain in the programme
========================================

The complete original-by-original table is `inventory.rst <packet/inventory.rst>`_.
`inventory.csv <packet/inventory.csv>`_ is the flat table and
`inventory.json <packet/inventory.json>`_ records the exact source, proof
and status evidence. `prioritized_batches.json <packet/prioritized_batches.json>`_
assigns every unfinished original to a next batch exactly once.

At this snapshot, 104 originals have usable full text: 100 PDFs and four
complete non-PDF representations. Twenty-seven have admitted recoveries
of specified principal results; 77 accessible originals still need complete
recovery. Another 57 originals remain full-source retrieval targets.
An accessible original is never excluded because its proof, common theory
or measurement is unfinished. Missing PDF does not mean missing manuscript.

The original list of 161 titles is unchanged. N006 and N007 were already
retrieved but their old access flags were stale, explaining 102 versus 104.
Files bearing the names of L097, L098, L101 and L123 contain other papers;
those files do not establish access to the intended originals. Some other
PDF-named files contain server-error HTML. Each actual usable representation
has a hash and an identified edition. Retrieval continues for the 57 without
claiming that all possible access routes have been exhausted.

The register separates full admitted contracts, author candidates, partial
results, conditional lemmas, missing census data and unread targets. Its
numbered-statement candidates are not automatically endorsed as principal
results. L022 is an accessible editorial: it remains an original without
an invented mathematical theorem. Source and full-formalization obligations
remain explicit, and this inventory adds no proof or conjecture credit.

Root independently checked the exact original IDs and the access/proof counts,
then replayed all 695 bound evidence files and the 13 structural controls.
This checks the programme and its evidence; it does not supply 161 new proofs.
The frozen author snapshot and its historical statuses are preserved.
The later L028 independent review and F015 root review are recorded in
admission.json; subsequent principal admissions will be additive.

For B/C costs, the now accepted complete available-text comparisons are at
`the comparison audit <../../available-text/v1/README.rst>`_. The frozen
inventory's earlier measurement statuses remain historical observations.
No primary PDFs, full rendered bodies or private N005 HTML are exported.
