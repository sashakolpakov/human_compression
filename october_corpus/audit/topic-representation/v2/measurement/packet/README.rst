Exact boundary and sensitivity
------------------------------

The JSON contains all thirty coordinates: five units, NFC/NFKC and three
extraction variants. The five units are whitespace words, alphanumeric runs,
characters without whitespace, characters with collapsed whitespace, and
UTF-8 bytes with collapsed whitespace. The variants are untrimmed raw,
selected raw and calibrated selected. Every coordinate has three whole-topic
fractions (written, printed-validation alternative, raw-validation
alternative) and one conditional fraction. All 120 unreduced numerators and
denominators, reduced fractions and decimal displays are also exported in
``comparisons.csv``. No metric is selected retrospectively to maximize a
factor.

All source pages, title, abstract, references, author addresses and original
arXiv banner remain counted. Only the exact accepted page/running furniture
is removed. Printed hyphens, code continuation marks and actual whitespace
remain. The 134 occurrence-bound visible-glyph repairs include three byte-9
right braces that are not whitespace. Four mapsto strokes are deliberately
separate from their adjacent arrows. The independent audit inspected all
27 PDF pages and the actual font dictionaries/outlines. This numerical
packet imports those accepted counts; it does not rerun extraction or alter
font rules.

Source vector figures, edges, colors, positions and formula layout have
unknown cost beyond their extracted labels. External proofs, historical
software and operational engineering costs are likewise unknown, not zero.
The source's Theorem A already answers its Question 2.4. The project claims
no new answer or fixed-rank-two representation.
