Highly symmetric hypertopes: complete principal recovery
=======================================================

This private package prepares the corrected principal contract of Fernandes,
Leemans and Weiss, Highly symmetric hypertopes, using the fixed 22-page
accepted author manuscript. Publication still requires root's operational
review and registration gate. No new measurements or question solutions are
proposed.

Read the five-page principal synthesis first, then the complete eight-page
residue theory and five-page finite certificate manuscript. The eighteen-page
complete_proof.pdf concatenates these three manuscripts without abridgement.
The previously published chamber and hexagonal common proofs, their programs,
reviews and historical evidence remain complete acquired dependencies; they
are not replaced by a citation to the synthesis. Page counts here describe
reading routes and are not compression measurements.

The source register contains 62 dispositions: nineteen named general clauses,
Construction 8.1, twenty-three finite table rows, three examples, three further
retained classification statements, five question dispositions, seven
contextual antecedents, and the corrected page-17 illustration. The marked
string correspondence is one of the nineteen clauses and is retained, not a
new proof. Together there are four explicitly borrowed correspondence or
classification inputs.

The geometric assumptions remain exact: the C-group converse requires flag
transitivity; the C+ converse requires an actual hypertope and exactly two
chamber orbits; faithfulness requires the common-core quotient; the geometric
reflection may be nontrivial even when its conjugation automorphism is the
identity. Proper truncations are not asserted to be thin.

Four corrections are explicit. The two table captions confuse full-group
order with chamber count in chiral rows. The Table 2 (3,2,2) group is the
parity subgroup of S5 x S4. Unrestricted toroidal wording must exclude the
unit-period degeneracies. The page-17 A6 rotation illustration fails IC+:
its two order-60 even parabolics meet in order10 rather than the required
cyclic order5. This last correction does not settle a general C+ converse.

Problems9.1 and9.2 and the chiral-table completeness conjecture remain
unanswered here. Previously accepted restricted answers to9.3 and9.4 are
acquired without new question or Lean credit. The finite tables are exact
positive certificates, not an exhaustive chiral classification.

Portable transport
------------------

The exact transport manifest records every logical path, uncompressed byte
length and SHA256, public object path, storage encoding and stored identity.
Byte-identical public objects are reused; larger text inputs may be losslessly
gzip-compressed. Markdown provenance is stored as .txt objects and restored
under its old name only inside the private replay tree. Accepted author
manifests, proof bytes, programs and receipts are never rewritten.

Only explicitly enumerated documentary assets are hash-only: original source
articles/text and rendered original pages. All generated proof manuscripts,
proof rasters, mathematical numeric inputs, execution receipts and review
records are retained. The original CHEVIE web receipt remains attributed as
the bounded source extraction it actually records, not a fabricated hash of a
full upstream download.

Historical preflight and final editions have separate exact locators. Six
finite preflight files differ from the final accepted files; each old hash is
restored exactly, with the inverse-delta reconstruction record retained.
These are historical execution inputs, not alternative accepted statements.
The public hexagonal predecessor manifests similarly resolve to their exact
old public or retained objects. No locator is silently retargeted to a newer
scientific edition.

The portable checker restores an isolated study/public tree from only the
public repository plus the staged payload. It verifies complete manifest
exports, principal and nested pins, then uses an explicit application adapter
to import the unchanged source-register validator. The adapter uses retained
structured source records and numerical certificates, not the excluded source
articles or a private filesystem fallback. It repeats the bounded source-join
controls, compares every page of the eighteen-page volume, builds the new
synthesis twice and renders all five pages. It does not rerun finite group
censuses or Lean. The exact final replay command follows; final distinct review and root
adoption are both bound.

All three immediate TeX manuscripts were scanned for literal input/include
or graphic dependencies; none requires an adjacent additional input file.
The complete older common proof trees retain their original TeX inputs.
