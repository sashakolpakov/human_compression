Affine proof dependencies
=========================

This candidate records the actual historical and current proof routes for the
accepted L012 contract: 33 inherited rows, one complete common paper, one
application and six complete required programs. It preserves the scientific
edition published at ``1eb5745`` and the numerical publication at
``fe91a420a9120a0566b474209c71e1732cc5bebe``. It introduces no mathematical
result, source, question credit, text ownership or numerical factor.

``logical_graph.json`` contains typed prerequisite-to-use arrows. Only
``proof_use``, ``definition_use``, ``hypothesis_use``, ``proof_assembly`` and
``retained_input_use`` enter prerequisite unions. Attribution, comparison,
formal evidence, validation and operational links do not. ``coverage.json``

closures. ``protocol.json`` fixes these conventions.

arXiv:2001.02945v2 preprint of 14 July 2024. They do not certify every source
statement. In particular, the first positive exponent in Lemma 4.5's R0

documentary, while the current theorem has the independently accepted repaired
proof. The source's Corollary 5.2 cross-reference to Theorem 1.2 is also flagged:
the displayed power-of-two argument uses Theorem 5.1. No journal edition was
collated here.
