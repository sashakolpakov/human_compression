Reading and charging the proofs
-------------------------------

An arrow points from a prerequisite to its use. The graph contains 119
argument, reference and evidence nodes, with 228 typed arrows. Historical
assertions, current proof premises, attribution and verification evidence
have different roles. A historical edge records the printed argument; it
does not endorse a source assertion that our review corrected.

Every counted passage has one of 194 exclusive owners. Interfaces for several
lemmas may share a statement or setup passage. We collect all prerequisites,
take the union of their passage owners and then sum the costs. Adding the
costs displayed in separate graph boxes would sometimes count text twice.
There is no compression ratio based on the number of nodes.

The audit preserves 120 full-document metric cells and 5,820 owner cells.
It checks 119 individual prerequisite closures, 99 unions for the 33 source
records, the complete application, the complete record and two additional
current outputs: 222 routes altogether. All six programs stay whole; their
shared appendix introduction is charged once in each union.

The full comparison also pays for unused common results, introductions,
references and other context. An application does not receive a second
discount for using a prerequisite already paid for in the common theory.
Separate topics pay for their own complete common papers. This topic has
one application; its acquisition order establishes no recurring sawtooth
pattern or historical research chronology.
