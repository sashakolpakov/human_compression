Target
------
Every fractured natural symmetric string C-marking of degree n>=5 and
rank r with 2r>n+2 has an internal sufficient split. Here sufficient
means a pure perfect split, or a perfect split with one trivial internal
factor and odd permutations in both actual groups meeting at its crossing.
The separately reviewed leaf and odd-attachment criteria then prove a
degree-and-rank extension. This does not yet prove the stable bijection.

Strong degree induction
-----------------------
Degrees5/6 with the strict inequality have rank n-1. The acquired complete
extremal classification gives the simplex, except for the S6 outer action,
whose transitive omissions violate fracture. A middle simplex mark is pure.
The one-edge forest r<=n-1 makes n<=4 impossible in the strict range.

At larger degree use the already accepted anchored_even conditional
reduction, which itself invokes the accepted trivial-factor theorem and
all the actual-wing/transport interfaces. Under strictly smaller degree
sufficient-split induction, the only residual is a critical symmetric

of degree6/8/10 and half that rank. The accepted small_even theorem makes
the latter exactly the primitive six-point rank-three A5; its actual
odd external factor P pays that theorem's kernel hypotheses.

The full degree/rank are n=m+5, r=m/2+4. The accepted small_symmetric_v2
and degree_ten packets already pay m<=10 (though the following argument
is general enough to avoid invoking the latter again). In P, use the
first anchored unique cut from anchored_first_split. The accepted

the subsequent tail and leaves precisely an actual middle Sym_(2k+1)
of rankk, paired at every colour, with distinct endpoint anchors.
