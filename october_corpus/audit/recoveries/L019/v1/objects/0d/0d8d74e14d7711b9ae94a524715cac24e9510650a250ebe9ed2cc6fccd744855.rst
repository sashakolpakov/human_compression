For S6 the first-mark representatives exhaust anchor-stabilizer conjugacy
classes of involutions moving the anchor. All later marks fixing the anchor
are tried. Adjacent noncommutation is necessary: the component moving the
anchor is transitive when the complete marking is transitive, and any other
commuting component fixing that anchor would fix every point. The literal
IP and order tests therefore lose no possible anchored marking. GoodCut
checks actual omission orbits and pointwise support, both orientations,
unique crossing, the trivial internal factor, and the correct two odd
attaching groups. These are exactly the asserted sufficient conditions.

For S8 the point stabilizer and its successive literal centralizers act
on the invariant candidate set and preserve every previously chosen mark.
OrbitsDomain with OnPoints uses permutation conjugation. Thus representative
selection preserves extension existence. The index screen follows from
independence: each remaining generator doubles the current subgroup order
at least. Prefix IP is necessary, and its early rank-two shortcut is sound
for distinct nonidentity involutions. At the last node the full order is
40320. Fresh replay recovers the complete node sequence and all nine actual
tuples, not a selected list substituted for enumeration.

The even-kernel premise is correctly weaker than the full leaf criterion.
The actual external transitive factor need not contain an odd permutation.
Its disjoint labels and single-point support intersection give the supported
alternating subgroup, and the original IP bounds its intersection with P_j.
The two nonpure fractured tuples fail this necessary inclusion: the even
supported kernels have orders 60 and 360, compared with previous supported
kernels of orders 10 and 60. All these are actual supported subgroups, with
no quotient-IP inference. The ninth tuple fails fracture. Hence the six
surviving cases have a pure first mark.

obligation. A pure first transposition attaches the anchor to only one tail
orbit, so transitivity forces the tail to be transitive on the remaining
seven points. In the whole attachment, omission of that mark has exactly
the external support and the seven-point tail as its two supported,

transports by the already proved one-point lemma; oddness persists under
enlargement. The m2 independence obstruction and the m4 transposition case
