              Controls and scope. A literal traversal of all square-one tuples independently agrees with the
              inner-orbit traversal for three matrices and six finite groups (18 cases, seven surjective inner orbits).
              Complete old and new normal kernel inventories agree bijectively as ordered marked quotients
              for three bounded presentations. During those changed searches all 676 composed conjugation
              images are also evaluated by the original nested formula and compared exactly. Invalid matrices
              and mismatched source presentations are rejected. The independent core checker still requires full
              intersection and actual flag-transitivity tests for positive hypertopes; this method does not weaken
              them.
                 Thepreserved first control driver accidentally captured the replacement function as its reference.
              Its result is not used for the original-versus-new traversal comparison; the corrected driver reruns
              all controls while capturing the installed function first. A pair of capped diagnostic profiles shows
              one index-1920, dimension-71 matrix step taking about 12.7 seconds by nested evaluation and 1.1
              seconds by composition. Neither profile completes its target bound, and neither is a new census or
              general performance theorem. All earlier capped searches remain uncredited.
              Attribution. The finite targets and surrounding algorithm are the installed LINS 0.9 imple-
              mentation, based on Firth’s normal-subgroup algorithm; the direct composition method is already
              present in GAP 4.15.1. Stabilizer-orbit traversal is standard. The present author supplies the Cox-
              eter interface, exact substitution proof, code, and bounded controls. The root supplied the original
              L033 probe; a separate reader supplied the acquired LINS interface audit. No novelty, whole-source,
              measurement, question, or Lean credit is claimed.
