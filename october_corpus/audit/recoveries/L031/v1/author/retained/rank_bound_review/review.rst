Exhaustion of the linear cases
-----------------------------

For h=ab, noncommutation gives h² != 1. Reciprocal primary spaces have
matching Jordan partitions. A nonsemisimple h therefore has exactly the
three kinds listed: one size-four unipotent block, paired size-two blocks
at reciprocal nonunit eigenvalues, or a size-two unipotent block plus a
simple reciprocal pair. The potential 3+1 partition makes the first
Jordan vector orthogonal to all four basis vectors: pairing invariance
successively forces B12=B13=B1y=0. Every remaining nonsemisimple
centralizer is abelian, so cannot contain the other nonabelian pair.
The semisimple list is likewise exhaustive: four distinct nonunit
values give a torus; repeated values give either one active reciprocal
plane plus a fixed plane, or two two-dimensional reciprocal spaces.

In the first surviving case the first pair has scalar commutant on its
active plane. A nontrivial involution on the fixed plane would make its
centralizer abelian, so it acts trivially there. The other pair acts
irreducibly on that plane. It has no fixed vectors or fixed covectors,
so there are no off-diagonal intertwining blocks. The two full
centralizers are the two separate symplectic plane factors.

In the second case a swaps the reciprocal eigenspaces. The pairing
matrix S is symmetric because a preserves the alternating form. If S
is nonalternating, Gram orthogonalization and square roots yield I2;
the displayed one-parameter isometries are exhaustive and commute.
For alternating S, the centralizer is the diagonal SL2. Two
noncommuting unipotents in SL2 have distinct fixed lines; solving both
commutant equations forces every two-by-two block to be scalar. The
other centralizer is thus the multiplicity SL2, and these two factors
commute. No general double-centralizer theorem is silently imported.

The six-string contradiction uses its two endpoint pairs and the two
middle generators. It needs no intersection property. For the exact
L031 Theorem6.1 interface, only standard simplicity of Sp4(q), q>=4,
is retained to exclude a disconnected full-group string.
