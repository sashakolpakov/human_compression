The groups PGL (2,q ) and PSL (2,q ) act via the fractional transformations
on the projective line PG (1,q ) of q + 1 points. The stabiliser of a point of the
projective line PG (1,q ) in PGL (2,q ) is isomorphic to Eq ⋊Cq−1. If q is odd, the
stabiliser in PSL (2,q ) of a point is isomorphic to Eq ⋊C(q−1)/ 2 and if q is even,
then PSL (2,q ) = PGL (2,q ). Moreover, the pointwise stabiliser of two points in
PGL (2,q ) is a cyclic group of order q − 1 whereas the pointwise stabiliser of two
points in PSL (2,q ) is a cyclic group of order ( q − 1)/2 if q is odd.
