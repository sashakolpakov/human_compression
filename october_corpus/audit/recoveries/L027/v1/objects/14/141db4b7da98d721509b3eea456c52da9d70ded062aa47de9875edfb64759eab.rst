a not +/-1, has (phi3*t)^2=M(2,-2), so |phi3*t|=2p, not4.

Normal forms and quotient
-------------------------
After putting the commuting outer pair in diagonal Klein D and applying
one duality if needed, an invariant coordinate point is on the middle
homology's axis. Put that point e3. Then t=[[A,0],[v,-1]], where
A=[[a,b],[c,-a]], a^2+bc=1, and vA=v. D projects onto all two-dimensional
sign matrices; its bottom entry is their determinant. The full group
is generated by D and t, independently of which two D involutions are
chosen as ordered outer marks.

If b,c both nonzero, diagonal scaling puts b=-a-1,c=a-1, a!=+/-1.
The +1 left eigenspace is span(1,-1), so v can be normalized either0
or(1,-1). In the latter case, (t*phi3)^2=M(2,-2) is in the actual
translation kernel N. Diagonal sign projections yield both coordinate
axes; action by t and repeated projection multiply coordinates by a.
Thus N=M(K^2), K=Fp(a). Conversely all matrices are over K, and the
translation kernel is contained there. Removing M(v) gives the pure
block A lift. Therefore G=K^2 semidirect H, with H generated by
D and pure A. Let h=order(lambda), lambda+lambda^-1=2a, and
m=lcm(h,2). The actual H is dihedral of order2m; K is the trace field.
At a=0, m=4, K=Fp and its signed permutation action gives
(D_(2p) x D_(2p)) semidirect C2 = D_(2p) wr C2.
For v=0 the group is simply H; rank3 IP in a dihedral group forces
a degenerate type[k,2] with k odd and groupD_(4k).

Uniform IP and generic types
----------------------------
Let d1=diag(1,-1,-1),d2=diag(-1,1,-1),d3=d1d2. The three ends
A_i=<d_i,t> have adjacent rotation orders h,h'=order(-lambda),2p.
The first two are translation conjugates of the corresponding pure
block dihedral subgroups. In the convention M(c)^-1 h_A M(c), the
required vectors are c1=(0,1/(1-a)),c2=(-1/(a+1),0).
Their difference is nonzero. No nonidentity rotation in H fixes a
nonzero vector, so its point stabilizer has order at most2. This proves
A1 intersection A2=<t>, through their actual complement embeddings.
The third end is M(Fp*v) semidirect <d3,h_A>, of order4p.
Any common element with A_i injects into the Klein image. An element
with image d3 in A_i has translation +/-2c_i, not in the K-line Kv,
so it cannot be in A3. Multiplication by common t similarly excludes
the image d3*h_A. Hence A_i intersection A3=<t>.
This establishes full rank3 IP for all three outer pairs and exact
new types [h,h'],[h,2p],[h',2p], up to reversal. It includes a=0:
[4,4],[4,2p]. It uses actual intersections, not quotient IP.

Triangular cases
----------------
If b=c=0, A diagonal. v=0 puts t in D and fails IP; v!=0 givesD4p.
If just one of b,c is zero, a=+/-1. Coordinate permutation/scaling
reduces to either
 t_product=[[1,0,0],[1,-1,0],[1,0,-1]]
or t_heis=[[1,-2,0],[0,-1,0],[1,-1,-1]], or the v=0 D4p case.
The first generates Cp^2 with independent sign changes, D2p x D2p.
In coordinates N=(x,y), d1 flipsboth,d2 flipsx,d3 flipsy,t=(1,1)d1.
A1=<d1,t> has diagonal translation line and order2p. A2 has y equal
the bit of the y sign of its D component; A3 similarly x equals its
x-sign bit. Thus A1 meets either in<t>, but A2 intersection A3 has
order4. The permitted nondegenerate types are[p,2p] and reverse.

For t_heis, put U(x,y,z)=I+xE12+yE31+zE32, with law
(x,y,z)(X,Y,Z)=(x+X,y+Y,z+Z+yX). Then t=U(2,1,1)d1.
Commuting this U with its d2 conjugate gives nonzero central root
coefficient-4; products then give both root directions. The full group
is Heis_p semidirect D. Its first end A1 is
 U(2k,k,k^2)d1^epsilon. A2's kernel over D is
 {U(x,0,x/2)}, and A3's is {U(0,y,-y)}.
A1 intersects each in<t> by the nonzero paired x,y coordinates.
A2 and A3 have disjoint kernels. For image d2, A2 has translations
U(x,0,x/2), while A3 has U(2,t,t); equality would force x=2,t=0
and then1=0. For image d3, A2 has U(x-2,1,x/2-1), while A3
has U(0,y,-y); equality forces x=2,y=1 and then0=-1. Thus these
two images are excluded, and the common d1 image is uniquely t.
Their intersection is exactly<t>. Allthree pairs have IP and types
[p,2p],[p,2p],[2p,2p]. The finite triangular diagnostic p3,5,7
checks these exact assertions (read final receipt before packaging).

Irreducible case
----------------
Write middle homology t=-I+2u f with f(u)=1. If no point or line
is fixed by the whole group, every coordinate of u and f is nonzero,
since the diagonal Klein has exactly the coordinate eigenpoints/planes.
Then Q=diag(f1/u1,f2/u2,f3/u3) is nonsingular, t and D preserve it,
and Q(u,u)=1. Faithful SO3 embeds G in PGL2. Retained natural-subfield
Dickson classification excludes cyclic/dihedral/Borel (each fixes a
point or line in the adjoint3-dimensional representation), and A4
is not generated by involutions. Thus only S4,A5,PSL2(K),PGL2(K)
remain, exactly the source's Theorem5.2 alternatives. Rank3 C-markings
within those projective-line groups are retained separately, not rerun.
