The Gram correspondence
-----------------------
Centering removes translations without changing distances. An isometry
inducing a permutation of the finite labelled family preserves its
centroid, hence acts orthogonally on the centered affine span. Conversely
an invariant positive semidefinite matrix has a square root commuting
with each permutation, because the permutations preserve its eigenspaces.
Its image equals the square root's image, and the displayed vectors span
that image. The equal-Gram proof pays both well-definedness and
surjectivity of the congruence. This covers coincident points and the zero
placement as well as injective configurations.

The double-centering inverse is valid for every symmetric hollow matrix,
with positivity of the resulting matrix precisely the remaining
Euclidean condition. Hollowing is essential and is explicitly imposed in
the proof. Invariant unordered-pair coordinates form a finite-dimensional
linear subspace; their isomorphism with the centered invariant matrices
is onto the relevant subspace, so closedness of the positive cone is not
being inferred from an arbitrary nonclosed linear image. The dimension
is the Gram rank. Blending gives addition but does not assume that the
span fills the whole direct sum. Scaling by c on matrices scales points
by sqrt(c), including c=0.

Extreme rays
------------
For B=C+D with both summands positive, the kernel inclusion follows from
the zero quadratic-form criterion, and symmetry makes the summands
preserve im(B). A symmetric operator commuting with an irreducible real
action is scalar because its real eigenspaces are invariant. This
argument is valid for real, complex and quaternionic representation
types without asserting that their entire commutants are real scalars.
It proves the extreme-ray direction with a positive scalar for B.

In the converse, the invariant orthogonal projection Q need not commute
with B. The actual proof uses AQA and A(I-Q)A, A=B^(1/2), and both are
positive and G-invariant. They are centered since A1=0. Invertibility of
A on im(B) gives ranks dim(U) and dim(W)-dim(U), so both are nonzero
and neither is proportional to B. The decomposition into arbitrary
irreducible invariant subspaces at the end is applied to the placement,
not to an assumed simultaneous spectral decomposition of B and those
projections. Each projected family spans its summand. Its Gram image is
isomorphic to that irreducible summand, which permits application of the
first direction. Thus every cone element is a finite sum of extreme
rays, including the empty sum at zero.

Fixed spaces, faces and full symmetry
------------------------------------
For X=G/H, representative independence is exactly H-fixation of the
initial vector. Equality of two orbit points is governed by its full
stabilizer, yielding Stab(v)=H as the injectivity criterion. The average
is the fixed-space orthogonal projection because G is explicitly finite
and the representation orthogonal. A nontrivial irreducible has no fixed
vectors, and a nonzero vector's orbit spans it. Irreducibles with no
H-fixed vector do not contribute; different fixed vectors need not
produce one ray. Nothing here settles the survey's quaternionic question.

The recursive-face separation condition is necessary independently of
metric geometry. Conversely, vertex injection and separation imply
rank-by-rank injection by elementary induction on the actual sets of
incident preceding-rank faces. The proof does not confuse faithful group
action with faithful faces. It uses precisely the survey's recursive
set-of-face-images realization, retaining ranks and incidence; it makes
no additional embedded-manifold or noncrossing claim.

With that condition, an abstract automorphism fixing all vertices fixes
all faces, so Aut(P) acts faithfully on X. A geometric symmetry of the
ranked faithful realization gives an automorphism preserving distances;
an automorphism preserving B extends uniquely to an isometry of the
spanning placement. Both maps respect composition and are inverse. The
full group is therefore the Gram stabilizer inside Aut(P), not merely
the designated equivariance subgroup. Restricting to the affine span
also avoids extra ambient isometries fixing every point.

Supplementary exact controls
-----------------------------
An independent rational matrix script constructs two equivalent sign
summands of C2 and an invariant projection which does not commute with
B. The actual sandwiched decomposition has ranks2,1,1, remains centered
and invariant, and sums to B. This specifically checks the delicate
noncommuting case. Sixty-four hollow symmetric three-point matrices,
including non-Euclidean data, pass the exact double-centering inverse.
An equilateral triangle has six Gram-preserving vertex permutations,
strictly more than its three designated rotations. A faithful permutation
representation with a constant initial vector has a collapsed orbit;
a digon's two edges have identical recursive images despite injective
vertices. These controls illustrate why the written extra hypotheses
matter; they do not replace the general proof. Two independent
optimization-guard controls also reject before creating output.
