Mathematical joins
------------------

1. Neighborliness. For rank at least three, an ordered adjacent pair of
vertices determines a vertex-edge incidence. Extend it to a flag. In the
chiral case a type-two toggle preserves that incidence and changes flag
orbit. Thus both chosen ordered pairs have extending flags in one orbit;
an automorphism between those flags sends both vertices correctly since
an edge has exactly two vertices. Rank one and neighborly rank-two cases
are handled separately. The proof establishes the necessary two-transitive
action, without asserting a converse, a classification, or finite vertex
stabilizer hypotheses not present in the argument.

2. Rotation conventions and walks. I checked the actual change from
consecutive rotations to the common-base generators and back, including
the first two generator images. Simultaneous inversion allows the abstract
involution to be the identity; the geometric chamber exchange is separate.
The right flag-adjacency words commute with the left automorphism action.
Hole and zigzag traversal states retain the side information. In the
chiral case the odd walk changes flag orbit, explaining the factor two
in the commutator formula. Period calculations do not determine a polytope
or give a finiteness classification.

3. Universal regular amalgamation. The witness is a real hypothesis. Its
local regular flag actions prove that the complete facet and vertex-figure
presentations embed in the universal string quotient. The common section's
flag paths give the actual maximal-parabolic intersection, rather than
assuming IP survives quotienting. The retained string end criterion and
marked reconstruction then apply; the adjacency-equivariant map on flags
gives the cover of every witness. This does not establish compatibility of
arbitrary types or finiteness, and it does not replace the separately retained
oriented amalgamation theorem when a local type is chiral.

4. Oriented examples. The two orientations of square tori are exact input.
The already distinctly accepted finite packet proves orders 2000 and 960,
both order-40 local groups, their precise order-four intersection and the
remaining cyclic intersections. Its GAP presentation isomorphisms pay the
upper group orders; literal permutation closure alone would not do so.
The reflection tests pay chirality rather than merely rotational symmetry.
Complete local presentations give the stated oriented universal property.
I acquire that finite computation without re-enumerating it, and preserve
Hubard--Weiss attribution and the unacquired-primary-paper qualification.

5. Realizations. The cone proof uses centered invariant positive semidefinite
Gram matrices, with squared distances recovered by double centering.
Realization dimension is the actual image rank. Sum means blending followed
by restriction to the actual span. The extreme-ray argument uses real
orthogonal irreducibility: invariant self-adjoint maps are scalar on an
irreducible real image; conversely the positive split uses B^(1/2) Q B^(1/2)
and does not silently require Q to commute with B. For G/H placements,
H-fixity is not injectivity: exact point stabilizer H is required. Recursive
face separation and combinatorial faithfulness remain separate. The finite
cone theorem does not cover arbitrary infinite Euclidean actions, discreteness,
or all geometric/chiral classification questions.

6. Acquired answers. The positive 2016 L045 theorem supplies chiral rank-three

rank spectrum and finite exceptions; L045 adds only the declared chiral
rank-three existence. The common two-group quotient requires finite,
nondegenerate string C-markings and rank at least two; it has order 2^(2d-1).
The eight high-type ordered families do not classify arbitrary two-groups,
unmarked groups, all chiral cases or mixed-prime orders. The accepted
