Every input satisfies $(\mathrm{IP})_2$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each proper fixed-type set $J$, the stabilizer $H_J$ with parabolics
$H_J\cap H_i$ is the base residue. Flag transitivity identifies every
residue of that type with it, and $[G:H_J]$ gives the number of its
translates. The common theorem reduces each directional shadow decision
to the complete double-coset and flag-witness search. We run the exact
accepted sparse algorithm, changing only its descriptive retained-census
field. Nonempty shadows use canonical right-coset representatives; the
empty flag is the whole universe and is handled by the proved cardinality
criterion. Neither trivial panels nor digonal rank-two residues are
silently excluded.

There are 716 base residues including panels, of which 543 have rank at
least two. They give 1455 directional decisions. Every negative direction
has an actual normalizing permutation and failed intersection after the
complete witness search. Conjoining target directions and base residues
of rank $k$ gives $(\mathrm{IP})_k$. For $(\mathrm{WIP})_k$ a successful
target may be chosen separately for each residue. Full IP and weak IP
conjoin their respective rank-specific predicates for ranks two through
the input rank. Panels are automatic and rank zero is excluded. Applying
these exact quantifiers to all saved decisions proves the table.
\end{proof}
