The historical inventory is not silently upgraded from abstract access to full-text access. Failed current retrieval attempts included Springer chapter PDFs and author-host downloads; shell network requests failed DNS resolution, and the web tool did not supply the chapter PDFs. Existing author abstracts and the accessible L091 PDF provide the bounded primary-source basis above.

All three searches use exactly the same new search source. They terminate at empty levels for all 226 Borel anchors, and all 127 final representatives pass the independent parabolic verifier. The trusted imported data are the TomLib subgroup catalogues (40 classes for J1, 39 for M11, 147 for M12), not historical geometry lists. The corresponding GAP/TomLib versions and compressed data-file hashes are in each certificate. No Lean formalization of this complete classification engine is claimed.

The M12 table discrepancy and archive omission are left explicit. A separate calculation records both meanings of “remove truncations”: higher geometries within the local-2T class, or within the entire broader class. Neither convention turns the computed rank-three count into 11. Outer-automorphism and historical equivalence conventions remain relevant to any bibliographic diagnosis, so no novelty or literature-error claim is made.
