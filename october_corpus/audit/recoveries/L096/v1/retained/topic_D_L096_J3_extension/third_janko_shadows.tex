\section{The literal population and its qualifications}
The file \texttt{j3.rk3} in the fixed supplementary archive of L096 has
$21$ active rank-three families. Its final assignment interchanges
construction entries $8$ and $9$; all active indices below refer to that
final assignment. The literal permutations generate the same group
$G$ of order $50\,232\,960$, identified as $J_3$ by the retained source
model. Every supplied family passes actual flag-transitivity, firmness,
residual-connectivity, residual-weak-primitivity, local-$2$-transitivity
and rank-two-intersection tests. This is an exhaustive test of the
supplied roster, not an enumeration of subgroups or a new classification.

The general all-residue theorem and the sparse right-coset shadow adapter
are reused from their accepted packets. Primitivity is checked by the
standard block--overgroup criterion written in the HS companion: a proper
subgroup $H<G$ is maximal if and only if
$\langle H,x\rangle=G$ for every outside $(H,H)$-double-coset representative.
Degree one is excluded, negative cases retain actual intermediate
subgroups, and each residue is checked until a primitive type is found
or every type is exhausted. All $147$ base residues, including panels,
are checked on the literal $21$ inputs. No earlier Janko census supplies
a geometry predicate that this packet leaves untested.

L096 Tables~1 and~7 print a population of $17$ for $J_3$ and counts
$(5,8,4)$ for IP, WIP-only and failure of WIP. Our supplied-roster quotient
has $19$ classes and counts $(6,8,5)$. The discrepancy is retained
explicitly. Global completeness of the earlier classification cited as
L096 reference~[19], D.~Leemans, \emph{Supplement to ``The residually weakly
primitive geometries of $J_3$''} (2003), is not reproved here, nor are its
historical suffixes identified with active archive indices. Thus the
number $19$ below is exactly a supplied-roster conclusion.

\section{A cheaper exact automorphism transporter}
The full automorphism group computed from the literal group has order
$100\,465\,920$. Independently, the center of $G$ is checked to be trivial,
so $\operatorname{Inn}(G)$ has index two in $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$.
The program selects and verifies an automorphism $\alpha$ that is not
inner. Consequently $1,\alpha$ form a complete set of representatives
modulo inner automorphisms. This reduction concerns the automorphism
group; it does not require a splitting or an involutory choice of $\alpha$.

For two ordered subgroup families, loop over all target type permutations
and over $\beta\in\{1,\alpha\}$. Apply $\beta$ to the whole input family.
The remaining problem is simultaneous conjugacy by one element of $G$.
Place target subgroups in decreasing order of size, with fixed tie order.
Initially the permitted conjugating group is $C=G$. At each step find
$x\in C$ conjugating the current entry to its target, conjugate the
\emph{whole} current family by $x$, and replace $C$ by the normalizer of
the target inside $C$. After the last placement no normalizer is needed.
The product of the actual $x$'s is recorded for positive matches.

This test is exact. Inner automorphisms are a normal subgroup of index
two, so every automorphism has one of the two forms just tested. Once
some entries have been placed, the possible remaining conjugators form
a coset of their common normalizer. If one transporter $x$ to the next
target is chosen, the other transporters differ from it by an element of
that target normalizer. Induction therefore shows that each update
preserves all possible solutions. Success gives an actual automorphism
and one conjugator carrying the entire family to the target. Failure
exhausts the two outer possibilities and every type permutation.
The complete intersection-order profile is used only as a necessary
rejection filter, as proved in the HS companion.

This exact permutation-group method completes all $21$ rows, whereas a
bounded direct action of the full automorphism group on subgroups stalled
after seven rows. Those seven completed decisions are preserved and
agree. A separate validation compares the new method with the direct
full-automorphism method on $144$ pairs of subgroup triples in $A_5$,
including outer images and permuted targets; all $24$ positive and $120$
negative decisions agree. These finite validations support the
implementation; the preceding argument proves the general reduction.

\section{Explicit class separations and intersection results}
For $J\subseteq\{1,2,3\}$ let
$G_J=\bigcap_{j\in J}G_j$, with $G_\varnothing=G$.
The multiset of all eight orders $|G_J|$ is invariant under group
automorphisms and type permutations. On the supplied roster its buckets
are the singleton entries and
\[
 \{7,8\},\quad \{9,10,11\},\quad
 \{13,14\},\quad \{16,17\}.
\]
There are $16$ buckets. Actual complete-family transporters identify
$13$ with $14$, and $16$ with $17$; their type permutations,
automorphism images of every ambient generator, and the conjugating
permutations are retained as exact data. The other repeated buckets
split, with the following independent witnesses.

Entries $7$ and $8$ each have a unique parabolic of order $96$; these
parabolics contain respectively $27$ and $15$ involutions. Entry $9$ has
two parabolics of order $96$, each with $27$ involutions, whereas entries
$10$ and $11$ have two such parabolics each with $15$. These actual counts
are obtained from complete conjugacy-class rosters in the small
parabolic groups; group isomorphisms preserve them. Finally entry $10$
satisfies full IP, while entry $11$ fails WIP. The complete shadow
certificates, with the failed directional intersections, therefore
separate this last pair as well. These tests independently support the
negative transporter decisions and give $16+1+2=19$ distinct supplied
classes without relying on historical names.

\begin{proposition}
Modulo entire-family automorphisms of the retained $J_3$ action and type
permutations, the supplied roster has $19$ eligible classes. Every class
satisfies $\mathrm{IP}_2$. Full IP, equivalently $\mathrm{IP}_3$ here,
holds in six classes; eight more satisfy WIP; five fail WIP.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The order profiles separate different buckets. The explicit transporters
identify exactly the two stated pairs, and the preceding invariants
separate the other entries, so the quotient has $19$ classes. All supplied
entries were checked for the source geometry hypotheses, so these
predicates transfer across the actual family isomorphisms.

For each representative, the accepted residue theorem enumerates all
seven proper fixed-type subsets. Flag transitivity transports each base
residue to all flags of those types. Panels are checked for geometry
properties and only ranks two and three enter IP and WIP. This gives
$133$ base residues including panels, $76$ of rank at least two, and
$171$ directional shadow decisions. Their actual all/exists quantifiers
are reconstructed from the recorded values. The positive active
representatives are
\[
 2,3,4,7,8,10;
\]
the weak-only representatives are
\[
 1,5,6,12,16,18,19,21;
\]
and the nonweak representatives are
\[
 9,11,13,15,20.
\]
All rank-two residues satisfy IP, so the rank-three decision and full
residual decision agree. This proves the asserted supplied-roster counts.
\end{proof}

\section{Scope and reproducibility}
The roster, complete programs, actual positive automorphisms, small
subgroup conjugacy-class data, negative shadow witnesses and completed
execution records are bound separately. GAP's exact finite group
algorithms remain trusted. The default checker reparses the source,
checks every residual quantifier and seventeen semantic controls, and
preserves the $19$ versus $17$ discrepancy. Optional program replay
recomputes the partition, exact witnesses, local invariants and all
shadow decisions. The four original bounded eligibility batches are
retained as complete executions and can be rerun separately; no large
prior subgroup census is repeated.

No Lean formalization, complete historical-label correspondence,
whole-original recovery or compression factor is claimed. The original
Section~6 Magma listing and our complete programs remain visible for
any later symmetric measurement boundary. The shared residue,
maximality and transporter proofs are explicit dependencies intended
for a single combined exposition, not unpaid imports from another topic.
