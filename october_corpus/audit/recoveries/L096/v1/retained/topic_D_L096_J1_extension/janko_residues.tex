\begin{proof}
The preceding checks establish every hypothesis of the common residue
isomorphism and shadow recognition theorem. For every proper type subset
$J$, form $K=H_J$ and the parabolics $K\cap H_i$. For each remaining target
type, enumerate all normalized object--flag double cosets and all possible
witness types and stabilizer cosets. A positive answer covers every
required orbit. A negative answer records its actual normalizing
permutation and failed intersection after exhausting the entire witness
search for that pair. The rank-two shortcut is the independently proved
neighborhood-intersection criterion, including complete bipartite residues
and excluding comparison of an object with itself.

The exact run covers 37 base residues including panels. Its 26 residues
of rank at least two produce 65 directional type decisions. At rank $k$,
conjunction over every type in every base residue gives $(\mathrm{IP})_k$.
For $(\mathrm{WIP})_k$ a successful type may be chosen separately in each
residue. The complete saved decisions give the displayed table. The full
properties are the conjunction over $2\leq k\leq\operatorname{rank}$;
rank one is automatic and rank zero is excluded.
\end{proof}
The actual rank-two residue counts represented by our base flags are

$6118,4921$. Each is a sum of the actual indices $[G:H_J]$, without assuming
