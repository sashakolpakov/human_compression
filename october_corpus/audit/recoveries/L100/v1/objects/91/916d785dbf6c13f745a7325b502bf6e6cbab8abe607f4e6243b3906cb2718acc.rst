L100 literal automorphism and correlation names
=============================================

Finite boundary
---------------
The inputs are the accepted 29 literal coset models and their complete palette
automorphism groups, the fixed 20,160-element PSL(3,4), and the acquired diagonal,
field and projective-duality maps. No ambient subgroup, geometry or graph census
is repeated. The first bounded call constructs the 12 coordinate automorphisms
and tests their exact multiplication and their distinctness modulo inner maps.
Subsequent calls process explicitly listed models and retain partial results.

For a palette generator q, restrict q^-1 g q to one faithful object type to
identify its candidate element of the saved G. Then verify that identification
on EVERY object and palette vertex, for both actual generators of G. Thus G's
normality is proved by literal maps, not inferred from equal group orders.
Express both images uniquely as h^-1 rho(g) h, where rho is one of the 12
coordinate maps and h is one of the 20,160 saved elements. Identify the image
in the small outer group explicitly, retaining field and graph distinctions.

Kernel and splitting
-------------------
The complete acquired group order gives the conjugation kernel order only
after the image is identified. Any order-two kernel is central, but this alone
does not prove a direct product. Normalize short outer lifts by their exact
inner factors; retain actual kernel involutions and verify their commutation.
For the normalized lifts calculate the entire small multiplication cocycle.
Solve and then literally check a binary section correction. Only an actual
homomorphic section with the stated action proves the direct-product name.
A failed section is preserved and is not silently replaced by order matching.

---------------
The exact source-to-catalogue map is acquired from source_join_component_v2.
