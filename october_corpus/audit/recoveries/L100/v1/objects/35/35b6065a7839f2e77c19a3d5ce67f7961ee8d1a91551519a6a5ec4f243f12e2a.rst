Scope of the PSL(3,4) rank-two component
=======================================

This component addresses only the seven total rank-two classes and their
parabolic/intersection orders, type sizes, local degrees and observed local
double transitivity in the fixed22-page Halle edition of L100. It does not
recover the complete original. The source's29 geometries, higher-rank
exhaustion, full diagram invariants, precise G-conjugacy fusion multiplicities,
automorphism/correlation groups, construction proofs, kernels, truncation and
doubling claims remain separate obligations. No source-wide, conjecture,
measurement, Lean or historical-priority credit is claimed.

The source assumes RWPRI and IP2; local2T is an output. The new search keeps
all primitive local actions until its full incidence test. It does not apply
the stronger filter used for L091. Borrowed complete maximal-subgroup and
actual-overgroup interfaces remain explicit; no subgroup census is reproved.

The first alternate coset-action checker mistakenly compared ordered counts
from two unlabelled GAP right transversals. Its failed output is preserved.
The successor compares counts with multiplicity after sorting, and separately
checks all unordered pairs of local neighbours. It passes60 records and3098
neighbour pairs, and reconstructs all24 explicit automorphism witnesses.
No primary enumeration, result or mathematical filtering rule changed.
