\begin{abstract}
We assemble a complete finite proof of the classification in the fixed
22-page Halle preprint of Gottschalk and Leemans. All subgroup anchors,
primitive residue extensions and incidence isomorphism classes are covered.
The resulting catalogue has 7, 7, 11 and 4 geometries in ranks 2, 3, 4 and 5,
and none of higher rank. Literal projective constructions, doubling and
truncation maps, residue data, stabilizer structures and complete
automorphism and correlation groups are joined to the 29 printed rows.
Several ancillary numerical, incidence and naming assertions require
the explicit corrections below. The main classification is unchanged.
Previously established classification inputs, algorithms and historical
construction names are stated separately from the finite certificates.
\end{abstract}

\section{Statement, conventions and fixed source}
Let $G=\PSL_3(4)$ in its retained faithful 21-point realization, of order
20160. We consider finite coset geometries of rank at least two on which
$G$ acts flag-transitively. A geometry is \emph{firm} if every panel has
at least two objects, \emph{residually connected} if every residue of
rank at least two is connected, and \emph{RWPRI} if the stabilizer of
every flag acts primitively on at least one type of its positive-rank
residue. The condition $\ip$ says that every rank-two residue is a
partial linear space or a generalized digon. No local two-transitivity
condition is imposed in this classification.

We use right cosets with incidence given by nonempty intersection.
Inversion converts the source's left-coset convention to this one.
The standard firmness convention concerns panels, or equivalently
nonmaximal flags in a geometry; the literal phrase ``any flag'' in the
source definition must not be applied to a chamber itself. An isomorphism
may permute entire types. The group $\Aut(\Gamma)$ fixes every type,
whereas $\Cor(\Gamma)$ may permute them. An arbitrary automorphism of
the uncolored incidence graph is not silently called a correlation.

\begin{theorem}\label{main}
Relative to the explicitly retained complete subgroup and exact
computational inputs in Section~\ref{inputs}, the firm, residually
connected, $G$-flag-transitive RWPRI $\ip$ geometries are precisely the
29 literal models in Table~\ref{catalogue}, up to incidence isomorphism
with permutation of types. Their rank distribution is
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrr}
\text{rank}&2&3&4&5&\geq6\\\hline
\text{number}&7&7&11&4&0.
\end{array}
\]
All numerical diagram data, fusion multiplicities, structural groups and
source construction clauses have the explicit dispositions below.
\end{theorem}

The source is the Halle report 99--30, \emph{Geometries for the group
$\PSL(3,4)$}, by Harald Gottschalk and Dimitri Leemans \cite{GL}.
Its exact 22-page file, page transcriptions and native diagrams are

The original 55-clause reading register is preserved without alteration.

\section{Complete primitive residue extension}\label{exhaust}
The acquired broad panel theorem \cite{Panel} gives a finite reduction
that does not assume two-transitivity. For a chamber stabilizer $B$
and actual panel stabilizers $P_i>B$, put
\[
 K_\varnothing=B,\qquad K_S=\langle B,P_i:i\in S\rangle.
\]
Recognition checks all intersections
$K_S\cap K_T=K_{S\cap T}$, a maximal deletion
$K_{S\setminus\{i\}}<K_S$ for each nonempty $S$, typed coset Helly
for each subfamily of rank at least three, and the exact rank-two
partial-linearity-or-digon test. The intersection and Helly conditions
identify the residues with the corresponding coset geometries inside
$K_S$. Maximality gives primitivity by the subgroup--block correspondence,
even when the local action has a kernel. Connectedness gives generation
of a residue stabilizer by its panel stabilizers. These observations
establish both necessity and sufficiency, as proved in \cite{Panel}.

For completeness, take a target family of rank $r$. RWPRI supplies a
maximal deletion $H$ in its join $K$. By induction its rank-$(r-1)$
residue family has already occurred. The missing panel lies in $K$
but outside $H$; adjoining it therefore generates $K$. All maximal
overgroups and all eligible panels are tried. Stabilizer-orbit pruning
uses actual automorphisms of the old tuple, and the final successive
transporter/type-permutation test removes only equivalent families.
No numerical signature is used as a sufficient isomorphism test.

The rank-two test retains every double coset. If $B=P\cap Q$ and
$K=\langle P,Q\rangle$, the graph is a digon exactly when
$|P||Q|=|K||B|$. Otherwise for each nontrivial $BxB$ in $B\backslash Q/B$
the two neighbourhood sets
\[
 \{Qh:h\in P\},\qquad \{Qhx:h\in P\}
\]
must intersect in exactly one object. They already share $Q$; a second
common object is precisely a quadrangle. This is the general test,
not a shortcut valid only for two-transitive local actions.

The retained subgroup list has 95 $G$-conjugacy classes. Its permutation
model is transported to the fixed realization by a checked bijective
homomorphism with literal generator images. Complete local maximal
inclusion data are transported by the same map. Every one of the 95
Borel anchors has now returned its final empty extension level.
The final union has 145 full-group families up to inner conjugacy and
type permutation: 21, 25, 63 and 36 in ranks 2--5. Earlier deadlines
remain failures, not partial exhaustion certificates. The last anchor
reused its completely saved levels and recomputed its unsaved next
level from the beginning; its lost progress count was never treated
as saved family data.

No higher family is omitted: every proper panel addition enlarges the
join, so rank is bounded by the number of prime factors of $[G:B]$,
with multiplicity. The acquired terminal empty-level checks, rather
than agreement with the printed rank counts, establish exhaustion.

\section{From specified actions to incidence classes}\label{fusion}
Inner equivalence does not identify all geometries. The complete
automorphism group of $G$, of order 241920, is acquired with its actual
generator-image maps. Its action and type permutations fuse the 145
families. The last 42 families were compared incrementally with the
previous 103; previously completed comparisons were retained. The
saved exact generator maps justify reuse of the already computed
automorphism group and its cardinality. Computational faithfulness of
the acquired nice representation is a stated input, not inferred from
a cached size comparison.

Even full $\Aut(G)$ fusion alone would not classify bare incidence
geometries: an incidence isomorphism need not normalize the chosen
copy of $G$. The final comparison therefore uses the acquired palette
construction \cite{Palette}. Add one marker for every type, join it
to exactly that type's objects, and distinguish object vertices from
marker vertices by two colors. A color-preserving graph isomorphism
restricts to precisely a type-permuting incidence isomorphism.
Conversely any such incidence isomorphism extends uniquely to the
markers. Fixing the markers individually gives the type-preserving
automorphism group. Thus the exact colored-graph computation supplies
both bare equivalence and the full groups, without an uncolored shortcut.

All 29 resulting representatives have literal coset memberships,
incidence matrices and generator actions. Each of the 145 families is
joined to its representative; complete colored-graph comparisons
separate the representatives. The rank distribution in Theorem~\ref{main}
follows. The saved raw GAP output required a documented inverse of its
line-continuation serialization; the derived files are bound to both
the originals and 61 native round trips. A clean raw return alone is
not used as evidence that malformed JSON was a scientific artifact.

coordinate-to-permutation map is checked on the actual generators.
All ten pairwise incidence rules are tested, every partial flag
extends to a chamber, and the chamber orbit has length 20160.

\section{Ancillary corrections and qualifications}\label{corrections}
These corrections leave the principal list of incidence geometries
unchanged. Each is attached to a fixed source clause and exact
literal countercheck, not to an uncollated journal edition.
\begin{enumerate}
\item In the diagrams 5.1 and 5.2 the 210-object node has panel
order 1, not the printed 2. The complete construction flag counts
and source-node transports agree on this correction.
\item The printed 3.6 truncation link from 5.3 is impossible:
5.3 has no type of 210 objects. The corrected 5.2 link has an
explicit incidence isomorphism.
\item In the printed recipe for 3.3, the point-pair/line-pair
relation has degree 18 and admits a flag with no extension to the
Baer type. Requiring a common Baer subplane in the selected orbit
gives degree 6 and the certified construction. This concerns the
ancillary recipe, not nonexistence of Geometry 3.3.
\item Under the stated field convention for $P\Sigma L$, the
typed group of 3.5 is $U$, and the correlation group of 4.11 is
$T$, rather than the printed $\Sigma$. These are not relabelings
based on orders. The three outer involution cosets contain 120,
280 and 336 involutions, respectively; adding the 315 inner
involutions separates the three abstract extensions. Literal
outer images and full group equalities are also certified.
\item In diagram 2.3 the actual kernel $3^2:C_4$ in $3^2:Q_8$
has index two and no involution outside it. Thus it has no
complement. Under the usual split-colon convention the outer
colon in $(3^2:4):2$ must be replaced by an extension dot.
\end{enumerate}

Two further qualifications concern how a recipe is specified.
On p.~4 the two displayed corresponding cross-intersections for
butterflies alone do not force the advertised unique completion;
explicit alternative completions are retained. The certified
recipe imposes all four cross-intersections. This is a statement
about the displayed conditions, not an adjudication of every
external butterfly convention. On pp.~18--19 the oval/dual-oval
relative orbit choice is supplied explicitly, as noted above.

The generic residue-kernel dot defined on p.~3 does not assert
nonsplitting. Accordingly central $2.2^3$ extensions are proved
nonsplit separately, and unparenthesized $2^2\times2^2:L$ displays
are interpreted as the actual action on the entire radical; they
do not imply an unproved central factor or invariant complement.
The earlier working allegation of a missing horizontal bond in
3.5 was our own transcription error and is withdrawn. The native
source has that bond; it is not among the source corrections.

\section{Retained inputs, attribution and verification}\label{inputs}
The proof has the following explicit antecedent boundary.
\begin{itemize}
\item The complete TomLib subgroup classification for $L_3(4)$,
its literal transported realization, and complete local maximal
inclusion data are retained. GAP's exact subgroup, homomorphism,
transporter and automorphism algorithms, and GRAPE/nauty's complete
colored-graph computations are computational premises. Independent
small-model checks and positive witness maps do not reprove these
classification tables or software algorithms.
\item The source's quoted Tits connectivity criterion and
Buekenhout--Hermand coset-Helly criterion remain attributed with
their hypotheses. The actual general recognition and augmentation
arguments used here are the complete acquired proofs in
\cite{Panel}. We do not turn the source's short strongly-Boolean
sentence into a stronger theorem lacking its setting.
\item Classical terminology and uniqueness of $\PG(2,4)$ are
retained. Every concrete point, line, hyperoval, Baer set and
butterfly used by the current constructions is separately bound
to its literal finite certificate. The general rank-at-least-three
doubling theorem is retained exactly as quoted, with its $I_{1,2}$
condition; all four finite applications have direct maps.
\item The source's retrospective thesis reduction and earlier
Cayley/Magma computations are historical attribution. The present
proof uses the complete acquired panel computation instead of
claiming to rerun those programs. Earlier construction references,
including the Gewirtz and unital graph names attached to 2.3 and
2.7, are retained as attributed antecedents. All 29 current coset
models and all newly prescribed source recipes are supplied;
we do not claim newly to inspect every cited primary article or
recertify each external naming identification.
\end{itemize}
