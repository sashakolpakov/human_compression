Mathematical findings
---------------------

The dihedral proof is complete for every m>=3. All involutions are rotations
of order two or reflections. Independence follows by intersecting a singleton
with the subgroup of all other marks. With four marks, the central involution
and a nonadjacent reflection pair violate independence; without it, the first
reflection would have two distinct commuting partners. With three reflections,
commuting endpoints differ by the central involution. The coprimality condition
on their middle exponent forces one adjacent pair already to generate the
whole group, in both parities of m/2. With the central involution present, the
reflection subgroup excludes it exactly when its rotation order h is odd;
generation then forces m=2h. The central mark is an endpoint. Conversely the
explicit C2 times Dih(2h) decomposition proves all subset intersections. This
establishes maximum rank three precisely for m congruent to2 modulo4, and two
otherwise. The connected rank-two statement is preserved.

The H3 identification retains the classical Coxeter order120 explicitly.
The displayed involutions generate A5 because their pair subgroups have orders
6,10,4 with least common multiple60. The central twists satisfy the H3 Coxeter
relations and their triple product has fifth power z, giving a surjection onto
A5 times C2. Thus the retained order identifies that group. The rank-four
witness has exact subset intersections: the rank-two intersections are the
common involutions by their gcd orders, singleton exclusions follow from the
same intersections, and the extra central factor preserves all intersections.
There is no implicit inference from equal group orders alone.

For the census, every full string C-marking has independent prefixes satisfying
IP. Therefore the author's outside-subgroup, string and prefix-IP rejections
are necessary. Subgroup closure by generator multiplication is exhaustive for
a finite group. Rank<=6 follows from the successive subgroup indices, each at
least two. Rank-two prefixes automatically have IP because two distinct
involutions have disjoint nonidentity singleton subgroups. Stopping at full
generation loses no independent extension. Multiplication, closure bitsets,
subset intersections, canonical representatives and self-duality were read in
full. There is no overlooked random, library-classification or sampling step.

The automorphism equivalence is paid. The derived subgroup and center of
A5 times C2 are characteristic. A5 is perfect and centerless. Its five Sylow
2 subgroups are the five point-fixing Klein groups. An automorphism acting
trivially on those subgroups makes g and its image induce the same faithful
permutation there, so is the identity. Conjugation by S5 supplies all120
automorphisms. Minimizing over those and reversal therefore gives the precise
source equivalence; testing reversal before adding duality gives self-duality.
