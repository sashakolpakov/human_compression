Two-swap cases in the L044 unique-crossing argument
=================================================

Read two_swap_saturation.pdf (two pages) and regular_anchor_orbit.pdf
(four pages), then scope.json. The first preserves the literal pure/mixed
V4 distinction and excludes fully saturated paired rows. The second
excludes the regular-four-orbit case when that orbit contains the crossing
endpoint, including every small even block size. Neither is whole L044.

All inherited common proofs and exact word obstructions are paid and bound
in dependencies.json. There is no new required computation or Lean theorem.
The beta=0,gamma=1 case and the regular orbit away from its crossing
endpoint remain explicitly outside this freeze, as do other source branches.

Portable render (run twice for each file)::

  xelatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error two_swap_saturation.tex
  xelatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error regular_anchor_orbit.tex

All predecessors remain immutable. Distinct acceptance is pending.
