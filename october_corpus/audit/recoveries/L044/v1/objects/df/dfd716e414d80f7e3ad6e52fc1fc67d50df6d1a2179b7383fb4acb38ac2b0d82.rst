For each binary partition, the action of its maximal omission is a constant
translation of its binary coordinate. Transitivity of the common omission
makes the coordinate image one coset of the joint translation image and
makes all fibres equal. If that image were proper, an annihilating nonzero
linear form would make one coordinate depend affinely on the others.
The omitted generator for that coordinate preserves every other partition;
substitution into the affine relation proves that it also preserves its
own partition. Together with all the other distinguished generators it
would make the parent imprimitive. Thus all 2^t coordinate vectors occur.
When the fibres are singletons, the actual faithful action identifies the
group with the elementary abelian translation group, not merely a quotient.

Orbit growth requires no permutation action on a previous orbit set.
If no distinguished generator ever sends a point between two current
orbits, every current orbit is invariant under the transitive parent.
Otherwise adjoining such a generator merges at least two orbits. This
proves the stated bound of a-1 added distinguished generators.

In the main theorem, the first two coordinates give four blocks of size l.
Two distinguished involutions with independent images generate a group
whose orbits each meet all four blocks equally. If they commute, their
group is the actual Klein four-group and every orbit has four points.
Adding at most l-1 labels inside the original double omission gives a
transitive subgroup with at most l+1 labels. Its complement has at least
l-1 labels, each with transitive maximal omission, so the general lemma
gives 2^(l-1) dividing 4l. This excludes every l>=5: l=5 by divisibility,
and l>=6 by the displayed increasing inequality.

If the two generators do not commute, they cannot act by commuting
four-point translations on every orbit. At least one orbit has eight or
more points, and the orbit count is at most l-1. The transitive subgroup
therefore needs at most l labels. For l>=5, the resulting 2^l>4l is
impossible. At l=4 the complement must contain exactly four labels;
singleton refinement then makes the actual transitive subgroup abelian,
contradicting those two noncommuting generators. This handles the boundary
without assuming any faithfulness of an intermediate quotient.

The remaining commuting case has n=16 and a transitive subgroup on at most
five labels. The coordinate lemma bounds the complement by four labels,
so r<=9. Equality would force five independent involutions in the actual
elementary abelian group of order16, impossible. Thus r=8. Enlarging the
transitive distinguished subset to five labels is legitimate. The three
omitted labels give eight pairs and the actual translation image E8 on
them. Every permutation above that image is a translation followed by a
pair-flip vector. The translation permutation on sixteen points is even;
the flip vector must have even weight. This proves exactly the ambient
group E^8 semidirect E8 of order 2^7*8=1024. Full intersection properties
are inherited from the original marked subsets.
