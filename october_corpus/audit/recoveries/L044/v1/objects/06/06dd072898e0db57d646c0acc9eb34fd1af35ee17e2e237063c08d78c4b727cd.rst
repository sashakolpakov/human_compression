L044: neighbours of a square
===========================

A selection is chosen separately for each admissible square by maximizing its incident selected edges. This proves the full-neighbour property required for a complete degree argument. Two commuting branches are impossible; the remaining degree-four configuration is confined to eight vertices, contradicting connectedness when n>8. The ladder and invariant-pair conclusion remain to be proved. No whole-source or conjecture credit is added. Mathematical contributions during the diagnostic discussion are recorded in dependencies.json.
