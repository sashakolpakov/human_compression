The first theorem supplies literal generators, not just an abstract
group isomorphism. For odd b, the equivariant map
f(v)=v+(sum v_j)*(1,...,1) has kernel the constant vector and image
the even-weight subspace E. Conjugates of f(e1) span E, and the other
generators give the diagonal S_b. These permutations are all even,
transitive on the 2b points, and preserve the two-point blocks.
The first product has order four and the remaining consecutive
products have order three, with the stated distant commutations.

The parabolic formula G_I=f(V_t(I)) semidirect P_I proves full IP.
When 0 is absent, the vector subgroup is zero. Otherwise the orbit
of pair label 1 under P_I is exactly the initial t(I) labels, so
conjugation gives precisely the asserted vector image. The images
f(V_t) are nested even though f ceases to be injective at the full
space. Their intersection is therefore the smaller image. The Young
subgroups P_I intersect by refinement of interval partitions, which
is P_(I intersect J). Uniqueness of the vector/permutation coordinates
proves all intersections, including those involving the full set.

Every maximal omission is intransitive. Omitting the first generator
leaves the two natural copies; omitting another preserves two sets
of block labels. The first endpoint omission has no fixed point.
The last contains f(e1) and f(e2), whose supports together meet all
blocks, so it too has no fixed point. This verifies the claimed
absence of an endpoint anchor, also at b=3.

For odd b>=7, this family contradicts the literal universal clause

family itself is already printed in Corollary4.19(b), as the packet

contradict the main theorem for the full alternating group. All its
colours have multiple eligible edges, so it is also outside the
unique-crossing hypothesis of the accepted Proposition5.7 work.

The extension lemma is a direct commutation argument on the actual
support X. Since a generator supported on X fixes c^t outside X,
commutation and injectivity of t force it to fix c. Transitivity on
nontrivial X then makes the remaining endpoint move c. No claim
about quotient actions or arbitrary sesqui-extensions is implicit.

For the anchored rank theorem, choosing one edge for every colour
outside M and contracting actual M-orbits gives a forest: a cycle
would provide a path avoiding one selected colour, contradicting
the choice of its endpoints in different omission orbits. Therefore
d-|M|<=c-1. Each M-orbit meets all blocks, so c<=a. The minimal
block-image family is independent and Whiston gives |M|<=b-1.
For a,b>=3 with ab>=12, (a-2)(b-2)>=2 proves the desired bound.
