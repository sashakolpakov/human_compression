Mathematical checks
-------------------

The forest uses actual omission-subgroup orbits: a cycle after contraction
would give a path using only the omitted subgroup's generators, contrary to
the chosen crossing edge. No quotient intersection property or intransitive
quotient omission is inferred. The three nonnegative deficits have the exact
stated identity. Saturation identifies the full symmetric block image and a
faithful diagonal lift; global parity then makes both block sizes even.
This removes the D=1 cases and leaves exactly the listed D=0 or D=2 cases.

Primitive active intervals follow because two commuting nontrivial normal
factors of a primitive image are transitive, so the other factor's elements
are fixed-point-free. Only the two neighbors of the crossing color can move
its block, forcing the excluded degree-two exception. The anchored maximal
symmetric string uses the retained classification without projected IP. I
checked that the S5/S6 program enumerates all independent strings before
checking IP, rather than filtering candidates by IP. Thus it supplies the
actual interface claimed here. The degree3/4 anchored calculations and the
S4 tail alternatives are proved directly in the manuscript.

The same-side bound is now the two-anchor argument: all but the first two
image generators fix each anchor and its distinct image under the first
one. Independence remains in the simultaneous faithful image. The bound
on S_(a-2) times S_(b-2) therefore consumes two deficits. This replaces the
earlier unfrozen shortcut about outside generators fixing both endpoints.
It is not an assumption about those outside generators.

On opposite sides, the maximal interval supplies an endpoint omission
whose centralizer fixing the anchor is trivial. On a two-point block,
fixing one point fixes the block pointwise. Commutation transports that
fixation to the other blocks, leaving at most a single transposition.
Global evenness excludes it. This argument tolerates a kernel in the lift.
The binary anchored lemma treats degree4 explicitly. For degree5, the four
conjugate lifted star transpositions generate the sign graph; the additional
unsigned odd transposition generates the whole product and makes the last
marking redundant. Thus that twisted tail is genuinely excluded by
independence, not a computational extrapolation.

For D=2 saturation, at least two remaining generators centralize the full
block image and fix the two anchor copies. The remaining centralizer is S3
on three copies of a three-point action. Its even subgroup is A3 and contains
no involution. This covers all stated m>=3 endpoints.

For the single simultaneous swap with block sizes>=5, IP makes the diagonal
projection of C faithful. The normalized character vanishes on the full
anchor stabilizer and hence on the resulting symmetric product. The prefix
and suffix have trivial intersection by IP and are the two nontrivial direct
factors. The missing neighbor centralizes one entire factor and an endpoint
omission of the other. At an ordinary endpoint it is identity or one odd
transposition. The outer S6 endpoint is transitive S5; its centralizer cannot
contain a semiregular involution or element of order3, since their centralizers
in S6 have orders48 and18, both smaller than120. For the exceptional S4 tail,
a transitive D8 omission instead forces the anchor pair to remain invariant,
contradicting the original omission orbit's transitivity. These are the
required small cases; neither projected IP nor a smaller-degree alternating
rank theorem is used circularly.
