L044: the orbit budget of a small singleton prefix
==================================================

The full ambient interval count leaves at most three nontrivial orbits after deleting the first two marks of a small prefix. A single such orbit, and every internally swapped two-point orbit, are excluded by explicit invariant-pair arguments. The three remaining numerical rows and their odd-sign alternatives are retained as unresolved. This is a uniform bounded reduction, with no finite computation or whole-original credit.
