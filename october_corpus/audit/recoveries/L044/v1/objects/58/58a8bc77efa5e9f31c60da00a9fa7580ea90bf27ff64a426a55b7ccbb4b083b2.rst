=================================================

This is a private route audit, not a mathematical acceptance or a whole-original

review statuses. No public files, source counts, questions or measurements change.

The exact principal contract
----------------------------
The fixed 46-page arXiv:1605.09173v1 (30 May 2016), by Cameron, Fernandes,
Leemans and Mixer, states Theorem 1.1 on page 2: the maximum rank of A_n is zero
for n=3,4,6,7,8; three for n=5; four for n=9; five for n=10; six for n=11;
and floor((n-1)/2) for every n>=12. The original working contract explicitly
asks for this whole theorem, with lower existence retained separately if acquired.
The source attributes the small maxima to reference [13] and the large-degree
lower existence to [14]. Its new mathematical burden is the upper bound n>=12.
The conclusion on page 45 explicitly splits this burden into primitive omissions,
transitive imprimitive omissions, and all-intransitive omissions with or without
a paired fracture selection. A complete recovery must close all four branches.

The new critical-head completion packet is a branch theorem. It may legitimately
use an explicit strong induction at strictly smaller degrees >=12. It cannot by
itself establish that induction or remove the other three global branches.
