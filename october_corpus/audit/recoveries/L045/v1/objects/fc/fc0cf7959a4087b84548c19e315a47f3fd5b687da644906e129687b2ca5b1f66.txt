6 Attribution and verification scope
The existence result belongs to [1]. The choice of tori and the strategy of excluding maximal subgroups
and involution centralizers are also borrowed from that proof. The subgroup classification, conjugacy class
orders, outer-automorphism classification and uniform upper bound remain explicit external premises,
cited where used. They are not new theorems of this project.
The additions here are the outer-coset criterion, the root-lattice centralizer argument including the
finite checks, the explicit normalizer charge, and the complete constant inequalities. The latter distinguish
5
i2 fromν 2 before passing through an extension. No asymptotic estimate supplies an unverified finite
constant. The arithmetic certificates prove precisely the polynomial and lattice assertions they state;
they do not prove the retained classification theorems. The full L045 admission additionally requires
its classical, alternating and sporadic components. No new conjecture resolution, Lean verification or
compression measurement is claimed by this component.
