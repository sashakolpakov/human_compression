2 Named models and exact verification
Everypair.gis a literal permutation record containing the two witnesses, the full ambient generating
list, the parameters, the degree and the expected orders. A fresh GAP process resets the same recorded
constructor seed and callsSimpleGroupwith those parameters. It requires exact equality of the saved and
newly constructed generating lists. There is no recognition of a group from its order.
The named-model identifications are retained GAP 4.15.1 construction and catalogue premises [3]. The
L,UandSbranches constructPSL,PSUandPSp. Their projective matrix constructions and subsequent
faithful smaller actions are the documented named constructors. The odd and signed even orthogonal
branches take the derived subgroup of the appropriate special orthogonal action on lines, followed when
applicable by the constructor’s smaller action. Their identification and simplicity are retained; neither the
storedSizenor a simplicity flag proves the identification here. The three exceptional calls are explicitly
G2(3) PrimitiveGroup(351,7)
G2(4) PrimitiveGroup(416,7)
2F4(2)′ PrimitiveGroup(1600,20).
The exact primitive catalogue records name these groups. The constructor, classical projective interface,
primitive loader and actual catalogue files are pinned in the dependency ledger. Correctness of GAP’s
exact permutation algorithms and the named library inputs is explicit computational infrastructure, not
a new classification proof.
For every row the checker reconstructsHfrom the saved ambient generators without supplying its
order. It verifiesH=G, computes|H|, checksx, t∈Hand|t|= 2, and formsK=⟨x, t⟩without
supplying its order. It verifies|K|=|H|andK=H. Membership and equal finite order already imply
generation; the latter equality is a redundant explicit check. The four table entries are then computed by
exact permutation operations. The new groups are checked to have no preassigned size before the relevant
calculations.
A separate standard-library Python parser reads the original cycle notation directly, checks disjoint
cycles, and composes the corresponding right-action permutations. Cycle lengths and integer least common
multiples reproduce all four orders and|t|= 2, independently of GAP’s word calculations. It also parses
every ambient generator, but it does not purport to duplicate GAP’s large subgroup computations.
The source roster is constructed independently as the explicit sets of parameters above. Missing, du-
plicate, nonsimple, misrouted and displaced entries are rejected. Seven GAP controls damage parameters,
ambient generators, the involution, membership, generation, an order, and the word inequality. All must
reject before producing a positive certificate. Both Python entry points reject optimized execution before
output work. Every replay is finite; a timeout or nonclean terminal result grants no certificate. The
recorded searches merely supplied candidates and are not run by verification.
3 Attribution and limits
The root agent supplied the bounded candidate searches and the acquired word argument; this component’s
author supplies the deterministic certificate, independent literal oracle, source routing and explanation.
All original candidate and execution bytes are retained. An initial author setup used a directory ordinal
instead of the actual recorded seed for some constructors; that failed preliminary setup is preserved outside
the scientific replay. The final replay uses the recorded seed and changes no candidate.
These are thirty-five parameter entries, not thirty-five newly classified groups. In particularL 4(2) ∼= A8
overlaps the alternating family, andU 4(2) ∼= PSp4(3) occurs twice under the source’s parameters. The
group identities, geometric reconstruction and original existence theorem retain their attribution. This
component does not settle the infinite Lie families, ancillary enumerations, or the whole original source,
and adds no measurement or conjecture claim.
