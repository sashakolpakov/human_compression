formulas with rational arithmetic at 1,619 parameter choices, verifies the strict quarter bounds, and checks the
correction at seven fields. These finite checks are controls on the formulas, not premises for the uniform in-
equalities. The ordinary character table U 4(3) gives 2,835 involutions, agreeing with Proposition 3; atq= 7
the formula gives 2,683,975<7 8/2. A fresh clean table replay is retained with the component. The construc-
tion and calculations in this note are new recovery work by publication recovery, responding to root’s request
for explicit lower estimates. The fixed source is Leemans–Liebeck,Chiral polyhedra and finite simple groups,
arXiv:1603.07713v3, Lemma 2.4 and Section 4 (especially printed page 9). No claim of novelty or priority is
made for the classical formulas. The remaining existence argument must separately pay every bad-generator and
