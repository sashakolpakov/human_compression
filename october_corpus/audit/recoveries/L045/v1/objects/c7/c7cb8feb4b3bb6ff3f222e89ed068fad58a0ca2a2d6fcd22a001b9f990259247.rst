The flag argument
-----------------
Since n sends positive roots to negative roots, it takes B to its opposite.
An element centralizing n and stabilizing B lies in both B and B-minus,
therefore in T. In T its centralizing condition is precisely t squared=1.
The torus 2-torsion scheme is finite in every characteristic. In characteristic
two it need not be smooth, but its dimension is still zero. The orbit-fibre
dimension theorem thus bounds dim C_H(n) by dim H/B. No Lie-algebra fixed-space
calculation, smoothness claim or separability claim is used. This proves the
lemma with the stated connected reductive hypothesis.
