conjugacy types.
Attribution and limits.The three representations and triality action, finite automorphism
structure and Clifford construction are prior theory. The exponent calculation, primitive choice
separating spectra, and resulting coset and actual-line bounds are the arguments here. The
casesq= 2,3 have acquired finite witnesses. The separate maximal-overgroup cover and final
generation inequality remain necessary; no whole-original or new-conjecture claim is made.
