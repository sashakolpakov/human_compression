\section*{Complete enumeration and independent verification}
\begin{proof}
We apply the acquired permutation enumeration~\cite{method}, leaving its
mathematical engine unchanged. In the three symmetric groups, every first
involution is conjugate to one having $e$ disjoint transpositions, with
$1\le e\le\lfloor n/2\rfloor$. In $A_{15}$ the allowable values are
$e=2,4,6$. We classify by the full automorphism group of $A_{15}$, namely
$S_{15}$ acting by point conjugacy, so these are exactly the three initial
classes needed for that group as well.

The acquired proof visits each permissible next involution through its
orbit under the actual pointwise centralizer of the prefix. It pays both
the intersection test on the growing prefix and the transitivity pruning.
Every one of the resulting twenty-two initial branches terminates:
six branches in each of degrees twelve and thirteen, seven in degree
fourteen, and three for $A_{15}$. The rank limits are respectively
$7,7,8,7$. Thus its completeness theorem applies throughout these ranges;
a timeout is not interpreted as exhaustion. Filtering by the actual
generated group leaves $9030,16571,56749,12004$ tuples, respectively.
The full transcripts and the exact engine used in each branch are retained.

The search excludes a commuting adjacent pair for these targets by the
acquired indecomposability argument. Indeed, such a pair separates a
string C-marking into commuting nontrivial factors whose intersection is
trivial by the intersection property. These symmetric groups have no such
direct-product decomposition, and $A_{15}$ is simple. Hence the
noncommutation graph is the connected path on the marked generators.
An isomorphism of their underlying generating sets must preserve that
path. It either preserves their order or reverses it. All automorphisms
of the symmetric groups here are inner, and all automorphisms of
$A_{15}$ are induced by $S_{15}$. Consequently point conjugacy and
reversal give precisely the source equivalence relation.

We verify all positive tuples independently of the search state. The
verifier reconstructs each literal permutation, checks nonidentity,
involution orders and the distant commutations, and computes its actual
generated subgroup. It requires equality with the stated target group.
For every contiguous interval $[a,b]$ of at least three marks it checks
\[
 \langle t_a,\ldots,t_{b-1}\rangle\cap
 \langle t_{a+1},\ldots,t_b\rangle
 =\langle t_{a+1},\ldots,t_{b-1}\rangle.
\]
The acquired interval theorem~\cite{intervals} proves that these equalities,
with its rank-one and rank-two bases, imply the full intersection property.
These are equalities of actual permutation subgroups. They do not rely on
cached prefix results or on projection of an intersection property.
The four lists require $16986,25066,94392,13729$ interval comparisons:
$150173$ comparisons on $94354$ complete tuples.

For distinctness and reversal we use the acquired literal-array invariant
of~\cite{eleven}. Starting at a point, traverse its orbit in the order of
the marked generators and express all generators in that resulting order
of points. Minimize this array over all starting points. Transitivity
ensures that every traversal covers the whole domain. A point conjugacy
carries a rooted traversal to the corresponding rooted traversal, whereas
equality of two arrays supplies the actual conjugating bijection. Thus
two minimized arrays agree exactly when the ordered markings are point
conjugate. All arrays in each list are distinct. The minimized reversed
array is also located in that list; the resulting permutation of the list
is checked to be an involution. Its fixed points give the self-dual column.
Its number of orbits is therefore half the sum of the ordered and
self-dual columns, as asserted.

The higher-rank statements use acquired proofs, rather than further
search. For $S_n$, write $k=n-r$. The complete stable correspondence and
its finite base counts~\cite{stable} give constants $1,1,7,9,35$ for
$k=1,\ldots,5$, provided $n\ge2k+3$. In degree twelve the remaining
ranks have $k\le4$; in degrees thirteen and fourteen they have $k\le5$.
Each meets the threshold, and substitution gives the stated tails.
The retained independent-generator bound $r\le n-1$ for $S_n$ excludes
all greater ranks. For $A_{15}$, the complete acquired alternating-rank
theorem~\cite{alternating} gives
$r\le\lfloor(15-1)/2\rfloor=7$. This proves the theorem in every rank
occurring in the two source tables.
\end{proof}

\section*{Certificate, provenance and scope}
The twenty-two complete branch transcripts are stored with lossless
compression. Their original uncompressed hashes, terminal receipts,
rank headings and complete literal outputs are checked before any tuple
is used. The checker reconstructs the same involution universe and positive
lists from those outputs. Its default mode repeats all actual group and
interval checks and every literal conjugacy/reversal check. The option
\texttt{--replay-enumeration} additionally repeats the unchanged enumeration
branches with their recorded individual limits and compares the complete
results. The report explicitly distinguishes these modes. Interrupted
executions and incomplete transcripts cannot supply a count.

The final common validator differs from the previously executed positive
validators only by selecting the explicitly named target group and by its
completion marker. Its canonical traversal is unchanged. The final replay
compares every resulting array with the original complete positive phase,
allowing only that literal marker change. It also checks the entire reversal
permutation, rather than merely its number of fixed points. Damaged completion
and duplicate-class controls must reject, as must optimized Python execution.
The development record retains an initial extraction failure: the helper
mistakenly included intervening branch records after the involution list.
It stopped before a mathematical validation; the corrected delimiter changes
no census transcript or mathematical engine.

The original numerical results remain attributed to the survey and its
collaborators. This component supplies complete recovery and verification
for twenty-one formerly unrecovered experimental cells. The high-rank
entries were already acquired. No new open-conjecture result, priority,
whole-original admission, Lean certificate or compression measurement is
claimed. The manuscript leaves the survey's other claims to their explicitly
separate proofs and source qualifications.
