# Bounded source audit: diagonal extensions in dimension four

10 October 2026. This is a primary-source and search-scope record, not a proof of novelty or an exhaustive claim that a problem remains open.

The proposed construction gives a rank-four string C-group of type $\{4,2p,2p\}$ on the index-two group $L_2(p^e)$ for every odd prime power. With the previously proved rank bound, its maximum is four. It does not establish rank-three existence for these extensions. No claim is made here for diagonal unitary extensions or for linear field/graph extensions.

## Exact published target and retained input

- Dimitri Leemans, [*String C-group representations of almost simple groups: a survey*, arXiv:1910.08843v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1910.08843v2), dated 11 May 2020. Section 5.7, Theorem 5.8, Conjecture 2 and Problem 7 were reread. Problem 7 asks for maximum ranks throughout $\operatorname{PSL}_4(q)\le G\le\operatorname{Aut}(\operatorname{PSL}_4(q))$. The present theorem handles the index-two diagonal part of that larger question; it is not a solution of the entire problem. Conjecture 2 concerns the simple socle itself and is a different target.
- Brooksbank–Leemans, [*Polytopes of large rank for PSL(4,q)*](https://digitalcommons.bucknell.edu/fac_journ/1204/), *Journal of Algebra* 452 (2016), 390–400, [DOI 10.1016/j.jalgebra.2015.11.051](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2015.11.051). The primary institutional record and the introduction/Theorem 1.1 in the [indexed author upload](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/293026803_Polytopes_of_large_rank_for_PSL4Fq) were inspected. Their theorem supplies rank four for the odd-field simple group; the inspected introduction does not state the present uniform index-two theorem. This is indexed author text, not a newly retrieved or validated complete PDF. The earlier source-access limitation remains in force.
- The [author publication list](https://leemans.dimitri.web.ulb.be/papers.html) was checked through its 2026 entries. It lists the 2026 Leemans–Vandenschrick paper on **simple** groups. Its classification is not silently extended to arbitrary almost-simple groups. A publication list alone does not establish the absence of another result.

## Search scope and conclusion

The companion JSON records sixteen targeted queries involving PGL(4,q), PSL4 index-two/diagonal extensions, PGU(4,q), string C-groups, and regular polytopes, including 2025–2026 restrictions. The strongest relevant hits were the survey and the 2016 simple-group construction; many hits concerned projective dimension two, unrelated incidence geometries or polytopal linear algebra. No primary source explicitly giving this uniform index-two existence theorem was located in the inspected results.

This supports only **publication priority unverified**. Mathematical notation is poorly indexed, and unpublished manuscripts, theses, non-English sources and exhaustive citation chains were not all covered. The positive printed checkpoint is the survey's broader Problem 7, not a freshly certified claim that this precise subcase remained unsolved until today. No infinity score or whole-problem resolution is inferred.

The maximum-four conclusion also uses the repository's proposed bilinear-form obstruction proved in earlier phases. It is an explicit mathematical dependency, not an external published theorem established by this literature search.
