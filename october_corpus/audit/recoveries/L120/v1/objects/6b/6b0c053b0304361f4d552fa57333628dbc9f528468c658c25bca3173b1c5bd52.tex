We recover the $S_{10}$ row of Table~1 in the fixed edition of
Leemans' survey~\cite{survey}.  Its definition identifies generating
sets under group automorphisms.  As in the acquired eight-point
calculation~\cite{eight}, the connected noncommutation path shows
that this is exactly the equivalence generated by simultaneous
conjugacy and reversal of the ordered marking.

\begin{theorem}
The complete counts for $S_{10}$ are
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrr}
\text{rank}&3&4&5&6&7&8&9\\\hline
\text{ordered classes}&809&403&99&26&13&2&1\\
\text{self-dual classes}&17&3&5&0&1&0&1\\
\text{classes with duality identified}&413&203&52&13&7&1&1.
\end{array}
\]
There are no representations of larger rank.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply the complete permutation enumeration of~\cite{method}, without
altering its mathematical engine.  The first mark is a product of
$e$ disjoint transpositions, where $1\le e\le5$.  These are exactly
the five involution classes of $S_{10}$.  Each of the five independent
initial branches finishes through rank nine.  The retained Whiston
independent-generator bound excludes every larger rank.  Thus the
acquired prefix-orbit theorem gives a complete list, with no ordered
point-conjugacy repetitions.  Its rejection of commuting adjacent
marks is valid here because $S_{10}$ has no nontrivial direct-product
decomposition.  The resulting list contains $1353$ tuples, with
the first-row distribution above.

A separate actual-group verification checks the order $10!$ of each
generated subgroup, all marked involutions and distant commutations,
and all pairs of subsets of every marking.  There are $969\,024$
checks of
\[
 \langle\rho_i:i\in I\rangle\cap\langle\rho_j:j\in J\rangle
 =\langle\rho_k:k\in I\cap J\rangle.
\]
These checks certify the positive objects; completeness follows from
the finished enumeration and its acquired proof.

The independent coloured-traversal invariant of~\cite{eight}, now
minimized over all ten roots, verifies all ordered class distinctions.
It also identifies the unique retained class of each reversed tuple.
The resulting permutation is an involution, with fixed-point counts
$17,3,5,0,1,0,1$.  Its orbits therefore number half the sum of the
first two rows, which gives the last row.  All automorphisms of
$S_{10}$ are inner; the finite verifier also checks
$|\operatorname{Aut}(S_{10})|=10!$.  The noncommutation graph of
the marked generators is a path, so its only possible permutations
under a generating-set isomorphism are the identity and reversal.
This pays exactly the source convention.
\end{proof}
