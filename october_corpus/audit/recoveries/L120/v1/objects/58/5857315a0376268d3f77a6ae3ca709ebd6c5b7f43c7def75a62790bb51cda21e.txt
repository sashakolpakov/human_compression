singularity equations force the derivative to equal three, and all three

including their multiplicities. The split and nonsplit character sheaves
ramify at disjoint sets of four points. Their odd orders prevent the

The mixed character sum uses eight punctures in genus one, giving the
8√q estimate. Each single-character sum uses four punctures; removing

than applying an elliptic estimate to the reducible cubic.

## Equal cyclic orders and the factor-eight bound

**Review outcome: PASS with the fixed through-256 finite input retained.**
Root read the complete [cyclic TPP proof](phase13_cyclic_tpp.md) and the
earlier power-layer proof used by it. In a powerful normal subgroup with
bijective ordered cyclic coordinates, the highest nontrivial power
subgroup is an actual characteristic elementary abelian subgroup. Its
basis consists precisely of the top involutions of longest marked factors.

A unique longest factor produces a central marked involution. With two
longest factors, the 2-group action on their two-dimensional span has
image of order at most two. Each pair-generator fixes its own coordinate;
the action therefore fixes one marked coordinate globally. Both cases
contradict the previously proved center avoidance. No general linearity
of a power map or global injectivity of a TPP product is presumed.

The bounded-factor proof chooses a minimum within its own restricted
class. Central quotients, restriction to a pair-generated subgroup,
maximal-subgroup intersections, and equal power subgroups all preserve
the bound on the smallest factor. The previous reductions therefore
apply. This is the necessary justification for the uniform min≤8 result;
an unrestricted minimum-counterexample statement alone would not suffice.

Root freshly ran the focused action checker. Its report is byte-identical
to the author's: 18 marked dimension-two action cases pass, together with
the dimension-one case and the rank-three boundary example. It verifies
the hashes of the retained order-256 certificate inputs, without rerunning
that catalogue. The structural proof yields equal orders 2^e, e≥4, and
order 2^(3e−1) for an unrestricted minimum counterexample. Hence all
2-groups through order 1024 satisfy the inequality. The geometry adapter
gives the pair-order≤16 and hypertope-order≤2^13 consequences.

The unrestricted inequality remains open. In particular this proof does
not assert that every unequal-order triple satisfies it, and it does
not enumerate groups of order 1024.

Root also read the separate [top-action supplement](phase13_cyclic_tpp_top_action.md)
and freshly ran its checker. An order-four action on the three top
involutions would have a unique fixed line; a common fixed vector for
the 2-group then makes a marked coordinate central. The remaining
dihedral image is excluded by the third generator's Frattini coset.
The image is therefore C₂². Two rank-one commuting differences from
the identity cannot have distinct images without putting all three
basis vectors in one plane. Their common image and the marked fixed
coordinates force the unique displayed action and central line 111.
The kernel is exactly Φ(P). All implications pass review.

The fresh standard-library enumeration is byte-identical to the author's:
168 invertible matrices, 100 two-group image pairs, 38 marked third-coset
cases, and exactly one surviving action. This remains a necessary
condition; its non-coordinate central line supplies no contradiction.

Class-three collection makes γ₃ central elementary abelian and expresses
it using edge squares and adjacent triple commutators. The collection
indices, including both boundary cases, agree with the written formulas.

A hypothetical dependence of edge commutators modulo γ₃ first kills
each selected edge square. Commuting with each generator then kills the
two adjacent triple terms by the intersection property in the original
group. Cutting the path at the first selected edge separates the remaining
γ₃ factors. A final two-support intersection forces that edge commutator
to be trivial, contradicting nondegeneracy. Thus all d−1 edge images are
independent; order comparison identifies the marked quotient with the
path-cocycle group of order 2^(2d−1). Its intersection property is proved
from that identification, never assumed for arbitrary quotients.
