# Phase 20: bounded priority and current-status audit

Audit date: 2026-10-10. The proposed result concerns the unique diagonal extension $U_2(q)$ with $[U_2(q):\operatorname{PSU}_4(q)]=2$, for every odd $q\ge3$. Its proof has internal reviews and finite certificates. **Publication priority is unverified; this audit is not exhaustive.**

## A current named question, with a strict scope boundary

The [Kourovka Notebook, issue 21 (2026)](https://alglog.org/21tkt.pdf), Problem **21.101**, printed page **192** in the retrieved 317-page version, asks for the finite almost-simple groups admitting rank-three regular polytopes, equivalently generation by three involutions with a commuting pair. It attributes the question to Ya. N. Nuzhin and records the finite-simple case as answered. The title, preface and problem page were read directly from the primary PDF; page numbers in older search snippets differ.

This is a positive current checkpoint for the **broad classification question**. The present unitary family provides a proposed partial answer within that question. It does not resolve Problem 21.101 as a whole, prove that this particular subfamily was previously unknown, or justify an infinity marker. Field and graph extensions, other socles and unrelated outer automorphisms remain outside the construction.

## Primary follow-ups and distinctions

- [Nuzhin, *Generating Sets of Involutions of Almost Simple Groups and Their Applications*](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10469-026-09809-5), Algebra and Logic **64**, 26–32, has issue year 2025 but a version-of-record date of **24 January 2026**. The publisher abstract and references were read; its subscription full text was not obtained. It discusses involution-generation questions and the rank-three intersection criterion. It remains a priority lead, not evidence excluding an earlier unitary construction.
- [Markovskaya–Nuzhin (2025), Theorem 2](phase20_linear_rank3_intake.md), is a definite correction to the nearby linear status: $\operatorname{PGL}_4(q)$ for $q\equiv3\pmod4$ already has the required generation and rank-three representation. The separate N011 intake retains that theorem without rederivation credit.
- [Markovskaya, *On Generating Sets of Matrix Groups over Finite Fields*](https://www.mathnet.ru/php/getFT.phtml?jrnid=jsfu&option_lang=eng&paperid=1290&what=fullt), J. Siberian Federal University, Mathematics & Physics **18**(6) (2025), 756–769, concerns matrices of determinant $\pm1$ in the **linear** group. Its introduction, preliminary lemmas and §2.3 were read. Section 2.3 excludes the corresponding four-dimensional linear group; that does not exclude projective generating involutions whose lifts square to a nontrivial scalar. Its elementary primitive-square field lemma is also established background, not a novelty claim here. This source does not state the unitary diagonal result.
- The [2025 rank-three intersection paper](https://elib.sfu-kras.ru/bitstream/2311/156158/1/Baktybekov-Nuzhin.pdf) remains an attributed dependency already read in phase 19. The present proof supplies a same-marking argument, including the mixed rotation/reflection case; its Theorem A's existential wording is not silently strengthened.

The published rank-three classification for **simple** groups and the exceptional nonexistence for $\operatorname{PSU}_4(3)$ are separate from the present extension. Earlier phase-nine and phase-seventeen notes provide the independent rank upper bound and rank-four construction. Neither of those alone gives rank-three existence.
