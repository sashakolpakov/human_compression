# Phase-nine source and priority audit

Audit date: 10 October 2026. This is a bounded primary-source search,
not a proof of historical priority or an assertion that every question
below was still open immediately before this work.

| Source | Exact material inspected | Consequence retained |
|---|---|---|
| Leemans, [survey, arXiv:1910.08843v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.08843), §5.7 | Theorem 5.8, Conjecture 2, Problem 7 | Odd PSL4 rank-four existence; proposed maximum four; maximal-rank question for all almost simple groups with PSL4 socle. |
| Brooksbank–Leemans, [J. Algebra 452 (2016), 390–400](https://digitalcommons.bucknell.edu/fac_journ/1204/), Theorem 1.1 | Previously audited theorem and fresh primary repository record | Rank four exists for every odd q. This remains an external input. |
| Leemans–Vandenschrick, [author manuscript](https://dipot.ulb.ac.be/dspace/bitstream/2013/397490/3/simpleGroups6.pdf), Theorem 1.1 and introduction | Full exact exception list; statement immediately after the theorem | PSU4(3) admits no string C-group; every odd PSU4(q), q≥5, admits rank three. The manuscript is not a maximum-rank classification of these latter groups. |
| Brooksbank, [arXiv:1912.05512](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.05512), introduction, §2 and §5 | Main PSp4 theorem; discussion distinguishing odd-dimensional derived groups from even-dimensional orthogonal groups | No PSU4 maximum-four or uniform rank-four construction is claimed in the inspected sections. Orthogonal isometry-group constructions do not automatically give their simple derived subgroups. |
| Leemans–Vauthier, [regular polytope atlas](https://www.math.auckland.ac.nz/~dleemans/polytopes/) | Scope and unitary table | Small almost-simple group enumeration is useful background; no general odd-PSU4 rank-four theorem. The separately indexed CHIRAL atlas must not be confused with this regular atlas. |

The [publisher's institutional repository record](https://repozitorij.upr.si/IzpisGradiva.php?id=22370&lang=eng)
identifies the simple-group paper as *The Art of Discrete and Applied
Mathematics* 9(1) (2026), P1.07, DOI
[10.26493/2590-9770.1329.6f5](https://doi.org/10.26493/2590-9770.1329.6f5).
Its indexed primary metadata records online publication 24 November 2025
and repository deposit 5 January 2026. The ULB repository gives 2026-01-01
as the publication date. These are distinct publication/deposit/issue
metadata; none is used to infer a precise priority date for our
consequences. The locally read author manuscript has placeholder journal
dates. Direct browser opening of the RUP page and DOI was denied, but
the primary indexed record and the actual ULB full text were accessible.

Searches included exact PSU4/PSU(4,q), PGU4/PGU(4,q), PGL4/PGL(4,q),
orthogonal minus six, and PΩ6-minus terms with “string C-group”,
“rank four”, “maximum rank”, and “regular polytopes”. The author
publication catalogue and the above primary papers were checked.
No explicit existing uniform odd-PSU4 maximum-four theorem, nor uniform
rank-four existence theorem, was located. This negative search result
does not establish novelty or prove that the existence question is open.

The new proved scope is consequently recorded as a **rank upper bound
for two linear intervals**, with a separate elementary index-four
nonexistence obstruction. It is not a complete answer to survey Problem
7, which includes field and graph extensions; nor does it establish
the exact spectrum of PSU4(q) for q≥5 or of an index-two extension.
There is no named PSU4 maximum-rank conjecture in the inspected survey.

The audit's JSON companion records the hashes of the actual retained
source PDFs and the frozen original proof/checker. No external full text
is included in the original-artifact export.
