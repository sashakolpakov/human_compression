Finite end groups for alternating group constructions
====================================================

This private component recovers four uniform low-rank constructions of L038,
using one complete seven-page proof and four fixed finite end models. It does
not by itself discharge the source principal theorem. No original, conjecture,
Lean or measurement credit is requested.

Read finite_ends.pdf (or its complete TeX) first. The finite computations in
certificate.g use only degrees 10, 12, 15 and 16. The proof establishes exactly
why those finite models suffice for every degree in the four stated families.
model.g contains the explicit source permutations; no source PDF is needed to
execute the certificate. dependencies.json binds the acquired primary edition
and the previously reviewed rank contraction theorem used for lower ranks.

Replay with Python check.py --out NEW_DIRECTORY --study-root STUDY_ROOT --render.
GAP and pdflatex must be available; --gap may give an explicit GAP executable.
The checker rejects optimized Python, verifies all export/binding hashes, runs
the exact finite certificate and meaningful damaged-certificate controls, and
optionally rebuilds every native page. Independent mathematical review must
assess the uniform transfer proof; this script does not claim to formalize it.
