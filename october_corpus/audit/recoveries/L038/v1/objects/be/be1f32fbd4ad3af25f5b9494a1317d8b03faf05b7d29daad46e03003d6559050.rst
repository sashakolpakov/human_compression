Restriction kernels in extensions of string markings
====================================================

Private author packet. Distinct mathematical review is pending. It supplies
complete five-page written proofs of two exact criteria, not a complete recovery
of L019 or its main high-rank stability theorem.

Start with restriction_kernels.pdf and scope.json. Theorem 3 tests only actual
pointwise stabilizers in consecutive original prefix groups. Theorem 7 applies
two such lists to the separated split with one internal factor trivial. The
rectangle lemma proves the final end intersection directly; the potentially
false step is instead the intersection property of recursively obtained ends.
A supported image must not be substituted for its actual restriction kernel.

The separate dependency_audit.json found no use of either refuted statement in
the active B or Gluing proofs. This is an internal audit by a prior author,
not a new distinct acceptance of those scientific papers. Its source commit and

Formal scope
------------

RestrictionKernelCriterion.lean proves three actual arbitrary-group subgroup
lemmas. It does not formalize the permutation construction, end criterion or
uniform string intersection proofs. Cached dependencies are trusted only at the
pinned revisions. No sorry, custom axiom or native_decide is used.

check_kernels.g is the whole bounded diagnostic program, never a premise of the
quantified proof. It tests 600 subgroup/root cases, 180 old C-markings with roots,
312 literal split instances and 225 nested rectangles, plus five parity-family
parameters and four assumption controls. The runner rejects three damaged GAP
programs and two weakened Lean hypotheses. The GAP process remains trusted
computation; none of its finite tests is called an all-parameter proof.
