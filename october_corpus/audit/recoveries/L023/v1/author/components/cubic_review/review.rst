   subfield. It applies also to the PSL2 subfield normalizer, including the

   involution. Its two other involutions occupy one reflection class in the
   dihedral centralizer; the full normalizer is eight. The retained inner
   class has normalizer twenty-four. Double counting therefore gives one
   plus at most three subfields through a fixed ambient inner Klein group.
   The determinant square map removes the mixed contribution for odd

2. Projective descent is valid: a scalar twisted norm is fixed by the field

   not an incorrectly enlarged PGL subgroup.

3. Each generated J is stable under alpha. A Borel has cyclic Sylow two,
   excluding V. Dihedral groups other than V have the central characteristic
   involution in V. V itself has no alpha-fixed involution. The A4/S4/A5
   exclusions use an order-three automorphism with no fixed involution.
   The retained actual-subgroup form of Dickson, rather than merely a list
   of maximal subgroups, consequently places J in its stable M_d.

4. All degrees dividing n are charged together inside unique standard M_n;
   there alpha is conjugation by s and its odd-element centralizer has at
   most one involution. Other possible degrees have d=3v, gcd(d,n)=v and
   v<=n/2. Conjugate an alpha-stable M_d back to its canonical subfield:
   alpha becomes t F^n. Self-normalization forces t in M_d, retaining the
   nontrivial order-three field component. Descent gives fixed PGL2(p^v)
   and at most p^(2v) involutions. Four conjugates give the stated union
   bound without an assumption that all subfields lie in degree n.

   At q=9 the correct PSL involution sign gives 45-1-36=8.
