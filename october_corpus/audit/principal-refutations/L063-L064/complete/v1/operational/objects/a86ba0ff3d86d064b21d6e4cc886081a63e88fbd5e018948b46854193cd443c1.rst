Mathematical review
-------------------

The point-stabilizer bound applies to nonfaithful actions as written.
Primitivity supplies maximality, and the single orbit on the other points
supplies the divisibility condition. The simple-group proof correctly
separates natural Borels, torus normalizers, small exceptional groups,
and subfield groups. The bounded prime-power lists are exhaustive. In
particular the order-only S4 list includes t=8, but the subgroup premise
excludes S4 there; the manuscript does not confuse divisibility with
subgroup existence.

Both subfield obstructions are strict, including s=3. The first ratio
lies between one and two because
``s^3+s-2-(4s-4)=(s-1)^2(s+2)>0``. The second lies between three and
four because ``s^3+s-2-(8s-8)=(s-1)(s-2)(s+3)>0``. The even-field and
larger-extension order comparisons also hold at their least parameters.
No proper subfield case is left over.

The listed simple actions exist. For PSL(2,11), the chosen class has
eleven A5 subgroups, and the fifty-five A4 subgroups each belong to one
chosen member. This excludes suborbits of length five; no fixed point
and the A5 subgroup orders then force the other ten points to be one
orbit. For PSL(2,7) and PSL(2,9), the displayed linear and alternating
actions give one class, and the explicitly borrowed PGL fusion transfers
two-transitivity to the other class. This last implication is implicit
in the manuscript and is recorded here: an automorphism carrying B to B'
identifies their coset actions after twisting the group action.

The PGL proof does not incorrectly assume that C=B intersect PSL is
maximal in PSL. It uses the full subgroup list. A kernel disjoint from
the simple normal subgroup centralizes it, and the two unipotent groups
give the stated trivial-centralizer argument. The characteristic radical
O_p(C), semisimple eigenlines, characteristic rotation subgroup and
four-group normalizer handle their respective cases. The remaining
small groups are eliminated by the displayed bounds and fusion, and the
subfield ratios rule out all other cases. The t=3 exception is handled
separately by the three maximal subgroups of S4.

The solvable lemma is valid for the scalar affine subgroups at issue:
C is an actual subgroup of field scalars and consequently acts faithfully.
After dividing by the additive kernel of the action, a complement can
be conjugated by averaging. Each nonidentity scalar minus one is
invertible on V and on every invariant quotient, so transitivity on
nonzero quotient vectors is equivalent to the stated order equality.
The dihedral argument permits every index-two subgroup, rather than
only the cyclic one. Its degree-three cases have quotient S3.

All nine rows of Corollary 5 follow. In the maximal-A5 scope, a
characteristic-five ambient field has odd extension degree; its PSL
scalar torus therefore contains no C4, excluding C5:C4 above D10.
The E16:C3 alternative above A4 is retained in its actual characteristic
and dimension range. The odd-prime restrictions exclude affine cases
above D6, D8 and A4. The larger simple/projective overgroups must be
subfield types by the borrowed list, so their defining characteristic
cannot be changed merely because a small abstract isomorphism exists.
The table supplies possible abstract overgroup types under the stated
embedding conditions, not counts of overgroups above every fixed
ambient embedding.

Borrowing and limits
--------------------

exceptional isomorphisms, simplicity and indicated fusion facts remain
attributed premises. The 2006 paper is used for subgroup statements and
the elementary small subgroup tables only; its design classification is
not imported. Historical books have not been represented as freshly
consulted editions. The new component supplies a common elimination
proof and the elementary quotient/action arguments. It neither verifies
