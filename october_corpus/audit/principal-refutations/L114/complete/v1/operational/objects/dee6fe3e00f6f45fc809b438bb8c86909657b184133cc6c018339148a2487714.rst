One retained Sz(8) marking: actual incidence panels
=================================================

This diagnostic separates two possible meanings of a residue. On printed
page45, L114 defines Gamma_F for a flag F contained in the distinguished
chamber C by the intersection subgroups. On printed page46, its local
2-transitivity condition refers to any flag F contained in C and to
Gamma_F; it then explicitly says that agreement with the earlier definition
in [10] holds when the geometry is flag-transitive. The thinness definition
on that page counts actual chambers through flags. These exact contexts
must accompany any application of Theorem6.1 on printed page61.

The retained phase-five marking with parameters (p,p-prime)=(5,5) has
 a=w, b=z(2)wz(2), c=z(1), over F8 defined by x^3+x+1.
Its three parabolics are <a,b>,<a,c>,<b,c>, of orders14,10,10.
Their pair intersections have order2 and triple intersection is trivial.
Consequently their algebraic rank-one coset actions have two points and
are doubly transitive. This does not assume that these coset sets exhaust
the full incidence panels. If local double transitivity means the action
on each full incidence panel, that additional hypothesis must be checked.
In particular, a panel larger than two would violate that stronger
hypothesis for this marking, rather than refute an implication assuming it.
No unconditional source refutation is claimed in this request.

For missing type i and the other types j,k, every left coset of Gi incident
with the base Gj has a representative g in Gj. It also meets Gk precisely
when (gGi) intersect Gk is nonempty. Enumerating the at most14 choices of
g therefore lists the entire actual panel. Duplicate left cosets are
identified as literal matrix sets. A third coset is in the standard chamber
orbit exactly when it meets Gj intersect Gk. All three panels of this one
saved marking are output, including every coset and its two intersections.
No panel size is prescribed, and an output of two is retained as such.

Only the existing Field class and tiny dihedral closures are executed; the
old checker's main function, full trace checks and all other markings are
not run. There is no enumeration of the ambient Suzuki group, no GAP call,
and no subgroup-class census. Each reconstructed subgroup has at most14
matrices; each panel has at most7 candidate cosets, each of at most14
matrices. Data structures are statically bounded well below one MiB.
The owner enforces10seconds elapsed, oneMiB output, fourGiB prelaunch
reserve and twoGiB emergency floor, and reaps the child on every supervised
exit. The reused owner differs only in its descriptive status names.
These are sampled resource guards, not a hard bound on system allocator
overhead. One invocation is requested; there is no automatic successor.
