The rank-two tables quoted in L114
================================

accepted L115 preliminary, normalizer-family and corrected subfield-family
proofs. The numerical count tables and29 diagrams in L115 are not premises
for these parametric families and are not silently treated as complete.

The original notation uses s for the group parameter in these tables.
The full split torus normalizer is D= D_(2(s-1)); N+ and N- are the two
full nonsplit normalizers; S_t is a maximal simple subfield group.

These are the accepted normalizer families. Local2T follows exactly as
in the accepted L114 prime-pattern note: only row5, and rows9/11 at s8.
The 'q=8' in row11 must refer to this table's s=8. This is a variable
qualification, not a different ambient-group condition.

degree is prime. Its local2T column has YES only in row5. This agrees
with the same degree argument: a cyclic complement of order4 can act

In Table4, row12 is again universal. Rows13–15 are the full ambient
split/nonsplit normalizer joined to a maximal subfield through its full

are the same extensions short of the full ambient torus. Row17 is a pair
of distinct maximal simple subfields with a common simple subfield
intersection. The simple-intersection parameter must be at least8, in
accord with the source's stated exclusion of Sz(2) from 'Suzuki type'.
The accepted corrected subfield theorem supplies these families, their
precise field/prime conditions, unique embeddings and equivalence classes.

Printed row16 is not retained as a valid configuration. It has S_t as
one parabolic and the full central extension Z_s B_t as the other, with
intersection B_t. For relative field degree r>=3, choose an F_t-linear

so B_t is not maximal in the second parabolic. The entire corrected
family is the accepted set of central covers Z_W B_t, where W/F_t
has dimension1. Those configurations are locally2-transitive, contrary
to the inherited NO entry for the printed row and the exhaustive list.
This is the already accepted L115 central-space correction; it gives no
additional principal disposition count for L114. Theorem5.2 is imported
in its corrected form, not used literally to infer the false rank-three
