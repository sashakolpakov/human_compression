## How the reduction works

One image-lifting argument transports subgroup intersections through a
homomorphism. It supplies both marked extension results and the chiral-cover
criterion after the geometric prerequisites are proved. A separate exact-coset
argument transports incidence through a kernel in the chamber stabilizer.
Relative generation, a Frattini ladder and primitive coordinates then connect
the symmetric/finite-p-group results to the two-group constructions.

A single square-lattice model supplies the regular seed and the linear-part
test for chiral square maps. Explicit Gray-code face maps, affine markings
and a change of lattice basis prove the toroidal identifications. Endpoint
conjugation and a support criterion provide a common proof for the J1
constructions and generalized-cube halving, with typed Helly supplying actual
flag completion.

The full forest classification, bounded cyclic models, geometric extension
arguments, exhaustive residue census and local mirror induction remain inside
the replacement. All four complete GAP programs are embedded, totaling
15,421 bytes. The shared lemmas organize these necessary branches; their
short final corollaries do not erase the cost of the preceding proofs.
