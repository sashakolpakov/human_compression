# Current work and verified source material

The project is being reorganized into **umbrella theory U and focused
application manuscripts A, B, C, …**, each intended to recover a coherent
bundle of source results in about 20–25 pages. U and the first application
volume now have complete drafts; no modular compression factor is yet measured.
The [programme](MANUSCRIPT_PROGRAM.md) specifies their mathematical scope:
**U: Marked groups, kernels and intersections** and **A: Construction and
classification of two-group polytopes**. Read [U's 28-statement draft](UMBRELLA.md)
and [A's 21-page manuscript](MODULE_A.md). Root has read both complete
proof texts and verified the frozen export and rebuild; formalization,
final source admission and matched measurement remain incomplete.

**[B: Forest coordinates and primitive coset geometries](MODULE_B.md)**
is the next application manuscript, currently in preparation. Its planned
four-source bundle is N001, N003, N008 and L030, spanning 1998–2021.
This title and grouping add no source-coverage or compression credit.

The layers overlap. Retrieval is not complete reading; principal-result
coverage is not every assertion in a paper. The inventory is bounded and
is not an exhaustive bibliography of all collaborators.

Module A begins with five related two-power-group papers from 2018–2026:
L032, L035, F013, F016 and N009. Their existing derivations and certificates
have been reworked. The supported-kernel theorem supplies the edge, path
and fork constructions; all classification and geometric arguments remain
explicit. The ledger binds 44 source records and 17 multipart clauses.
The complete raw cost is 190,995 bytes including U and all four external
computational artifacts; A's 21 pages alone are not the whole proof cost.

A [reviewed hypertope attachment](MODULE_A_HYPERTOPES.md) now recovers
L034's complete principal construction, including the exact presentation
and corrected quotient structure. Its coset-intersection theorem adds a
reusable geometric argument; the required local-involutivity correction
is explicit. This is the eighteenth principal-result source. A new
six-source edition of A remains to be integrated and measured.

The preceding combined manuscripts are a source bank, not the product,
organizing structure or benchmark of this programme. The latest working
compilation adds the complete commuting-involutions contract L066. Its
[independent review](research/case_study/phase114_commuting_pairs_review.md)
checks 41,175 raw configurations and 138,240 subgroup intersections and
requires the [corrected proof ledger](research/case_study/phase116_commuting_pairs_corrected_ledger.json).
All sixteen defective new spans were repaired. This provides reusable
proof material; it is not a new modular compression claim.

Separately, the [five-family cover theorem](COVER_FIVE_FAMILIES.md) now
establishes multiplicity for every parameter in the five checked source
families. Two fixed affine constructions and a uniform pullback argument
handle the four two-primary rows; the odd-prime family has its earlier
proof. Independent mathematics, computation and source-scope reviews pass.
Historical priority and current openness remain unverified. The broader
arbitrary-polytope conjecture is unresolved here.

A new census will account for the questions and conjectures in all 161
entries, including explicit access/reading gaps, exact source statements
and later literature. Solving related questions through shared arguments
continues alongside the module work.
The [question-results catalogue](CONJECTURE_RESULTS.md) now collects eleven
existing entries: eight recorded answers under their exact stated scope
and three qualified family or interpretation results. This is an inventory
of existing claims and evidence, not eleven newly audited solutions or a
count of conjectures confirmed still open before our work.

The latest independently reviewed entry is
[Equal-label polytopes and quadratic symmetries](EQUAL_LABEL_POLYTOPES.md).
It gives every-rank constructions for specified infinite families of
labels and identifies the universal presentations at labels three and
four. The full conjecture, historical priority and complete Lean proof
remain unresolved. It adds no known-source compression credit.

Forty-five existing Lean components retain their individual statements,
hypotheses and trust records. They are reusable material, not a certificate
of the whole new programme. Formalization will follow our actual U and
module proofs, for both old results and conjecture claims.

The latest component proves the structural part of U.SK: actual generation
of the simultaneous fiber product, every parabolic kernel profile and full
IP. The [independent review](research/case_study/phase133_supported_kernel_review.md)
and fresh public compilation cover all ten new declarations. Its kernel
profile is a conclusion, not an assumption. The order formula and separate
corollaries remain outside this admitted certificate.

The [measurement protocol](PROOF_LIBRARY.md) compares original source
lengths with complete modules and with U charged once across modules. It
tracks gross additions, removals and marginal costs to test the proposed
sawtooth pattern. No such pattern has yet been established. Earlier
compilation measurements remain historical, tied to their exact artifacts;
for example, the twelve-source token lower bound is 59,636/44,269. It is
not a measurement of U or Module A.
