# Current work and available proofs

The active task is to turn the existing reviewed manuscripts into
**topic umbrellas and families of complete application papers**. See the
[programme](MANUSCRIPT_PROGRAM.md), [complexity protocol](TOPIC_COMPLEXITY.md)
and [dependency pilot](DEPENDENCY_PILOT.md). The existing combined A–D manuscripts
are material for that task, not completed Aᵢ/Bᵢ families.

The first completed pair is [Topic A’s umbrella and L032 application](TOPIC_A.md),
with independent review, complete 15+4-page texts and 34 inherited Lean checks.
This reorganizes an existing recovery and adds no principal-source credit.

The [equal-label polygon application](EQUAL_LABEL_POLYGONS.md) also recovers
F014’s complete stated rank-three main results. Its five Lean declarations
verify the general algebraic core. The
[projective-line proof](PROJECTIVE_LINE_POLYTOPES.md) now recovers two more
complete principal classifications, with independent review and fresh
finite and Lean checks. The [third application](PROJECTIVE_LINE_MANIPLEXES.md)
recovers the complete specified PSL₂ maniplex contract, with its source
corrections and a new string-generator interface. Principal-source coverage

within this topic. The [matched measurement](TOPIC_E_MEASUREMENT.md) gives
conditional local factors of 1.62× and 2.38×, but a total written factor of
0.835× after charging the full umbrella. This remains the frozen first-two-application
baseline and excludes the third application. Six additional
[Lean matrix declarations](provenance/phase123_review.md) now verify its
actual normal-form centralizers; full field descent remains written.

The [odd-kernel application](ODD_KERNEL_POLYHEDRA.md) is a complete
eight-page recovery of L014’s principal results. Its character and
cyclotomic arguments begin Topic F; a separate umbrella and additional
applications remain unfinished. Five Lean declarations verify specified
algebraic steps. The source marking correction is stated explicitly.

The [mixed-prime application](MIXED_PRIME_POLYHEDRA.md) adds a complete
nine-page recovery of L012’s principal contract and a five-page reusable
affine theory paper. It retains the full Topic A extension framework and
the earlier cubic proof. Independent review accepts the uniform arguments,
corrected source periods and finite nonexistence base. Three new Lean
declarations verify the sign obstruction only; no ratio or new question
credit is claimed.

The [four-family PGL conjecture](PGL_MANIPLEX_FAMILIES.md) now has a complete
reviewed affirmative answer. It is separate from principal-source recovery:
the question catalogue has twelve entries, including eleven complete answers
under their stated hypotheses. The new proof uses the centralizer theory and
adds eleven scoped Lean declarations; full formalization and priority remain
separate obligations.

The [locally faithful cover paper](LOCALLY_FAITHFUL_COVERS.md) adds a general
abelian criterion and an exact restricted Tomotope classification, with
independent review, integer certificates and three scoped Lean proofs. It
adds no principal source or complete question answer; the general finite
multiplicity problem is still under investigation.

The [primitive-square proof](PRIMITIVE_SQUARES.md) has passed a detailed
independent arithmetic audit, covering every field configuration and the
complete finite reduction. Six new Lean declarations verify its polynomial
steps. This strengthens an existing result without adding a question entry.
