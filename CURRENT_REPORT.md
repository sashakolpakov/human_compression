# Current work and verified source material

The project is being reorganized into **umbrella theory U and focused
application manuscripts A, B, C, …**, each intended to recover a coherent
bundle of source results in about 20–25 pages. U and the first application
volume are in preparation; no modular compression factor is yet measured.
The [programme](MANUSCRIPT_PROGRAM.md) specifies their mathematical scope.

| Intake layer | Count |
|---|---:|
| Indexed mathematical works | 161 |
| Complete texts retrieved | 102 |
| At least one named result/component rederived | 39 |
| All identified principal results rederived | 17 |
| Distinct sources represented in the existing working compilations | 13 |

The layers overlap. Retrieval is not complete reading; principal-result
coverage is not every assertion in a paper. The inventory is bounded and
is not an exhaustive bibliography of all collaborators.

Module A begins with five related two-power-group papers from 2018–2026:
L032, L035, F013, F016 and N009. Their existing derivations and certificates
are being reworked, not independently rediscovered. A supported-kernel
theorem may unify previously separate construction arguments; its general
proof and every application hypothesis are under independent scrutiny.
Full classification and geometric obligations remain explicit.

The preceding combined manuscripts are a source bank, not the product,
organizing structure or benchmark of this programme. The latest working
compilation adds the complete commuting-involutions contract L066. Its
[independent review](research/case_study/phase114_commuting_pairs_review.md)
checks 41,175 raw configurations and 138,240 subgroup intersections and
requires the [corrected proof ledger](research/case_study/phase116_commuting_pairs_corrected_ledger.json).
All sixteen defective new spans were repaired. This provides reusable
proof material; it is not a new modular compression claim.

Separately, the [five-family cover theorem](COVER_FIVE_FAMILIES.md) now
establishes multiplicity for every parameter in the five checked source
families. Two fixed affine constructions and a uniform pullback argument
handle the four two-primary rows; the odd-prime family has its earlier
proof. Independent mathematics, computation and source-scope reviews pass.
Historical priority and current openness remain unverified. The broader
arbitrary-polytope conjecture is unresolved here.

A new census will account for the questions and conjectures in all 161
entries, including explicit access/reading gaps, exact source statements
and later literature. Solving related questions through shared arguments
continues alongside the module work.

Forty-three existing Lean components retain their individual statements,
hypotheses and trust records. They are reusable material, not a certificate
of the whole new programme. Formalization will follow our actual U and
module proofs, for both old results and conjecture claims.

The [measurement protocol](PROOF_LIBRARY.md) compares original source
lengths with complete modules and with U charged once across modules. It
tracks gross additions, removals and marginal costs to test the proposed
sawtooth pattern. No such pattern has yet been established. Earlier
compilation measurements remain historical, tied to their exact artifacts;
for example, the twelve-source token lower bound is 59,636/44,269. It is
not a measurement of U or Module A.
