Uniform mathematical argument
-----------------------------

For an overgroup D of W semidirect K*, multiplication by its contained scalar
part shows D=(D intersect V) semidirect K*. Closure under all scalars makes
the additive intersection a K-space. Thus covers are exactly one-dimensional
quotient spaces. The resulting affine action has the stated kernel W and
is two-transitive; faithfulness is required only after quotienting by W.
The proof does not mistake an additive plane for an intermediate subfield.

In even characteristic PGL2 and PSL2 agree, and the normalized affine matrix
has unique coefficients. Intersection with the natural subfield subgroup is
therefore exactly U_K K*. The two local indices and kernels follow directly.
The maximality input applies: s>=4 and prime extension degree, with s=4 paid
by the A5 row and s>4 by the subfield row in the retained maximal-subgroup
classification. Thus a translation outside K together with H generates G.
This supplies connectedness. Rank two has no additional connected residue of
rank at least two to check. Both rank-one residues are primitive because the
two local actions are two-transitive, and H is maximal in G. Simplicity gives
faithfulness of the whole action. The maximality input is explicitly retained,
not inferred from the finite program. The plane count is the number of lines
in F/K and is correctly restricted to subspaces, not geometry classes.

I also checked the elementary projective-action conventions: right versus
left cosets changes neither the intersection, degrees nor transitivity.
Every incident pair has a common representative, proving flag transitivity.
Every singleton extends to a flag. Local degrees s and s+1 are at least four,
so firmness and thickness hold. There is no additional local-faithfulness
hypothesis in the actual source definition that would exclude the Q action.
