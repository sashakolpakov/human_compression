Apply UD.MaximalAugmentation to \(G=J_2\). The retained TomLib catalogue has 146
subgroup classes and 568 maximal-inclusion classes, of which 417 are
locally two-transitive. These are finite-group classification data, not
a geometry list. The complete search tests 4,072 maximal-augmentation
orbit representatives, distributed by ranks two through six as
\(644,1226,1931,271,0\). Every one of its 146 Borel anchors reaches an
empty terminal level.

The final fusion uses the full automorphism group and exact successive
subgroup transporters, not just equality of subgroup-order signatures.
All 30 final representatives pass a separate check through their maximal
parabolics: generation by panels, reconstruction of every panel by
intersections, firmness, typed Helly, all residue-generation conditions,
all RWPRI deletions, two-transitive panels and every rank-two
partial-linearity/digon test. The three rank-four Borel orders are
1,1,8; their maximal-parabolic order multisets are respectively
{[}12,150,60,60{]}, {[}150,150,60,60{]} and {[}384,384,6048,6048{]}.

\textbf{Corollary D3.J2.} Under the stated complete subgroup catalogue
and exact finite-group operations, these are all firm flag-transitive
residually connected \(J_2\) geometries satisfying RWPRI, \((2T)_1\) and
\((\mathrm{IP})_2\): eight in rank two, nineteen in rank three and three
in rank four, with none in any higher rank.

\textbf{Proof.} UD.MaximalAugmentation proves exhaustion, including
proper acting subgroups during intermediate stages. The complete finite
output and separate final-witness tests give existence and exact
full-automorphism/type equivalence. An empty terminal level at every
Borel anchor excludes every further rank. This is not an inference from
the absence of a rank-five object alone. \(\square\)
