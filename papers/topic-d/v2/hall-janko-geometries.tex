\begin{abstract}
We recover the fixed principal census of Leemans for the Hall--Janko group:
eight classes in rank two, nineteen in rank three and three in rank four,
with none in any higher rank. The common recognition and augmentation
theorems account for arbitrary chamber stabilizers, including the nontrivial
one occurring in rank four. Complete exact programs enumerate, fuse and
independently verify the returned geometries, conditional on the stated
subgroup catalogue and finite-group algorithms.
\end{abstract}
The source is D.~Leemans, \emph{The residually weakly primitive geometries
of $J_2$}, Note di Matematica \textbf{21} (2002), 77--81. We use its five-page
journal article. The fixed target is the complete census under its stated
axioms and equivalence, not every description of the classified objects.

A target geometry is firm, flag transitive and residually connected. It is
residually weakly primitive (RWPRI): in each residue some type action of
its flag stabilizer is primitive. Its panel actions are two-transitive,
the property denoted $(2T)_1$, and its rank-two residues are partial linear
spaces or generalized digons, denoted $(\mathrm{IP})_2$. The latter is a
geometric condition, distinct from subgroup intersection identities.
The equivalence permits all automorphisms of $J_2$ and all permutations of
the types. It concerns the specified group action; no identification of the
full automorphism group of every incidence structure is inferred from it.

The complete theorem UD.PanelRecognition translates these axioms into the
actual panel subgroups and their coset actions. Theorem UD.MaximalAugmentation
then reduces classification to stabilizer actions on extension fibers and
proves termination by strict subgroup chains. These common proofs are paid
in full in this topic. The following application supplies their finite data
and verifies every local condition.

\section{Inputs, equivalence and complete finite evidence}
The search uses the complete subgroup-class and maximal-inclusion data for
$J_2$ supplied by GAP's TomLib. It does not use a list of known geometries.
For every subgroup $B$, conjugate inclusions are reconstructed by
UD.Transport. The action of $N_{J_2}(B)$ on the actual panel subgroups may
have a kernel; replacing it by its permutation image preserves the orbits
because every image element has an acting lift. All incidence and subgroup
tests are still performed on the original subgroups. No faithfulness of
this auxiliary action is assumed.

For a final geometry, the action of $J_2$ itself is faithful. Its kernel is
a normal subgroup contained in every proper type stabilizer. Simplicity
therefore makes this kernel trivial. This fact is separate from the possible
kernel of a normalizer action used to accelerate the enumeration.

The full programs are the search, the fusion under $\Aut(J_2)$ and type
permutations, the final verification, and the shared geometry tests. Each
of the 146 Borel anchors must have a completed checkpoint whose next level
is empty. Intermediate geometries for proper acting subgroups are retained
as required by the induction; stopping after finding the desired rank-four
objects would not prove completeness. The final fusion uses transporters
between actual subgroups, not a comparison of their orders alone.

The replay driver checks pinned input bytes, creates a fresh working tree,
checks every anchor and empty terminal level, and checks all thirty final
witnesses. The historical certificate-builder script is not invoked. Exact
GAP algorithms and completeness of the named subgroup data remain retained
inputs. The full programs, their outputs and those assumptions form the
computational proof; the table of totals is only its summary.

\section{The exact source boundary}
The source's main article, on printed pages 79--80, also displays rank-four
diagrams, local annotations, full geometric automorphism and correlation
groups, and a doubling relation between examples. These assertions are not
all recovered here. They are printed-body omissions, not merely missing
annotations in an external supplement. They remain excluded from the fixed
principal census contract. No new historical classification or additional
source credit is claimed.
