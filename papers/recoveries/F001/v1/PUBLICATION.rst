The rank spectrum of the projective symplectic group in dimension four
======================================================================

This recovery proves the complete principal theorem in Peter A. Brooksbank,
*On the ranks of string C-group representations for symplectic and orthogonal
groups*, arXiv:1912.05512v1 (11 December 2019), Theorem 1.1. The group PSp4(q)
has string C-group ranks 3, 4 and 5 for every prime power except q=3, when it
has none.

Begin with the `principal assembly <rank-spectrum/principal.pdf>`_. The
`odd existence proof <odd-existence/odd_endpoints.pdf>`_ gives uniform
parameter control and exact finite boundary certificates. Its complete
extension search excludes every rank for q=3. The characteristic-two
construction, the q=2 boundary and rank contraction use complete acquired
proofs whose existing public copies are listed in the portable inventory.

The `mixed involution proof <mixed-involutions/mixed_bound.pdf>`_ gives the
odd upper bound for both involution types. It analyzes the endpoint
centralizers and uses a normal reflection subgroup within the unchanged
proper end subgroup. Field descent, the even sign module and an exact
deleted symmetric-group calculation finish the alternatives. The
`negative reflection bound <negative-reflections/reflection_bound.pdf>`_
is a complete lower dependency of this argument.

A `separate correction <auxiliary-correction/orthogonal_rank_bound.pdf>`_
refutes the source's stronger auxiliary Lemma 4.1 for arbitrary orthogonal
subgroups. A faithful deleted permutation representation gives S7 rank six
in O5(7) and SO5(7). The recovered principal theorem concerns the full named
classical groups and does not use this false auxiliary bound. This is a
principal recovery with an auxiliary correction, not a refutation of the
principal theorem.

and its new components, two for the separate correction. The complete
