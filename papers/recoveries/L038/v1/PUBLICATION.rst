Every rank in the alternating group interval
===========================================

This recovery proves that A_n has a string C-group representation of every
rank from 3 to floor((n-1)/2), for every n >= 12. It recovers Theorem 1.1
of Fernandes and Leemans, arXiv:1810.12450v2 (4 January 2019).

The complete proof consists of three manuscripts:

* `Finite end groups for alternating group constructions <finite-ends/finite_ends.pdf>`_ (7 pages).
* `Signed permutation groups at the ends of alternating group constructions <signed-ends/signed_ends.pdf>`_ (7 pages).
* `Every rank in the alternating group interval <rank-interval/principal.pdf>`_ (3 pages).

The finite end calculations and signed permutation groups provide the
constructions. The acquired rank-contraction theorem then fills the intervals
and replaces several separate lower-rank constructions. Three earlier maximal
constructions remain explicitly attributed theorem inputs.

All degree and rank endpoints are covered. Some auxiliary exact types differ
from those in the source; the source routes identify each such case. The
upper bound on the largest possible rank is an earlier result and is not
newly proved here.

The two degree-twelve witnesses were selected from the existing L071 records.
Both are checked directly for generation and every subset intersection.
Completeness of that earlier census is provenance, not a premise needed
for these two existence assertions.

The public evidence retains the complete acquired papers, exact finite inputs,
independent reviews and reproducible checks. The 3,200 finite subset-pair checks
support the specified bases and witnesses; the infinite families are proved
by the written arguments. No complete Lean proof, open-conjecture result or
numerical compression factor is claimed.

The publication gate and original register record the final reviewed principal recovery.
