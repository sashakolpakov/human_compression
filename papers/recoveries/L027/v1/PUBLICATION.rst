The principal results
---------------------

including its exact type, full end stabilizers, self-duality and chirality.

oriented classes and their unbased interpretation, and the corrected
quadratic-field regular analogue. It also proves the uniform rank bound:
neither PSL3(q) nor PSU3(q) has a chiral polytope of rank greater than five,
including the nonsimple q=2 unitary boundary.

The subgroup classifications, nine Table 1 parameter rows and remaining table

have types [3,7,3], [3,8,3] and [3,9,3]. Their complete generation and indexed
intersection certificates are retained, and their full automorphism
