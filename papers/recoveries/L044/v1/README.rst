Highest ranks for the alternating groups
========================================

This recovery proves the exact principal theorem of Cameron, Fernandes,
Leemans and Mixer, *Highest rank of a polytope for A_n*, in the fixed
46-page arXiv:1605.09173v1 edition of 30 May 2016. The largest rank is
zero for n=3,4,6,7,8; three for n=5; four for n=9; five for n=10; six
for n=11; and floor((n-1)/2) for every n>=12. Zero means no generating
string C-marking exists. The historical theorem remains attributed to
its source authors.

Begin with the `complete principal proof <principal/principal.pdf>`_.
It joins the exact small-degree maxima and independently acquired lower
constructions to a strong induction. Proper primitive omissions use the
accepted permutation-group reduction. The imprimitive cases use actual
centralizers, binary coordinates and the complete omission arguments.
The intransitive cases use eligible edges, orbit and support bounds,
and the invariant pair partition in the equality case. Each smaller-degree
invocation is explicit; a quotient action is not given IP without proof.
