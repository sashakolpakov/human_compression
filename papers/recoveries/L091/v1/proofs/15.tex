\paragraph{A smaller intrinsic structure.}
Every listed family has exactly one type $a$ of cardinality $n=352$.
It is therefore fixed setwise by every correlation. Let $b$ be its base
object, $P_a=G_b$, and let $O_k$ be the $P_a$-orbits on that type,
of sizes $d_k$. For each type $j$, the point shadow of its base object
is $S_j=b^{P_j}$. If $e_{jk}$ counts the ordered distinct pairs in
$S_j$ belonging to pair orbit $k$, then the number of type-$j$ objects
incident with both entries of such a pair is
\[
                 m_{jk}=\frac{[G:P_j]e_{jk}}{352d_k}.
\]
To prove this, count pairs consisting of an object and an ordered pair
in its shadow. The total is $[G:P_j]e_{jk}$; the pair orbit has
$352d_k$ elements. Transitivity makes the fibre size constant.
Objects with equal shadows are still counted with their proper
multiplicity. For $j=a$, equality is the within-type shadow convention,
so all off-diagonal counts vanish.

The sorted multiset of $([G:P_j],m_{jk})$ over all types is invariant
under every correlation. Its distinct values therefore define intrinsic
relations on ordered distinct points. In all six literal models the
point-stabilizer subdegrees are $1,175,175,1$; the non-diagonal signatures
define two relations of valencies $176$ and $175$. The unique valency
$175$ relation is checked to be symmetric. It gives a graph on just
$352$ vertices. This construction never requires a transversal or
permutation action on any of the larger object types.

\paragraph{Literal coordinates and faithfulness.}

A point shadow determines a type-$j$ object exactly when its setwise
stabilizer in $E$ is the image of $P_j$. All types in families

type initially has shadow stabilizer of order $1440$, whereas its

Adjoin the set of encodings of its type-$3$ neighbours. This is the

The saved certificate also records the preceding redundant refinement

Thus any typed automorphism fixing the $352$ objects pointwise fixes

base encoding has stabilizer exactly its parabolic, and induction

\paragraph{Characteristic group and full correlations.}
A complete nauty calculation gives graph-group order $88\,704\,000$
in every case. Every returned generator is checked on every adjacency
row. For their literal permutation group $D$, GAP checks
\[
             |D|=88\,704\,000,\qquad E\leq D,\qquad D'=E.
\]
In particular $E$ is characteristic in the full graph group.
If $c$ is any correlation and $g\in G$, the action of $cgc^{-1}$ on
the intrinsic point type therefore agrees with some $h\in G$.
The typed automorphism $h^{-1}cgc^{-1}$ fixes that type pointwise,
so the preceding faithfulness argument makes it trivial. Hence
all full correlations normalize the specified $G$.

The acquired chamber-stabilizer argument now gives
\[
 |\Aut(\Gamma)|=|G:B||S|,\qquad
 |\Cor(\Gamma)|=|G:B||T|.
\]
Complete successive subgroup transporters test every possible type
permutation. Every positive witness is checked on the entire actual
parabolic family; impossible order or intersection-order patterns are
only rejection criteria. The resulting $S,T$ are those in the table,
with $S\cap G=B$. Therefore every typed automorphism lies in $G$.
The centralizer of $G$ in the full correlation group is the checked
quotient $(T\cap G)/B$. It is trivial for $21,39,54$, and has order
two for $22,37,56$. In the latter cases it is central, intersects the
centreless $G$ trivially, and together with $G$ has the full correlation
order. This proves the direct products in the theorem.
