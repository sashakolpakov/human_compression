Rank augmentation from a supported transposition
================================================

The `common paper <packet/augmentation.pdf>`_ proves the valid small-orbit
cases by controlling supported permutations, block systems and parity. Its
exact intersection test replaces the invalid six-point case. The
`source application <packet/source_application.pdf>`_ recovers the corrected
principal contract of De Saedeleer--Leemans--Mulpas,
*A rank augmentation theorem for rank 3 string C-group representations of
the symmetric groups*, arXiv:2212.12563v1 (23 December 2022), register L020.

The application includes the entire `eighteen-point counterproof
<../../../corrections/rank-augmentation/v1/written/case_three.pdf>`_. Its
three pages remain fully charged even though they were published earlier.
All three whole programs appear in the `current appendix
<programs/programs.pdf>`_; the earlier two-page appendix inside the frozen
scientific packet is superseded for measurement, not added to it.

The whole written factor is 1.200143; the conditional application factor is
2.383939 after acquiring the entire common paper, all declared programs and
retained premises. The whole computational comparisons are 0.864612 with
printed code and 0.884362 with raw code: both are expansions. The `reader
<../../../audit/topic-rank-augmentation/v1/measurement/packet/reader.pdf>`_
shows every cost, all companion papers and the dependency graph.

The first two cases of source Theorem 4.1 are proved. Its third case is
replaced by a counterexample and an exact criterion. The three literal
source examples are checked; the introductory Table 1 census is retained.
The larger-orbit and higher-rank questions are not claimed solved. Two
generic Lean lemmas and thirteen previously published finite-obstruction
lemmas verify specified parts, not the complete source or geometric theory.

Exact frozen files keep their author-stage labels. The separate `admission
<../../../audit/topic-rank-augmentation/v1/admission.json>`_ records current
scientific, appendix, input and numerical acceptance. Source PDFs and text
remain private; numerical replay is self-contained.
