\section{Intersection, flags and universality}
\begin{lemma}\label{lem:helly}
For three subgroups $H,K,L$ of a group, all pairwise intersecting
triples of their right cosets have a common point if and only if
\[
 H\cap KL=(H\cap K)(H\cap L).                          \tag{F.2}
\]
If four subgroup types satisfy this for every triple, and the three
intersection subgroups inside any one type also satisfy it, every
pairwise intersecting four-coset family has a common point.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Translate an intersecting pair of cosets to $H,K$. Choose
$h\in H\cap Lx$ and $k\in K\cap Lx$. Then $hk^{-1}\in L$, so
$h\in H\cap LK$. Taking inverses in (F.2) gives
$h=l_0k_0$ with $l_0\in H\cap L$ and $k_0\in H\cap K$.
Now $k_0=l_0^{-1}h\in Lx$ is a common point.
Conversely the common-point property applied to these
normalized triples gives the factorization. For four cosets, intersect
each of the other three with a fixed fourth. Global triple intersection
makes these three cosets inside the fourth pairwise intersecting.
The local three-coset property supplies their common point.
\end{proof}

For each of the two actual finite groups, both required checks enumerate
all 16 generated subgroups $G_J$, including $G_\varnothing=1$, and
test all 256 equations
\[
 G_J\cap G_K=G_{J\cap K}.                              \tag{F.3}
\]
They also test (F.2) for all four triples of maximal parabolics, and for
the three subgroups $G_i\cap G_j$ inside each $G_i$ (four more tests).
All equalities hold as full sets, not merely as cardinalities.
Lemma~\ref{lem:helly} therefore proves the coset geometry flag-transitive
in every rank up to four.

The markers are nontrivial involutions. Equation (F.3) and the full typed
coset Helly property therefore satisfy UA.Geometry of the common paper.
The coset system is thin, residually connected and flag-transitive, and
the displayed group is its full type-preserving automorphism group.

The modified presentation $V_t$ comprises precisely the circuit
relations and the four displayed ordered rank-three period relations.
By the marked isomorphism, $P_t$ is therefore also the
universal marked group with those local presentations. In particular
an alternative compatible marked regular geometry is a quotient;
the local maps are isomorphisms when its parabolics have those exact
presentations. Neither literal replacement changes this group.
The distinction between ordered local constraints and an unordered
list of toroidal maps is retained throughout; we do not claim to
classify every permutation of the local data.
