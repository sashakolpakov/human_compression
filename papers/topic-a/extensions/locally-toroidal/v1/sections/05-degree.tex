\section{A smaller faithful permutation action}
After the drawings, \cite[p.~6]{source} suggests that $4s$ is ``most probably''
minimal. This is not a proved assertion. In that generality it fails:
$3D\cap5D=15D$ gives an injection
$E/15D\longrightarrow E/3D\times E/5D$. The disjoint union of the two
faithful actions has degree $12+20=32<60$. This is an upper bound, not
a classification of minimum degrees. We make no claim of historical
priority or of resolving a question that is currently open.
