\section{The literal even-exponent construction}
Keep even $n\geq4$, now with $e\geq2$, and write $A=O/\pi^nO$.
Replace its additive module by
\[
M=\{(v,j)\in A\times\mathbb Z/p^2\mathbb Z:
                 v\bmod\pi=j\bmod p\}.
\]
Set $s=(1,1)$, $z=(p^{e-1}\pi^{r-1},0)$ and
$T(v,j)=(\zeta v,j)$, $S(v,j)=(\bar v,j)$.
Then $|M|=p^{n+1}$, $|s|=p^e$ and all the involution and carry
conditions of the cyclic-carry theorem of~\cite{UF} hold. The subgroup generated
by the $T$-orbit projects onto $A$. It contains
\[
\sum_{i=0}^{p-1}T^is=(0,p),
\]
which generates the projection kernel. It is therefore all of $M$.
Here $w=(p^{e-1}\pi^{r-1},p)$ has order $p$. Projection to $A$
shows $\gen w\cap\gen s=0$, since the nonzero first coordinate
of $w$ is in coordinate $r\geq2$. Thus the group has order
$2p^{n+2}$, type $\{p^2,2p^e\}$ and is chiral and non-tight.
These are exactly all the source's even exponents $m\geq p+3$:
$n=m-2\geq p+1$ is even, so $e\geq2$.

Set $s_1=s$, $s_i=(\pi^{i-1},0)$ for $i\geq2$, and
$x=(0,-p)$. The cyclic norm identity gives exactly the source relation
\[
x=-\sum_{i=1}^{p-1}\binom pi s_i-[s_{p-1},\beta].
\]
It also gives $x$ central of exact order $p$; $Sx=x$.
The source quotient by $\gen x$ is the preceding odd-exponent
presentation, so its order is at most $p\cdot2p^{n+1}$.
The displayed model has this order and satisfies every source
relation, proving equality and the exact presentation bridge.

There is a minor correction to the printed lower-bound argument.
The proposed map in Section 6, Claim 4 to
$C_{p^2}\times C_p$ sends $\sigma$ to $1$ and $\beta$ to a
nonidentity element of order $p$, so violates
$\beta^\sigma=\beta^{-1}$. The map to $C_{p^2}$ sending
$s_1$ to a generator and $\beta,\sigma$ to $1$ does respect the
relations and sends $x$ to a nonzero $p$th power. This suffices
for that lower bound; the full model above proves it directly.
