\begin{abstract}
We recover the three main theorems of Kong, Feng, Hou, Leemans and Qu
for groups of order $2p^m$: the structural and tightness characterization,
tightness at $m=3,4$, and the complete stated non-tight constructions.
The common cyclotomic lattice supplies exact presentations for every
parameter. An elementary small-kernel argument includes the prime
three without a classification table. We also audit the regular
byproducts: three asserted families exist for every odd prime, while
the fourth exists exactly for the prime three. The previously proved
cyclic lift is included with its separate attribution and scope.
\end{abstract}

\section{Exact source scope and retained inputs}
The source edition is the complete 22-page accepted manuscript of
\cite{KFH}, accepted 19 January 2026. The structural theorem recovers
its Theorem 1.1, the uniform reversal theorem recovers Theorem 1.2,
and the odd/even constructions recover both cases of Theorem 1.4.
The cyclic-carry presentation comparison includes the precise
Section 3 generators, their mixed orders, conjugation formulas and
the full Section 6 central extension. The maximal-class assertion
follows directly: in the odd family the lower-central terms from
the second onward are $\pi A,\pi^2A,\ldots$, so the class is $n$
for a group of order $p^{n+1}$.

The four clauses of Remark 1.3 are all decided with explicit models
or an obstruction. Its fourth clause requires the prime-three
qualification proved above. The printed Section 6 map to
$C_{p^2}\times C_p$ is repaired by the explicit module model and
the valid cyclic quotient. In the small-order proof we impose every
weight-four relation and use a nonzero dependence of the two
central triple commutators; we do not rely on the source's
asymmetric presentation-order shortcut. None of these qualifications
is reported as a new conjecture solution.

The only source-specific classical classification inputs retained in
this application are the two odd-primary subgroup facts stated at
the point of use. Their original books are not retained as complete
local PDFs, and their external proof costs remain unknown.
The first application retains its separate Cunningham--Pellicer
input unchanged. The source's small-order group classification,
Conder list and maximal-class existence theorem are not premises
of the proofs here. Introductory census percentages, survey history
and tight examples cited from Cunningham are not regenerated.

All finite programs are validation of the uniform written proofs.
Their complete files, including the small-group exploratory scan
when retained, must be inventoried; a successful finite scan is not
substituted for a quantified theorem. The generic formal statements
are scoped in the common paper. There is no full Lean proof of any
source main theorem, no new source admission in this private draft,
and no numerical compression claim.
