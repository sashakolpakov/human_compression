unchanged L014 validation programs. Two new model programs test the
carry construction and corrected regular families; two complete
exploratory scans are retained as diagnostics. The last two are the
previously written central-lift checks. None is a finite premise of
the uniform written proofs. The operational drivers and generic Lean
files are inventoried separately. In particular an exhaustive bounded
SmallGroups scan is not a classification proof for all odd primes.
