\title{Chirality kernels and the smallest regular cover}
\author{Human Compression project}
\begin{document}\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
We recover all proved conclusions of Cunningham's eight-page author manuscript of 25 July 2023. An intersection lemma reduces polytopality of the mirror mix to the intersection of two embedded chirality groups. We verify the local mirror identifications and the regular-end induction before making that reduction. The applications include chiral square torus maps, all five specified atomic tight families, and an exact finite computation producing two distinct minimal regular polytope covers. The older classification needed for the five families is retained explicitly. Later results about the source's questions are recorded separately and are not substitutes for the source-specific proofs.
\end{abstract}
