\begin{abstract}
We give complete applications of the common coset-intersection theory to the constructions in Piedade--Tranchida, arXiv:2505.24662v1. The free-product and coherent-amalgam theorems include their converses, residue behavior and exact panel indices. Indexed HNN extensions recover the firm and residually connected branches; an explicit nonfirm input disproves the unrestricted geometric conclusion. The semidirect and Artin constructions have exact presentation and parabolic adapters. A full-space reflection argument separates Shephard generators, and the affine example is treated through its actual quotient intersections, including its exceptional input parameter. Concrete examples and the limit of graph iteration are proved in the same conventions.
\end{abstract}
Throughout, ``the source'' means the exact 60-page arXiv v1 specified above. We use left cosets, finite type sets, and a specified action on indexed types. A maximal parabolic means the subgroup assigned to one type; it need not be a maximal proper subgroup. Faithfulness, firmness, residual connectedness and flag transitivity are separate properties. The common paper \emph{Coset intersections under group operations} is a complete declared proof dependency, not an unpaid reference to a proof bank.

The target results and examples remain attributed to Claudio Alexandre Piedade and Philippe Tranchida. Literal false or incomplete statements are distinguished from the valid corrected conclusions. All 81 named source records have a separate exact disposition and migration map; that inventory does not claim to reprove every stronger unused auxiliary assertion. In particular the broad fundamental-geometry and categorical questions remain open within this edition.

\section{Proof boundary and reproducibility}
All arguments used above are present either here or in the complete common paper. The additional retained inputs are the faithful affine Coxeter realization and the classical geometric reflection interpretation of the triangle examples. For the affine Weyl semidirect description an exact reference is P. Etingof and X. Ma, \emph{Lecture notes on Cherednik algebras}, Section 7.12, page 58; faithfulness of the standard Coxeter presentation remains the classical affine reflection theorem. The local lattice and quotient computations are proved above.

The complete integer program \texttt{affine\_example.py} is a proof premise for precisely $s=2,3,4$, checking all 675 parabolic intersections and 10,125 product identities in those cases. The uniform argument for $s\geq5$ is written. The complete programs \texttt{presentation\_identities.py} and \texttt{literal\_twist.py} are validation: the presentation inverse maps and the all-$s$ twist theorem have independent written proofs. Their full files, with this distinct role, form the program appendix; no search trace is used to replace a proof. The operational checkers and existing partial Lean evidence are inventoried separately. No new formalization is claimed.

The general existence of a fundamental geometry independent of all choices, and categorical universal-property or naturality statements, are not proved here. The two-loop obstruction concerns a specified sequential recipe. Several nontrivial type orbits are already covered by the later general common orbit theorem and receive no new question credit. Unneeded RC1-only auxiliary claims outside the stated flag-transitive hypotheses are not silently certified. The integrated edition awaits a review of its migration and presentation, separate from the accepted complete precursor proofs.
