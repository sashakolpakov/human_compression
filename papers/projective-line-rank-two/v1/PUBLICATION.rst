Rank-two geometries over a projective line
==========================================

Read proof/classification.pdf or its complete six-page TeX source. This is
a reviewed recovery of the principal classification in Julie De Saedeleer
and Dimitri Leemans, On the rank two geometries of the groups PSL(2,q):
part I, Ars Mathematica Contemporanea 3 (2010), 177--192. It classifies
the locally doubly transitive, residually weakly primitive rank-two
systems with a Borel or full torus normalizer as one parabolic, for q>=4.
It does not classify systems with an arbitrary first subgroup. There are
no adjacent TeX inputs and no unpublished mathematical appendix.

The proof and the corrected source convention
--------------------------------------------

The proof reduces local double transitivity to exact subgroup double
cosets. Primitive abelian normal images give two uniform even-field
families and leave fourteen exceptional fields. The complete finite
certificate checks actual intersections and all second subgroups in
those fields. It determines existence, exhaustion, conjugacy and full
automorphism counts, graph invariants, residual primitivity and the
rank-two intersection property. The exceptional E16 count in PSL2(64)
is proved by scalar subspaces.

The source's definition uses unordered parabolic sets. Its two ordered
D12/D12 classes at each of q=11 and q=13 become one unordered class.
The proof retains both counts explicitly and exhibits actual type-swap
transporters. It also corrects the split index repeated on printed page
188, the missing denominator two for the odd nonsplit normalizer in
Table 3, the q=17 D16 vertex count 153 rather than 136, and the
unqualified prime-reflection local-action assertion. The small q=2,3
boundaries are separated from the simple-group hypothesis.

The original PDF identity, source URL, exact principal routes and every
qualification are in scope_routes.json and frozen_source_audit.json.
Dickson subgroup alternatives, maximality and natural subfield clauses,
standard group orders and the projective action remain named classical
inputs. The original principal geometry tables are not premises. Unused
numerical subgroup multiplicities and other Table 4 actions are not
newly certified. The higher-rank strong-Boolean assertion is not imported.
Named Petersen, Desargues and Perkel examples remain attributed context.
This is a principal-result recovery, not a claim to reprove every source
sentence or a broader current-status search.
