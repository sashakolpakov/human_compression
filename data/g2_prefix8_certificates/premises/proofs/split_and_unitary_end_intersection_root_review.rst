The further exclusion of an end equal to SL3(11) is sound. Two distinct
commuting half-turns have a common eigenbasis consisting of their two
distinct positive lines and the remaining joint negative line. An
order 19 element on the natural three-space is irreducible, because
the multiplicative order of 11 modulo 19 is three. For the third
half-turn, a zero coordinate of its positive row vector would preserve
a coordinate hyperplane, and a zero coordinate of its projection
column would preserve a coordinate line. Both contradict this
irreducibility. The resulting nonsingular diagonal Gram matrix makes
all three involutions orthogonal. Its determinant-one isometries have
eigenvalue one in dimension three, contradicting the order 19 element.
Thus the end is the whole specified normalizer.
