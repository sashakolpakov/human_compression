Independent review of the order19 and order4 criterion
=====================================================

The stated implication is correct under its natural Cayley group premise.
This is a written review of a classical corollary. No matrix, word, field,
model, computational certificate or current candidate was evaluated.

The maximal subgroup input is the full list in the proof of Nguyen's
Theorem4.4, before any restriction on representation degrees is used:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0910.4994v1, printed pp10--11. Root read that list
directly. At11, its order19 alternatives are precisely the linear
normalizer and J1. The prime-field hypothesis removes proper subfields;
the other listed types have orders prime to19. Retaining the possible
L2 exceptional envelopes does not change this divisibility conclusion.

The line argument has an essential geometric hypothesis. In the retained
Wilson text, Section4.2.6 explicitly makes the full linear normalizer a
stabilizer of a pure line, with its outer half allowed to negate a chosen
vector. Root read this passage. Thus a moved line excludes this entire
normalizer, whereas a generator which merely negates a fixed vector does
not. Conjugating an embedding carries its stabilized line with it; no
uniqueness of a maximal subgroup conjugacy class is used.

Here is an independent check of the uniqueness step. Since the order19
operator is semisimple in characteristic11, all its nontrivial simple
constituents have degree3. Inversion is not a Frobenius power on primitive
19th roots, because the subgroup generated by11 modulo19 has odd order3.
Every such constituent therefore differs from its dual. A nondegenerate
invariant symmetric bilinear form pairs these constituents with equal
multiplicity. A nontrivial part must consequently have dimension at least6
and a multiple of6. Dimension7 leaves a single trivial constituent, whose
form is nondegenerate. Every invariant rational line is fixed pointwise,
since its scalar has order dividing both19 and10. The supplied fixed
vector therefore spans the unique invariant rational line. A nonzero
moving minor contradicts preservation of the normalizer's line.

The exceptional alternative is excluded by exact order4, rather than by
a claim that every moved19 line already generates the full group. Root
checked Lemma3.9(2) and the definition of maximal element orders in
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.07907v1. Root also read the order and subgroup
entries on https://brauer.maths.qmul.ac.uk/Atlas/v3/spor/J1/. The elementary
Sylow2 argument and the listed spectrum independently imply that J1 has
no element of order4. The equations X^4=I and X^2!=I give exactly the
required obstruction. Both words and the moving generator must belong
to the same actual end subgroup.

Neither ingredient promises a short witness. A missed search remains
unpaid. The natural ambient group, its faithful coordinate transport,
and word membership remain separate certificate duties. To reject a
string marking after proving that an end equals the whole group, its
missing generator must also be joined and nontrivial: the intersection
property with that singleton would otherwise force the missing generator
to be trivial. This step does not supply an original-prefix census.
