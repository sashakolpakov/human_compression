Human Compression
=================

This project reconstructs the principal results of a fixed mathematical corpus
through complete common theories and applications. Each recovery records its
source contract, proofs, corrections, retained inputs and verification scope.
The length comparison measures those declared written representations; it does
not measure historical effort, originality or the shortest possible proof.

What the factors mean
---------------------

For one topic, the written factor divides the sum of its complete source
manuscripts by the entire common paper plus every complete application and
required written companion. The common theory is paid once within that topic.
Every other topic pays its own complete common theory even where arguments
overlap. No common paper or application is silently supplied for free.

An individual factor divides a source manuscript by its complete application.
It is conditional on acquiring the whole common theory, the declared required
programs and data, and any retained mathematical premises. Acquisition costs
and unknown costs remain explicit. A conditional saving can coexist with a
whole-topic expansion. Neither comparison is a ratio of theorem counts.

**Executable software is excluded symmetrically from both sides of the written
comparison.** This includes precisely identified embedded source listings;
mathematical algorithm and proof prose remain counted. Required computations
retain their proof roles and full-file inventories. Optional validation,
operational drivers and partial formal artifacts are distinguished. Historical
source software costs are unknown; there is no software-inclusive factor.

Each input policy fixes the edition, complete document boundary, extraction
rules, font repairs, normalization and unit before evaluation. The reported
text does not price unextracted graphics, layout, unavailable classical proofs
or historical software at zero. Corrected false source claims and unrecovered
ancillary material are stated in the source maps. These comparisons add no
conjecture-answer credit and do not assert complete Lean formalization.
The `current protocol <audit/protocol.pdf>`_ gives the definitions and limits.

Reading and reproduction
------------------------

Start with the topic guide and its source map; then read the complete common
paper and applications. Each scientific admission links the immutable author packet and distinct
review. Measured editions additionally link the input audit and numerical review. A frozen packet may retain
an earlier candidate label; the later admission records its accepted status.

Check the published archive, bindings and configured audit replays from the
repository root::

    python3 scripts/verify_snapshot.py

Compile selected papers directly from their declared TeX inputs, with the
compiler specified by the catalogue and a fresh output directory::

    python3 scripts/build_papers.py --paper audit/protocol --output-dir /tmp/human-compression-protocol

Repeat ``--paper`` for other exact catalogue IDs. The builder defaults to
XeLaTeX and uses pdfLaTeX where a document explicitly declares it. Source PDFs
are not redistributed; input recounts use separately supplied originals with
exact hashes. Topic readers give their portable replay commands and dependencies.

Formal statements have explicit scopes, hypotheses and axiom reports. The
`legacy formal runner <audit/formal-project/README.rst>`_ covers its named
components; other topic packets have separate runners. Integrity checks do not
imply a fresh compilation of every theorem or a rerun of every finite census.
