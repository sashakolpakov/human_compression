# Admission of infinitely many minimal covers in four families

10 October 2026. The complete
[surface manuscript](../research/case_study/phase211_surface_covers.md)
is admitted within the scope of its frozen
[independent review](../research/case_study/phase218_surface_cover_review.md).
The [reader](../INFINITE_MINIMAL_COVERS.md) states the result and attribution.

For every member of the four specified two-primary families, and every
odd prime ℓ, there is a finite minimal regular polytopal cover of degree
ℓᵉ over the monodromy group, with 1 ≤ e ≤ 34. This implies infinitely
many pairwise nonisomorphic minimal covers. The result holds for all
β ≥ 5 and every independent sign pair in the retained presentations.
The fifth, odd-prime family keeps its earlier two-cover theorem.

## Proof and review

The root read the complete manuscript, both author programs, the
independent native computation and review, and the definitions and
proofs of all six Lean declarations. The topology proves a uniform
theorem from explicit surface hypotheses. Both the projected
intersection and the common covering-kernel obstruction are treated.
The nonorientable case correctly requires every complementary
component to be nonorientable in odd characteristic.

The source bridge, orders, presentations and chirality are retained
from the earlier reviewed family work. The new surface proof supplies
every odd prime. Coprime transport does not assume that the extension
splits. Finite descent and the nontrivial prime-power degrees prove the
existence of infinitely many minimal descendants; no exact exponent or
explicit minimal quotient is claimed.

The independent review additionally reconstructs the Tomotope from the
primary automorphisms and all pairwise-incident flag tuples. Its
multiplicity was already proved by Monson–Pellicer–Williams, so this
application receives no newly solved-question credit. Exploratory
claims that three other groups have no polytopal flag action are
outside this admission.
