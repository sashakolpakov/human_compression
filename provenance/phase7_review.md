## Uniform Suzuki constructions

The root read the complete [descent proof](../research/case_study/phase7_suzuki_descent.md)
and [two-family extension](../research/case_study/phase7_suzuki_torus_families.md),
including the fixed-point interpretation, cyclic complement argument,
exceptional endomorphism convention, torus sign, maximality choices,
residual connectedness and thickness. A separate agent reviewed both proofs
and the primary construction statements, reran the two checkers in scratch
space and independently implemented the exhaustive binary-matrix audit.
No gap was found in these stated claims.

The companion argument includes selected primes that already divide the
base torus order. Its valuation argument, rather than an assumption that
all cyclic factors are coprime, gives the required subgroup intersections.
Only the quotient orders used for the simultaneous coordinate map need be
pairwise coprime. Coset coordinates are sets; the relevant parabolic
intersections need not be normal subgroups of the attached parabolic.

The root verified every distributed artifact, prior-input and formal-source
binding in [the intake record](../research/case_study/phase7_suzuki_intake.json).
This last operation checks bytes; the preceding written reviews and fresh
executions supply the mathematical evidence. The all-rank results retain
Lang–Steinberg and the specified classical Suzuki subgroup facts. They do
not classify every relative subgroup position or restore the previously
disproved exhaustive list.

## Formal and symbolic scope

`CosetCoordinates.lean` was freshly compiled with Lean 4.32.2 and the recorded
mathlib revision. Its prerequisite was rebuilt without overwriting that
prerequisite's historical report. The two theorems prove the abstract
equivalence between coordinate coverage and common-coset-point attachment.
They do not formalize Lang descent, finite fields or the Suzuki instantiation.
All thirteen saved Lean reports were checked against their source hashes;
this is not a claim to have freshly compiled all thirteen during this phase.

For the [PSL4 endpoint reductions](../research/case_study/phase7_psl4.md), the
root reviewed every multiplicity and nilpotent-block case and its earlier

requires the mixed-sign treatment in phase3 §6. A fresh root execution
reproduced all 30 exact symbolic checks; its
[separate report](../research/case_study/phase7_psl4_root_check.json) preserves

themselves certify the written module-theoretic case coverage.

The subsequent [zero-product proof](../research/case_study/phase7_psl4_zero_product.md)
was independently read in full by root and the separate reviewing agent.
Both found the rank-(1,1), rank-(1,2) and rank-(2,2) cases exhaustive.
The review checked that the semisimple splitting generators really form a
finite 2-group, so Maschke applies in every odd characteristic. A separate
integer Smith-form audit found only nonzero factors 1 and 2 in the
centralizer systems. Root freshly reproduced the 54 final symbolic checks
and exported a [separate run report](../research/case_study/phase7_psl4_zero_product_root_check.json).
The mathematical conclusion is no rank-five representation for any odd q;
rank six and the full maximum-rank conjecture remain unresolved here.
