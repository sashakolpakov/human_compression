# Mandatory correction of a quoted diagonal decomposition

The first Module A and several older compilations repeated the incorrect
dihedral-factor pairing printed in N009, Lemma 5.1(ii). Its purported
factor <b,b^a> has order four, independently of the parameter. The
[mandatory correction](../research/case_study/phase138_N009_decomposition_erratum.md)
gives the correct pairing and proves its full affine semidirect-product
decomposition for every positive parameter, including one.

Root independently rendered and viewed the exact primary page, checked
the whole written proof, and wrote a separate seven-case affine regression.
The [review](../research/case_study/phase143_n009_decomposition_review.md)
identifies the affected frozen bodies and explains why the center proofs,
complete finite census and principal classification targets are unchanged.
This corrects an error missed by the earlier review; it does not add
another classification result or credit the source authors' classification
as new work.

The [five-file binding record](../research/case_study/phase143_n009_export.json)
preserves the author correction, independent review, new finite checker
and fresh report. Primary text and the rendered source page are excluded.
The current first-edition U+A package costs 196,962 raw bytes after adding
the full 5,967-byte correction. The six-source additive package costs
214,717 bytes. Historical length factors measure the older artifacts;
no matched ratio is asserted for the corrected package.

The correction will be incorporated in the new A edition. All original
bytes, source counts, finite classification outputs and existing formal
certificates remain preserved.
