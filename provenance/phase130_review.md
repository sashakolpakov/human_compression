## Mathematical scope

The paper identifies the universal completion kernel by the fundamental
group of the finite end-coset complex. It identifies actual lift
discrepancies and proves the exact finite abelian criterion and its
elementary-abelian specialization. The prime-group obstruction applies
also to nonabelian p-kernels. Rational survival gives a sufficient
condition for infinitely many finite minimal covers; it is not asserted
for every polytope monodromy.

The Tomotope's integral homology is C₂ × C₂. Independent numbering and a
different spanning tree reproduce that answer and its nonzero discrepancy.
The actual voltage group has order 73,728, preserves end orders 576 and 1,152,
and satisfies all 256 subgroup intersection equalities. Independent
conjugation on its kernel gives exactly one invariant line, outside the
discrepancy, producing its unique smaller C-quotient of order 36,864.
These are all covers with abelian kernel preserving both ends. Vanishing
modulo every odd prime excludes all nontrivial odd-primary kernels under
the same local condition.

The order 36,864 cover is attributed to Monson–Pellicer–Williams,
Proposition 4.5, with identification through its marked map and end orders.
Prior order 73,728 constructions and the source's infinite multiplicity
theorem retain their attribution; equality of group orders alone is not
used to identify a cover. Bass–Serre theory, cellular homology and the
recursive string C-group criterion remain explicit classical inputs.
