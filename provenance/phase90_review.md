# Descriptive manuscript titles and a separate question-results catalogue

10 October 2026. The active manuscripts are named **Umbrella theory U:
Marked groups, kernels and intersections** and **Module A: Construction
and classification of two-group polytopes**. The overview, programme,
manuscript map and current report use these titles. Subsequent modules
will be named once their mathematical scope is established.

The new [question-results index](../CONJECTURE_RESULTS.md) keeps solutions
and qualified answers separate from compression of published results.
It summarizes ten existing entries, not ten new or newly audited proofs.
Eight record answers under the exact stated scope; two require a family
or interpretation qualification. Current-openness and priority checks
remain incomplete. No source-level geometric answer has a complete Lean
formalization, although the exact presented-group infinitude result in
the toroidal entry is fully formalized.

The [frozen catalogue manifest](../research/case_study/phase124_question_results_manifest.json)
has SHA-256
`da0e18f62dec165975a1d4831c79fbea876d8c5cabe8d8becd718f774e7fe04d`.
Its three exports are copied byte for byte. All 56 retained private
evidence bindings were checked. Of the corresponding existing public
files, 51 are byte-identical. Four have navigation or temporary-path
substitutions with unchanged mathematics; the fifth is the living formal
README with later certificate documentation. The
[public binding record](../research/case_study/phase125_question_catalogue_public_bindings.json)
records both hashes and every difference. The frozen private hashes are
not represented as hashes of those five public variants.

This publication adds no mathematical theorem, Lean certificate, priority
clearance or compression factor. It makes the existing evidence and its
limitations easier to locate. U and A remain in preparation and review;
the old combined manuscripts remain source material for reworking.
