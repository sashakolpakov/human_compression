## Consequences of the dimension-four form theorem

Root reviewed the complete [linear/unitary application](../research/case_study/phase9_linear_unitary.md).
An independent agent checked the same proof, with particular attention to
q=3, all degree-two weight coincidences, absolute irreducibility in
characteristic three, projective centralizers and the connected-string
reduction. Both found no gap. The inherited phase-eight normal-form theorem
and its proof dependencies remain explicit inputs to this phase.

Root freshly ran both distributed exact checkers. The first verifies 45
uniform weight witnesses, six degree-one pairs, 256 integer matrix-entry
checks, even-permutation facts and cyclic quotient tests. Its
[root report](../research/case_study/phase9_linear_unitary_root_check.json)
is separate from the author and independent reports. The second checker
reproduces the character-difference gcds: 36 equal one and nine equal two.
This proves separation for every torus exponent at least three, rather
than extrapolating from a finite sample of fields.

The rank-at-most-four statement covers odd-field linear intermediates
PSL4≤G≤PGL4 and PSU4≤G≤PGU4. Index-four extensions have no involution
generating set. The retained unitary existence theorem gives rank three
for odd q≥5; rank-four existence is undecided here. The source's broader
almost-simple-group problem, including field and graph extensions, remains
incomplete. Publication priority remains unverified.

## New formal certificate

Root wrote `TransvectionForms.lean`, checked it privately, and then freshly
compiled the distributed source using the portable runner with the pinned
Lean/mathlib environment. The public saved report is the latter execution.
The independent agent's [statement review](../research/case_study/phase9_transvection_forms_review.md)
binds the same source and the earlier private compilation, also preserved as
a [separate historical report](../research/case_study/phase9_transvection_forms_private_check.json).

The formal proof uses actual matrix products and transposes: exact congruence
invariance under the elementary transvections forces every entry to vanish
when a third coordinate is available. It applies over any ring and includes
the `Fin 4` instance. The printed dependencies are only `propext`,
`Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`, with no admitted lemmas. The
[scope note](../research/case_study/formal/TransvectionForms.scope.md)
identifies the multiplier reduction and group-theoretic steps that remain
outside Lean. The frozen application note predates this additional certificate
and retains its original review hashes.

The repository now has fifteen saved Lean certificates. Only the new module
was freshly compiled in this phase; saved-report consistency for the others
does not claim a new execution of them.
