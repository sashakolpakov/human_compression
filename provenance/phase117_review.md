# Admission of the general theorem on minimal regular covers

10 October 2026. The complete [kernel and covering proof](../research/case_study/phase216_general_covers.md) and [geometric filling proof](../research/case_study/phase216_geometric_filling.md) are admitted after the [independent review](../research/case_study/phase222_general_cover_review.md) and a fresh public formal replay. The [reader](../MINIMAL_REGULAR_COVERS.md) gives the result and its argument.

An abstract polytope of finite rank has a minimal regular polytopal cover. That cover is unique up to rank-preserving isomorphism if and only if the monodromy group, with its distinguished generators, is a string C-group. Neither flags nor covers are required to be finite. This answers Cunningham–Pellicer's Problem 5 in the inspected author manuscript dated 1 July 2018, and the corresponding expectation in Cunningham's 25 July 2023 manuscript, §4. The earlier filename `korbit2016v1.pdf` is not an edition claim: its title page dates the bound bytes to 2018.

## Mathematical review

The root read both complete proofs, the five new Lean declarations and their actual definitions from the frozen kernel-chain module. The review checked the use of actual polytope flag actions, reduction of relations to consecutive intervals, and the amalgam normal forms that restore the remaining ranks. The chain-union argument supplies maximal kernels above every admissible kernel, not merely the existence of one maximal element. Normality makes the group correspondence agree with unbased rank-preserving isomorphism and covering order.

The geometric argument was checked against the primary statements of Behrstock–Hagen–Sisto, Chen–Heuer, Caprace–Fujiwara, Bestvina–Fujiwara, Hull and Dahmani–Guirardel–Osin. The factor system uses all nontrivial standard parabolic cosets, with threshold one. Gate images and parallel classes are treated explicitly. The equivariant comparison transfers both hyperbolicity and acylindricity to the graph generated by the entire union of proper parabolics. Missing hyperplane labels and two applications of the contracting-interval estimate give a uniform projection bound.

Hull's result uses that same, possibly infinite generating set. The filling result preserves its entire union, including the identity, rather than just a preselected finite ball. The elementary subgroup normalizes every power subgroup because the centralizers of nonzero powers have the same primitive cyclic generator. Thus every sufficiently large exponent is available. Two consecutive exponents recover each normal generator; they are not restricted to multiples of a fixed integer. These points are essential to the final equality between the join of admissible kernels and the monodromy kernel.

## Formal execution

The frozen author packet was copied unchanged. A fresh public replay of `phase216_check.py` compiled the five new and six retained declarations and rejected all three altered hypotheses: removing disjointness, removing separation by coordinates, and replacing consecutive exponents by exponents differing by two. The fresh public [independent runner](../research/case_study/phase223_public_replay/review/report.json) also passed. It checks the fourteen author bindings before and after execution, repeats the formal checks, and reconstructs four independent finite countermodels to false weakenings. These countermodels do not test the geometric theorem. The source ledger records the exact separately acquired primary PDFs; public formal execution does not require redistributing them.

The new [CoverKernelGeneration.lean](../research/case_study/formal/CoverKernelGeneration.lean) proves the disjoint-coordinate argument, intersection preservation under injection on the union of proper parabolics, and three deductions from normal generation and the supply of consecutive powers. Normal generation and power supply remain explicit hypotheses. The written proof establishes them; no new Lean axiom pretends to formalize the geometry. All eleven reported declarations use only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.

## Attribution, boundaries and accounting

The substantial geometric machinery and standard group–polytope correspondence are borrowed and credited. The contribution here is their stated application to actual flag kernels, the graph comparison and projection argument, interval extension, and the resulting join theorem. Historical priority is unverified; bounded later-literature searches do not establish that this is the first proof.
