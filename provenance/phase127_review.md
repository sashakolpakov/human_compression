## Accepted scope and required corrections

presentation of Theorem 4.1 and Corollary 4.2, the complete existence
range of Theorem 1.3, all three affine families of Theorem 4.3 and
Corollary 4.4, and all three families of Theorem 5.1 and Corollary 5.2.

extension presentations and exceptional seed are included.

This excludes the general classification problem, the separate full
Table 1 census, ancillary seed uniqueness and historical survey.
It adds one distinct principal contract, not one credit per theorem or a
claim that every statement of the paper is replaced.

The first period equality in the first branch of source Lemma 4.5 has
its right-hand exponent corrected from $+2M$ to $-2M$. The literal signs
force an extra rotation relation. The $n=10,s=4$ literal presentation has
order 1536 and rotation order eight; the corrected presentation has
order 3072 and rotation order sixteen. The uniform corrected argument
proves the surviving main existence result. This correction is limited
to the bound arXiv edition; the journal formula was not collated.

transition formula before freezing. The final theory uses normalized

distinct marked generators and nondegenerate adjacent orders where the
full rank-three intersection test needs them. The independent
counterexamples and final proofs are retained, rather than silently
discarding the failed versions from the review history.
