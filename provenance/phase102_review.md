# The generated parity mix in Lean

The [parity-mix package](../PARITY_MIX.md) proves the full intersection
property of the actual group generated by paired markers, under the
specified two-primary, odd-adjacency, character and factor string/IP
hypotheses. It also proves intrinsic generation, nonidentity involutions
and string commutation. All-support profiles and overlap character
equations are derived within the proof.

The [independent reviewer](../research/case_study/phase157_parity_mix_review.md)
read all twelve new sources and their complete mathematical interfaces,
ran fresh and isolated builds, and passed 35 interface/refusal checks.
That reviewer authored the retained interval lemmas; their independent
root review is recorded in phase155. Root read the final consumer,
intersection argument, connected-fiber proof and source bindings, and
freshly compiled all eighteen local sources from the public tree.

The [root admission](../research/case_study/phase163_parity_mix_root_admission.md)
and [export record](../research/case_study/phase163_parity_mix_public_export.json)
bind the public evidence. There are now 63 scoped Lean modules: 62 without
native-evaluation axioms and one historical supplement retaining four
recorded evaluation axioms. The new package's 75 declaration reports use
only standard axioms. Module counts are not research-result counts.

No source coverage, conjecture-answer count, compression factor or
historical-priority claim changes. Numerical order formulas, specific
finite-field groups and their characters, and the complete polytope
application remain outside this formal package.
