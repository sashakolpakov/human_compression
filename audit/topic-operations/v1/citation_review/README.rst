Review of the operations citation editions
=========================================

The two exact proposed patches are accepted. The root reviewer independently
computed their complete textual differences and compared all 18 theorem/proof
environments byte for byte. Eleven common-theorem interfaces were reviewed,
including the finite-type restriction, retained Borel subgroup, quotient
faithfulness reduction, full orbital presentation and exact product orders.
The infinite joins and lamplighter arguments remain local to N006.

A fresh two-pass build passed all thirteen page-text comparisons. The four
pages containing the changed opening prose and references were visually
inspected by the root reviewer. The author separately viewed all thirteen.
The three underfull notices are declared and harmless; there are no missing
glyphs or overfull boxes. This review does not claim to rerun scientific GAP
or Lean proofs; the complete earlier scientific reviews remain bound.

The unchanged N006 sporadic companion papers remain required, as do their
complete programs and data. Acceptance of these two citation editions is not
acceptance of a measurement input list or any new compression factor.
