Topic B: proof routes and acquisition costs
===========================================

Open reader.pdf for the two-page explanation, explorer.html for the offline
paired graph, or default_graph.svg for a static named-result view of B1.
The reader has thirty clause routes and four complete application routes.
All 348 fine edges are retained. A compact graph hides proof branches under
their actual parent result; costs still refer to unions of text owners.

The complete written acquisition sequence is U_B, then B1, B2, B3, B4.
Whole-paper additions are 14,119, 7,368, 14,165, 11,128 and 12,281 NFC
characters excluding whitespace. The cumulative cost is 59,061. Every
complete paper includes its context and references. Previously paid
passages used by a later proof are displayed separately, never subtracted
again from a full local-paper cost.

The admitted overall comparison remains (x + 57,539) / 59,061, where x is
the missing original N001 PDF cost. There is no numerical overall factor
or historical cumulative curve. Conditional factors for N003, N008 and
L030 remain 1.254, 1.576 and 1.811 after acquiring the whole common theory.
Every source and new-paper document cost, all ten calibrated metric
coordinates, and expanded external/image unknowns remain as admitted.
No graph-node ratio, new source credit or question credit is introduced.

Logical-v2, corrected weighted-v2, independent ownership acceptance and
admitted measurements are copied byte-exact into inputs/. The copies
contain JSON evidence and locators, not primary-source text or images.
The original mathematical and ownership packets remain immutable.
This derived reader requires its own independent review before admission.

Rebuild and check from bound study files::

  python3 -B prepare.py --study-root PRIVATE_STUDY --out FRESH_DIRECTORY
  python3 -B -O check.py --packet FRESH_DIRECTORY --out FRESH_REPORT
  node test_explorer.js FRESH_DIRECTORY/explorer.html FRESH_DIRECTORY/default.svg
  python3 build_reader.py --packet FRESH_DIRECTORY --out FRESH_RENDER --xelatex xelatex
  python3 static_graph.py --packet FRESH_DIRECTORY --out FRESH_STATIC

The JSON/HTML checker also works directly on this copied packet, without
private sources or archived Markdown. It checks 100 acquisition cells,
80 complete-document cells, all thirty routes and eight semantic mutations.
The deterministic DOM harness checks all source/new contract-and-clause
views, all fine nodes/edges and 1,840 closure sums. This is not a real-browser
layout test. The static Matplotlib view uses the same twelve default nodes
and fourteen collapsed prerequisite edges; its SVG/PDF/PNG are portable
alternatives to the browser's own exported default.svg.

The render scripts need Matplotlib, pypdf, XeLaTeX and STIXGeneral.
The two final reader pages were visually inspected after a clean render.
Generated PDF metadata may vary between builds; page text and the exact
JSON acquisition account are the reproducibility targets.

The fixed intended public location is audit/topic-b/v2/dag/reader/.
The HTML is offline for its graph, table and detail behavior. Its five-level
relative links to current papers require the companion repository. They
point to precise current TeX files rather than private proof-bank paths.
Logical prerequisites and comparison/attribution/verification links are
separate in both graph controls and result details.

Box costs can overlap. Complete totals count every passage once. Historical
and new node categories have different granularity and are explanatory,
not a numerical comparison of mathematical quality.
