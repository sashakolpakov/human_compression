Distinct operational review of the L071 public evidence
======================================================

Accepted for restoration and verification of the completed evidence. This
is an operational portability review, not a new mathematical review, source
admission or census. The mathematical reviews of the compatible-action,
alternating, Co3 and remaining-model components remain separate premises.

I read all six portable Python files, all four supplied adaptation diffs
and porting.json. Each diff was independently regenerated and matched
exactly, with the original and adapted file hashes checked. Historical
absolute paths are mapped explicitly into the restored study or repository
tree. Resolution rejects undeclared external paths, missing files, and
escapes after symlink resolution. There is no fallback to private source
files. The three documentary inputs omitted from the payload are explicitly
identified: the primary L071 PDF and extracted text, and the local GAP
reference passage. Their hashes are compared as metadata only; the checker
does not misdescribe this as fresh source-content verification.

The alternating and Co3 adaptations replace primary-source parsing with
the already reviewed frozen source tables. The Co3 native build and page
rendering are removed. Both changes are explicit in code, diffs and reports;
prior source reading and native review are retained attestations. Every
other receipt, complete literal assembly, type comparison, generated-code
identity and exact-warning boundary from the acquired checkers is preserved.
The long censuses and full subset-IP calculations are not repeated here.

The transport is pinned to SHA-256
460ef417002d7ebbbbc536a889b8d1a6924dc6740b0c774072064e716d549c3a.
Its stored payload sizes/hashes and decoded sizes/hashes are checked before
restoration. Deduplication uses the decoded size/hash identity, with copies
in a disposable cache rather than hard links to source or public repository
files. Every acquired manifest and every one of its listed exports is
checked after restoration. All twenty-one complete acquired packet export
sets are present, not only selected mathematical claims. The narrow source
omission set is checked explicitly.

I ran the final checker from checker01 with stage02 as its payload and
math_compression as the existing-public input, writing a fresh run01 below
this review's working directory. It completed successfully: 1,057 logical
records, 1,054 restored files, 688 distinct payloads, twenty-one packets and
624 acquired exports. All four subprocess checks completed with empty
stderr. Their reconstructed evidence covers 12,904 alternating markings,
22,908 Co3 markings, 6,868 other-model markings and the 6,047 actual source
archive correspondences. The original strict warning receipts are restored
unchanged; the separate assessment still rejects unknown diagnostics,
changed programs, incomplete executions and missing matches.

Eleven additional operational controls rejected unsafe relative/absolute
paths, an undeclared external private path, restoration escapes, missing
public evidence, changed documentary hashes or sizes, a changed restored
pin, and Python optimization. The final entry point checks __debug__ and
clears PYTHONOPTIMIZE for child checkers, preserving inherited assert-based
checks. A legacy private absolute evidence path was also positively checked
to resolve to the restored file. These controls concern the execution
boundary, not the proof of the group-theoretic results.

This review does not certify the later catalogue registration or its fifteen
source dispositions; that transition has a separate review. It grants no
whole-original, new question, Lean or compression-factor credit. The source
reading, full manuscript review, exact mathematical computations and their
attribution remain recorded in the acquired component reviews.
