Singleton topics: concrete consolidation routes
===============================================

All ten topics should remain in scope while these routes are tested. None
is excluded and no current factor changes. A common theorem must recover
the complete applications with their tables, counterexamples and retained
inputs; putting related titles under one heading is insufficient.

* F: the L014+L007 scientific edition is already distinctly accepted. Finish
  its complete two-source measurement rather than seek another construction.
* Affine + A: the square presentation and seeds are actual repeated proofs;
  the one-edge extension is explicitly UA.SK. Retain the exact affine kernel
  and lattice certificates. L003/L121 are possible later applications.
* Matching + Operations: the complete orbital Coxeter presentation is the
  same theorem. The matching kernel is its antipodal-pair specialization.
  A merger still needs the full halving/geometric and quotient adapters.
* Gluing + B: prove one supported-factor/forest attachment theorem covering
  both existing routes. The impure-leaf counterexamples prohibit dropping
  the support condition.
* RankContraction + RankAugmentation: build one two-application rank-change
  volume with exact generation and end-intersection hypotheses. The two
  operations are not inverses; keep the augmentation counterproof in full.
* MathieuCensus + D: stabilizer-orbit augmentation is already the same finite
  action argument. Keep all431 source rows,81 pairs, companion tables and
  mandatory input data. Generic orbit counting does not supply a census.
* ChiralEnumeration: L071/L046 are concrete regular-marking applications of
  the exact orbit/prefix-search theory; all their source outputs remain to
  be recovered. L096 supplies a related exact IP test.
* Representation: rework existing L126 clause proofs through restriction and
  unique extension. Do not replace specified geometries by an existence
  theorem or use finite cleaning for the infinite free-basis branch.
* Mixes: test the all-flags fiber theorem on L016 voltage covers, keeping
  faithfulness, thinness and polytopality distinct.
