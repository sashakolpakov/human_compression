Odd symplectic existence and the exceptional field
=================================================

This bounded F001 component proves ranks three, four and five for every
odd q >= 5 and excludes every rank for PSp(4,3). The uniform endpoint
argument is a six-page written proof. Finite premises comprise four
explicit groups and all 4096 ordered subset pairs, eight field-parameter
rows, and a complete extension certificate for PSp(4,3).

Replay with Python check.py --out FRESH --study-root STUDY --gap GAP --render.
The uniform proof is not Lean formalized by this script. The finite GAP
certificate constructs the exceptional group from symplectic transvections
and proves the small examples faithful before comparing their full orders.

The original remains pending: this component does not prove the upper
bound five in odd characteristic. The separately accepted counterexample
to the literal arbitrary-subgroup Lemma 4.1 is not a counterexample to the
principal spectrum theorem. No new original, conjecture, Lean or
measurement credit is claimed. Classical inputs, acquired contraction and
the source of the endpoint idea are identified in dependencies.json and in
the written proof.
