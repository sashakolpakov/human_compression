Independent source-contract audit of N010
=======================================

Disposition
-----------
The draft has the correct 34 numbered problems, their order, physical-page
locators and duplicate-label distinctions. I read the entire fixed TeX,
including the bibliography, and directly viewed source pages 3, 5, 6, 7, 9,
11, 12, 14, 17, 18, 19 and 20. The exact literal checks independently match
all 34 bodies to source_inventory02. The draft is a useful source contract,
but should incorporate the corrections in suggested_contract02.json before
principal assembly. Neither this audit nor that proposed successor is a
mathematical principal acceptance. The original hash-bound draft is unchanged.

Questions in a 2006 source are not automatically currently open. No current
literature survey was conducted, and no new solution credit is awarded.
The later accepted project theorems mentioned below retain the authorship
and dates of their original mathematical results.

Scope corrections
-----------------
Problem 1 says toroidal polyhedra. Replacing this by arbitrary maps can
change the domain when the poset axioms are not imposed. Problem 7 asks
first about both regular and chiral maps, then about regular universal
quotients; its range is 1 <= j <= floor(q/2). The two questions should not
be collapsed. In Problem 11 the preceding paragraph requires any regular
constituent to be directly regular, and enantiomorphic choices are part
of the data. The numbered Problem 19 is literally about universal
objects, although the surrounding programme emphasizes a finite
classification. Both facts are retained instead of silently inserting
finite into the question. Problem 32 begins with regular, chiral or other
polytopes, then asks its more specific regular/chiral rank question.

Two unnumbered directions should be added: allowing regular star-polytope
vertex-figures in Problems 12--13, and the extension programme with only
facets prescribed. The corrected inventory therefore has 34 numbered
problems and 14 secondary records, rather than 12. These numbers count
records, not independent conjectures: for example the three rank-six
family completeness assertions share one record, and Problem 28 contains
three affirmative conjectural classes. Do not advertise 48 separately
formulated conjectures.

The draft's 40 bibliography entries covered its context subset. The full
source has 66. The proposed successor includes all 66, so the spherical
self-duality, edge-transitivity, extension, nonfacet, finite-transitivity
and uniform-polytope attributions are no longer lost merely because
those results were not repeated in a context heading.

Literal source qualifications
-----------------------------
On page 12 the condition t != 0 OR s != t for a chiral toroidal map
includes its own regular cases. In the same nondegenerate parameter
domain the complement of t=0 OR s=t is the conjunction t != 0 AND
s != t. Record the source spelling and the correction; the Boolean
observation is not a proof that every tiny parameter gives a polytope.
On page 9 a citation sentence uses rank n=2 for the plane toroidal maps,
although the surrounding explicit formulas put them at rank 3 in E2.
Use the actual rank/ambient-dimension formulas and flag the indexing
ambiguity. The recursive realization definition has another limited
index range j=1,2,3 in an otherwise arbitrary-rank discussion; a general
replacement needs the actual rank-dependent range.

The abstract and introduction disagree about the meeting year. The
source is fixed by arXiv math/0608397v1 and its TeX version8/15/06, not
by the later PDF compilation date or that inconsistent event description.
The small-genus paragraph credits regular and chiral catalogue coverage,
while its Conder--Dobcsanyi bibliographic title names regular maps. Keep
this as historical source attribution unless the actual chiral input is
separately located. This is not a present-day completeness assertion.

The polarity assertion is a statement here about rank-three regular or
chiral polyhedra, not an unrestricted higher-rank assertion. The
finiteness criterion for spherical Coxeter tessellations concerns the
universal tessellation, not all quotients of a hyperbolic Coxeter group.
The source's t-transitive graph terminology includes failure of
(t+1)-arc-transitivity; a statement that merely says arc-transitive is
weaker. In the semisparseness definition the first two conditions range
over conjugates; the displayed third condition is stated for Sigma
itself. Problem 34 quantifies both end conditions and equation (10) over
every conjugate. Its general proposed converse is not supplied by a
special endpoint theorem with extra local hypotheses.

Mathematical reuse and the remaining joins
-----------------------------------------
C01--C02: accepted chamber and L052 residue theory supplies actual
stabilizers, IP interfaces and the reflection obstruction. The survey's
rotation marks are not named the same way: with alpha_0=1,
alpha_i=sigma_1 ... sigma_i and sigma_i=alpha_(i-1)^(-1) alpha_i.
For i>=2 the string relations make alpha_i involutions. Thus simultaneous
alpha_i inversion induces exactly sigma_1 -> sigma_1^(-1),
sigma_2 -> sigma_1^2 sigma_2, and fixes sigma_i for i>=3. This short
convention join should be written in the assembly. It does not justify
turning an arbitrary C+ coset geometry into a polytope without its string,
thinness and residue hypotheses. Retain the precise standard
string-group/polytope reconstruction theorem where needed.

C03: retain the cited rank-three polarity and surface classification
results. No acquired all-surface classification was found here. C04:
a short flag-walk proof should identify each of the four displayed words
with a complete walk period, including the factor two for chiral zigzags.
A group-order calculation alone is not that proof. C05: the universal
amalgamation theorem is conditional on existence, and its oriented
chiral version has two relative choices. Keep the cited theorem and
cross-polytope/neighborliness criterion explicit; the illustrative group
orders 2000 and 960 still need either their exact retained theorem or a
small literal presentation verification. Do not infer them from the
existence of the group presentation.

C06: global spherical/projective and toroidal classifications are prior
results cited by this survey and may be retained as such, with exact
rank and lattice hypotheses. They are not outputs of this audit.
C07: the public corrected L084/L095 manuscripts establish the marked
J1 x PSL2(19) universal example and its own quotient census. They do not
establish the whole separate list of 17 locally projective universals or
its total 441 quotient count. A correction to a subcount in one source
must not be blindly subtracted from 441 without the original global
classification interface. C08: positive lattice/period applications
and the new L052 examples can be reused in their exact parameter
ranges; they do not prove the rank-six finite-list conjectures or all
rank-four chiral universal classifications. Retain the known allowed
rank/type restrictions separately from the conjectures. C09: catalogue
ranges and orientability choices remain dated, attributed inputs.

C10--C11: the parent owns the new Gram-matrix realization proof; no
parallel proof was attempted. The join must distinguish a faithful
group representation from faithful recursive face images and from the
full geometric symmetry group. Squared-distance uniqueness is up to
Euclidean congruence on the affine span. The cone includes degenerate
and nonfaithful realizations; irreducible blocks require the actual
vertex-stabilizer fixed spaces. These are hypotheses for the replacement,
not a newly accepted theorem in this audit. C12: the regular/chiral,
finite/infinite, planar/skew-face geometric lists should stay separately
attributed to the exact classifications. A generic Wythoff proof does
not enumerate them.

C13: the coset-incidence dictionary helps with the forward medial graph,
but the exact arc-transitivity and semisymmetry statements require their
Monson--Weiss/Monson--Pisanski--Schulte--Weiss interfaces. A connected
bipartite cubic graph is not automatically the medial graph of a
polytope. C14: Petrie schemes use adjacency permutations on flags, not
polytope automorphisms. Retain the cited acoptic families and the exact
necessary local condition. C16: retain Hartley's all-polytopes-as-quotients
and semisparseness theorems with their quantifiers. The public special
locally projective endpoint test does not resolve Problem 34.

C15 and the group questions admit stronger concrete reuse. The accepted
2016 L045 result gives a chiral rank-three polytope for each of the 26
sporadic simple groups, including the Monster, answering Problem 31's
existential request. It is not an enumeration of all such polytopes.
For Problem 32, accepted L038 gives all regular ranks from 3 through
floor((n-1)/2) for n>=12, and independently accepted L044 excludes larger
ranks. The complete small censuses give A5:{3}, A9:{3,4}, A10:{3,4,5},
A11:{3,6}, and none at degrees3,4,6,7,8. The A11 gaps matter. L045 gives
chiral rank-three existence for n>=8; it does not give every possible
chiral rank. These are acquired earlier results and grant no new
question credit.

For Problem 30, the reviewed two-group common quotient theorem applies
to finite nondegenerate string C-markings, with no class bound. The
high-type paper gives its actual all-parameter rank-three existence and
specified complete eight-family classification. Neither is a
classification of every unmarked group of the two requested orders,
and neither settles all chiral or mixed-prime cases.
