N010 retained inputs and acquired proofs
=======================================

This register is a source audit for the principal manuscript. It grants no
whole-original or newly solved-question credit. All 51 rows are tied to exact
locations in the fixed survey and its 66-item bibliography. A reference in the
survey does not mean that the cited primary text was independently inspected.

The global locally projective totals are particularly limited: seventeen
universals and 441 quotients are recorded as source-attributed assertions. The
accepted correction to 137 quotients in one family supplies no justified new
global total. No subtraction or edition identity is inferred.

The two square-torus example orders are separately proved and computationally
checked in corpus106_N010_oriented_examples_v1; that author packet still awaits
distinct mathematical acceptance. The other acquired proof joins are explicit.

The metadata-only historical searches sought the precise cited editions. They
were not a current-literature conjecture search, and their publisher abstracts
are not classification proof premises.
