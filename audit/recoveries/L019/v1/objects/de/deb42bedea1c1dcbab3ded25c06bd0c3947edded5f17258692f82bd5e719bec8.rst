Small even factors
==================

Read small_even.pdf and scope.json. The theorem leaves the critical
symmetric-factor argument open. check.py --out FRESH --render replays
all seven short GAP cases, every literal boundary check and native output.
The original source lists are reused unchanged. GAP errors, nonempty stderr,
incomplete completion markers and optimized Python all fail the checker.
The initial failed leaf_filter.g attempt is preserved as historical evidence.
No whole original or stability theorem is claimed.
