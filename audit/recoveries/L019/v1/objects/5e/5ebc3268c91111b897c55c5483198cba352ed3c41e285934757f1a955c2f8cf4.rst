Bounded third degree-ten diagnostic
===================================

This is a new pruning and execution method, not an unchanged retry of the
two earlier capped searches. It retains their45-second bound and preserves
both old directories. A timeout supplies no complete classification.

Target: a natural S10 marking of rank6, with point1 moved only by its first
mark, every maximal omission intransitive, a nonpure first mark, and all
even supported-prefix kernel conditions supplied by an actual attachment.
Pure first marks are already sufficient in the critical whole configuration.

New necessary pruning: every current prefix mark must be essential to the
orbit partition of that prefix. Indeed adjoining another generator to a
partition replaces it by the equivalence relation generated by that partition
and the new generator's pairs. Equal initial partitions remain equal after
the same future marks are adjoined. An inessential prefix mark would therefore
make its final maximal omission transitive along with the full marking,
contrary to fracture. This argument uses actual point orbits, not a quotient
intersection property.

New C-group check: store the groups generated by all suffix intervals of the
old prefix. After appending a mark, construct its new suffix intervals. Each
is a string marking; its two end omissions are already C by downward induction.
The acquired standard end criterion therefore checks it by the single
intersection of those end groups with the expected old middle interval.
Rank-two intervals are C because the two nonidentity involutions are distinct.
All terminal tuples are checked again by the former complete all-subsets
intersection oracle. This is an exact criterion, not an IP relaxation.

All remaining conditions and centralizer conjugacy reductions are the earlier
ones. The source and acquired classification attributions remain unchanged.
The diagnostic makes no general degree or whole-original claim, and its code
and any terminal evidence still require review before a mathematical admission.
