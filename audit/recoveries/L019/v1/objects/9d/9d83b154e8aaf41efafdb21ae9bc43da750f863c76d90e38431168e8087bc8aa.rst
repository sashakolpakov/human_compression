L019 Section 9: the remaining inverse and counting contract
===========================================================

This is an audit of obligations, not an acceptance of an inverse theorem.
The fixed source is Cameron, Fernandes and Leemans, The number of string
C-groups of high rank, arXiv:2212.12723v2, 11 January 2023. I read the full
source Section 9, its Section 6 operation and hypotheses, the introduction's
quotient convention, and the actual acquired restriction-kernel and
odd-attachment criteria. Source physical pages38,41,42 were directly viewed.
The author's working subdivision_cut_transport01 and sufficient-split

1. Exact objects and thresholds
------------------------------
Sigma^kappa(n) is the set of rank r=n-kappa C-markings of S_n modulo BOTH
ordered-marking isomorphism and full reversal (intro physical pages3–4).
The principal theorem is constancy for n>=2*kappa+3. A one-step proof must
therefore work on domains 2r>=n+3, and on codomains of degree M=n+1 and rank
R=r+1 satisfying 2R>=M+4. Contraction preserves kappa exactly:
(M-1)-(R-1)=M-R. Do not prove only a stronger range and lose the first
transition from n=2*kappa+3.

Natural permutation conjugacy represents abstract marked S_n isomorphism
when n is not6. The degree6 outer automorphism must be treated through the
acquired representative qualification, not assumed to preserve fractures.
In the required range its kappa=1 simplex boundary is separate and paid.

2. Three different sets of cuts
------------------------------
Let U(X) be the internal indices of perfect splits whose marking has a
trivial internal factor on at least one side. This is exactly the source
P_X with indices0,r-1 removed. It is NOT the set of all colours with an
eligible edge.

Let S(X) be those indices of U(X) where the repaired two leaf-kernel
families hold. The acquired criterion makes this the exact set at which
the stated extension is C. A pure split or the stated two odd attaching
groups supplies a sufficient way to lie in S(X). The forthcoming existence
assembly proves S(X) nonempty; it does not prove S(X)=U(X), nor min U(X)
in S(X).

Let C(Y) be adjacent internal perfect-cut pairs that have the actual
singleton joining point and one pure mark, and whose contraction has
been proved to be C and to generate S_(M-1). This is an additional group
property, not a synonym for two adjacent usable cuts.

3. Forward choice: source Proposition9.4, physical38
----------------------------------------------------
The source chooses i=min U(X) and invokes its printed Theorem6.4. That
printed general theorem has a separately accepted counterexample and
cannot be the repaired interface. To retain the source rule, prove both
leaf families at THIS least index. Alternatively define a different
rule in S(X), then pay its preservation and inverse statements afresh.
Existence of one sufficient split does not certify a different least
usable split.

The current working geometric transport note proves the following exact
statement for subdivision at a usable split (with the chosen side pure):
all old usable cuts other than i retain their property, with later labels
shifted, and i is replaced by i,i+1. It uses the actual orbit identity
new omission=<old omission,(a c)> and preserves each original internal
factor. This supports min U preservation, once frozen and reviewed.
It does not preserve S automatically: insertion of a pure transposition
can change the parity of an attaching group and hence its kernel test.

4. The least eligible PAIR: source9.5 versus9.6–9.9
--------------------------------------------------
On physical38, the promised inverse and Induction9.5 require j=min U(Y)
to satisfy j+1 in U(Y), with one of marks j,j+1 pure. Propositions9.6(a),
9.7,9.8,9.9 instead state existence of SOME such j. Their literal printed
conclusions do not establish that promised least-pair statement. The new
recovery needs one of the following complete replacements:

* prove the actual selected j=min U(Y) has the required adjacent geometry
  and group properties; or
* prove a choice-independent or canonical contraction theorem for a
  different explicitly defined set C(Y), and show it matches the forward
  construction on every equivalence class.

The new higher-density adjacent-pair theorem must be proved at
2R>=M+4. The current sufficient-split result is a one-cut existence result;
it is not an adjacent-pair theorem. An asserted corridor of pure marks
would be a useful possible organizing theorem, but remains an unproved
hypothesis, not a premise in this audit.

5. Actual contraction: geometry, group and IP
--------------------------------------------
First prove that the chosen consecutive cuts meet at a single point c
fixed by every other mark, and have the literal form
u=(a c), v=(c b)beta, with beta fixing a,b,c and supported on the right
(or the exact reversed version). Adjacent labels and a pure mark need
this actual support derivation; a diagram alone is not the proof.

Then q=u v u fixes c and replaces the pair in the shortened ordered list.
In the reversed pure-second case q=v u v. Prove all of the following:

* every remaining mark preserves Omega minus{c}; q is a nonidentity
  involution distinct from them and all distant commutations hold;
* the generated group on M-1 points is the required S_(M-1), not merely
  a transitive group or an induced quotient of an enlarged subgroup;
* every two generated subgroups of the contracted marking intersect in
  their common-label subgroup. A complete end-parabolic induction is
  an acceptable substitute, provided its restricted kernels are paid;
* the contracted marking lies in the claimed degree/rank range, and its
  chosen extension reconstructs Y with all internal factors unchanged.

Neither IP inheritance for a subfamily nor a restriction-to-an-orbit
argument proves contraction IP: q is a new conjugated generator, so the
shortened family is not a subfamily of Y. Nor may one identify the full
stabilizer of c in an enlarged parabolic with the old generated subgroup
without proof. That is exactly the kind of restriction kernel addressed
by the acquired repair. The general L037 rank-reduction theorem is not
automatically this local contraction; any use must check its literal
marking and product-order hypotheses.

6. Injectivity and surjectivity as actual inverse equations
-----------------------------------------------------------
For defined maps E and D on the declared classes, pay BOTH

  D(E([X]))=[X]    and    E(D([Y]))=[Y].

The first needs recognition of the inserted point/pair after the chosen
subdivision and no earlier eligible choice being introduced. The second
needs every target to possess the chosen contractible configuration and
the contraction to return to the declared domain. Knowing only that
some adjacent pure pair exists does not identify the inverse of the
least-index map. Cardinality equality cannot be inferred before both
class maps are well-defined; the sets being finite does not fix this.

7. Isomorphism and reversal
--------------------------
Ordered point-conjugacy transports all geometric data and leaf tests.
Abstract automorphisms require the degree6 qualification above. The
literal reversal indices are:

  E_i(X)^* is conjugate to E_(r-1-i)(X^*),
  D_j(Y)^* is conjugate to D_(R-2-j)(Y^*).

These are local compatibility formulas, not a proof that a least-index
choice is equivariant. Reversal sends min U(X) to the reflection of
max U(X). Thus one must prove that extension at the relevant opposite
choices yields the same unoriented isomorphism class, or supply another
canonical orientation/choice with a proved compatible inverse. Choosing
a lexicographic orientation by itself establishes well-definedness but
not the two inverse equations. An equivariant bijection of ordered
isomorphism classes would be sufficient and also account correctly for
self-dual classes; simply dividing ordered counts by two is invalid.

8. Precisely what the existing components supply
------------------------------------------------
The acquired fracture, actual-wing, split-inheritance, odd-attachment,
critical first-cut/endpoint and anchor-deletion results are ingredients
for sufficient-split existence. The degree9 boundary and final assembly
are separate at this audit time. The leaf theorem supplies the exact
forward kernel test; odd attachment pays it only under its explicit
hypotheses. The existing geometric subdivision note pays orbit transport
and U-label bookkeeping, subject to its own future distinct review.
None of these currently states the needed canonical inverse theorem.

After a complete bijection, iteration proves source Theorem1.1/9.10 and
the stable classification reduction. Any exact constants1,1,7,9,35,48
claimed in the principal scope still need their exact base classifications
with the same isomorphism-and-duality convention. The general constancy
argument alone computes none of them. No new census is requested here.
