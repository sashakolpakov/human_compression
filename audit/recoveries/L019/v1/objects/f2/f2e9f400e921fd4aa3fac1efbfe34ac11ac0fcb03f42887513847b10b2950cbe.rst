Balanced pure pairs and finite starting counts
=============================================

Working deduction, not yet independently reviewed. The accepted pure-cut
bijection is the sole new structural input. Exact finite counts below are
not assumed. This route concerns L019's original finite-base obligations.

Let n=2r-3, r>=4, and let the marking be the natural symmetric C-marking
in its ordinary representative (degree5 simplex handles the small endpoint).
The accepted theorem gives a nonempty interval of pure perfect cuts. At a
cut i define d(i)=2i-|A_i|, the surplus of its prefix. Its suffix surplus
is 1-d(i), since the two ranks sum to r-1 and degrees sum to n.
At consecutive pure cuts the prefix rank and degree each increase by one,
so d(i+1)=d(i)+1. At the first pure cut, d<=0: otherwise the actual-wing
bound makes that prefix symmetric, its old pure-leaf extension is C, and
backward leaf propagation makes the preceding mark a pure perfect cut.
Positive surplus cannot occur at rank0 or1, so that preceding index exists.
Likewise at the last pure cut, the suffix surplus is <=0 and hence d>=1.
There is therefore exactly one consecutive pure pair at which the two
surpluses are d=0 and d=1. Both cuts are internal. Write their geometry
as A,{c},B with marks (a c),(c b). If preceding/following ranks are p,q,
then |A|=2p, |B|=2q and p+q=r-2. Both p,q>=1.

Each wing is a transitive C-marking, rooted at its attaching point. Orient
its marks away from that root (reverse the left wing). Later marks fix the
root by distant commutation with the nearest pure bridge. Its pure-leaf
extension is an actual old interval and therefore C, equivalently all exact
full leaf-kernel inclusions hold. Each wing is fractured: if omitting a
wing mark kept its wing transitive, the same omission in the whole graph
would remain transitive through the two bridges and the other wing. The
whole marking is fractured by the acquired high-rank result.

Conversely take any such two rooted wings on disjoint sets A,B and join
through new c by (a c),(c b). String relations hold. Their separate pure-leaf
extensions are C. Joining first by q=(a b) is C: intervals crossing q obey
the one-bridge rectangle from the accepted contraction proof, while the
intervals ending/starting at q are point-renamed pure-leaf submarkings.
The connected full bridge group is the actual full Sym(A union B).
Subdivision of this pure perfect q is C by the acquired pure extension
lemma, and gives the desired marking on 2p+2q+1 points and rank p+q+2.
Its two indicated pure cuts have d=0,1. Thus this is exactly its unique
balanced pair, even if the pure interval continues farther in either
direction.

An ordered point-conjugacy preserves that unique pair and restricts to
rooted conjugacies of the two oriented wings. Conversely those two rooted
conjugacies extend by fixing c. Reversal exchanges the two wings. Symmetric
ambient degree>=7 has only inner automorphisms; degree5 has the separately
acquired ordinary simplex class. Consequently the boundary classes modulo
marked-group isomorphism and duality are unordered pairs of rooted wing
classes whose ranks sum to r-2=kappa+1.

Define w_p to be the number of rooted point-conjugacy classes of transitive
fractured string C-markings of degree2p, rankp, with root moved only by the
first mark and satisfying all full leaf-kernel inclusions. Then

 c_kappa = sum_{p<q, p+q=kappa+1} w_p*w_q
           + (w_h*(w_h+1)/2 if kappa+1=2h else 0).

This is a proposed theorem to write and review, not a reliance on the
source's numerical c_kappa. The source values would require
w_1,...,w_6=1,1,6,3,11,19. These are comparison targets only; a mismatch
must be investigated rather than overwritten. Degree2/rank1 is elementary.
Degree4/rank2 can be exhaustively checked directly. For the others a
bounded, literal derivative of the accepted anchored degree-ten search
tries all involutions, rooted centralizer conjugacy orbits, every exact
prefix IP, orbit-essentiality and every full leaf kernel. It retains every
transitive terminal group, not merely Sym or Alt. Final independent
verification and algorithm review are still required before using counts.
