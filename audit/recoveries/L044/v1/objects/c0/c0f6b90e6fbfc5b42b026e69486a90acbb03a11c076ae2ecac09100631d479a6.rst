L044: two moving orbits in a singleton prefix
===========================================

An intransitive small singleton prefix cannot leave exactly two moving orbits and exactly the two fixed points a,b after deleting the first two marks. Support invariance settles all but one case; the actual order-six first prefix then distinguishes the exterior set by its orbit sizes. The literal hypothesis is assumed directly, independently of the separate orbit-budget packet. No finite computation or whole-original credit is added.
