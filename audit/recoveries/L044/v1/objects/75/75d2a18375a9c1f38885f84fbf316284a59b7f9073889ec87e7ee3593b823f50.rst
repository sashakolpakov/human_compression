Centralizers of a primitive action on pairs
==========================================

A direct point-action argument establishes when centralizing the chosen block-generating subgroup forces preservation of the pair system. The only exception is a regular dihedral group of prime degree, too small in rank for the present application. This justifies the missing-label attachment and, with the acquired corrected primitive-string bound, closes all primitive block images other than the symmetric and alternating groups. Those residuals and the whole principal source remain pending. No new computation or whole-original credit is included.
