L044: selected ambient cuts and three prefix orbits
==================================================

The confinement lemma counts the orbits of a full contiguous interval subgroup while permitting an arbitrary selected subset of its colours. Every selected-colour ambient eligible edge must be confined to the given proper invariant set. The complete proof audits the unselected middle moves and the actual exit colours. Combined with the elementary internal pair forest, it excludes the last f=2,c=3 small-prefix orbit configuration. Whole singleton and principal-source assembly remain separate; no original credit is added.
