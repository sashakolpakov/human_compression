======================================================

Accepted as a bounded L044 component, for degrees four through eleven.
Together with the already accepted larger-degree theorem this proves the
corrected anchored bound in every imprimitive degree. It does not restore
the false unanchored reading of the original proposition or complete L044.

parameters at least three, degree nine is the only small case; equality

hypothesis and closes the delicate case that parity alone does not close.

For two-point blocks, equality gives a faithful diagonal S_b with its
inherited intersection property. Direct indecomposability puts its marked
generators in an interval. The extra generator must be at an end, while
the anchor must be at the opposite end. The small endpoint lemma first
kills its block action and then its even binary flip vector. There is no
use of an intersection property merely projected onto a quotient.
