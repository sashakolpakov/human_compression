An alternating group on the pairs
=================================

The full alternating block-image branch reduces to a faithful endpoint subgroup. Its actual permutation centralizers, including both degree-eight boundaries, force the omitted generator to preserve the original pairs. The argument explicitly retains smaller-degree induction and complete finite maxima. It neither splits the central extension nor invents a block action for the omitted generator. This is a bounded component of L044, with no whole-original credit.
