Distinct review of the critical prefix kernels
==============================================

The frozen two-page argument is accepted under its stated critical-head
hypotheses. This is a bounded mathematical acceptance, not a complete
recovery of L044. Root suggested the initial parity fibre and extension
of pure support. The present reviewer did not contribute to that argument
before reviewing this edition.

For the merging lemma, the cycles (a b z), using two common points,
generate the alternating group of the union. At every subsequent step
both U and its P-translate contain C; both U and its t-translate contain
C union C^t. Hence the merging operation remains valid until the finite
invariant union is attained. No primitive-group theorem is hidden here.

The supported head fixes the exterior involution delta. Products of
conjugates of tau cancel delta and yield all pure cycles (d b e), giving
Alt(D union {b}). The retained critical-tail lemma supplies tau(x)=x
without B-transitivity. Thus P3 fixes x and P4 has exactly the displayed
eight-point orbit. A distant generator centralizes the pure alternating
group two prefixes earlier, so fixes that support pointwise. Overlap
therefore persists and proves the full induction on the actual orbit.

An element of P_k has equal restriction signs because the ambient group
is even. Each exterior image j has one lift; multiplying by the pure
Alt(C_k) kernel gives every core permutation of that same sign. This
proves the exact fibre description even if J_k has no odd element.
The exterior image has not been discarded. If two successive core orbits
were equal, all remaining generators would preserve them. Global
transitivity then makes the core all of Omega, so the preceding prefix
already equals the full even group, contrary to independence from IP.

For the omission bridge, the supported head and the natural tail meet
only at point 6. Adjoining the head attaches its five other vertices to
the one local orbit containing 6, without merging any other local orbit.
All j-edges lie in Z, so their eligibility is identical. The final formula
is intersection with the actual pointwise stabilizer Alt(Z), followed by
ambient IP on the two marked index sets. It makes no assertion about IP
in a permutation quotient.
