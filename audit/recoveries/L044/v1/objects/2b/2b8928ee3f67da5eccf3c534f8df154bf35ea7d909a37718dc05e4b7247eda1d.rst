Independent review of the two-block branch for L044
=====================================================

Reviewer: /root. Author of both new components: /root/enumeration_review.
I read all ten new lemmas and their proofs, checked their interfaces to the
previously accepted endpoint and extremal-generation results, and directly
viewed all eight rendered pages. Three fresh XeLaTeX two-pass builds reproduce
the frozen page texts exactly and have no warnings. PDFLaTeX was initially
tried, then discarded as a reproduction engine because the author used
XeLaTeX; its spacing differences do not affect the mathematical review.

The hypotheses throughout are the accepted unique-crossing setting, an even
transitive string C-group, all maximal omissions intransitive, and exactly
two orbits for the distinguished omission. The results cannot be exported
without those hypotheses. In particular the four-row completion uses the
global omission hypothesis, not just the local interval intersection property.

The two literal block-image generators must lie at the adjacent indices.
When the anchor orbit is paired, a mixed basis would leave its pair invariant:
the exceptional near generators commute with the opposite swap. Hence the
literal basis is pure; this is not a change of generators. The displayed
M={tau_A,tau_B} in saturation Lemma1 is an editorial notation error and is
read as M=<tau_A,tau_B>, as confirmed by the author. No frozen file was changed.

For the saturated paired case, equality of independent length gives the
whole symmetric row product, using the retained L047 normal-quotient argument.
The prefix and suffix are direct factors by the original intersection
property. Natural symmetric centralizers, endpoint omissions, evenness and
the already accepted four-row centralizer calculation exclude each size.
The independent-length statement is used on faithful row images; no projected
intersection property is silently assumed.

For a regular four-point orbit containing the anchor, the parity count makes
both block sizes even. Removing the two literal neighboring swaps and their
neighbors gives the saturated row product S_(k-2) x S_(l-1). If both factors
lie on the right, x is trivial or odd. Otherwise the factors split as claimed.
The pure right swap preserves the four-point set, and the mixed right swap
reduces to the accepted endpoint cases. In the ordinary endpoint case, the
two large orbits of <P,x> are matched by that literal swap. Centralization
and evenness force the stated action of the crossing generator, so the
previous exact word makes the far generator redundant.

The C2 x S_m lemma keeps its central generator distinguished. The quotient
argument uses an independent minimal quotient subfamily and the retained
extremal-string theorem, with m odd and the separate m=3 argument. The latter
is valid because lifts with different signs make one neighboring pair have
product order six and therefore generate all of C2 x S3. The k=4 application
handles possible character graphs: a nontrivial graph would force preservation
of the four-point set. The adjusted element y0=y*z^eta is only a word in
available generators and is never treated as a new distinguished generator.
The k=2 cases use the already proved two-row word identities unchanged.

The new commuting-factor lemma is immediate from (a^l)^r=(a^r)^l. With a
paired anchor it makes the right interval fix the entire left orbit, excluding
a regular orbit away from that anchor. In the completely paired case it gives
exact disjoint support. Each side then has rank at most its block size. Equality
at sizes >=6 is odd by the ordinary odd-degree symmetric endpoint. Equality
at size4 has the two explicitly listed even endpoint actions. Contracting the
four swap pairs gives edges 0--3--2--1; the remaining three generators are
transitive on all eight points. Omitting the far generator globally consequently
leaves both original orbits transitive and retains the crossing edge, contradicting
the standing hypothesis. Thus each side has rank at most its size minus one.

Disposition
-----------
ACCEPTED as bounded written results, with the stated editorial interpretation.
Together with the previously accepted alpha=1 endpoints and exact deficit
formula, these close the case of two blocks on both original omission orbits.
Mixed block counts and other L044 source obligations remain pending. This
does not add a recovered original, solve a new conjecture, establish a new
compression factor, or add any formal declaration. The entire cited prior
theory remains an acquired dependency.
