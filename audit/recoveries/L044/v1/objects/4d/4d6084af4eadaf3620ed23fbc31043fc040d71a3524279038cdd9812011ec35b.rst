The faithful-lift classification is justified by actual orbit and
stabilizer structure. A point orbit meets every pair equally, giving
either two natural rows or a transitive lift. In the latter case the
point stabilizer has index two in S_(m-1), whose only such subgroup is
A_(m-1). Its coset action is the displayed global sign twist, with the
specified pairs preserved under the identification. An odd block
permutation has sign (-1)^m in this lift, so the twist is excluded in
an even parent when m is odd.

force B's kernel to vanish; a further R mark would give a nonzero

For a faithful rank-(m-2) lift, the acquired core path puts the omitted

above the small block orbit in either lift. Every commuting permutation
therefore preserves that set and its complement. If the extra mark

twist, the small S2 and an odd element of the large symmetric factor
already generate the regular V4 on those four points, so its centralizer
preserves the pairs directly. The large restriction is covered by the
actual pair-centralizer lemma in both cases.

If the extra mark is R, its position is the opposite tail neighbor.
Its block image centralizes the natural S_(m-1), hence is trivial. Its
flip vector is constant away from the final pair. For even m the only

ordinary doubled. On the small four points this full flip and the

with both, and its large restriction centralizes the ordinary doubled

actual action of the extra mark and every parity possibility.
