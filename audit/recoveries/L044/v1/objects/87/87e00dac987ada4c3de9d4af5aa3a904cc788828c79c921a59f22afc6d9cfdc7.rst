Distinct working addendum: the outside attachment lemma
=======================================================

The exact 35b2a57e note has been read in full and is sound. It supplies an
explicit needed justification for the TT claim that every outside tail mark
is plain, including a C label adjacent in the original string to an R label.
The earlier working review did not separately articulate this distinction;
that record is preserved, and this supplementary acceptance records the
additional argument and its authorship by l044_resume.

After collapsing the connected J core, selected C edges form a tree. Thus
each outside component has exactly one exit to Y. Follow the path from an
outside selected edge c to that exit e. Consecutive selected edges give
noncommuting projected involutions: they share an outside endpoint, and
no additional edge can involve any outside point. If the last edge enters
Y, evaluation at the previous outside endpoint sends it into Y in one
order and to the shared outside point in the other. Extra edges within Y
cannot cancel this disagreement. The labels form a simple C-only path.

If c fails to commute with K, it must be the outer neighbour of r or s.
The exit e also fails to commute with K, because a centralizer preserves
the distinguished unique nontrivial K orbit over Y. Two distinct such
labels cannot both be noncommuting neighbours of the same R mark, since
l already occupies one of that mark's two possible positions. They must
attach to opposite ends of r-l-s. Their C-only path then completes a cycle
in the noncommutation graph, contradicting its containment in the original
string path. Therefore c centralizes K.

For TT forms A/B the core has a trivial marked centralizer, by its labelled
eight-point path. Hence any such outside C mark fixes every core point,
forcing global row character zero and excluding all additional Y edges.
It is exactly its one doubled selected transposition. This argument must
be included in the final TT manuscript before the exit calculations; it is
not supplied by the diagnostic data or by the phrase far C mark alone.

No equality classification beyond this conditional interface, whole-original
credit, computation, formalization or measurement is claimed here.
