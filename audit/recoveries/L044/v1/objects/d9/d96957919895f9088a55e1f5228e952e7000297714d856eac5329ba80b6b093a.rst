Completeness of the search
--------------------------

The threshold is floor(n/2), which is the integer form of r >= (n-1)/2.
All primitive catalogue actions of degrees 2 through 11 are visited.
Testing parity on a generating set decides containment in A_n. Taking
all order-two conjugacy classes and generating their union is an exact
necessary test for generation by involutions. Neither test is based on
the desired source table. The seventeen eligible actions are searched
to the independent-family bound n-2 retained from the accepted common
theory. Trivial and very small even actions cannot supply a missing
involutory primitive string at the lower boundary.

At each prefix, every next involution outside the current subgroup,
commuting with all but the last generator and not with the last, is
considered modulo the pointwise conjugation stabilizer of the prefix.
This stabilizer lies in the full permutation normalizer of the ambient
group. Its orbits preserve the candidate set and every necessary test;
updating by a centralizer is precisely the stabilizer for the extended
prefix. Thus the pruning does not identify different fixed-colour
markings improperly or lose a possible continuation.

The prefix independence test and the displayed intersection equality
are necessary consequences of full IP. No sufficiency is inferred from
the latter alone. A proper transitive prefix would lie in the maximal
omission obtained by deleting the last generator of any longer marking,
contradicting the stipulated intransitivity. A prefix needing k more
independent letters has index at least 2^k. All letters after the next
one commute with the whole prefix; their subgroup T meets the prefix
trivially by full IP. In particular T meets its centre trivially, so
|C_G(H):Z(H)| >= |T| >= 2^q. This justifies both order screens.

At a terminal generating tuple the programs separately check the rank,
every maximal omission's orbit partition, and every marked subgroup
intersection. The loop over two subset masks really checks all
2^(2d) equalities. Consequently terminal acceptance has the full
required meaning even though intermediate pruning checks less.

Source identification and fresh computation
------------------------------------------

The coloured graphs are deterministic involutory transition systems,
including fixed-point loops. On a transitive graph a colour-preserving
map is determined by one vertex image. Trying all such images and
propagating every transition, while rejecting inconsistency and
noninjectivity, is a complete simultaneous-conjugacy test. The checker
separately tests reversal of the whole ordered string; it does not
permit arbitrary permutations of colours. Every computed tuple has
one source row, each source row is attained, source rows are distinct
even under reversal, and computed ordered tuples are distinct before
reversal. Orders and Schlaefli symbols alone are not the identification.
