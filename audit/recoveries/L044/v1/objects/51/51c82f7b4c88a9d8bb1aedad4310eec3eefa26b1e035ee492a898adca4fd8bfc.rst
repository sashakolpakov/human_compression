Choosing commuting ends before the intervening marks
====================================================

This is a working proof for the new traversal, not a completed census claim.
The acquired L071 orbit/centralizer/double-coset theorem remains the basis.

Fix a target rank r and the label order 1,r,2,r-1,... . At a partial tuple
on the selected set I, let H be the group generated by its marks and S
their pointwise centralizer in the full automorphism action A. For the
next label j let Q centralize precisely the already assigned marks with
distance greater than one from j. Then S is a subgroup of Q. The possible
new involutions are exactly the involutions of G intersect Q outside H,
with noncommuting adjacent products whenever an adjacent mark is already
assigned. The latter restriction loses no marking of the nonabelian simple
group G: a disconnected marking splits G into nontrivial commuting normal
subgroups. In each Q class choose representatives of C_Q(y) backslash Q/S.
The acquired double-coset lemma gives exactly one candidate per S orbit.

For partial IP, divide I into maximal contiguous intervals. The string
conditions make the groups of different intervals commute. Each interval
must be a C-marking, checked by the complete recursive end criterion. The
product of these interval groups maps onto H. This map is injective exactly
when the product of their orders equals the order of H. If injective, the
intersection property in H is the coordinatewise intersection property of
the interval factors. Conversely, full partial IP makes every interval a
C-marking and makes each factor intersect the product of the other factors
trivially, hence makes this product map injective. This proves the partial
test used in enumerate_ends_v2.g, including disconnected partial sets.

Every restriction of a complete C-marking passes this test. The next
pointwise stabilizer is C_S(x), independently of the chosen label order.
Induction on the number of assigned labels therefore proves completeness
and uniqueness of full ordered automorphism orbits. If H already equals G
before all labels have been assigned, no independent extension exists; the
branch is discarded. At the final label require H=G. Exact conjugation of
tuples to their reversals then accounts for duality.

For the odd-field finite cases the run computes a Sylow 2-subgroup P and
the complete conjugacy classes of its subgroups, after an actual isomorphism
to a polycyclic group. An elementary abelian subgroup has exponent at most2;
the largest order among these representatives therefore gives the exact
2-rank e. Every elementary abelian2 subgroup of G lies in a conjugate of P.
The alternating marks of a rank-r C-marking generate an elementary abelian
subgroup of order2^ceil(r/2). Thus r<=2e. This is a finite calculation, not an
assumption that ranks beyond the source's table vanish. Exact software
algorithms for Sylow subgroups, subgroup classes, and their orders remain
declared premises. No general odd-q rank theorem is claimed.

The previous traversal's minimum-rank lookahead is separate: at a prefix
of length m<=2, reaching rank4 requires a mark after the next one. That
mark centralizes all current marks and is outside H. If the normal closure
of all involutions in that pointwise centralizer, restricted to G, is
contained in H, no such extension exists. Omitting stored generating tuples
of length<4 is also harmless because they cannot have independent extensions.
