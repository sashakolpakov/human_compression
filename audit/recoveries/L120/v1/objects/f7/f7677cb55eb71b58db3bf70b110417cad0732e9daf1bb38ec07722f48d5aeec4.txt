For clarity we also verify the class identifications outside the group enumerator. Root the coloured
permutation graph at a point, discover vertices in breadth-first order, and at each vertex inspect the
marks in their given order. Write every marked permutation in this new numbering and take the least
resulting array over the eight roots. This array is invariant under point conjugacy. Conversely equality
