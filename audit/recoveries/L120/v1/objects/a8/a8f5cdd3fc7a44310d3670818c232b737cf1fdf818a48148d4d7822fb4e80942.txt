# Phase 19: bounded status audit for the unitary rank-three construction

Audit date: 2026-10-10. **Publication priority remains unverified.** This is a bounded primary-source check, not an exhaustive literature review and not evidence that an unlocated result is open.

## Exact result being compared

The companion construction gives a rank-three string C-group for the diagonal index-two extension of $\operatorname{PSU}_4(q)$ when $q\equiv1\pmod4$ or $q\equiv3\pmod8$, with odd $q\ge5$. For $q\equiv7\pmod8$, the same matrices generate the simple socle. They do not establish the index-two extension in that class, $q=3$, or any linear diagonal extension. This note does not identify the covered extension families as a named published conjecture.

## Primary sources and reading boundaries

1. **Leemans--Vandenschrick, 2026**, *On simple groups that admit a string C-group representation*, Art Discrete Appl. Math. 9, P1.07. The introduction and Theorem 1.1 of the [author manuscript](https://dipot.ulb.ac.be/dspace/bitstream/2013/397490/3/simpleGroups6.pdf) explicitly retain earlier rank-three existence for the nonexceptional simple groups. Thus rank-three existence for $\operatorname{PSU}_4(q)$, odd $q\ge5$, is already known. The article classifies **simple** groups; it does not automatically settle their diagonal extensions. [Institutional publication metadata](https://repozitorij.upr.si/IzpisGradiva.php?id=22370&lang=eng&print=) confirms the 2026 volume and an online publication date in November 2025. The author's [current publication list](https://leemans.dimitri.web.ulb.be/papersbyyear.html) was also inspected.

2. **Baktybekov--Conder--Nuzhin--Rezantseva, 2025**, *On Regular Polytopes of Rank 3*, J. Sib. Fed. Univ. Math. Phys. 18(4), 498--505. Directly read the [full primary PDF](https://elib.sfu-kras.ru/bitstream/2311/156158/1/Baktybekov-Nuzhin.pdf), especially Theorems A/B and §2. Theorem A restates the 2013 existence alternative; Theorem B specifies the actual marking and isolates a possible Klein-four dihedral intersection. The companion proof now supplies an elementary argument eliminating that alternative when there is no nontrivial cyclic normal subgroup. This is retained intersection theory, not a new uniform generation theorem in that source.

3. **Conder--Oliveros, 2013**, *The intersection condition for regular polytopes*, J. Combin. Theory Ser. A 120, 1291--1304, Theorem 4.1. The original full article was not obtained in this audit. The theorem and same-marking corollary were read in [Conder's primary slides, pp. 21--22](https://www.fields.utoronto.ca/programs/scientific/13-14/discretegeom/Slides/Conder.pdf); the 2025 primary article supplies a later precise statement. Do not infer that the original full proof was read.

4. **Brooksbank--Leemans, 2019**, *Rank reduction of string C-group representations*, [arXiv:1812.01055v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.01055). Theorem 1.1 and Corollary 1.2 were read in the cached primary text. Their sufficient local hypothesis fails for the all-even rank-four templates here, and they explicitly do not assert necessity. The new triple is constructed directly; this source is not a generation shortcut.

5. **Leemans--Moerenhout--O'Reilly-Regueiro, 2017**, *Projective linear groups as automorphism groups of chiral polytopes*, [Proposition 2.3](https://arxiv.org/html/1606.08017). Directly reread the complete Dickson maximal-subgroup list for odd projective rank-one groups. This is the retained classification input for the new parabolic projection; it says nothing by itself about the target four-dimensional unitary group.

6. **Nuzhin, 1997**, *Generating triples of involutions of Lie-type groups over a finite field of odd characteristic*, parts I/II. Primary [MathNet metadata for part I](https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/al2384) and its series references were inspected; the part-II DOI fetch failed. No complete primary proof from these papers was read during this bounded check. Existing simple-socle coverage is supported here by the explicit later primary statement in item 1, not by extrapolating these older titles to almost-simple extensions.

The small almost-simple [Leemans--Vauthier atlas](https://www.math.auckland.ac.nz/~dleemans/polytopes/) was checked for its scope. It enumerates socles below one million, so it is not a uniform result for the present $\operatorname{PSU}_4(q)$ families.

## Search boundary and conclusion

Fourteen web searches used the exact phrases listed in the companion JSON, including PGU/PSU4, three involutions, diagonal extensions, rank-three polytopes and regular maps. Search hits on complex unitary reflection groups, factorization of almost-simple groups, and three **conjugate** involutions were not substituted for the required commuting-endpoint theorem. A current August 2026 paper on $G_2$ and triality groups was identified as a different family.

No exact uniform diagonal-extension theorem matching the two covered congruence classes was located in these searches. This limited negative result does not establish novelty or current openness. The defensible scope is an internally reviewed explicit construction, recovering known simple-group existence in one branch and producing proved extension-family statements whose publication priority remains to be checked. No infinity or solved-open-problem marker is warranted from this audit.
