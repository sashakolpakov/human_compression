Rank three
----------

The hypothesis rules out generation by any pair of the three involutions,
so distinctness, independence and properness of the adjacent dihedral
subgroups follow. A subgroup of a dihedral group containing its reflection
has a cyclic rotation subgroup of index two. Except for the four-group,
that cyclic subgroup is unique. Equality of the two rotation subgroups
therefore supplies a nontrivial cyclic subgroup normal in both adjacent
subgroups and hence normal in their generated group.

The exceptional four-group case is fully handled. Its two distinct rotation

normal commuting dihedral factors U,V. Their commutator subgroups are cyclic

under the stated hypothesis, forcing G abelian and contradicting the same
hypothesis. The endpoint four-group intersections and independence then
