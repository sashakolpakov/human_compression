\title{The complete string C-group counts for $S_8$}
\author{Human Compression project}\date{}
\begin{document}\maketitle
We recover the $S_8$ row of Table~1 in Leemans' survey~\cite{survey},
using the independently reviewed finite enumeration method~\cite{method}.
The survey identifies generating sets under group automorphisms.  Here
an ordered representation retains the order of its marked involutions;
duality reverses that order.  We verify below why these conventions give
the stated comparison.

\begin{theorem}
The numbers of string C-group representations of $S_8$ are as follows.
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrr}
\text{rank}&3&4&5&6&7\\\hline
\text{ordered, modulo automorphisms}&126&71&20&2&1\\
\text{self-dual ordered classes}&10&1&2&0&1\\
\text{modulo automorphisms and duality}&68&36&11&1&1
\end{array}
\]
There are no representations of larger rank.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every nonidentity involution in the natural action is a product of
$e$ disjoint transpositions for exactly one $e\in\{1,2,3,4\}$.
The four possibilities are the four initial branches of the acquired
enumeration.  Each branch begins with the specified representative,
and the exact centralizer acts on all possible subsequent marks.
The complete method proves that its successive orbit representatives
exhaust the ordered tuples up to simultaneous point conjugacy.  It also
pays every commutation restriction, the actual subgroup intersection
test and the pruning of prefixes which cannot generate $S_8$.
The inherited independent-generator bound gives rank at most seven;
the four branches are therefore complete for this group.  All four
terminate, without reaching their time limits, with the first row above.

We independently check each of the 220 retained tuples.  Its marks are
nonidentity involutions with the required distant commutations, and their
generated subgroup has order $40320$, hence is $S_8$.  For every pair of
subsets $I,J$ of its marks, we explicitly form the two generated subgroups
and verify
\[
 \langle\rho_i:i\in I\rangle\cap\langle\rho_j:j\in J\rangle
 =\langle\rho_k:k\in I\cap J\rangle.
\]
This performs $71\,296$ actual subgroup-intersection checks.  These
checks validate the returned objects; completeness is supplied by the
four finished enumerations and the acquired method, not by the number
of positive objects alone.

For clarity we also verify the class identifications outside the group
enumerator.  Root the coloured permutation graph at a point, discover
vertices in breadth-first order, and at each vertex inspect the marks
in their given order.  Write every marked permutation in this new
numbering and take the least resulting array over the eight roots.
This array is invariant under point conjugacy.  Conversely equality of
two arrays supplies the required point bijection, so it is a complete
invariant.  This is the unrooted version of the previously acquired
coloured traversal~\cite{balanced}.  The 220 arrays are distinct within
their ranks.  Reversing each tuple matches exactly one retained array,
and the induced permutation of the classes is an involution with the
fixed-point counts in the second row.  Thus its orbit counts are
$(126+10)/2$, $(71+1)/2$, $(20+2)/2$, $(2+0)/2$ and $(1+1)/2$,
respectively.  Finally, every automorphism of $S_8$ is inner.  The exact
finite check also verifies $|\operatorname{Aut}(S_8)|=40320$; since
$Z(S_8)=1$, point conjugacy already accounts for all automorphisms.
Adjacent marks cannot commute, by the acquired direct-factor argument
for $S_8$.  Thus the graph of noncommuting marks is a path.  An
isomorphism of their generating sets preserves this path, whose only
automorphisms are the identity and reversal.  Consequently the last row
is exactly the source's isomorphism convention.
\end{proof}

\section*{Evidence and attribution}
The counts are existing results reported in the source, not new
classifications.  The acquired common method contains the actual
enumeration proof and its small controls.  This component executes that
unchanged method for the four fixed $S_8$ branches and supplies the
complete literal output, independent subgroup validation and reversal
maps.  It does not import an unverified source count as a premise.
All arithmetic and subgroup operations use exact permutations in GAP;
the additional canonicalization uses only Python integer arrays.
The separate checker reruns the four branches, reconstructs their
literal inputs, and repeats both validations.  A timeout, damaged
receipt or missing terminal marker gives no certificate.

The ranks six and seven were already covered by the acquired stable
counts.  The present result newly pays the three source cells in ranks
three, four and five.  The rest of the survey contract, including the
larger unrecovered experimental rows, remains separate.  No whole-source
admission, compression measurement or new conjecture resolution follows
from this bounded component.
