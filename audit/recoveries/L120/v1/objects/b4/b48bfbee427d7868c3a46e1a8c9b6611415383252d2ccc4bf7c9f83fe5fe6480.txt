Complete enumeration and independent verification
Proof.We apply the acquired permutation enumeration [2], leaving its mathematical engine unchanged.
In the three symmetric groups, every first involution is conjugate to one havingedisjoint transpositions,
with 1≤e≤ ⌊n/2⌋. InA 15 the allowable values aree= 2,4,6. We classify by the full automorphism
group ofA 15, namelyS 15 acting by point conjugacy, so these are exactly the three initial classes needed
for that group as well.
The acquired proof visits each permissible next involution through its orbit under the actual pointwise
centralizer of the prefix. It pays both the intersection test on the growing prefix and the transitivity
pruning. Every one of the resulting twenty-two initial branches terminates: six branches in each of degrees
twelve and thirteen, seven in degree fourteen, and three forA 15. The rank limits are respectively 7,7,8,7.
Thus its completeness theorem applies throughout these ranges; a timeout is not interpreted as exhaustion.
Filtering by the actual generated group leaves 9030,16571,56749,12004 tuples, respectively. The full
transcripts and the exact engine used in each branch are retained.

The search excludes a commuting adjacent pair for these targets by the acquired indecomposability
argument. Indeed, such a pair separates a string C-marking into commuting nontrivial factors whose
intersection is trivial by the intersection property. These symmetric groups have no such direct-product
decomposition, andA 15 is simple. Hence the noncommutation graph is the connected path on the marked
generators. An isomorphism of their underlying generating sets must preserve that path. It either preserves
their order or reverses it. All automorphisms of the symmetric groups here are inner, and all automorphisms
ofA 15 are induced byS 15. Consequently point conjugacy and reversal give precisely the source equivalence
relation.
We verify all positive tuples independently of the search state. The verifier reconstructs each literal
permutation, checks nonidentity, involution orders and the distant commutations, and computes its actual
generated subgroup. It requires equality with the stated target group. For every contiguous interval [a, b]
of at least three marks it checks
⟨ta, . . . , tb−1⟩ ∩ ⟨ta+1, . . . , tb⟩=⟨t a+1, . . . , tb−1⟩.
The acquired interval theorem [3] proves that these equalities, with its rank-one and rank-two bases,
imply the full intersection property. These are equalities of actual permutation subgroups. They do
not rely on cached prefix results or on projection of an intersection property. The four lists require
16986,25066,94392,13729 interval comparisons: 150173 comparisons on 94354 complete tuples.
For distinctness and reversal we use the acquired literal-array invariant of [4]. Starting at a point,
traverse its orbit in the order of the marked generators and express all generators in that resulting order
of points. Minimize this array over all starting points. Transitivity ensures that every traversal covers the
whole domain. A point conjugacy carries a rooted traversal to the corresponding rooted traversal, whereas
equality of two arrays supplies the actual conjugating bijection. Thus two minimized arrays agree exactly
when the ordered markings are point conjugate. All arrays in each list are distinct. The minimized reversed
array is also located in that list; the resulting permutation of the list is checked to be an involution. Its
fixed points give the self-dual column. Its number of orbits is therefore half the sum of the ordered and
self-dual columns, as asserted.
The higher-rank statements use acquired proofs, rather than further search. ForS n, writek=n−r.
The complete stable correspondence and its finite base counts [5] give constants 1,1,7,9,35 fork= 1, . . . ,5,
providedn≥2k+3. In degree twelve the remaining ranks havek≤4; in degrees thirteen and fourteen they
havek≤5. Each meets the threshold, and substitution gives the stated tails. The retained independent-
generator boundr≤n−1 forS n excludes all greater ranks. ForA 15, the complete acquired alternating-rank
theorem [6] givesr≤ ⌊(15−1)/2⌋= 7. This proves the theorem in every rank occurring in the two source
tables.
Certificate, provenance and scope
The twenty-two complete branch transcripts are stored with lossless compression. Their original uncom-
pressed hashes, terminal receipts, rank headings and complete literal outputs are checked before any tuple
is used. The checker reconstructs the same involution universe and positive lists from those outputs.
Its default mode repeats all actual group and interval checks and every literal conjugacy/reversal check.
The option--replay-enumerationadditionally repeats the unchanged enumeration branches with their
recorded individual limits and compares the complete results. The report explicitly distinguishes these
modes. Interrupted executions and incomplete transcripts cannot supply a count.
The final common validator differs from the previously executed positive validators only by selecting
the explicitly named target group and by its completion marker. Its canonical traversal is unchanged.
The final replay compares every resulting array with the original complete positive phase, allowing only
that literal marker change. It also checks the entire reversal permutation, rather than merely its number
of fixed points. Damaged completion and duplicate-class controls must reject, as must optimized Python
execution. The development record retains an initial extraction failure: the helper mistakenly included
intervening branch records after the involution list. It stopped before a mathematical validation; the
corrected delimiter changes no census transcript or mathematical engine.
The original numerical results remain attributed to the survey and its collaborators. This component
supplies complete recovery and verification for twenty-one formerly unrecovered experimental cells. The
high-rank entries were already acquired. No new open-conjecture result, priority, whole-original admission,
Lean certificate or compression measurement is claimed. The manuscript leaves the survey’s other claims
to their explicitly separate proofs and source qualifications.
