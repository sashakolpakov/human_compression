Orthogonal tori and their projective orders
Human Compression project
This working note supplies the torus interface for the orthogonal part of the generation
argument. It is not yet a reviewed component. It does not classify maximal overgroups or esti-
mate the centralizers of reversing involutions. In particular it does not claim the full existence
theorem.
