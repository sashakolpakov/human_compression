=============================

The saved permutations prove existing existence assertions. No whole-original,
conjecture, Lean or measurement credit is claimed.

  python3 check.py --out /fresh/absolute/path --public-root /path/to/human_compression

are reused from the public repository. dependencies.json identifies every
required file. --gap-root can point to the recorded GAP installation layout.

group classification and the Schulte--Weiss reconstruction theorem.
