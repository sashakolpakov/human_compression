subgroup containing the chosenxlies in one of the following actual proper subgroups: in PSL n, one extension-field
group (GL n/r(qr).r)∩Gfor each primer|n; in odd-dimensional PSU n, the corresponding unitary groups for

source’s excluded small parameters, is the named borrowed classification; it is not inferred from these containment
facts or a numerical sample. The acquired Bamberg erratum adds the odd-prime extension-field groups in odd
unitary dimension, already present in the source’s Table4.

Attribution and limits.The source is Leemans–Liebeck, arXiv1603.07713v3, Section4 and Tables3–5. The
new direct torus/descent proofs avoid its small-field application of Seitz Theorem2.8. The exact radical count
and scalar-fibre bounds pay previously implicit interfaces. Root suggested organizing reversers asT-torsors over
outer cosets; the present single-coset exclusions and linear/unitary estimates are this author’s deductions. The
unavailable full classification texts are not represented as acquired or reread, and the source’s incorrect unitary
degree-four half bound is not used. The finite35 component and classical lower theorem retain their separate
source and reviewer roles.
