Graphs of girth five and diameter four
=====================================

Five pages give the complete general middle-graph theorem, its exact
labelling interface for a finite census, and two explicit constructions
using biaffine planes. The lower-bound and middle-graph ideas belong to
the source authors. Biaffine amalgamation is inherited from earlier
literature; the current proof supplies the coordinate and diameter details.

This packet is a candidate for distinct mathematical review. It is not
a complete recovery of L013: the exact finite classifications, source
examples and their correspondence require the separate census packet.
No factor, original count, conjecture answer or new Lean claim is added.

Replay::

  python3 -B -O check.py --out /fresh/path --render --examples

The examples flag runs eleven optional checks, including the small
parameter exceptions. The uniform written proofs do not use those checks
as premises. check.py is a binding/build driver, and check_examples.py is
the entire optional validation program. No mathematical program or data
is required by the five-page theory itself. External classical proof
cost and historical source-software cost are unknown, not zero.
