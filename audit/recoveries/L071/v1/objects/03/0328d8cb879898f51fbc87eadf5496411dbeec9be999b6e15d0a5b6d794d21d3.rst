Independent review of compatible permutation actions
====================================================

Reviewer: publication_recovery. The author is root. The reviewer did not
contribute to the proposed proof or counterexample.

I read the complete two-page manuscript, its eight exported files, and
the source Theorem 4.1 and proof on physical pages 5--6 of the bound PDF.
Both source pages and both freshly rebuilt manuscript pages were viewed.
The source states that separate embeddings of G and Aut(G) in Sym(n)
suffice, then uses a compatible conjugation action in the proof. The
qualification in this packet accurately distinguishes those assertions.

Mathematical findings
---------------------

For an involution, every point is either fixed or lies on a unique edge
of its colour. Thus a colour-preserving bijection of these multigraphs
conjugates each distinguished permutation. Since each tuple generates
the entire embedded group, the bijection normalizes that group. The
reverse implication is immediate. Faithfulness makes the induced map
to Aut(G) well defined, and its kernel is exactly the centralizer.

If f induces alpha, then f(gx)=alpha(g)f(x), so the stabilizer of f(x)
is alpha(G_x). This proves necessity of the orbit multiplicity condition.
Conversely, equality of the multiplicities pairs every transitive orbit
with an orbit of the required stabilizer type. On cosets the displayed
map gH -> alpha(g)alpha(H) is well defined, bijective, and alpha-equivariant.
Conjugate choices of subgroup representatives yield equivariantly
isomorphic coset actions. Taking the disjoint union therefore realizes
alpha as a normalizing permutation. This argument covers intransitive
actions and repeated isomorphic orbits as well as transitive actions.

The regular action corollary follows because its only stabilizer is the
trivial group. The polytope conclusion uses the explicitly retained
standard correspondence between regular abstract polytopes and their
ordered string C-group generators. It does not silently prove that
correspondence. Similarly, the natural alternating-group application
retains the classical automorphism theorem for n >= 7.

The V4 example has involutions a=(1 2), b=(3 4)(5 6), and ab of three
transpositions. They have three distinct cycle types, so every permutation
normalizing this embedded V4 fixes all its elements by conjugation.
The abstract automorphism interchanging a and b is consequently not
induced. The two rank-two tuples are string C-groups, and their colour-zero
edge counts differ. An unrelated S3 embedding exists in Sym(6), exactly
as required to refute the literal weaker assumption. No rank or graph
convention conceals this counterexample.

Execution and scope
-------------------

I inspected the full checker. Its permutation multiplication is function
composition; conjugation, closure, and the exhaustive automorphism tests
use that convention consistently. The fresh exact enumeration of all 720
permutations on six points gives normalizer order 16, centralizer order
16, and trivial induced automorphism image. The regular V4 action has
normalizer order 24, centralizer order 4, and image order 6. These finite
checks corroborate the explicit example, not the general proof.

All eight exports and the one source binding match their pinned bytes
and hashes. Two fresh TeX passes succeeded. The two fresh PDF pages have
exactly the same extracted text as the author's PDF, and direct visual
inspection found no clipping or unreadable formulas.

The bounded theorem, corollary, and correction are accepted without
requested mathematical changes. The elementary classification of finite
G-sets is standard and is attributed as such. This review supplies no
whole L071 recovery, finite classification, open-conjecture answer, Lean
formalization, novelty assertion, or numerical compression factor.
