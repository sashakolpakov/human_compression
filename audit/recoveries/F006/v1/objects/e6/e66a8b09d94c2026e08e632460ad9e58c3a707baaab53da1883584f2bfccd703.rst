Restriction kernels in extensions of string markings
=====================================================

The `five-page paper <restriction_kernels.pdf>`_ gives an exact test for when
adding a transposition at a new point preserves a string C-marking. The test
uses pointwise stabilizers in consecutive groups generated by the old marks.
It needs at most k-1 substantive tests for a marking of rank k. The paper
also gives a necessary and sufficient criterion for subdividing a separated
split when one internal factor is trivial, with uniform positive families.

The proof distinguishes an induced action from permutations supported
independently on the same points. That distinction detects the lost parity
condition in the transitive counterexample to the unrestricted extension
statement. The standard string end criterion is retained and attributed;
the valid forward central twist is reproved. The remaining proofs are
complete in this paper. No historical priority is asserted.

The `review and evidence <../../../audit/restriction-kernels/v1/README.rst>`_
include three generic subgroup Lean proofs. The restriction maps and complete
string criteria remain written proofs. This adds supporting theory, with no
new original-manuscript admission, conjecture answer or compression factor.
The main high-rank stability theorem remains a separate problem for recovery.
