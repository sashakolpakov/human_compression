Rank contraction and rank intervals
===================================

This edition recovers the principal results of Brooksbank and Leemans,
Rank reduction of string C-group representations, arXiv:1812.01055v2
(3 May 2019; six pages). It counts as one original, L037.

Read the complete common theory `umbrella.pdf <packet/umbrella.pdf>`_ (three
pages), the application `rank_spectra.pdf <packet/rank_spectra.pdf>`_ (five
pages), and `programs.pdf <packet/programs.pdf>`_ (one page, the entire GAP
file). The corresponding TeX, code and Lean source are in packet/.

The common argument treats a central twist and an odd dihedral bond, then
proves contraction and iteration. Its elementary dihedral argument replaces
a classification citation. The application gives the source rank intervals,
its finite matrix example and all three alternating-group diagrams.

The contraction theorem and earlier construction seeds retain their original
attribution. The end criterion, named classical seeds and prior alternating
classification remain external inputs. The printed Corollary 1.3 needs an
indexing qualification, recorded explicitly in the application. No historical
priority claim is made. Conjecture 5.1 remains open in this work.

Four Lean lemmas verify the odd-power recovery and membership/character
interfaces. They do not formalize the full contraction induction, classical
seeds or the finite examples. Full source coverage and computational evidence
are detailed in `the admission <../../../audit/topic-rank-contraction/v1/admission.json>`_.
