Abstract
We identify actual automorphism groups of twenty-two marked quotients already certified
as regular rank-four hypertopes. Explicit homomorphisms, normal kernels and complements
establish the group descriptions in Table 3 of Fernandes–Leemans–Weiss. The proof uses no
new normal-subgroup search. The two excluded orders and the ninth diagram remain separate
obligations of the complete source contract.

Finite verification.The three certificate files contain only literal permutations, specified sub-
group generators, homomorphism images and outside elements. The verifier reads the original core
tuple and reconstructs every group from generators. It checks containment, exact orders, normal-
ity, intersections, elementary abelian tests, each claimed full generated group, and actual bijectivity
of each generator homomorphism. The twelve series records, nine direct-model records and one
central-extension record have exactly the22 distinct keys in Table 1. Five damaged certificates in-
dependently corrupt a model image, an elementary kernel, an outside involution, the order4 central
kernel, or the linear-group bridge; each is rejected.
These are direct positive certificates. Their earlier search routines are candidate providers only,
and their execution is not a completeness premise. The accepted core separately supplies the
necessary incidence mathematics and complete normal-quotient searches. Default replay checks
all22 groups and the five damaged inputs; it does not rerun those searches. Python optimization is
rejected before output. Complete native sources are included.
Sources, software and scope.The source is M. Fernandes, D. Leemans and A. I. Weiss, the
fixed sixteen-page L033 article, Table 3 on physical page14. Its positive group entries are the objects
recovered here; we make no priority claim. The exact permutation, homomorphism and finite-field
calculations use GAP4.15.1. Its documented PSL(2, q) constructor is the action of determinant-
one matrices on projective lines, and the standard alternating/symmetric constructors are named-
model interfaces. These software interfaces are retained explicitly, not formally verified by this
computation. The affine model itself is built from the stated vector action. The acquired LINS
completeness and finite classification premises are unchanged and are not used for a new census
here.
