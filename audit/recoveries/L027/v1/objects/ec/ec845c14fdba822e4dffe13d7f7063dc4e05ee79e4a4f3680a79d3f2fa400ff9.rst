Full mathematical audit
-----------------------

The span argument is uniform, including nonprime fields. The set of cube
differences is stable under multiplication by x^3 and contains x^3-1 != 0.
Its span is thus a module over F_p[x^3]. A proper subfield has size at most
sqrt(q), whereas the order of x^3 is at least (q-1)/3 > sqrt(q)-1 for q > 4.
Consequently the module is the whole field. The two resulting radical lines
have determinant x-1, so their sums give the full two-dimensional radical.
The torus exponent map is surjective modulo q-1 without division, hence it
works also when 3 divides q-1. This establishes the full SL end stabilizer
before the scalar center is removed.

The symmetric invertible D gives an involutory graph automorphism, with the
three displayed images checked exactly. Its action on the two radical
parameters is invertible; thus both opposite root pairs are in the generated
group. The two written commutators provide the remaining root groups.
Elementary elimination then establishes SL generation and hence PSL
generation, independently of overgroups.

For the actual projective intersection, preserving the two point and line
pairs forces a diagonal or a 23-monomial representative of determinant one.
The pair of ratios {1,x} admits only the stated diagonal ratio 1 or monomial
ratio x, since x^2 != 1. The determinant then yields exactly h(beta) or
h(beta)b, so no extra central scalar is ignored. Powers of c which also lie
in <b> must act scalarly on the entire point line; their ratio x^n forces
q-1 to divide n. The dual argument pays the other cyclic intersection.

The reversal proof treats the entire automorphism group. H has no fixed
point: its radical fixes exactly the point line, the diagonal torus leaves
only its two coordinate points as common fixed points, and b swaps them.
The dual end A has a fixed point but no fixed line. This rules out the graph
coset for an automorphism preserving A. For a semilinear candidate, the
unique involution axes fix p, and their first axis singles out e among the
three a-eigenpoints. The displayed form of the linear part follows. Its
projective scalar in the c intertwining equation equals one by evaluation
at p. Primitive x then excludes the inverse eigenvalue option because
p^i+1 < q-1; the fixed option removes the field automorphism. The three
individual c-eigenpoints force the linear part to be scalar, contradicting
inversion of a. The same reasoning works in characteristic two: the
rank-one matrices g+I still determine the unique involution axis.

The final orders account for the scalar center through x^(3m)=1 for b's
even powers. Odd powers swap the two points and cannot be scalar. The
explicit involutory graph automorphism carries the marking to the correct
inverse-reversed dual tuple. All hypotheses of the retained correspondence
are therefore met.
