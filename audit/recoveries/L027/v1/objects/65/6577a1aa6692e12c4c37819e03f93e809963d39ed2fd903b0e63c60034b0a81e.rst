Fixed PSU3(17) source clause: possible bounded witness route
==========================================================

This note is an unproved search/recovery route, not a new accepted result.
The source physical24 reports three enantiomorphic pairs of types [3,7,3],
[3,8,3], [3,9,3], without the [3] attribution attached to the previous q11
sentence. No target has been launched and no whole-source credit is due.

Let u,v,w be unitary involutions and put a=wv, b=vwu, c=uw. Then ab=u,
bc=v, abc=w, so the three interval-involution relations hold identically.
Require |uw|=|wv|=3. In odd characteristic not3, write each involution as
-I+2zz*/(z*z), with Hermitian conjugation t -> t^17. Normalize w with
centre e3 and u with centre (alpha,0,1), Norm(alpha)=3. A candidate v then
has centre (x,y,1), Norm(x)+Norm(y)=3. All relevant v occur, because
|wv|=3 is precisely Norm(v)=4 after its last coordinate is normalized1.
There are about5000 candidates (exact count not yet computed).

Test the three requested orders |b|=7,8,9, followed by actual generation,
all C-plus intersections and full-Aut chirality. A natural projective
permutation action has4914 isotropic points; any heavy call needs a strict
cap and completed-case receipts. Do not infer generation/IP from types.
Generic triples are likely to have improper maximal end groups, so this
is not already a construction.

For a potential chirality simplification, all automorphisms of PSU3(17)
are induced by PGU3(17) and the order2 field map. A reversing automorphism
fixes u,w and sends v to wvw (using |wv|=3). The linear projective
centralizer of u,w is diag(1,r,1), Norm(r)=1, since their2-dimensional
irreducible S3 space is span(e1,e3), with e2 its complement. The other
coset has representatives diag(alpha/bar(alpha),r,1) followed by the
field map. On centre (x,y,1), equality with (-x,-y,1) requires x=0 in
the linear case, or x/alpha+bar(x/alpha)=0 in the semilinear case; the
remaining y equation has a norm-one solution whenever y!=0. This needs
a careful zero-y/generation qualification before it is a theorem.

Completeness versus existence remains separate: testing these types can
recover their positive examples, but cannot assert that no other types
occur. The exact source wording must control any later census claim.
