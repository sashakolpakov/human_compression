Proof and finite completeness
-----------------------------

The flag enumerator exhausts every type subset by intersecting the exact
allowable object sets. Its induction on the ordered type set proves both
completeness and absence of repetitions. Comparing each complete flag list
with the projections of the full chamber set establishes that every partial
flag extends; merely computing a chamber orbit would not establish this.
The complete chamber orbit then proves flag transitivity. Its 20,160 distinct
images under a group of that order prove regularity and a trivial kernel.

Every literal subflag of a base chamber is considered. The group fixing that
subflag is computed from the complete group action, and its actual action on
each residue type is retained. Conjugation by flag transitivity transports
these checks to every flag. Rank at least two residues are connected, panels
have at least two extensions, and every residue has at least one primitive
type action. The primitive-action lemma is valid: orbital graph components
are invariant equivalence classes, and a pair in a nontrivial block yields
a disconnected graph. Rank-two residues are either complete bipartite or
have no quadrangle, exactly the required IP2 alternative.

Double transitivity is an output, not an assumption. The type-one panel
action in both rank-five constructions is regular C3 and is not doubly
transitive. The final flags are false,true,true,true,true. The earlier author
draft overstating this property and its correction remain preserved. Source
2.2 likewise has the already reviewed false,true local flags. No source
refutation is inferred from the corrected author prose.

Independent mathematical checks
------------------------------

independent.py imports no author helper. It uses a fixed multiplication table
for F4 and closes the two displayed matrices, normalizing scalar multiples.
It obtains 20,160 distinct determinant-one projective matrices and checks
their complete point actions. The classical ordered-basis count gives
|GL3(4)|=(64-1)(64-4)(64-16); determinant and scalar quotients each have order
three. Thus this closure is the full PSL3(4), not just a subgroup assigned
that name. Its actual two generators agree with the retained permutations
under the explicit coordinate map.

All 54,264 six-subsets are tested independently by determinants of triples,
rather than by the author's line masks. They give exactly the 168 retained
hyperovals and their three complete matrix-group orbits. Literal set
incidence replaces bit-mask incidence. Flags are enumerated in reverse type
order, and the lexicographically last chamber supplies the base flag,
different from the author's first chamber. Primitive actions are checked by
generating the invariant equivalence relation on each point pair using
disjoint sets; no author orbital-graph or point-stabilizer routine is used.

The complete independent run 83153 passes in 9.041 seconds. Across the two
models and eight route invocations it checks 308,619 flags, 300 primitive
actions and 4,142 neighbour pairs, with exact agreement on flag stabilizer
orders, local transitivity and extension numbers. These totals include
repeated truncations obtained from different parents; they are not counts
of globally distinct geometries or computations of a full classification.

normalizer_check.py independently reconstructs the diagonal and Frobenius
maps from the field coordinates. Their complete hyperoval-orbit actions
generate S3. Conjugation by an invertible diagonal matrix and field
Frobenius preserve determinant-one matrices, proving normalization of the
full group. All 24 saved maps are checked on every object and all 974,904
incidence/nonincidence pairs. This proves independence of the ordered choice
of hyperoval orbits; it does not compute the full automorphism group.
