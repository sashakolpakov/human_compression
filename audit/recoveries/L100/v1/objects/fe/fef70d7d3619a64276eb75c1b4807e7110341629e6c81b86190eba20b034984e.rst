Source and mathematical interfaces
----------------------------------

The fixed six-page source was read in extracted text; physical page 2 was
directly viewed. Section 2 states six rank-two geometries, including two
that occur as higher-rank truncations. Its Table 1 instead reports four
rank-two geometries after removing truncations. The present component
recovers the six-total clause only; it does not establish the truncation
statement, the four nontruncation count or any higher-rank result.

The full two-page current proof and both native pages were read. For an
actual flag-transitive G-action the stabilizer/coset identification is exact.
Primitivity on one type makes its stabilizer A maximal; local double
transitivity makes H maximal in A. First conjugating in G and then in A
normalizes the pair (A,H). B must subsequently range over actual overgroups,
not merely conjugacy classes. Conversely H<B<G and A intersection B=H imply
B is not contained in A, hence <A,B>=G; this proves connectivity. The two
indices prove firmness, and common coset representatives prove flag
transitivity. Neither local action is assumed faithful.

The common-neighbour formula uses the correct right-coset order:
Ab=Ada iff b(da)^(-1) lies in A. Double transitivity at A and transitivity
on its type propagate its value to every distinct pair sharing a neighbour.
Thus c=1 precisely excludes quadrangles, whereas the separate equality
[B:H]=[G:A] recognizes complete bipartiteness. The latter is essential and
is tested with c=2 in the independent control.

The declared quotient uses Aut(G) and optional type interchange. The four
literal bijective homomorphisms pay every identification among ten retained
pairs. The six distinct unordered parabolic-order profiles prove inequivalence,
without a premise that a computed automorphism group was exhaustive. For
this particular six-row result these profiles also give distinct unordered
type sizes |HS|/|A|,|HS|/|B|, so even an unrestricted incidence isomorphism
cannot merge two rows. This observation does not enlarge the declared
source contract or recover full automorphism/correlation groups.
