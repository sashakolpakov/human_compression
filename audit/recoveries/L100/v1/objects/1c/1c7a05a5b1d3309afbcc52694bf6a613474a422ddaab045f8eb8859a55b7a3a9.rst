Literal source joins for the PSL(3,4) catalogue
==============================================

Status and edition
------------------

This is an author working component, not a distinct acceptance or a whole-paper
claim. The source is the complete 22-page Halle Reports on Algebra and Geometry
99-30 preprint, downloaded as PostScript and locally converted to PDF. Its exact
documentary record distinguishes it from European Journal of Combinatorics 24
(2003), 267--291. The 25-page journal edition is not acquired or collated here;
no assertion about its literal diagrams follows from these preprint readings.

The acquired census supplies all 145 rank-at-least-two families in the fixed
permutation group, with all 95 Borel anchors exhausted. The separately acquired
full-Aut/type fusion gives 29 representatives. The acquired palette calculation
and its exact serialization repair give the full literal coset incidence
structures, their geometric automorphism/correlation generators and orders,
and 29 distinct bare typed-incidence classes. The group/subgroup inventories,
full Aut calculation and GRAPE/nauty completeness remain explicit computational
and classification premises; this component does not recreate those searches.

Source nodes and edges
---------------------

Every diagram node is identified by its actual position on native pages7--13,
not by an inherited type number. diagram_inventory02.json records its printed
type size, panel order and boxed local-kernel order, every visible edge and
every absent edge, as well as local-2T markers and source fusion multiplicities.
The earlier provisional inventory is preserved: its two mistaken positive
2T markers for3.5 and4.11 are corrected here. The source has neither marker.
All seven pages were inspected again at220dpi; this also distinguishes single
and double bonds and separates numeric edge labels from nearby group exponents.

For each literal geometry choose the identity-coset chamber. For a type subset
J the residue consists exactly of objects of types in J incident with every
base object of complementary type. Actual flag transitivity, already acquired,
transports this base computation to every flag of the same type. In particular:

* A panel order is its cardinality minus one. The relevant intersection of
  parabolics acts on that panel; transitivity of a point stabilizer on all other
  panel objects is tested literally, without a2T pruning assumption.
* Each rank-two residue is retained as a complete finite bipartite graph.
  Breadth-first distances give each type's eccentricities; the minimum cycle
  length divided by two is its gonality. Complete bipartite residues are digons.
* The local kernel at an object is exactly the subset of its parabolic fixing
  every object in its residue. Every element is tested through its actual
  right-coset permutation. No structure is inferred from kernel order alone.

All printed circular labels are checked by mapping each degree-two object to
its two endpoints and obtaining every pair exactly once. Each printed Petersen
label is mapped explicitly to the disjointness graph on the ten2-subsets of a
five-set, with its15 edges. Each affine label is checked on16 points and20 lines:
unique lines on point pairs and five parallel classes give a projective
completion. An explicit21-point map takes every completed line to the retained
coordinate PG(2,4). All resulting incidence rows are checked. These52 maps
establish named rank-two geometries, not just matching girth or diameter.

There is one actual catalogue representative for each of the29 source diagram
signatures, after recording the discrepancies below. That is a comparison
within an acquired complete finite catalogue, not a general assertion that a
diagram determines a geometry. Abstract names of Borel/parabolic/kernel/full
automorphism groups are not settled merely by their orders.

Literal construction maps
-------------------------

The accepted projective construction and census use different ordered generator
lists. The complete generated permutation groups are checked equal as sets on
the same21 labels. No coordinate labels are silently identified: the acquired
coordinate-to-retained map is reused. Direct equivariant joins with six ordered
hyperoval choices do not reach some representatives, so an actual duality is
used. A coordinate normal vector labels its orthogonal projective line; the
resulting21-line permutation is transported back to the fixed labels. This
map is checked bijective on all20,160 group elements and on every generator
edge, proving that it is an automorphism of this actual group.

For a catalogue parabolic H_i and a proposed construction object x_i, every
generator of H_i fixes x_i after the chosen automorphism alpha. The map

    H_i g  ->  x_i ^ alpha(g)

is therefore well defined. The implementation additionally checks every coset
member, every object bijection and every incidence and nonincidence row. The
explicit type permutation is retained. The two full constructions identify
preprint5.1 with catalogue26 and5.2 with catalogue28. All eight previously
accepted truncation routes are checked by the same literal maps. This involves
ten positive maps, not a fresh graph-isomorphism or subgroup census.

Preprint corrections and limits
-------------------------------

In both5.1 and5.2 the unique210-object node is construction type5, the unordered
pairs of projective points. The accepted construction and explicit maps show
that its panel order is1. The native preprint diagrams print2. The other
21-object node of order2 is the line type and is correctly printed. The actual
type correspondence prevents interchanging these two readings.

The preprint diagram3.5 draws no bond between its two120-object nodes. This is
also inconsistent with its other printed data under the ordinary convention
that an absent bond means a digon. The1120-object stabilizer has order18, and
the Borel has order3, so its rank-two residue has six chambers. Both remaining
panel orders are1, so every residue vertex has degree2. Flag transitivity gives
three vertices of each type; residual connectedness forces the six-cycle.
Thus the missing bond must have gonality3. The actual catalogue gives exactly
this six-cycle. This deduction does not depend on naming a geometry from its
diagram, or on a new subgroup classification.

All three observations refer only to the fixed preprint. These corrections do
not supply the unpaid constructions, doubling hypotheses, abstract group-name
identifications, or journal-edition comparison. No source, question, Lean or
measurement credit is claimed by this working component.
