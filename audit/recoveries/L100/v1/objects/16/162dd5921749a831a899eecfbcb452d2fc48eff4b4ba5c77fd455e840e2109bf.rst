Bounded L100 construction component
==================================

The fixed source is the 22-page Halle preprint, not an uncollated journal
edition. Native physical pages 12--16 have been read directly. The next
component concerns the literal constructions 5.1 and 5.2, Lemmas 4.1--4.3,
Table 1, and only those truncation identifications that receive explicit
finite maps. It will not assert higher-rank exhaustion, full automorphism
groups, conjugacy-fusion counts, doubling, or recovery of the whole source.

Existing evidence is the accepted rank-two result.g and its two generators
of the named degree-21 PSL(3,4). No previous higher-rank executable or census
was found in the inspected source directory or the L100 work directories.
The source intake explicitly records that absence. No unchanged census will
be repeated.

Finite boundary
---------------

Construct PG(2,4) literally, with GF(4)=F2[w]/(w^2+w+1). Obtain a line in
the retained permutation action from the five-point orbit of the accepted
order-960 line stabilizer. Build and verify an explicit incidence-preserving
bijection from coordinate points to the retained 21 labels. For each actual
retained generator reconstruct a determinant-one matrix and check all 21
images. Thus no agreement of two independently ordered point sets is assumed.

The complete 54,264 six-subsets are tested for the hyperoval condition.
Their orbits under the two literal generators are found by exact closure.
The construction then has 21 lines, 21 points, two 56-element hyperoval
orbits and 210 pairs. Its ten relations are exactly those printed on p14;
5.2 changes only the line--second-hyperoval relation on p16. All flags are
enumerated by intersecting exact adjacency sets, including maximal partial
flags, so chamber counts alone cannot mask non-geometry defects.

For each constructed model, compare its complete chamber set with the orbit
of one chamber under the literal group. Check every residue of a base chamber
by explicit incidence graphs; flag transitivity transports these checks.
Residual weak primitivity is tested on the actual flag stabilizer action,
using the elementary connected-orbital-graph criterion, not a local2T filter.
Rank-two common-neighbour tests pay IP2 and all rank-one local actions are
reported separately. Table 1 is checked at every triple flag, or equivalently
by a proved flag orbit together with its exact base extensions.

Truncations retain the literal objects and incidence. Their maps are identity
maps on the retained types; any further claimed isomorphism will have an
explicit object bijection checked against every incidence. A finite diagram
profile alone will not be used as an isomorphism certificate.

Limits
------

The planned checks are finite construction certificates, not a classification
of all PSL(3,4) geometries. Source automorphism, correlation, kernel and fusion
claims remain unpaid unless separately stated and checked. No external
sources, Lean, measurements or new original intake are added. All work is
private under geometry/psl3_4; predecessor editions remain unchanged.
