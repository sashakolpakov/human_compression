The mathematical route
----------------------

The enumeration is complete under the specified G-action and induced
Aut(G), optionally type-swapping, equivalence. Primitivity makes A maximal;
local double transitivity makes H maximal in A. Conjugation first in G,
then in A, normalizes these two choices. At that point B must range over
actual overgroups of H. It is not sufficient to take G-conjugacy classes
of overgroups without retaining their positions relative to A and H.
The converse uses H < B and A intersection B = H to obtain B not contained
in A, whence maximality gives generation and connectivity. Flag
transitivity follows directly from a common representative of incident
right cosets.

is understandable, but this explicit form pays the firmness sentence
without any convention about double transitivity on a singleton. The
actual driver already tests n > 1. No faithfulness of the local actions

absence of a quadrangle; the separate degree equality is precisely
