Distinct review checked all 76 fractions, 49 CSV rows and 139 actual bars
in each vector format. All six reader pages were read and viewed. Frozen
author files retain their candidate wording; the later `root admission
<admission.json>`_ gives the current accepted status. Earlier editions are
preserved without changes to their proofs, costs or historical counts.

There are now 35 measured and 35 admitted principal-result recoveries, out
of 106 accessible originals in the fixed 161-title programme. The remaining
71 accessible originals await complete recovery and 55 await retrieval.
These categories are maintained separately; every accessible original
remains in the programme. This update adds no mathematical, source-intake,
conjecture or formalization credit. Each topic pays its whole common theory
independently, including overlap with other topics.

A factor near one shows little saving by these particular proofs and this
length measure. It does not establish originality or minimum proof complexity.
For example D has source cost 47,894, applications 29,855 and common theory
15,632, giving 1.053 overall. Its applications save about 38 percent before
that acquisition cost. Further reuse must be demonstrated by complete
applications before it can improve the measured result.
