\section{The mathematical object being recovered}

Before comparing lengths, each original receives an edition-bound source
contract. Its principal results, parameter ranges, exceptional cases, geometric
identifications and computational obligations are stated explicitly. A complete
application must prove the relevant hypotheses of the common theory and supply
all local arguments needed for those outputs. Retained classical theorems,
classification tables and catalogue constructions remain named premises.

A false source assertion is recorded as a correction, not as a theorem proved
with its original wording. Unrecovered ancillary statements, illustrations,
classifications and open questions remain explicit. Recovery of the principal
contract does not assert reproduction of every assertion in the source.
Consequently, a full-source length comparison is a comparison for the declared
principal outputs, not a claim that every source passage has an equivalent
replacement passage. The source still contributes its complete counted
manuscript, including ancillary exposition and references.

Scientific acceptance, input acceptance and numerical acceptance are separate.
An author packet, distinct review and later admission retain their exact bytes
and roles. Earlier candidate labels in a frozen packet are interpreted through
its subsequent admission. Existing proofs can be reorganized or reused, but
moving a proof between files does not itself establish a new mathematical result.

\section{Whole topics and conditional applications}

For a topic $T$, write $L_1,\ldots,L_k$ for the complete source manuscripts,
$U_T$ for its entire common theory, and $A_1,\ldots,A_k$ for its complete
applications. An application includes every required written companion.
Under the topic's fixed manuscript counting convention $c_T$, the whole factor is
\[
 R_T=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^k c_T(L_i)}
 {c_T(U_T)+\sum_{i=1}^k c_T(A_i)}.
\]
All common papers and additions actually used in the topic belong to $U_T$.
The whole common theory is charged once within that topic. Separate topics pay
their own common theories in full, even when their arguments overlap. There is
no cross-topic pooled factor or unrecorded discount for previously written work.

The individual comparison is
\[
 r_{T,i}\mid\mathcal K_T=\frac{c_T(L_i)}{c_T(A_i)}.
\]
Here $\mathcal K_T$ includes the \emph{whole} common theory, the declared
required programs and data, and any retained mathematical premises. Their
acquisition costs and unknown costs are reported separately. Any additional
sibling-application premise must be included explicitly in the acquisition
boundary; it cannot be supplied for free. The accepted current A edition, for
example, pays all 41 common pages across three complete papers and all ten applications in its whole-topic
comparison, rather than allocating only selected common paragraphs to each one.

A factor greater than one records shortening in the stated coordinate; a factor
below one records expansion. A conditional saving may coexist with a whole-topic
expansion. Such results are retained without changing the metric or dropping
applications to obtain a preferred value. These factors are not measures of
historical effort, mathematical originality or minimum proof complexity.

\section{Text representations and sensitivity}

The input protocol is fixed and reviewed before factors are evaluated. It binds
the exact source edition, every complete replacement PDF, document boundaries,
software masks, font evidence, normalization and units. Titles, abstracts,
body text, captions and references remain included. Exact running furniture
can be removed only in the declared selected-text coordinates; an untrimmed
coordinate preserves it. Some accepted topics use that untrimmed coordinate
as primary. The overview retains each topic's accepted choice rather than
silently replacing them with a single new convention.

The usual sensitivity family combines raw untrimmed, raw selected and
calibrated selected text with NFC and NFKC normalization and five units:
whitespace-separated words, alphanumeric runs, non-whitespace characters,
characters with collapsed whitespace, and UTF-8 bytes with collapsed whitespace.
The exact definitions and any additional representation sensitivities are those
of each frozen input protocol. A display-rounding choice changes no exact
fraction. A near-unit factor may reverse under another accepted unit; this is
reported rather than suppressed.

Calibration identifies the actual PDF font dictionary and encoding, the text
event and the visible glyph. Font names alone need not identify an encoding.
Repairs, including visible glyphs extracted as whitespace, require occurrence
and outline evidence. A chosen proxy is declared as a proxy. Raw extraction
remains available for sensitivity; calibration is not a transcription of all
visual mathematical content.

Complete authored renderings, including the accepted N001 and L108 sources,
are distinguished from unavailable original PDFs. Website navigation or
duplicated reference widgets are not article text. Images, diagrams, layout and
unextracted labels have unknown cost; their absence from text extraction is not
a zero-cost estimate. In particular, the L035 source contains scientific image
content not represented by its extracted text. All current factors are subject
to their documented representation limits.

\section{Symmetric software treatment and proof artifacts}

The current scalar is manuscript-only. Concrete executable program and
implementation-listing costs are excluded symmetrically from source and
replacement. This rule applies to required computations as well as optional
validation. Written mathematical algorithms and proof prose remain counted on
both sides. A software role alone cannot justify counting a source listing
while omitting the corresponding replacement program.

For L008, L028 and N006, independently reviewed masks remove the exact embedded
implementation blocks, retaining surrounding mathematical statements and
exposition. Their restored manuscript comparisons use those accepted boundaries.
The \href{overview/v11/listing\_audit/README.rst}{listing audit} and
\href{overview/v12/matched\_inputs/README.rst}{matched input packet} preserve the
locators and sensitivity evidence. Earlier one-sided software-inclusive values
remain historical observations and are not current comparisons.

Required computations remain proof premises. Each topic inventories every
whole required program, certificate, mathematical data file and decoder, with
separate roles for optional validation, execution drivers and partial formal
artifacts. Reused identical content may be counted once within an actual topic
dependency closure, with every occurrence and role disclosed. No file is pruned
merely because a shorter word certificate or a previously run search is available.
Retained external representations and catalogues remain explicit trust and
acquisition boundaries.

A printed program appendix and its raw files are alternative representations,
not two additive copies of one program. A printed appendix need not contain a
required data file; the data still belongs in the complete inventory. Historical
source software and full external proof costs are generally unknown. Accordingly,
there is no current software-inclusive factor. No missing cost is interpreted
as zero, and incompatible units such as pages and bytes are never added.

\section{Proof dependencies and formal evidence}

A logical graph records actual prerequisite-to-use relations with source and
proof locators. Mathematical premises, definitions, retained theorems,
computations, validation, attribution and operational dependencies have distinct
roles. A citation's presence does not by itself prove logical dependence.
A graph accepted for an earlier edition is not automatically a complete graph
for an enlarged topic.

Where passage costs have been accepted, each counted passage has one owner.
For an output $t$, let $D(t)$ be its prerequisite closure and $P(v)$ the passage
owners used by node $v$. The known passage cost is
\[
 W_{\rm known}(t)=
 \sum_{p\in\bigcup_{v\in D(t)}P(v),\;c(p)\text{ known}}c(p).
\]
The union prevents repeated charging of shared passages. Unknown costs remain
listed separately; context outside that closure is still paid in the
whole-manuscript comparison. Parent and child views must preserve this total.
Raw node counts are not compression factors.

For an explicit acquisition order and a previously acquired library $K_{i-1}$,
a sequential account can report the new closure cost
$c(D_i\setminus K_{i-1})$. It requires actual accepted dependencies and costs;
it does not establish a sawtooth pattern in advance or alter separate topics'
whole-common charges.

Lean evidence is limited to the actual compiled declarations and their stated
hypotheses, dependencies and axiom reports. A theorem assuming an unproved
recognition or geometric hypothesis does not formalize that hypothesis.
Successful compilation and declaration counts do not establish complete source
formalization. Finite certificates likewise require an explicit argument from
the checked computation to the claimed result. The current numerical comparison
adds no complete-Lean or conjecture-answer claim.

\section{Reproduction and historical records}

Current TeX/PDFs, source maps and admissions are routed through the public
catalogue. Original source assets are separately supplied where redistribution
is unavailable. Portable input and arithmetic checkers record their exact
versions, hashes and output boundaries. The canonical builder uses each
manuscript's declared compiler and full TeX inputs. Repository integrity checks,
formal compilations and expensive finite replays are different operations;
evidence states which actually ran.

Historical editions remain immutable. The
\href{documentation/v1/previous/README.rst}{preceding README} and
\href{documentation/v1/previous/protocol.pdf}{preceding protocol} retain the old
chronology and comparisons. The \href{intake.json}{intake record},
\href{archive\_markdown.json}{archived notes} and earlier overview admissions
remain evidence for their stated editions. A documentation update supersedes
reader-facing status and policy statements without rewriting those records.
Separately reviewed question answers retain their own exact scope; an absent
historical proof has no measured length and does not produce an infinite
compression factor.
