Steiner application: independent written and source review
=========================================================

This packet reviews the thirty-five L092 clauses in the complete current
``steiner-geometries`` application and its common Steiner interfaces. It is
not the review of L009, the entire Topic G edition, or a new source intake.
Root separately reviews the common paper and triangle application.

The full fourteen-page L092 source text, all current Steiner TeX sections,
the common S.1--S.6 proofs and H.1--H.3 interface were read. The two retained
classification statements were checked at their actual primary locations:
BDL Theorem 6.2 on printed pages 42--43 and Kantor Theorem 3 with its proof
on page 73. The entire twenty-two-page application was visually inspected;
source Figures 2 and 3 and the page containing Lemma 5 were viewed directly.
``proof_reading.json`` records a mathematical finding for every source row.

The review requested one additive notation repair: the order-five quotient
in B.SemilinearSixteen is F16*/F4*, not the rendered F16/F4. The original
author freeze is preserved. The final review binds the separate correction
and checks that its source rows and mathematical arguments are unchanged.

The reviewer contributed the earlier Hermitian transport and path-length
work. Their distinct root acceptances, phases 275 and 208, are reused and
explicitly bound. This review does not call those inherited contributions
new independently adjudicated proofs. The current Steiner exposition and
source interface were written by a different agent. The separately frozen
program/formal review is reused without another GAP or Lean run.

coverage and the recorded scope, with negative controls. It checks evidence
integrity; it is not a mechanized verification of the written proofs.
Use its ``--help`` for explicit packet, correction, study and repository
arguments. No primary text, source image, private Markdown proof or build
product is exported here. No new principal, conjecture or formal credit and
