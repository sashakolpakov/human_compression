Independent review of the mix criteria
=====================================

This review accepts the complete corrected principal-result contract for
F005, Cunningham and Hubard, arXiv:2506.19334v1. It binds the unchanged
main author packet and its separate addition on two medial orbits.
The primary has 21 physical pages; the complete replacement has five
common pages, twelve local pages and seven pages printing all three
mathematical programs. Measurement is a separate step.

The common argument repairs the first coordinate of an orbit intersection
and tests the remaining fiber. The review distinguishes this argument
from the retained geometric path characterization, normalizer actions,
and the hypotheses needed to reduce the number of flags tested.

The original's 38 recorded entries comprise 35 principal or background
dispositions and three final open problems. The latter remain unanswered.
Five literal printed assertions have counterexamples; two further
counterexamples use the explicit middle-color convention described in
the review. These are corrections to source assertions, not new answers
to those three problems. Historical priority is not claimed.

The distinct programs reconstruct six permutation actions, a pair of
regular input markings, and a 144-flag affine polytope with a polytopal
2592-flag regular cover. They import no author model. Their complete
finite intersection checks support the written proofs; they are not
general theorems or Lean certificates. The two generic orbit/fiber Lean
declarations were separately replayed with standard axioms. The geometric
applications and finite counterexamples are not formalized end to end.

``check.py`` accepts explicit paths to both frozen author packets and
an optional primary PDF. With ``--run-models`` it reruns the independent
models and six semantic controls. An omitted primary is reported as
not rechecked; no private primary PDF, extracted text, font or raster
is exported. ``review.json`` records mathematical acceptance and its
attribution limits; ``visual_review.json`` records the actual page review.
Neither this packet nor its replay grants another original or a numerical
compression factor. Those require the subsequent measured admission.
