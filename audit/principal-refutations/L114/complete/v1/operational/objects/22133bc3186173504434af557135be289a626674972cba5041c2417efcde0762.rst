Source application, before any returned outcome
----------------------------------------------

Set G0=<a,b>, G1=<a,c>, G2=<b,c>. Suppose the returned lists prove that

For missing type0, every such coset has a representative in G1. List

actual panel. If more than two occur, this particular geometry disproves
the literal universal thinness conclusion, despite meeting the stated

One panel is enough to detect the issue. G is transitive on the incident
edges of each type pair, since two incident cosets have a common
