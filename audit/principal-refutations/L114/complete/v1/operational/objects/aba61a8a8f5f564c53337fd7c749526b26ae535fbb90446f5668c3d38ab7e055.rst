Preliminary results for the rank-two Suzuki source
=================================================

The final manuscript is preliminaries.tex and native03/preliminaries.pdf
(five pages). Root authored the proof and directly inspected all five
native pages. A distinct mathematical review is still required.

The component treats precisely source L115 Lemmas 3.1--3.7, 5.1--5.3 and
6.1. Lemma 3.2 receives its necessary proper-subfield qualification. The
remaining numbered statements, family tables and global counts do not
receive credit here. No open conjecture, Lean theorem or compression
measurement is claimed.

The matrix/root-group conventions, standard ovoid realization and Suzuki
maximal-subgroup classification remain explicit classical premises. The
general group lemmas and arithmetic are proved in the manuscript. The
already accepted scalar-subspace correction is retained as provenance;
none of its field computations is repeated.

Small controls
--------------

Run check_arithmetic.py with ordinary Python. It checks 50 odd exponents
and 240 subfield parameters. Run controls.g with GAP -q -b -T -m 64m
-K 512m, under a 60-second bound. The successful controls enumerate the
actual maximal subgroups of the order-448 point stabilizer and 34 small
cyclic semidirect actions, including actions that are not faithful.
These controls supplement the written proofs and are not premises for
their assertions in arbitrary parameters. A successful GAP control must
have empty stderr and the final PASS_L115_PRELIMINARY_CONTROLS marker;
the process exit status alone is insufficient.
