Suzuki geometries: three separately scoped source corrections
=============================================================

This package supplies reviewed counterexamples to specific principal claims
in three papers by Dimitri Leemans. It does not recover every true result
in those papers or provide replacement complete classifications. Read the
eleven-page suzuki_refutations.pdf volume, or the separate complete TeX/PDF
manuscripts under L110/proof, L114/proof and L115/proof. Their lengths are
three, four and four pages. There are no adjacent TeX inputs.

The sources and the exact failures
---------------------------------

L110 is The residually weakly primitive pre-geometries of the Suzuki simple
groups, Note di Matematica 20(1)(2000/2001),1–20. Its Theorem 5 and literal
Construction 11 omit a rank-four example in Sz(2^3465). The four parabolic
field degrees are1155,693,495,315; their 16 distinct intersections have 32
prime-degree maximal inclusions. The source's pure-subfield construction
permits rank only through 3 for these four distinct prime divisors. The other
ten constructions require soluble parabolics. The argument uses the exact
weak subgroup RWPRI definition. No geometry or flag-transitivity conclusion
for this rank-four example is needed or asserted.

L114 is The rank 3 geometries of the simple Suzuki groups Sz(q), Note di
Matematica 19(1)(1999),43–63. Theorem 5.5 omits an actual rank-three geometry
in Sz(32768), with parabolics Sz(8),Sz(32),C533:C4. Their pairwise meets
have orders 20,52,164 and the triple meet has order 4. All seven RWPRI
conditions hold. A subgroup-product factorization proves flag transitivity;
the panels have sizes 5,13,41 and every required residue is connected.
For exponent 15=3*5 only source shapes 1–5 apply. The only matching type
pattern requires the two Suzuki parabolics to meet in order 8, not 20.
The source's later qualification about realization of listed lattices does
not remove this omission from its necessary list.

The same L114 note separately refutes Theorem 6.1's composite-q-minus-one
uniqueness assertion. The acquired L115 family has local degrees 65 and8,
whereas the unique geometry claimed in that theorem has degrees 2 and 32768.
The difference already defeats bare incidence isomorphism. The rank-three
example itself is not locally doubly transitive and is not used to claim
otherwise. No geometric intersection property or higher-rank extension is
imported from the old compatible-tori work.

L115 is The Rank 2 Geometries of the Simple Suzuki Groups Sz(q), Beiträge
zur Algebra und Geometrie 39(1)(1998),97–120. Its Lemma 6.9 uses the full
ambient centre where maximality requires a two-dimensional scalar subspace.
The resulting covers are absent from Theorem 7.1/Table 11. At q=32768,s=8,
there are 585 inner-conjugacy classes in the exhibited family and 41 orbits
under Aut(G), exceeding Table 12's printed total 14. Both local actions are
doubly transitive, of degrees 65 and8. The forty-one count uses the source's
equivalence under automorphisms of the specified Suzuki group. It is not
a count of bare incidence isomorphism classes or the corrected global total.
