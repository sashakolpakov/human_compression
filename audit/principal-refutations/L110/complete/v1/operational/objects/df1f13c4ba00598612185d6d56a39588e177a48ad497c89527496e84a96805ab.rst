Mathematical audit
------------------

1. Overgroups and local actions. With Q=VH, V abelian and normal, every element
of L>=H can be written vh with h already in L, so L=(L intersect V)H. The
inverse construction TH has intersection T precisely because V intersect H is
contained in T. H-invariance is both necessary and sufficient. The regular
quotient V/(V intersect H) on cosets, and its stabilizer's conjugation action,
give the asserted block and double-transitivity criteria without a faithfulness
assumption on H. Full nonzero K-scalars force exactly K-linear invariant
subspaces. The maximal extensions therefore have quotient K-dimension one.

2. Suzuki coordinates. I independently checked the displayed multiplication
law and diagonal weights, including the convention U(a,b)h(t), rather than
substituting a different common Suzuki convention. The exponent restriction
to K is valid because n/d is odd, and (n-d)/2 is divisible by d. The elementary
gcd proof for theta+2 is valid for every odd n. Thus H_K induces all K-star
scalars on the center, and U_K acts trivially there. The center intersection
with B is exactly Z_K. The order |Q_T|=|T|s(s-1), the covering condition
dim_K T=2, and <B,U(0,c)>=Z_(K+Kc)B follow from the actual group law.

For prime extension degree r>=3, the full additive quotient F/K has dimension
r-1>=2 even though there is no intermediate field. Consequently B is not
maximal in Z_F B. At s=8,q=512 the explicit orders 448<3584<28672 are correct.
This refutes exactly the inference in the source's Lemma6.9 proof on printed112.

3. Actual incidence geometry. Unique upper/lower triangular coordinates show
S intersect Q_T=B: the coordinates a,t already lie in K, and the remaining
matrix entries lie in K exactly when b does. This uses the faithful standard
subfield model, not a subgroup-order guess. Q_T is not contained in S; the
retained maximality of S therefore gives <S,Q_T>=G. The rank-two coset graph
is connected, and every incident pair is a translate of the base pair. The
local valencies are s^2+1 and s, so the system is firm. In rank two this pays
all required residual connectivity. B is maximal in S by the primitive ovoid
action and in Q_T by the proved scalar criterion, while S is maximal in G.
These are precisely the source RWPRI conditions. The two actual local actions
are the ovoid action and the affine line action K:K-star, both doubly transitive.
No inference from a quotient group having the same order is used.

4. Equivalence. If an inner automorphism maps (S,Q_T) to (S,Q_T'), it preserves
their intersections B. Simplicity and maximality give N_G(S)=S. A point
stabilizer in this doubly transitive ovoid action fixes no second point, hence
N_S(B)=B. Thus the inner transporter is in B, whose central action is by
K-scalars and preserves each T. Conversely equality of T plainly gives the
same configuration. Field automorphisms preserve S and B; after the inner
part is removed, the full transporter is exactly B:<phi>. Type reversal is
excluded by solubility. The resulting equivalence on T is exactly Frobenius
on projective K-lines of F/K. This pays both the conjugacy and full specified-
action isomorphism claims, rather than counting only labelled embeddings.

5. Analytic counts. The odd-order averaging argument gives the exact fixed
additive quotient (F/K)^sigma = F^sigma/K^sigma. For n=9, phi^3 is K-linear
of order3 and has neither a nontrivial eigenvalue in K-star nor a nonzero
fixed quotient vector. Hence all nine projective points lie in one phi-orbit.
For n=15, phi^3 similarly fixes no projective point. Each power phi^j with
5 not dividing j generates a group containing phi^3, so fixes none either.
For sigma=phi^5, the fixed additive quotient has 32/2=16 elements. If
sigma(v)=lambda v, scalar normalization uses the bijection k->k^3 on K-star;
because sigma acts by fourth powers on K, this indeed makes kv fixed. Each
invariant K-line has exactly one nonzero fixed vector, giving15 invariant
lines. The same count holds for phi^10. Burnside gives(585+15+15)/15=41,
with3 orbits of length5 and38 of length15. No finite search is a premise
of this analytic proof.

6. Source contradiction. For s=8, Q_T has order3584 and a nonabelian normal
Sylow2 subgroup: U_K is already nonabelian by its displayed root law. It is
neither the source's full-center mixed group, another Suzuki group, a dihedral
group, nor a cyclic odd group extended by C4. A configuration with the sole
nonsoluble parabolic Sz(8) must occur in the corresponding source rows; a
type interchange cannot hide this missing soluble parabolic. The table's
claimed exhaustive list therefore omits genuine RWPRI configurations. The
native Table12 entry e=7 is literally14, and its convention is the specified-
action convention already checked. Forty-one inequivalent members of this
one family disprove that total. No assertion about the other rows or the
correct total across all families is required.
