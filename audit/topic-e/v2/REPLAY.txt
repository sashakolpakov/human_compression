Current common proofs and measurements

From the repository root:
  python3 papers/topic-e/v2/check_common.py --out /fresh/finite-run

With pypdf 6.13.0 and separately supplied source PDFs:
  python3 audit/topic-e/v2/measure.py --repo . --sources-root PRIMARY_ROOT --protocol audit/topic-e/v2/protocol.json --out /fresh/measurement.json
  python3 audit/topic-e/v2/recount.py --repo . --sources-root PRIMARY_ROOT --out /fresh/independent.json

The primary PDF paths, exact hashes, glyph calibration and page furniture
are specified in protocol.json. The primary PDFs are not redistributed.

The first command replays all required finite calculations, diagnostic
samples and five mutations. The latter commands recount full documents
under the same boundary using two implementations. Mathematical acceptance
and all retained inputs are in admission.json and independent_proof_review.json.
Measurement acceptance is separate, in measurement_admission.json.

Overall written compression and conditional application compression are
the headline measures. Complete required-program costs and the diagnostic
sensitivity are separate. Unknown historical code and classical-library
proof costs are not zero. Partial Lean verification is inventoried in
formal_inventory.json; full formalization cost remains unknown.

The previous proof edition is a baseline, with three full application
contracts on both sides. The earlier two-source audit remains one level
above this directory. New source or solved-question credits are not added.
