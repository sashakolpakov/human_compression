\noindent The specified main results of three historical manuscripts follow
from one common theory and three complete applications. Counting the full
source manuscripts and the full replacement gives
\[
 R_E=\frac{c(L083)+c(L079)+c(L021)}{c(U_E)+c(A_1)+c(A_2)+c(A_3)}
 =\frac{71{,}375}{39{,}303}=\boxed{1.816019\ldots}.
\]
Here $c$ counts NFC characters other than whitespace in the bound PDF
extractions. The entire common theory is paid once in this topic. Any
overlap with another topic does not reduce that charge.

Once the common theory and its required program are available, the complete
applications have the following conditional factors:

Each conditional factor is $c(L_i)/c(A_i)$ given the complete shared
theory and program. It does not describe the cost of proving that
application independently. No introductory text, proof, reference or
required local argument has been removed from the application cost.

The sources are Leemans--Schulte's \emph{Groups of type $L_2(q)$ acting on
polytopes} and \emph{Polytopes with groups of type $PGL_2(q)$}, and
Leemans--Toledo's \emph{Maniplexes with automorphism group $PSL(2,q)$}.
The exact author editions and results recovered are fixed in the protocol.
L079's semilinear census and conjecture, and L021's final PGL question,
are excluded from those results; their printed source text is nevertheless
counted. The previously reviewed corrections to L021 remain included.

The common theorem concerns four distinct string involutions whose two
end groups are proper and isomorphic to $S_4$ or $A_5$, with both outer
labels odd. It derives the three exact marked groups, their intersection
property and uniqueness. One proof of the projective reduction, one finite
calculation and one geometric argument serve all three applications.
The maniplex application also uses the common extension theorem and proves
its complete families for the stated ranges of primes. The historical local
classes and universal presentations remain attributed to the earlier work.
No additional source, conjecture solution or historical priority is claimed.

\noindent\textbf{Required programs and the complete cost record.}
The complete required program has $7{,}565$ characters. Thus the denominator
including it is $46{,}868$, and the corresponding source-text factor is
$1.522894\ldots$. Including the complete diagnostic program adds $5{,}173$
characters, giving denominator $52{,}041$ and factor $1.371515\ldots$.
Historical program costs and the full costs of the classical results are
unknown. These additional measurements therefore do not claim to be exact
ratios for every artifact on both sides. The same declared classical
background is available to the historical and replacement proofs.

The new required and diagnostic programs together have $12{,}738$
characters, $139$ more than the previous programs. The improvement in the
written proofs persists when the diagnostics are kept. Available Lean
certificates verify only their stated supporting lemmas; the cost of a
complete formalization remains unknown.

applications. It is an accounting order, not a reconstruction of discovery
