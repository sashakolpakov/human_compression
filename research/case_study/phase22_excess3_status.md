# Bounded prior-art status for the third-excess formula

10 October 2026. Publication priority remains **unverified**.

Twelve targeted web queries were made for the exact coefficients,
third-excess terminology, two-power string C-group counts, minimum-flag
polytopes and nilpotency-class counting. The returned primary records
did not identify the displayed all-rank cubic formula. This limited
search, particularly its low-recall exact-coefficient queries, does not
establish novelty or the absence of an existing classification.

Known overlap is explicit. Gomi, Loyola and De Las Peñas's
[2018 order-1024 classification](https://cdm.ucalgary.ca/article/view/62475)
already contains the rank-four component. The phase21 source-bound
comparison matches every type count after converting ordered markings
to classes under reversal. The cubic is not presented as a new discovery
of those finite cases.

Hou and Schulte's [2026 common-quotient paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20691)
states the general canonical-quotient result. Its relation to the
retained forest proof has already been recorded in the earlier source
audit; the quotient itself is not claimed as new here. Hou, Feng and
Leemans's [two-power construction paper](https://difusion.ulb.ac.be/vufind/Record/ULB-DIPOT%3Aoai%3Adipot.ulb.ac.be%3A2013/292444/TOC)
also appears in the search, with its abstract describing constructions
for prescribed admissible types and orders. These related results do
not by their abstracts establish or refute the new exact counting claim.

This bounded pass read the returned primary abstracts/metadata and used
the earlier exact N009 table audit; it did not reread every proof in the
related corpus or verify all forward citations. No named open conjecture,
whole-paper credit or publication-priority claim is assigned by this
status note. The mathematical proof and exact computation are separate
from this bibliographic limitation.
