# Focused primary-source recheck

10 October 2026. The author-uploaded primary text of Lubotzky--Mann,
*Powerful p-groups. I. Finite groups*, was opened again for the final
cyclic-TPP dependency check:
[author full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/265533173_Powerful_p-groups_I_Finite_groups),
[journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/0021-8693(87)90211-0).

The inspected material was printed pages 499--501, specifically the
statements and nearby proofs of 4.1.1, 4.1.3, 4.1.9 and 4.1.11.
They support the retained power-subgroup closure, iterated-power
identities, generation by powers of the given generators, and ordered
cyclic factorization. In particular 4.1.11 explicitly starts with a
given generating list; its formulation is not merely an existence
statement for some alternative list.

This is selected primary-text reading, not a claim to have reread the
entire article. The online rendering has OCR errors in mathematical
symbols, so the conclusions also retain the prior exact phase11 source
audit. No downloadable PDF was obtained in this recheck, no new source
PDF hash is asserted, and no third-party full text is distributed.
