# Bounded primary-source comparison for the all-parameter L003 construction

Audit date: 10 October 2026. This supplements the historical `phase33_source_status.md` with the now-complete all-parameter mathematical target. It does not replace that record or establish bibliographic priority.

## 1. Exact source problem and present scope

The cached [Fernandes–Leemans–Ivić Weiss author manuscript](https://ria.ua.pt/bitstream/10773/23862/1/2018DFW.pdf) was reread at the end of §8, Table 8 and Problem 9.2. The question asks whether the examples in rows 5–8 extend to an infinite family with ordered parameters
\[
((3,0),(2,0),(1,1),(s,0)),\qquad s\ge2.
\]
The surrounding discussion concerns finite examples. Table 8 additionally describes universal examples; universality is not stated as an extra hypothesis in the literal problem sentence.

The reviewed mathematical result is now stronger than the even-parameter target in the earlier status audit: the frozen tensor construction and geometric adapter give a **finite regular hypertope at every integer $s\ge2$**, with the exact four marked local groups. The proof uses Fibonacci-modulus matrix quotients for $s\ge4$ and separately certified small bases for $s=2,3$.

This is a positive construction matching the displayed existence question. It supplies neither a formula for the universal groups' orders nor an identification of the constructed groups with those universal groups. It does not certify all assertions in the source paper. Calling the mathematical existence question answered by this construction and calling the result a new resolution of a currently open problem are different claims: the latter still requires the priority comparison below.

## 2. Zhang 2024 remains a material unread comparison

The primary [publisher preview](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0021869324001534) of Wei-Juan Zhang, *Abelian covers of regular hypertopes*, Journal of Algebra 650 (2024), 123–144, DOI [10.1016/j.jalgebra.2024.03.017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2024.03.017), was read again. It describes abelian group covers, gives rank-five through rank-seven applications, and explicitly allows diagrams beyond those examples. The preview names Theorems 4.1 and 4.4 but does not expose their complete statements or proofs. The [ResearchGate record](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/379578350_Abelian_covers_of_regular_hypertopes) still reports no supplied full text. Fresh exact-title, theorem-number, author/institution and PDF searches did not produce a primary manuscript. The direct full publisher-page opening failed; the indexed preview remained readable.

Consequently this pass still **cannot match all hypotheses and conclusions of Theorems 4.1 and 4.4**. The displayed example ranks do not exclude a rank-four application of the general method. Equally, the unavailable theorems cannot be asserted to prove the particular family without an exact match.

The present all-parameter proof differs from our earlier fixed-base abelian-voltage construction in its stated mechanism: it uses one integral tensor representation, reduction modulo $F_s$, and a bounded finite-word membership certificate for IP and typed Helly. This is a description of the proof actually given, not a proof that its outputs cannot also arise from Zhang's construction. The precise unresolved comparison is whether N015 already yields the same four marked residues for every integer $s$, or contains a general theorem that implies that conclusion with verified inputs.

## 3. Accessible related work with a distinguishable target

The cached primary [Fernandes–Leemans–Piedade–Weiss manuscript](https://dipot.ulb.ac.be/dspace/bitstream/2013/301629/3/FLPWrev.pdf), *Two families of locally toroidal regular 4-hypertopes arising from toroids*, was reread at Theorem 4.3 and the relevant part of §5. Theorem 4.3 has the same circuit labels but local parameters $(t,t),(1,1),(2,0),(2,0)$. Its fixed residue orders are therefore 36, 48 and 48, with variable order $36t^2$. The discussed corrections concern other rows of the earlier table.

The target here instead has fixed local orders 36, 48 and 108, and variable order $12s^2$. Thus the displayed Theorem 4.3 is not already the same marked family, even after reordering the residue types. This comparison concerns the exact accessed theorem, not an assertion that every consequence of that paper has been ruled out.

The primary authored text of [Ens, *Rank 4 toroidal hypertopes*](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/325538081_Rank_4_toroidal_hypertopes) was checked at its introduction and definition of the classified objects. Its scope is quotients of rank-four irreducible affine Coxeter groups. This is distinct from a general classification of rank-four geometries with toroidal rank-three residues. The [author's institutional thesis abstract](https://yorkspace.library.yorku.ca/items/817197b7-6bb7-41be-9ca7-6f4f738aa3ca) confirms the affine construction convention. No full reading or new theorem coverage is credited for either work in this audit.

## 4. Search result and remaining obligation

Fresh searches combined the exact L003 title, Problem 9.2, the ordered parameter tuple, Fibonacci, the N015 title, theorem numbers, author and institutional domain. No primary passage explicitly giving the same all-integer-parameter existence result was located. Several results merely cited the original paper or discussed other families. That is a bounded search result, **not proof of novelty or current openness**.

The most important unresolved priority item remains access to the complete N015 covering definitions and Theorems 4.1/4.4. A rigorous comparison must check the exact marked local residues, admissible parameters, IP and flag transitivity, rather than only common vocabulary or a shared Coxeter diagram. No author was contacted, no inaccessible full text was reconstructed from snippets, and no negative search result has been turned into an absence claim.

The appropriate current description is: **an internally reviewed and reproducible all-parameter finite-existence construction matching L003 Problem 9.2, with bibliographic priority unverified.** This report does not claim universal finiteness, a universal-group order formula, or whole-paper coverage.
