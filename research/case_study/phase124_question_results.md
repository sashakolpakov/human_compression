These results belong to the question-solving track, separately from compression of known published results. This catalogue retrieves and binds existing proofs and checks; it is not a fresh review of every proof or a new computational replay. The qualifications below are part of each claim. No historical-priority claim is cleared.

The PSL2 classification consequence is not counted as an extra answer. These counts are bookkeeping, not an importance score.

## Mathematical review and computational evidence

Each JSON row binds the exact written proof, available review records and computation records. The Suzuki pentagon, central-lift, semilinear-spread, standard-coset obstruction, toroidal family and five-family cover entries have dedicated internal reviews. The older PSL2 proofs report internal independent review in their proof/corollary records; a separate detailed audit of their complete arithmetic chain was not located in this pass. The unital proof likewise has inherited internal-review claims and source/status checks, but a standalone detailed mathematical audit was not located. Those gaps should be repaired rather than silently upgraded.

None of the source-level geometry answers is completely formalized in Lean. The all-s≥6 infinitude of the exact three presented groups in the toroidal entry is a complete formal theorem, with standard axioms only. Its identification with the source geometry is still a written interface. Computations have their stated finite scope; they are not proofs by extrapolation.

## What remains unresolved

The general rank-spectrum conjecture, sharp PSL4 rank bound, PSU3 chiral existence, unrestricted rank-three two-group hypertope order bound, generalized-polygon EGR existence, higher-rank equal-label F014 quotient, general minimal-cover multiplicity and the listed universal classifications remain unresolved here. The wider161-paper census is ongoing and includes historical questions already solved by others.

## Attribution and current status

Each answer retains the original source question and authors in the JSON. Published base constructions and standard theories are dependencies, not new discoveries. Bounded later-source searches are dated evidence; failure to locate a later resolution is not proof that a conjecture was still open. In particular, the toroidal abelian-cover overlap and the complete later cover literature remain explicit gaps. No infinity compression ratio follows from these entries.
