The kernel theorem can be proved in its full three-subgroup form once, then
its marked-IP version is an immediate corollary. This genuinely removes the
duplicate first-element lifting argument from N016. The square-torus affine
model can likewise be introduced once; the N016 linear-part proof then refers
to that proved model. The new toroidal bridge removes N013's retained lattice
names by an actual face-poset and marked-lattice proof. It does not replace
the other, unrelated retained classifications.

The forest's laminar flag proof cannot be replaced by Boolean deletion:
deleting one member of an adjacent transposition pair destroys their order-three
relation. Endpoint substitution is generally not a quotient homomorphism, so
K0 cannot replace its subgroup analysis. The recent full marked parabolic
comparison theorem is not invoked without its application-specific product
membership/injectivity hypotheses; none has yet been proved that shortens
the endpoint adapter. The exact J1 census and the order-256 cyclic relation
census have different universes and are both required. Chiral multiplicity
conjectures do not follow from the kernel criterion and remain unproved.

## Proposed continuous proof order

1. A single set of marking, coset and source-scope conventions; all retained
   inputs stated exactly. Keep thin regular, ordinary string-polytope, and
   oriented C+ recognition as distinct interfaces.
2. General K0, its marked-IP specialization, exact coset transport K1,
   typed-Helly recognition, and the finite anchored Helly test.
3. Relative generation, the Frattini ladder, primitive coordinates, and all
   infinite-scope corrections.
4. Tree components, laminar flags, parity markings, two-block overgroups and
   the full extremal symmetric recognition proof.
5. Deletion maps, compatible products and independent cyclic edge inflation.
6. A single affine-lattice section: the complete square seed and recognition
   proof, then the explicit Gray-code cubical model and lattice bridge. The
   latter can refer forward to the geometric halving theorem only for its
   regularity; its coordinate proof is independent.
7. Complete graded-support, bounded-cyclic-core, binary extension and
   bare-presentation proofs, including the order-256 completeness argument.
8. All prescribed-section, alternating-assembly and infinite-extension proofs,
   with their exact FAP/rank and retained-geometry requirements.
9. The endpoint subgroup theorem and graph-double-cover realization, including
   the full parity-consistency condition and the lattice-polytope application.
10. Generalized-cube parity modules, exact polygon counts and all locally
    spherical inputs; all toroidal cases invoke the proved affine bridge.
11. J1's six constructions and exhaustive residue-extension theorem, followed
    by its explicit classification and no-rank≥5 corollary.
12. Mirror universal kernels, local chirality identifications, regular-end
    induction, the criterion, all five classified-family applications and the
    finite distinct-minimal-cover proof.
13. Explicit source corollaries, corrections and exclusions, followed by every
    necessary census or finite-certificate algorithm in full.

Use unique theorem prefixes for the new operation and chiral branches.
Preserve existing Unified labels when practical, but replace every moved
section and appendix reference mechanically and check each resulting reference.

new core, its adapter and its charged background; a list of notes is not the
