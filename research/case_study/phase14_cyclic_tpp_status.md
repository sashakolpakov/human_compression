# Bounded status audit: the 2019 hypertope order conjecture and cyclic TPP

10 October 2026. Original internal literature audit. This records a bounded search, not publication priority or external peer review. No source full texts are redistributed here.

**Finding.** The proposed finite-2-group cyclic-TPP theorem implies the exact lower-bound assertion in Hou–Feng–Leemans, Conjecture 3.3 (2019). I found no later primary result settling that conjecture or stating the same all-class cyclic-TPP theorem. However, the latest **explicit statement that the particular problem is a conjecture** located in this audit is the original 2019 paper. Later citations and related 2026 work are not a fresh confirmation of open status. Consequently the defensible description is “proposed proof of the published 2019 conjecture; current priority unverified,” not a certified first solution.

## Exact published target

Hou, Feng and Leemans, *Existence of regular 3-hypertopes with 2^n chambers*, Discrete Mathematics 342(6) (June 2019), 1857–1863, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2019.02.022). The [institutional author manuscript](https://dipot.ulb.ac.be/dspace/bitstream/2013/287227/5/Hy2g.pdf) explicitly identifies the published version. Its page 9, Conjecture 3.3, asserts the following nonexistence statement:

For positive integers $n,s,t,l$ satisfying $n\geq10$, $s,t\geq2$, $l\geq1$ and $n<s+t+l$, no regular rank-three hypertope has type $(2^s,2^t,2^l)$ and automorphism group of order $2^n$.

Theorem 3.2 gives existence for the complementary range $n\geq s+t+l$ under the same remaining hypotheses. The paragraph after Conjecture 3.3 records the cases $l=1$, $n\leq10$, and the case where one product of two maximal parabolics is itself a group. This audit read those statements, the relevant geometry definitions and flag-transitivity criteria in §2, and the construction's final flag-transitivity argument. It does not independently re-audit every relation in Theorem 3.2. The [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.09034) currently lists only v1, submitted 23 January 2019.

The source uses **regular hypertope**, including flag transitivity. A rank-three C-group alone is insufficient for the proposed lower-bound adapter. The previously checked adapter uses the typed coset-intersection identity supplied by flag transitivity, not merely pairwise subgroup intersections.

## Exact comparison with the proposed group theorem

The group theorem concerns three **cyclic subgroups** $A,B,C$ of a finite 2-group $P$, with

$$abc=1\ (a\in A,b\in B,c\in C)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad a=b=c=1,$$

and concludes $|A||B||C|\leq|P|$. It does not assert this inequality for arbitrary subgroup triples or arbitrary finite groups. Nor does TPP assert injectivity of the ordered multiplication map $A\times B\times C\to P$; that stronger property is used only where separately proved from powerful-group factorization.

For the hypertope, independent distinguished involutions give $|G:G'|=8$. The three cyclic subgroups generated by squared pair rotations lie in $G'$, have orders $2^{s-1},2^{t-1},2^{l-1}$, and have TPP by the flag-transitive coset-intersection identity. Thus the group inequality gives $2^{s+t+l-3}\leq2^{n-3}$, exactly $n\geq s+t+l$. The argument still works when one square-rotation subgroup is trivial. These are mathematical deductions in the proposed proof package, not claims found in the external literature. The reduction is written in [phase3_order_bound.md](phase3_order_bound.md); the proposed complete continuation is [phase14_cyclic_tpp.md](phase14_cyclic_tpp.md).

Combined with the original existence result, the proposed lower bound would give an if-and-only-if parameter criterion in the source's stated range. The cyclic-TPP theorem is stronger in group-theoretic scope; it is not logically equivalent to the geometric conjecture.

## Current TPP literature: overlapping hypotheses are different

Murthy's [arXiv:2602.15796v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.15796v1), submitted 17 February 2026, gives bounds for arbitrary subgroup triples in nilpotency class two. Theorem 4.1 concerns a commutator subgroup of order $p$; the other main statements use center index or character degrees. These do not state the all-class cyclic-factor theorem. Fresh inspection covered the abstract, introduction, relevant theorem contexts and cyclic/conjecture searches; a broader earlier reading is recorded in [phase11_odd_nilpotent_status.md](phase11_odd_nilpotent_status.md). The paper includes extraspecial examples with subgroup ratio greater than one, so the restriction to cyclic factors must remain explicit.

Murthy's [arXiv:2512.16730v7](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.16730v7), last revised 9 January 2026, treats a normal abelian subgroup of prime index. Corollary 4.3(2) supplies an overlapping ambient $p$-group case. Its conjectures concern arbitrary **subsets** in groups with a cyclic normal subgroup of prime index; this is a different placement of the word “cyclic.” Fresh inspection checked the current version history, conjecture statements and their context; the prior audit records the main theorem/proof reading. Neither source is used here as an external validation of the new proof.

The earlier audit also records Hedtke–Murthy's [2011 preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1104.5097v2), published in 2012: normal-factor results and the known nonnilpotent order-21 cyclic triple of size 27. The proposed result retains the published Lubotzky–Mann powerful-group factorization theorem and existing TPP reduction ideas. None of those ingredients should be described as newly discovered.
