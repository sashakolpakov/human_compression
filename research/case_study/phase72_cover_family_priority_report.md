# Odd-prime cover family: bounded literature and attribution audit

10 October 2026. This additive note preserves the phase49 theorem and phase59 mathematical review. It makes no historical-priority or certified-current-openness claim. Primary PDFs remain private; the exportable material is this original analysis, the adapter note and the machine-readable records.

The main correction to the presentation is attribution: **polytopality of the two constructed covers and of their common mix follows from published facet-cover criteria.** Phase49 supplies a self-contained specialization and additional exact group calculations. Neither its general mix method nor every displayed order should be presented as new.

## Exact target and result being compared

The target is the entire valid odd-prime atomic Γ1 family in Cunningham–Pellicer, Table 2/Theorem 4.7 of the checked 2020 author version: p is odd prime, α≥2, q=p^α, and k1=k2=k∈{1,…,p−1}. Equality of the two parameters is required by that theorem; this is not an arbitrary restriction to part of its valid Γ1 family. The source construction, its intersection property, exact orders and tight normal form remain substantial retained inputs. It is the first chiral-facet/chiral-vertex row of Table 4, not the first row of that whole table. [Checked primary version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2009.04566v1).

Phase49 proves two distinct rank-preserving minimal regular polytopal covers Q,Q′ for every such parameter, each of degree p over the smallest regular maniplex R. It also computes their common cover H, its C_p² kernel, exactly two minimal covers within the quotient interval below H, and a family-specific universal-kernel join identity. It does not classify every minimal cover of P or address all five atomic families.

## What the repository adds within this comparison

The additional work is an explicit all-parameter specialization: the action identities; W=⟨C²,Z⟩ as the exact normal closure of C²; W∩F=⟨Z⟩; the largest common marked quotient R/W≅T/⟨Z⟩; the resulting degree-p cover orders; both local injections into R; the two distinct conjugation characters; and the complete C_p² quotient interval of H, including the join conclusion. These calculations turn the published construction into a proof for every valid odd-prime Γ1 parameter. The named polytopality and minimality mechanisms remain credited to the earlier work. “Additional here” describes the repository’s derivation relative to the texts read; it does not assert first discovery.

Cunningham’s §4 proposes analogous multiplicity for the five tight families. Phase49 establishes the specified odd-prime family portion of that older proposal, conditional on its explicitly retained construction and standard recognition inputs. The broader question of whether a nonpolytopal smallest regular cover can have a unique minimal polytopal regular cover is also discussed in Cunningham–Pellicer’s *Open problems on k-orbit polytopes*, Problem 5; the present family does not decide it. [Cunningham author text](https://gabrielcunningham.com/WhichChiralPolytopes.pdf), [k-orbit author version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1608.07993v1).
