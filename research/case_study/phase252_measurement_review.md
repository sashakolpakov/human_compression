# Independent review of the Topic E measurement

10 October 2026. **Accepted for the stated conditional length comparison.**
The source boundaries, complete replacement papers, exact program charge,
independent arithmetic, retained inputs, dependency diagrams and final reader
have been checked. On the designated metric the complete written replacement
is longer than the two matched historical passages. The accepted packet

## What is being compared

The intake comprises the higher-rank PSL2 classification and specified
geometric properties in L083, followed by the higher-rank PGL2 classification
in L079. Their principal conclusions were accepted in phase234 and their
division into one shared theory and two complete applications in phase243.
This review does not confer new mathematical coverage. Neither complete
historical paper is replaced: their separate chiral, one-Petrie-relation and
semilinear branches are outside the comparison.

I read the three complete frozen phase238 papers and their entire required
program `phase230_finite_check.g`. The shared paper contains the rank and
facet restrictions, centralizer descent, six local marking classes and ten
universal groups. The first application supplies the PSL2 quotient and
geometric arguments; the second supplies the PGL2 quotient argument and
the simplex identification. The second does not depend on the first.
The finite program is a mathematical dependency of both: it computes orders
of the presented groups and verifies their actual marked intersections and
the stated geometry. A chosen permutation image alone would not suffice.
The code cost must therefore be paid with the shared theory.

The five inherited Lean declarations check the abstract normalizer argument.
They are verification artifacts, not an omitted proof of the finite table
or of either application. No end-to-end formalization claim follows from
this measurement.

These intervals contain the historical subgroup classifications and auxiliary
proofs actually used in the principal arguments. Counting their written

by the replacement. The subgroup multiplicities and historical intersection
lemmas have that distinction in the mathematical coverage ledger.

Every removed interval inside the selected material is a printed page number
or running journal header. The three replacement papers are charged in full,
including their abstracts, attribution, explanations of verification and
references. The program appendix includes its title and the complete program,
including comments. Its extracted sequence of non-whitespace characters
agrees exactly with the program, after removing the appendix title. No
program character has been replaced by a shorter mathematical assertion.

The independent checker directly reads the six PDFs with pypdf 6.13.0. It
verifies their hashes, every page interval, every selected and removed span,
and the protocol hash. It computes alphanumeric runs by a character scan,
without importing the author's implementation or the historical metric
helper. NFC and NFKC are applied only at measurement. No source formula or
extraction artifact is silently repaired. This preserves the same extraction
discipline on both sides, though differences between font encodings and
typesetting remain limitations of these representation-dependent proxies.
In particular, code wrapping can put a line break inside an identifier or
string, changing whitespace-token counts without changing any non-whitespace
character. Mathematical superscripts are flattened by extraction, some
historical ligatures survive until NFKC, and line-end hyphenation is counted
as extracted. The program appendix is readable, but its extracted text is
not asserted to be a whitespace-preserving executable copy of the source.

## Historical reuse and external inputs

The source side already reuses mathematics. In L079, the characteristic-two
exclusion on page 4 explicitly invokes L083; the subgroup argument on pages
5–6 is repeated from that earlier work; and the proof of Theorem 10 on page 7
directly invokes L083's Table 1. The historical total counts the earlier
paper once and the later paper as printed, without adding a second table
cost. A diagram must show these dependencies rather than depict two isolated
historical proofs. Citation numbers for the published earlier paper differ
from numbering in the bound arXiv edition; edges should identify their
mathematical content as well as the literal citation.

The source's exclusion of PSL2 rank at least five is borrowed from
Leemans–Vauthier on L083 page 2. Our shared paper proves its corresponding
rank restriction. The available background must include this source-specific
external theorem as well as Dickson's subgroup results, ordinary projective
group facts and the group–polytope correspondence. Saying that this is a
common available library does not mean that both sides invoke every item.
The comparison is conditional on its availability, not an unconditional
account of discovering and proving all those results.

The dated `phase251_clarification.md` resolves one wording correction.
L083 page 9 says that the table can be obtained with software such as Magma,
or by hand. That sentence does not establish the existence or size of an
actual historical source program. The unavailable artifact cost is unknown,
not zero; the lower-bound argument remains valid. The original protocol's
stronger sentence about programs having been used has received an explicit
clarification, without changing the already bound protocol bytes.

The final DAG corrects the initial source locators and removes a proposed
arrow from the bound L083 odd-label lemma to the L079 odd-label lemma: the
latter cites basic centralizer facts in a differently numbered published
edition. Those retained facts belong to the external group-theory node.
The actual repeated subfield counting argument still justifies its separate
cross-paper arrow. On the replacement side, the group–polytope input feeds
the table's face-count and incidence conclusions explicitly. I read all
final node locators and arrows and found no further correction necessary.
The nodes group theorem statements and carry no numerical weights; their
numbers cannot be interpreted as complexity.

The complexity plot uses the independently checked six costs. The acquisition
bar includes U_E and its program; the two local bars use complete application
papers. The cumulative graph shows the theory already paid at the initial
point, then adds each application. The historical sequence adds the actual
printed passages. Its initial zero means that no intake paper has yet been
added; it does not assign zero cost to retained classical mathematics. I
replayed the graph generator in a separate directory and viewed its native
PNG rendering. Labels and legends are legible and the plotted values agree
with the data. Only one acquisition and two applications are shown, so no
long-run sawtooth behavior is established.

and their collapsed-text hashes agree with the independent implementation.

their reports agree with the frozen author reports. The author manifest's
15 exports and 11 retained files all match their hashes. Its SHA256 is
`da71693522888abe269ff545cf07f26e131b728df93d4a9d42eb931f843cd2a0`.
The bound final reader has valid fraction displays, no unexpected control

I accept publication of these particular conditional length measurements,

additional source coverage or a newly solved question. It also does not
