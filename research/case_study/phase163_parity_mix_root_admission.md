## Exact mathematical boundary

The final theorem assumes a pointwise two-primary group E, involutive
markings in E and G, genuine homomorphisms to a commutative group with
common nonidentity marker value, odd consecutive product orders in G,
string commutation in both factors, and full IP for both factor markings.
It concludes full IP for the literal paired generated subgroups of E × G
and for their intrinsic marking in the generated mix. Intrinsic generation,
nonidentity involutions and string commutation are also proved.

Ambient finiteness and ambient generation are not final hypotheses.
The marker value cannot silently be the identity, and the actual characters
must be supplied. The final theorem does not assume a mixed IP statement,
a generated-fiber identification, a component profile or overlap character
equations. Those conclusions are derived. Rank zero and empty supports
are included.

Root checked the main consumer chain explicitly. Coprime powers isolate
odd-order elements in the second coordinate. Paths and generation then
identify each actual interval mix with the character fiber. The final
consumer binds genuine support components to the overlap theorem. Projection
and factor IP place both coordinates on the common support; refinement of
their actual component coordinates derives both sets of character equations.
The retained finite-interval cancellation theorem is applied to those
equations, and actual local fibers yield the mixed intersection. The
injective subtype map transports the ambient result into the generated
group without an extra mixed-IP premise.

The formal connected-fiber proof works under a weaker two-primary
hypothesis than the finite argument in the earlier written note. It is
a direct coprime-power proof of the needed classical subgroup consequence.
The package formalizes the earlier group construction; it does not claim
historical novelty for coprime powers, subdirect products, commuting
components or order-connected sets.
