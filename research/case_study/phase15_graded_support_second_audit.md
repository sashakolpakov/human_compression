# Second independent written review of graded support separation

10 October 2026. Internal mathematical review by a separate agent. This is not external peer review, an executable verification, or a publication-priority determination.

I read all five sections of `phase15_graded_support.md` independently of the author's proof development. No mathematical gap was found in the stated finite two-group/forest theorem.

* In the descending lifting lemma, equality of the graded images of $a\in A_j$ and $b\in B_j$ gives $ab^{-1}=n_A n_B$. The rearrangement $n_A^{-1}a=n_Bb$ is valid without commuting either subgroup. Its common value lies in $A\cap B$, so the images meet trivially. The exact finite index product then proves the asserted set product $D_j=A_jB_j$; no quotient intersection property has been assumed.
* The support lemma follows from the derivation identity and Jacobi. Degree-one generation of the lower-central graded Lie ring is the standard fact needed here. For involution-generated groups, multilinearity makes every homogeneous component an elementary abelian two-group; no division by two enters the argument.
* Among participating edges, the selected maximal-depth edge has no participating edge on its descendant side. A forest has exactly that one crossing edge. The relation therefore places its degree-two cross bracket in the Lie algebra generated by the other side, while all other cross brackets vanish. This is precisely the hypothesis of the support lemma, rather than an assumed splitting of the original group.
* Burnside's basis theorem applies because marked IP makes the nonidentity involution generators irredundant and their elementary abelianization gives $\Phi(G)=G'$. In particular $G'\cap\langle r_v\rangle=1$. The decomposition of the crossing commutator in $G'$ is then reduced by actual marked subgroup intersections first to $\langle r_x\rangle$, then to $\langle r_y\rangle$, forcing it to be trivial. These two uses of actual IP are both justified and do not assert subgroup normality.
* Independence of edge commutators yields order $2^{d+m}$ for the class-two quotient. The bilinear cocycle model has exactly this order and its support description proves IP, so the marked quotient identification is valid. For the path, the adjacent product orders are four and the quotient has order $2^{2d-1}$.

The retained input boundary is elementary lower-central graded Lie theory, Burnside's basis theorem, finiteness/nilpotency and the original marked IP. No finite catalogue, source Frattini-kernel formula, intermediate quotient IP, or nilpotency-class bound is used. This review does not address novelty or claim that the source's specified kernel is correct; it reviews the replacement canonical quotient theorem only.
