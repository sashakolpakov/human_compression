## Proof audit

**E0 and the relative-generation interface.** The chamber-matching induction works for every finite rank and does not require a faithful action or finite group. At each step the current flag stabilizer is transitive on a remaining type by actual residual weak primitivity; it fixes previously matched elements. Geometry supplies chamber extensions, so chamber transitivity yields flag transitivity. Transitivity on incident pairs gives the claimed coset representation. Firmness then gives a nontrivial panel orbit and hence a panel element outside the omitted stabilizer, proving all indexed intersections distinct. The downward maximality induction recovers every parabolic from the Borel and panel choices. Importantly, this proof does not assume residual connectedness in order to handle the source's firm-RWPRI hypotheses. The new chapter states and proves the needed extension of the existing relative-generation interface to arbitrary groups of finite rank.

**E1/E2, including infinite groups.** A finite maximal chain makes the group finitely generated relative to its bottom subgroup. A union of a chain of proper overgroups cannot contain all of those finitely many extra generators, so Zorn's lemma gives a maximal overgroup. This is exactly the missing justification needed before excluding a product `N H_i = G`; one cannot discard an arbitrary infinite Frattini subgroup by a finite nongenerator slogan. In the ladder induction, normality makes each product a subgroup, maximality leaves the two endpoints, and the lower endpoint equates intersections indexed by different sets because the step index is strictly below the first failure index. This contradicts distinctness. All steps are valid without finite-group order arguments. Taking the normal subgroup to be the Frattini subgroup proves the full stated geometric infinite-group conclusion.

**E3 and faithful kernels.** Because the normal subgroup lies in every type stabilizer, each source coset is the full inverse image of its quotient coset. This proves equality of all incidence/intersection patterns and common-point witnesses, rather than only a map on distinguished chambers. It works for pregeometries and preserves actual residues. Subgroup correspondence or, equivalently, equality of the induced permutation actions gives the primitive properties. The action kernel is exactly the core of the Borel, equal to the intersection of the individual cores. The Frattini quotient is Frattini free by the maximal-subgroup correspondence; the no-maximal-subgroup case is correctly treated separately. The result concerns subgroup families under a fixed quotient map and incidence isomorphism, not equality of automorphism-orbit counts under different acting groups.

**E4 and the Prüfer-product diagnostic.** Under the stated maximal-overgroup hypothesis, a subgroup containing the Borel generates with the Frattini subgroup exactly when it is already the whole group. Applying this both to the full family and to each deletion proves generation and independence equivalence. For `D = C_(p^infinity)` and `G = D × C2`, every maximal subgroup has prime-order quotient because `G` is abelian. Divisibility kills the image of `D` in each such quotient, leaving `D × 1` as the unique maximal subgroup. Thus it is the Frattini subgroup. The generator of the second factor is an independent generator in the quotient but does not generate `G`. This is a genuine counterexample to the printed unrestricted converse, with a one-element family. It does not refute individual Frattini elements being nongenerators, the finite version, the version for relative finite generation, or the geometric result E2.

**E5 and all p-group examples.** The only retained p-group input is that the finite Frattini quotient is elementary abelian. Containment of the Frattini subgroup in the Borel makes that Borel normal; the further quotient is a vector space. Formula (R) makes the panel images spanning, and relative independence makes them a basis. Therefore every parabolic is the specified coordinate span. Coordinate assignments prove all-flag completion and complete incidence between types, not just rank-two tests. They also prove residual primitivity and regularity of the faithful translation action on chambers. Conversely all independent dual functionals give examples, and independent scalar rescaling is exactly the ambiguity in their kernels. The displayed count is correct for indexed subgroup families, not automorphism orbits. The rank-two coset-product calculation and the subsidiary subspace-dimension calculation are complete.

**Residual test and core identity.** Applying E2 inside each residue is legitimate: firmness and actual RWPRI are inherited, and the residue Borel remains the original Borel. This gives the exact necessary Frattini-containment test. No inclusion of a residue's Frattini subgroup in the ambient Frattini subgroup, converse test, or algorithmic runtime improvement is assumed.
