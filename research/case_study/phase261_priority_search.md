# Bounded priority and source audit for the four-family question

Audit date: 10 October 2026. This records a bounded search, not a proof of
historical priority or an exhaustive forward-citation review. No authors
were contacted.

The exact question is the final paragraph of Section 5, physical and
printed page 9, in the May 15, 2023 author manuscript of Leemans–Toledo,
*Maniplexes with automorphism group PSL(2,q)*. I read all eleven pages of
the local primary and visually inspected page 9. It asks for an infinite
family in each of four end-group configurations. It does not demand
every prime power or a classification. The bound manuscript is available
from the [institutional author archive](https://dipot.ulb.ac.be/dspace/bitstream/2013/359689/3/projectivemaniplexes_final.pdf).

The [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.10195) inspected during this
audit lists only version 1, submitted 22 August 2022. It is an earlier
edition than the bound author manuscript. The [journal landing page](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0012365X23002133)
identifies Discrete Mathematics 346(9), article 113527 (2023), DOI
10.1016/j.disc.2023.113527. This audit does not claim that the complete
typeset journal edition was collated against the author manuscript.

The following exact queries were sent to web search in three batches:

1. `"maniplexes" "PGL" "infinite"`
2. `"Maniplexes with automorphism group PSL" conjecture`
3. `"PGL" "maniplexes" Leemans Toledo four`
4. `"maniplex" "projective general linear" conjecture`
5. `"PGL" "maniplex" "2024"`
6. `"PGL" "maniplex" "2025"`
7. `"four" "families" "maniplexes" PGL`
8. `"2208.10195" "PGL"`
9. `"PGL2" "maniplex" -site:researchgate.net -site:sciencedirect.com -site:dipot.ulb.ac.be -site:portal.mardi4nfdi.de`
10. `"projective" "maniplexes" "conjecture" PGL`
11. `"Toledo" "PGL" "maniplexes" 2026`
12. `"regular maniplexes" "linear groups" infinite`

Results repeatedly led to the original author manuscript, its earlier
arXiv version, the journal landing page, bibliographic indexes and papers
on other maniplex questions. No accessible primary claiming a solution
of this exact four-family question was identified by these queries.
Index snippets and search-engine publication-date labels were not used
to establish a theorem or a paper's date. There was no paywalled later
candidate with a title suggesting this exact result in the returned
results, but the search did not enumerate every citing paper, thesis or
unindexed manuscript.

Accordingly the supported conclusion is: the question is explicitly
posed in the bound 2023 source, and this bounded search found no later
solution. It is not justified to replace this with “first proof”,
“previously unsolved as of 2026”, or “exhaustive literature search”.

The subgroup inputs in the proposed solution were checked against the
primary Giudici manuscript, *Maximal subgroups of almost simple groups
with socle PSL(2,q)*, arXiv:math/0703685. Theorem 2.2 is on physical and
printed page 4; Theorem 3.5 is on physical and printed page 10. Both were
visually inspected. Its maximal-subgroup lists are retained classical
inputs; they are distinct from the four-family conjecture.
