# Formal consequences of supported kernels

Author certificate candidate, 10 October 2026. This addendum is separate from the frozen and independently reviewed SupportedKernelGeneration module. Independent review of the addendum is pending. The written target remains **Marked groups, kernels and intersections**, frozen U version 2.

SupportedKernelConsequences proves the order assertion of U.SK and three of its four numbered corollaries: local recognition on an antichain, separator factorization, and lower-central kernel equality. Each final statement takes the actual marked construction hypotheses used in the reviewed core. It calls the proved kernel profile; it does not replace that construction by an assumed profile or an isolated-word hypothesis. The Boolean-deletion corollary remains a separate formal obligation.

## Exact statements and their relation to U

Write H_L for the subgroup actually generated by the tuple markers with labels in L, and B_L for the subgroup generated by their base coordinates. The support index J is finite; individual supports and label sets are finite. The ambient base and local groups can be infinite and nonabelian. None of the new consequences needs an additional global finiteness assumption on the label type. The full U construction specializes these statements directly.

All declaration names are in MathCompression.SupportedKernelGeneration.

| Declaration | Exact certified conclusion |
|---|---|
| baseProjection_range | The image of the actual projection H_L → B is B_L. This follows from map-of-generated-subgroup, without a support hypothesis. |
| kernelCoordinatesEquiv | An explicit equivalence between the projection kernel and the dependent product of local kernels whose supports lie in L. This helper assumes the stated profile. The final order theorem supplies that premise using the already proved construction. |
| parabolic_cardinality | Nat.card H_L equals Nat.card B_L times the product of Nat.card ker(q_j) over supports contained in L. Every construction hypothesis is explicit and the profile is a conclusion of the retained theorem. |
| local_projection_bijective | If support containment implies equality of support indices, the local coordinate projection H_(scope s) → E_s is bijective. It is a homomorphism by definition, hence gives precisely the marked isomorphism of U.SK.Local. No order comparison is used. |
| separator_factorization | If B_(L∪M) = B_L B_M and each support contained in L∪M lies wholly in L or wholly in M, every element of H_(L∪M) factors as an element of H_L followed by an element of H_M. The reverse inclusion follows from subgroup closure and inclusion of labels. This proves U.SK.Separator when L∪M is the full marker set. |
| pure_kernel_mem_lowerCentralSeries | If every local kernel is contained in the n-th Mathlib lower-central term of its local group, each pure local kernel lies in the same term of its actual support parabolic. The proof reuses triangular_isolation. |
| lowerCentralSeries_eq_kernel | If in addition the n-th term of the base is trivial, the n-th term of every H_L is exactly H_L intersected with the base-projection kernel. This includes U.SK.Central and also records the conclusion for all parabolics. |

Mathlib numbers the lower-central series from zero: lowerCentralSeries S 0 = S. Consequently its index n corresponds to the conventional gamma_(n+1), and U.SK.Central with c ≥ 1 uses n=c−1. Its assumption ker(q_j)=gamma_c(E_j) supplies the local inclusion in the formal statement. No shift in nilpotency convention is implicit.

Nat.card assigns zero to infinite types. The cardinality theorem is valid with that convention and its finite specialization is the ordinary order formula (2.5). It is not an assertion about infinite cardinal multiplication or about an unproved finiteness bound.

The same literal construction assumptions as the retained core are required: distinct supports; local coordinates trivial off their support; compatibility of the local quotient with the selected base map on every marker; that map fixing each retained base marker; generation of every local group by its support markers; and trivial kernel on every proper local parabolic. Global base generation and base IP are unnecessary for these consequences on already generated parabolics. Application-specific constructions still have to prove the literal assumptions.

## Proof details and actual reuse

For the order formula, the projection range is identified on generators. A kernel element is sent to its supported local coordinates. In the opposite direction, a tuple in the selected local kernels is extended by identity on every other support. The previously proved profile places this tuple in H_L, and both composites are checked coordinate by coordinate. The group index formula and the cardinality of a dependent product now give the result.

For local recognition, suppose a word on support S has trivial S-coordinate. The compatibility equation and the fact that its base lies on S imply that its base is trivial. The proved profile leaves possible nonidentity coordinates only on supports contained in S. The antichain hypothesis excludes every distinct such support; the S-coordinate itself was trivial. This proves injectivity. Surjectivity is the local marked-generation hypothesis transported through map-of-generated-subgroup.

For a separator, factor the base of x as a product of base elements on L and M, and lift these two factors to a in H_L and b in H_M. The residual n=x(ab)⁻¹ lies in H_(L∪M) with trivial base. Split its coordinate tuple by assigning to L every support contained in L and to M every remaining coordinate. The no-straddling hypothesis and the proved profile place the two pure tuples in H_L and H_M. A conjugate of the latter pure tuple by a remains in H_M: each local quotient kernel is normal, and conjugation leaves an identity coordinate equal to identity. The equality

    x = (n_L a) (a⁻¹ n_M a b)

then proves the ordered factorization. This argument proves the required conjugate membership; it does not assume normality of H_M and does not commute elements within a local kernel.

For the lower-central assertion, map-of-lower-central-series and local marked generation lift each local kernel element into the appropriate lower-central term of H_S. Its base is trivial by exactly the compatibility and fixed-base argument already used for local recognition. The retained actual profile shows that its only possible unwanted coordinates are on proper contained supports. The existing triangular_isolation theorem is applied to the lower-central subgroups, using monotonicity under support containment. It removes the smaller coordinates inside the required lower-central subgroup and isolates the desired pure kernel. This is a second use of the established induction, not an additional assumption that the desired pure commutators already exist.

Finally, functoriality sends the lower-central term of H_L into the trivial corresponding term of B. Conversely the profile expresses every element of the base kernel in H_L as a product of pure kernels supported in L, and the preceding result places every such factor in the lower-central term of H_L. Thus both inclusions are proved.

## Replay and trust

Run the exact wrapper with a new output path:

    python3 -B -O research/leemans/phase129_supported_kernel_consequences_check.py \
      --packages /path/to/pinned/.lake/packages \
      --lean /path/to/lean \
      --output-dir /path/to/new/output

The wrapper checks hashes of all four local sources and the dependency manifest, the pinned Lean version, and every pinned package revision and its tracked-file cleanliness. It rebuilds LiftCertificate, CoordinateKernels, SupportedKernelGeneration and SupportedKernelConsequences. It expects seven new axiom reports and sixteen retained reports, allowing only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. No unproved placeholder or native-evaluation axiom is accepted.

Two pinned Mathlib modules, GroupTheory.Commutator.Finite and GroupTheory.Nilpotent, are additionally rebuilt from their exact source hashes because their compiled objects were absent from the available cache. A private namespace overlay links the other cached Mathlib objects. It excludes every filename beginning with either rebuilt module's stem before the compiler writes its outputs, so those outputs cannot overwrite a linked cached object. The links themselves do not impose filesystem read-only permissions. The runner writes only the explicitly fresh module targets and reports; it does not rebuild or modify the package checkout.

The installed compiler and the remaining cached package objects are still trusted. Recompiling these six source modules is not a rebuild of all Mathlib. The rejection program separately checks changes to the new source, retained core, retained coordinate module and dependency manifest, and rejects four kinds of occupied output path while checking they remain unchanged. These are author controls, not independent review or a substitute for Lean's proof checking.

## Remaining scope and attribution

This is a formalization of the existing U proofs, with their actual dependency structure retained. The general cardinality, lower-central functoriality and monotonicity facts are borrowed from Mathlib; the support induction and kernel-profile proofs are reused from the reviewed phase129 core. The all-parabolic formulations record what these proofs establish. They do not imply a claim of historical priority.

Derived Boolean deletions, typed Helly transfer, geometry recognition and application-specific hypothesis bindings are not certified by this addendum. It is neither a formalization of every theorem in U nor of any whole application manuscript. No open conjecture, new source-level result or compression ratio is claimed here. The checkpoint separates the new module's acquisition cost from the retained proof modules; no old module is silently counted as a new proof.
