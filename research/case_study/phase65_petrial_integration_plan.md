The exact principal contract is Theorem 1: a thin regular residually connected rank-three geometry whose Petrial is thin regular has a commuting pair in its diagram. The abstract states the thin-only hypothesis; the proposed proof establishes that stronger version. Lemma 3.1 is a walk-definition adapter. Lemma 3.2(i), (iii) and (iv) are genuine additional derivations. The unconditional embedding in Lemma 3.2(ii) is false and is replaced by its exact action kernel, with an elementary counterexample. The unnumbered equality of geometric length and chamber-step order is not imported and has an explicit nonthin counterexample. The external atlas examples and historical map classification are outside the contract.

The shared theorem has an actual common hypothesis check, not just common vocabulary. An action on constructed objects and an action on retained objects have the same kernel when constructed objects admit an injective equivariant encoding by retained elements/subsets and forgetting some constructed objects maps equivariantly onto all retained objects. That kernel is the intersection of retained stabilizer cores. Quotienting by it gives the faithful image; if the resulting geometry is thin, residually connected and chamber-transitive, the image is the full automorphism group. An order-two preimage of a panel stabilizer forces the kernel to vanish.

Applications inside the proposed continuous manuscript:

1. The old N008 coset-core identity becomes a direct specialization of the shared theorem, together with existing exact coset transport K(b).
2. In the endpoint construction, retain old vertices and all higher objects. New vertices encode an old vertex and a color; a lifted higher object encodes its old label and the two vertex subsets of its double-cover component. Forgetting is surjective, and simplicity recovers every omitted old edge from its endpoints. Thus the same theorem proves faithfulness for the actual acting group, including the color-preserving subgroup in the bipartite case. No endpoint marking is treated as a homomorphic image.
3. For a set-valued Petrial, retained objects are the old vertices and edges, while faces are exact pairs of subsets. Its action kernel is therefore core_G(<a>), where a is the old face-changing involution. Since <a> has order two, this is <a> precisely when a is central and is trivial otherwise. This supplies the correct unconditional source interface.

All these arguments allow infinite groups and residues.
