## The old quotient criterion applies without a terminology gap

I read the entire local proof chain in Breda D’Azevedo–Jones–Schulte, PDF pages 7–9 (printed 1260–1262), and checked the exact relations (2.3). I also freshly inspected the rendered page containing Lemma 3.2. Its input is a marked group satisfying the rotation relations, a marked quotient with the rotation intersection property, and injectivity on one end subgroup. It does **not** require the group being recognized to come from a previously proved polytope. [Publisher source](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-2011-033-4).

For Q = R⋄T, the repository already proves that T is directly regular and that its facet is the marked F. The projection Q → T sends the displayed generators to the displayed generators, and its restriction to the facet is an isomorphism: that facet is the diagonal graph of the identity on F. The required rotation relations hold coordinatewise. Lemma 3.2 therefore gives Q's intersection property. Compatible mirror automorphisms give direct regularity; duality gives Q′. No unsupported claim about whether R satisfies an older face-poset definition of a prepolytope is needed.

For H = Q⋄Q′, phase49's already proved end injections into R identify their marked facets through F. The facet subgroup of H is again the graph of that marked isomorphism, so H → Q is injective there. Lemma 3.2 applies again, or Lemma 3.3 applies to the two now established polytopes with identical marked facets. The vertex injection is useful later but unnecessary for this polytopality step.

Cunningham's *Variance groups and the structure of mixed polytopes*, Propositions 3.1, 3.3 and 3.5–3.7, already supplies the normal-kernel intersection/product formulation, variance kernels, common marked quotient, fiber-product description and finite order formula. [Checked author manuscript](https://gabrielcunningham.com/VarianceGroups.pdf).

The same prime-degree argument applies after phase49's additional calculation proves degree p: every regular cover of P factors through its smallest regular maniplex R, and a proper quotient of Q over R must then be R itself. The older criterion alone does not establish that degree. Phase49 still has to identify W = ⟨C²,Z⟩ as the precise normal closure, prove W∩F = ⟨Z⟩, and identify R/W with T/⟨Z⟩ as a marked quotient. Those explicit calculations are additional repository content relative to the checked comparisons, without a first-discovery claim.

I also checked the exact Problem 5 and covering conventions in Cunningham–Pellicer's 2016 author version of *Open problems on k-orbit polytopes*. Proving two covers for this one family does not decide it. [Checked source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1608.07993v1).
