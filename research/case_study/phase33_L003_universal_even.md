# Universal regular objects for the even L003 parameters

Date: 2026-10-10. This separate corollary does not change the frozen varying-parameter construction or the fixed-parameter-six packages.

## 1. Exact statement

Let \(W\) be the Coxeter group on involutions \(r_0,r_1,r_2,r_3\), with pair orders dividing
\[
 (m_{01},m_{02},m_{03},m_{12},m_{13},m_{23})=(6,3,2,2,3,6).
\]
Put \(x=r_0r_1\), \(y=r_0r_2\), \(z=r_0r_3\), and define \(U_s\) by adding
\[
 (y^{-1}x^{-2})^3=1,\qquad (zx^3)^2=1,
\]
\[
 (x^{-1}z(y^{-1}z)^2)(zx(zy)^2)=1,\qquad
 (y(zy)^2)^s=1. \tag{1}
\]
Let \(P_s\) be the source rotational presentation on \(x,y,z\), with the six relations
\[
 x^6=y^3=z^2=(zx)^3=(zy)^6=(x^{-1}y)^2=1
\]
and the four relations (1).

**Theorem.** For every even integer \(s\ge2\), the standard marked coset geometry of \(U_s\) is a regular rank-four hypertope with the source's exact ordered parameters
\[
 ((3,0),(2,0),(1,1),(s,0)).
\]
It is universal among marked regular hypertopes with that diagram and those ordered marked residues: every such hypertope group is a generator-preserving quotient of \(U_s\), and the quotient map is injective on each maximal marked parabolic. Moreover,
\[
 U_s\cong P_s\rtimes C_2, \tag{2}
\]
where the nonidentity element of \(C_2\) acts by simultaneously inverting \(x,y,z\). Whenever \(6\mid s\), both \(U_s\) and \(P_s\) are infinite.

For every even \(s\), a finite quotient retaining all four residues is supplied by the frozen even-parameter construction. This theorem does not assert that \(U_s\) itself is finite, identify it with that finite quotient, or determine its order at the other even parameters.

## 2. The finite examples force all local injections

Write \(s=2m\). The group \(G_m\) in [the even-parameter construction](phase33_L003_even_family.md) satisfies every relation in (1), so there is a generator-preserving epimorphism
\[
 U_s\longrightarrow G_m. \tag{3}
\]
For the maximal parabolic omitting type \(i\), the local rotation substitutions into source equation (2.3) are, respectively,
\[
 (zy,zx),\quad (y^{-1}z,y^{-1}),\quad
 (x^{-1},x^{-1}z),\quad (x,y).
\]
Consequently these four parabolics of \(U_s\) are quotients of the full regular toroidal-map groups with orders
\[
 36,\quad 12s^2,\quad 48,\quad108. \tag{4}
\]
The four images in \(G_m\) attain exactly these orders, by the frozen proof and literal certificate. Each finite upper bound is therefore attained already in \(U_s\), and (3) is injective on every maximal parabolic.

The original all-maximal quotient-transport theorem now applies to (3). The image group has full marked subgroup IP and typed coset Helly. Thus so does \(U_s\). The associated coset geometry is flag transitive; IP makes its chamber stabilizer trivial and its panels have size two. In every residue, the remaining distinguished involutions generate its chamber action and connect its chamber graph. Hence the geometry is residually connected and regular, with full type-preserving automorphism group \(U_s\). The local order comparisons identify the exact marked toroidal residues, not only their orders.

Every other marked regular hypertope with the stated diagram and local residues satisfies the Coxeter and local presentation relations in (1). Its distinguished generators therefore define an epimorphism from \(U_s\). The restriction to each maximal parabolic is an epimorphism between the same finite marked toroidal groups, so is an isomorphism. The induced coset map is the claimed residue-preserving quotient. Universality here is explicitly in this marked regular category.

## 3. Why the rotational presentation needs no extra relators

The even subgroup \(W^+\) has the usual Reidemeister–Schreier presentation
\[
 \langle x,y,z\mid x^6,y^3,z^2,(x^{-1}y)^2,
                   (x^{-1}z)^3,(y^{-1}z)^6\rangle.
\]
Because \(z^2=1\), the last two relators are equivalent to the source's \((zx)^3\) and \((zy)^6\). Conjugation by \(r_0\) induces the automorphism \(\tau\) simultaneously inverting \(x,y,z\).

Let \(N_s\) be the normal closure in \(W^+\) of the four local words (1). It is important to check \(\tau(N_s)=N_s\); otherwise quotienting the full Coxeter group could silently impose more rotational relators.

The four local torus parameters are of the forms \((a,0)\) or \((a,a)\). In the retained triangular-lattice model, the parameter lattice is generated by \((a,b)\) and \((-b,a+b)\), and its point group is generated by
\[
 R(u,v)=(-v,u+v),\qquad S(u,v)=(v,u).
\]
For \((a,0)\) the lattice is \(a\mathbb Z^2\). For \((a,a)\), write \(v_1=(a,a)\), \(v_2=(-a,2a)\). Then
\[
 Rv_1=v_2,\quad Rv_2=-v_1+v_2,\qquad
 Sv_1=v_1,\quad Sv_2=v_1-v_2.
\]
Thus every local torus kernel is normalized by all reflections of its local Coxeter triangle. This calculation is unchanged when the varying parameter is any positive \(s\), since its lattice is \(s\mathbb Z^2\).

Choose a local reflection \(r_j\) in one of those triangles. For each element \(f\) of its local torus kernel,
\[
 r_0fr_0=(r_0r_j)(r_jfr_j)(r_jr_0).
\]
The middle element is still in that local kernel, and the outer conjugating element belongs to \(W^+\). Therefore conjugation by \(r_0\) preserves its normal closure in \(W^+\). This also handles the local triangle omitting type zero. Applying the argument to all four kernels proves \(\tau(N_s)=N_s\).

The full normal closure in \(W\) consequently equals \(N_s\). All added relations are even words, so the sign character of \(W\) survives, and \(r_0\) still gives an order-two complement. It follows that
\[
 U_s=(W^+/N_s)\rtimes\langle r_0\rangle=P_s\rtimes C_2,
\]
as asserted. This is a presented-group identification, not an assertion that the particular voltage image is faithful on \(P_s\).

## 4. Infinitude and retained boundaries

If \(6\mid s\), the defining relations give an epimorphism \(U_s\to U_6\): the relation \(T^6=1\), where \(T=y(zy)^2\), implies \(T^s=1\). The frozen fixed-parameter theorem proves \(U_6\) infinite. Hence \(U_s\) is infinite, and its index-two subgroup \(P_s\) is infinite as well. The earlier integral representation independently witnesses the same infinitude for every \(6\mid s\). No order formula for \(U_s\) is inferred from finite examples or from the size of that representation's image.

The primary source is Fernandes–Leemans–Ivić Weiss (2018), [equations (2.3)–(2.4), section 3 and section 8](https://ria.ua.pt/bitstream/10773/23862/1/2018DFW.pdf). The retained noncomputational input is the triangular-lattice presentation and order of a regular toroidal map, together with the standard even-subgroup presentation of a Coxeter group. The finite examples and generic quotient transport are separately certified packages. This corollary makes no new finite computation, no full Lean formalization claim, no odd-parameter classification, and no current-literature priority claim.
