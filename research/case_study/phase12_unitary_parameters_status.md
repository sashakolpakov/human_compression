# Source and priority boundary for the characteristic-three unitary family

Audit date: 10 October 2026. This is a bounded source check, not a determination of first priority.

The exact mathematical claim under review is a regular rank-four construction for PSU₄(9^e), for every e≥1, with type {4,9^(2e)−1,6}. It combines the phase-eleven matrix template and intersection theorem with a new parameter-existence argument. It does not claim a solution for every odd q or for the remaining diagonal extensions of PSU₄(q).

The proof retains standard character-sum machinery from Nicholas Katz, [*Gauss Sums, Kloosterman Sums, and Monodromy Groups*, Annals of Mathematics Studies 116, Princeton University Press, 1988](https://web.math.princeton.edu/~nmk/Katz-GKM.pdf). The primary author-hosted PDF was opened and the following passages read: §§2.3.1–2.3.2, printed pp.31–32, for Euler characteristic and the trace formula; §3.6.1 and its proof, printed p.40, for the weight bound on compactly supported first cohomology; and §4.3, printed pp.59–60, for character sheaves from Lang isogenies of smooth connected commutative groups. These inputs include nonsplit tori. The eight-page introductory context and unrelated monodromy theorems have not been represented as fully audited.

For the group-theoretic context, the retained primary Leemans–Vandenschrick manuscript *On simple groups that admit a string C-group representation* was reread on its first two pages, including Theorem 1.1 and the statement immediately following it. This establishes existence in rank three for odd PSU₄(q), q≥5, and excludes PSU₄(3) at every rank; the inspected statement is not a rank-four existence theorem. [Institutional publication metadata](https://difusion.ulb.ac.be/vufind/Record/ULB-DIPOT%3Aoai%3Adipot.ulb.ac.be%3A2013/397490/Details) lists the published work in *The Art of Discrete and Applied Mathematics* 9 (2026), article #1.07. The previously retained direct manuscript URL returned an institutional redirect error during this audit; the saved primary manuscript was therefore used for the stated reading scope.

Seven fresh search queries combined PSU4/PSU(4,q), unitary groups, regular polytopes, rank four and the author names. They did not locate a primary source explicitly asserting the uniform PSU₄(9^e) family. Results about chiral polytopes, rank-three maps, real or complex Lie groups, and orthogonal isometry groups do not establish this exact claim. These limited searches, together with the [earlier bounded unitary source audit](phase9_linear_unitary_status.md), do not certify that the family was previously unknown. Priority remains unverified; no named published conjecture specific to this characteristic-three rank-four family is asserted.

The exact queries, retained-source checksum and fresh checker hashes are recorded in the companion JSON. No third-party full text is included in the public proof package.
