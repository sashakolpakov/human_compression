## 1. The nonabelian base causes no cocycle gap

The marking of H is independent: actual IP prevents any marked involution from belonging to the subgroup generated by the others. Burnside's basis theorem therefore supplies the stated coordinate characters on H/Φ(H). The cocycle uses only those homomorphisms, not commutativity of H.

For each external edge, expanding χ(bc)=χ(b)+χ(c) gives the cocycle identity. It is normalized, so the multiplication has an identity and inverses. Its restriction to H×H vanishes because an edge in E₀ has an endpoint outside I. Thus H embeds with its actual multiplication. The cross-edge commutator is the individual central edge coordinate; each such coordinate is generated and independent. The vertex markers generate the base and these commutators generate the full central kernel.

## 2. Every parabolic is identified exactly

The coordinate set in equation (2) is closed under the actual multiplication because absent vertex characters vanish. Products of the indicated markers project onto every desired base element. Any central coordinate introduced while taking those products lies on an edge within S and can be removed using the available edge commutators. This proves equality with the stated coordinate set, not merely containment or an order estimate.

Set intersections now reduce to the actual marked IP in H and intersections of the vertex/edge coordinate sets. This establishes all global parabolic intersection equalities without assuming deletion retractions on H. Such retractions would be a stronger hypothesis and are not needed here.

Projection of a commutator to the base lies in H′. The embedded H supplies all of H′, and the cross-edge commutators supply all of Z. Characters vanish on H′; hence the cocycle produces no extra terms involving H′, and Z is central. The derived group is therefore exactly H′×Z and the next lower-central subgroup is exactly the embedded γ₃(H). The parabolic formula also proves that its least marked support is unchanged.

## 3. Attachment and extraction are inverse

The forest quotient determines |H| from its induced graph and |γ₃(H)|. The coordinate construction then has exactly the required order 2^(2d−1+k), with IP, involution markers and noncommuting edges. Its kernel support is exactly J and its marked subgroup on N[J] is H. ## 4. Gap patterns do not overcount

For a support run, its closed neighborhood adds at most one vertex on either side. An internal gap of length one contributes a single shared neighborhood vertex; a gap of length two contributes two adjacent neighborhood vertices. These are different bounded core shapes. A gap of length at least three contributes two separated boundary vertices, and every additional interior vertex lies outside the core. Its ordered induced forest and distinguished J-subset are therefore independent of that extra length. Similarly an end gap of length zero and one of positive length give two distinct bounded core patterns, while further length at a positive end lies outside the core.

A subset J of an ordered path has one unique sequence of support-run lengths, exact short-gap categories and long/end excess lengths. Distinct such data cannot yield the same J at a fixed rank. Distinct marked core isomorphism classes cannot yield isomorphic global ordered markings, by extraction. Reflection of the path is not an allowed identification unless it sends each indexed marker to itself; the counting convention is expressly ordered marking, not unmarked group isomorphism or duality.

Hence each finite pattern contributes precisely m z^b/(1−z)^r, where m is its number of admissible core classes. This remains valid when r=0, as a single monomial. Excluding ranks below three changes only finitely many coefficients.

There are at most t≤k support runs, at most 3k support vertices and r≤t+1 expandable gaps. Thus the coefficient polynomials have degree at most k. The explicit construction with k order-64 seeds gives the lower bound binomial(d−2,k), so the eventual degree is exactly k for every fixed positive k.

Finally b≤3k+3(k−1)+2=6k−1. If r=0, both end gaps vanish and all internal gaps have length at most two, so b≤3k+2(k−1)=5k−2. Therefore the isolated monomial contributions have vanished when d≥6k−1, and all other terms have their ordinary polynomial coefficient formula throughout that range. This is a sufficient stabilization bound; minimality of the bound and explicit coefficients for arbitrary k are not claimed.
