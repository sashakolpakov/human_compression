For A, the reuse is concrete. Unique paths determine forest subgroup intersections; laminar sets complete flags; a sign kernel handles the second symmetric marking family. These mechanisms feed both the 1998 property theorem and the 2002 classification. One relative parabolic-generation induction supplies the rank/independence consequences and, after the Frattini ladder and quotient, the 2005 coordinate classification. The latter requires additional proofs, all present in the assembled body. Its 23 adapters are a coverage inventory, not 23 independent discoveries or a compression ratio.

The answer to “do the main results become relatively straightforward corollaries?” is **yes, relative to this proved core and its stated inputs**. For example, even-rank primitivity becomes the observation that a tree with an odd number of vertices has no balanced edge cut. Finite-p-group RWPRI geometry becomes coordinate linear algebra after the shared parabolic formula. However, the extremal symmetric recognition and arbitrary-parabolic recovery are substantive proofs inside the core, while the foundational 1997 classification and both Whiston inputs remain imported. Their classification dependence has not disappeared.

The finite version and a conditional infinite replacement are proved. Its geometric Frattini result still allows arbitrary groups at finite rank. Quoted Gaschütz and Archer results are disclosed imports; they are not needed by the new coordinate core. Historical counts, open questions and specified auxiliary material remain excluded. This supports the **corrected main-result contract**, not literal reproduction of every source statement.

For B, kernel transport and Boolean deletion supply actual intersection and splitting arguments. Those costs occupy substantial manuscript sections and Appendix A. F016's semiregular and free-extension geometry also retains substantial earlier construction theorems. The final adapters are short because this work has been done or expressly imported, not because all four papers follow from a tiny elementary lemma.

The [primitive-saturation bridge](phase38_primitive_saturation.md) connects A's Frattini branch with B. An irredundant marking of a finite p-group fixes a unique primitive coarsening of each omission action. For forest involution markings, the canonical class-two quotient has exactly compatible parabolic kernels. Substitution then gives all face and induced-residue indices in the prescribed-square family. The root reviewed the written proof and replayed finite diagnostics. This is a useful common theorem. It does not absorb A's symmetric classification: adjacent tree transpositions have odd braid relations incompatible with the proposed deletion maps. A single seven-source manuscript remains future work.
