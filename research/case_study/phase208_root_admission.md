# Admission of the corrected main results for design constructions

## Decision and exact coverage

Accept the corrected principal results of D. Leemans, *A Generalization
of a Construction due to Van Nypelseer*, Beiträge zur Algebra und
Geometrie 46 (2005), 561–574 (L092). The admitted deductions include the
rank-three applications, the complete higher-rank Steiner classification,
and the rank-six construction with the native M24 action. The complete
source comparison is in the unchanged phase186 contract, read together
with the independent phase199 review and its additional path correction.

This is one additional record of recovered main results. It is not a
claim that every assertion of the paper follows unchanged, or that the
new family of topic application papers has been written and measured.
False source assertions are recorded as corrections, not as corollaries.

Theorem 1 retains its hypothesis of a thick connected **partial linear
space**. The conclusion concerns the linear, dual-linear and (4,3,4)
alternatives. The chamber configurations have four vertices and three
edges. The printed conventional four-edge formulation is false: the
independent affine-plane example has 216 chambers and a regular ASL(2,3)
action of order 216, but 432 such four-edge paths. No broader necessity
theorem for arbitrary rank-two starting geometries is admitted from the
abstract alone.

Theorem 11 gives precisely the binary affine-plane design in dimension
three, the GF(4)-subline design on PG(1,16), and the three large Witt
designs. Its proof keeps residual connectivity separate from chamber
transitivity. The source's incorrect intermediate connectivity claims
are not used as premises. The construction, geometric properties, object
counts, native actions and displayed stabilizer structures are supplied.
The two rank-five residues of the M24 construction are nonisomorphic;
their identifications with Pasini's named geometries Γ23(4) and Γ8(4)
remain unproved here.

## Reusable mathematics and attribution

The construction remains attributed to the source and its predecessors.
The replacement organizes its proof through a residue isomorphism with a
Boolean join, a uniform reduction to derived rank-three designs, and one
equivariant chamber fiber over an ordered tuple. Necessity in the residue
theorem uses a complete ordinary chain. The exceptional types are treated
explicitly in both the connectivity and intersection arguments.

The projective and affine bases, determinant-one actions, plane invariant
in higher dimension and binary affine identification are proved directly.
The projective proof includes order two, which the higher-rank binary
application needs. Explicit maps from the large Witt derivatives to the
coordinate plane PG(2,4) supply their geometric bases. Full small-Witt
automorphism computations justify the exclusions by chamber counts.

Two substantial classifications are **borrowed inputs**: Buekenhout,
Dehaye and Leemans, Theorem 6.2 of L102, and Kantor, Theorem 3 of
*Homogeneous Designs and Geometric Lattices*, including the semilinear
upper bound in its proof. L092 itself cites them as Theorems 2 and 10.
They are not credited as new consequences of the elementary recursion,
nor is an extra source-recovery count assigned for citing them.
The native Mathieu actions and their classical interpretation are also
retained inputs. Full group computations rely on GAP and DESIGN/GRAPE;
checking the displayed generators alone would not prove completeness.

The packet also includes the cyclic-biplane counterexample to the
unrestricted neighbor-connectivity implication, the corrected deleted-line
and subline results, and uniform Hermitian residue labels correcting the
specified Figure 2 entries. The existing Hermitian-unital conjecture proof
is retained without a second solution credit. Historical priority remains
unverified.
