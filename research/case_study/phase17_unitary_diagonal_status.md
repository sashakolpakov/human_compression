# Bounded primary-source audit for diagonal unitary extensions

10 October 2026. Publication priority remains unverified. This record distinguishes the proposed extension theorem from the published simple-group results and from questions about graph or field automorphisms.

The two phase-seventeen proofs propose rank-four existence, of type $\{4,2(q+1),2p\}$, for the unique diagonal index-two extension of $\operatorname{PSU}_4(q)$ for every odd prime power $q=p^e\ge3$. This group is $\operatorname{PGU}_4(q)$ when $q\equiv1\pmod4$ and its index-two subgroup over the socle when $q\equiv3\pmod4$. Their maximum-four conclusion retains the earlier proposed bilinear-form upper bound. Rank-three existence is not asserted.

## Primary checkpoints actually read

- Leemans, [*String C-group representations of almost simple groups: a survey*, arXiv:1910.08843v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1910.08843v2), 11 May 2020: definitions, Theorem 3.1 and §5.7 were reread in this investigation and phase sixteen. Its printed Problem 7 concerns the **linear** PSL4 socle. It is not a named conjecture about these unitary extensions. No named maximum-rank question specifically for PGU4 was located in the inspected survey sections.
- Leemans–Vandenschrick, [*On simple groups that admit a string C-group representation*](https://dipot.ulb.ac.be/dspace/bitstream/2013/397490/3/simpleGroups6.pdf), listed as *Art Discrete Appl. Math.* 9 (2026), #1.07 by the [author publication list](https://leemans.dimitri.web.ulb.be/papers.html). The locally cached primary manuscript's introduction and Theorem 1.1 were reread. They rule out the **simple** group PSU4(3) in every rank. They do not rule out its diagonal extension. Their retained positive rank-three result concerns nonexceptional simple groups and is not imported for the extensions. A fresh network opening of the manuscript failed; the reading used the previously preserved primary PDF/text.
- The generation proof's retained rank-one subgroup list is Proposition 2.3 of the [2017 PSL2 chiral-polytope paper, arXiv:1606.08017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.08017), already inspected in the frozen unitary-generation chain. It is a group-theoretic input, not an existing source for the new four-generator template or its intersection property.

## Search boundary

Ten targeted fresh queries, preserved in the companion JSON, tested PGU4, projective general unitary groups, PSU4 index-two extensions, regular polytopes and string C-groups. The preceding phase-sixteen audit also searched linear/unitary diagonal variants. Relevant primary hits led back to the simple-group classification and the survey; many other hits concerned polar spaces, cohomological rank or complex reflection polytopes. No primary theorem matching the present uniform diagonal index-two construction was located in the inspected results.

This is a bounded status audit, not exhaustive priority research. A negative search cannot establish that an exact subcase was still open. The proposed theorem extends the paper program's almost-simple rank questions, but this record attaches no named unitary conjecture, no whole-survey-problem resolution and no infinity score to it. Full orthogonal isometry extensions under exceptional low-dimensional isomorphisms are not automatically the same as the diagonal unitary extension; graph/field automorphisms remain explicitly outside scope.

The q3 example also has an exact finite generation certificate. Its distinction from the simple-socle nonexistence theorem is mathematical, not a correction to that published theorem.
