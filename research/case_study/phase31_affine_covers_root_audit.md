## Additional corollary: any degree parameter coprime to six

This is a consequence added by root, separately reviewed by the survey
agent. It does not alter the frozen author's prime-indexed statement.

For every integer n≥2 with gcd(n,6)=1, put

    Π_n=[Π,Π]Π^n.

This subgroup is characteristic, and the genus-four surface presentation
gives Π/Π_n≅(C_n)^8. The associated connected regular cover has degree
t=n^8, and every seed symmetry lifts by the same argument as in the
reviewed proof. If ℓ is the smallest prime divisor of n, every nonidentity
deck element has order at least ℓ≥5. The author's general numerical

    ℓ(E−V−F)=6ℓ≥30=E.
