## 2. A general coprime affine pullback lemma

Let G be a finite marked group, f:G->D a marked epimorphism with kernel N, and let V be an elementary abelian p-group on which G acts through D. Suppose p does not divide |N|. Let Hbar be a subgroup of V semidirect D generated by specified affine lifts of the marked generators of D, projecting onto D. Define H inside V semidirect G using the same shifts and the marked generators of G. Use either affine convention consistently; here the author's earlier right-action notation gives `(v,g)(u,h)=(v^h+u,gh)`.

For every marked subset I, write G_I, D_I, H_I, Hbar_I for the corresponding generated subgroups and N_I=N intersect G_I. The image of H_I in Hbar_I is onto, as is H_I->G_I.

**Full-parabolic-preimage fact.** H_I is the entire preimage of Hbar_I in V semidirect G_I under the map to V semidirect D_I.

Proof: N_I acts trivially on V. For any n in N_I choose an element m in the cyclic subgroup <n> with m^p=n; this exists because p is coprime to the order of n. Surjectivity H_I->G_I gives some `(v,m)` in H_I, and `(v,m)^p=(0,n)` since pV=0. Thus H_I contains the whole standard N_I. Its image is Hbar_I, so it is the entire preimage. This argument does not assume N is abelian or contained in G_I. Only its intersection N_I is used. For a 2-group G and odd p the coprimality is automatic.

In particular H is the full preimage of Hbar, and ker(H->G) is isomorphic to ker(Hbar->D), with its actual vector-space structure and dimension. The same statement holds for the kernel of each parabolic projection. No kernel order is inferred solely from a projected image order.

## 3. The exact pullback defect-kernel condition

Assume Hbar has the intersection property. For two marked subsets I,J put K=I intersect J. By the full-preimage fact, an element of H_I intersect H_J has its Hbar image in Hbar_K. Its G-coordinate lies in

```text
(G_I intersect G_J) intersect f^-1(D_K).
```

Because f(G_K)=D_K and G_K lies in G_I intersect G_J, that group equals

```text
(N intersect G_I intersect G_J) G_K.
```

Indeed, for g in the first group choose k in G_K with f(k)=f(g); then gk^-1 lies in N and in both parabolics. Conversely every such product belongs to the first group.

It follows that

```text
H_I intersect H_J = H_K
    if and only if N intersect G_I intersect G_J <= G_K.     (* )
```

For necessity, each standard element `(0,n)` with n in N intersect G_I intersect G_J lies in both lifted groups; IP puts it in H_K and hence n in G_K. For sufficiency, the displayed coordinate characterization and the full-preimage description of H_K give equality. This is an exact criterion, not merely a sufficient dimensional bound.

If the two old endpoint groups A=G012 and B=G123 are C-groups, all local instances of (*) inside an endpoint hold automatically. The recursive string criterion then reduces the remaining obstruction to

```text
N intersect (A intersect B) <= C,    C=G12.                    (**)
```

Thus N may extend outside C, even outside both ends, without preventing the pullback. But condition (**) is indispensable. The fixed cover's IP alone does not remove a defect already killed by f.

If A intersect B=C disjoint-union C*kappa, verifying (**) is equivalent to f(kappa) not belonging to f(C). A statement that f(kappa) is merely nonidentity is weaker and does not suffice: it could be a nontrivial element of f(C). More complicated excess intersections require every relevant coset to be treated, or an exact subgroup computation proving (**).

## 4. Type and degree transport

Identify H with the fiber product of G and Hbar over D. The marked product rho_i rho_(i+1) then has order the least common multiple of the orders of its two coordinates. Suppose the old adjacent order is m_i, its image in D has order bar_m_i, and the affine extension gives adjacent order p*bar_m_i. If p does not divide m_i, the new adjacent order is exactly p*m_i. If the fixed affine adjacent order is bar_m_i, it remains m_i. These claims require the *exact* fixed adjacent orders and the exact source marked orders.

A p^d fixed covering kernel yields |G|p^d for the full pullback and the same parabolic kernel dimensions. It does not assert that the dimension of the ambient module is the kernel dimension, or that any resulting cover is minimal.

## 5. Distinct minimal descendants and the common-base requirement

For a finite polytopal marked cover H_p->G with p-power kernel and G nonpolytopal, choosing a maximal admissible normal subgroup of its kernel gives a minimal regular descendant Q_p of degree p^a over G for some a>=1. The regular-cover/normal-quotient and smallest-regular-maniplex correspondences remain explicit geometric inputs. A new all-parameter proof must establish that G is that correct smallest regular maniplex for each source parameter.

If two constructions use distinct primes p and ell over the *same marked G*, their minimal descendants have degrees p^a and ell^b with a,b>=1, hence different group orders and cannot coincide. No explicit value of either exponent is needed for this existence argument. Having one F3 model for one sign or one family and one F5 model for another would not prove the paired conclusion; each source parameter needs both transports.

For the relation kernels J_p,J_ell inside K0=ker(F->G) of the same universal marked group, the index of J_p J_ell in K0 divides both p^d and ell^e. Coprimality gives equality. This establishes the stated join property provided both covers are already proved admissible; it does not presume that their mix is polytopal.
