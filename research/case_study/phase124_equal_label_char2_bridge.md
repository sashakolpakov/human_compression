# Equal-label power relations from a singular radical

10 October 2026. Frozen author note; root has read the algebra and retained theorem interface, with separate hash-bound review pending. Not fully formalized. This is an additional partial attack on Li–Zhang, *Regular 3-polytopes of type {n,n}*, arXiv:2505.09505v1, Conjecture 3.7. It proves finite existence for every rank and the labels below, retaining the published symmetry construction of Brooksbank–Ferrara–Leemans (L031). It does not identify the universal group defined by the source relators or prove that universal group's intersection property.

## 1. The elementary local identity

Let F be a field of characteristic two, V a vector space with quadratic form Q, and B(x,y)=Q(x+y)+Q(x)+Q(y) its polar form. V may be degenerate. For Q(u)=1 write

\[
\sigma_u(x)=x+B(x,u)u.
\]

This linear map preserves Q and is involutory. If z is singular, Q(z)=0, and u lies in z-perp, define

\[
T_u(x)=x+B(x,z)u+\bigl(B(x,u)+Q(u)B(x,z)\bigr)z. \tag{1}
\]

For all u,v in z-perp,

\[
T_uT_v=T_{u+v}=T_vT_u,\qquad T_0=1,\qquad T_u^2=1. \tag{2}
\]

Here multiplication means composition of linear maps, with the rightmost map applied first. To check (2), put b=B(x,z). Since B(u,z)=B(v,z)=0, applying either T preserves b. The coefficient of u+v in the composite is b, and its coefficient of z is

\[
 B(x,u)+B(x,v)+b\bigl(Q(u)+Q(v)+B(u,v)\bigr)
 =B(x,u+v)+bQ(u+v).
\]

This proves the claimed formula directly, including when z is radical in all of V. Thus the T_u form an elementary abelian group (possibly with a nontrivial parameter kernel). If Q(u)=1, then Q(u+z)=1, B(u,u+z)=0, and direct composition gives

\[
\sigma_u\sigma_{u+z}=T_u. \tag{3}
\]

If g preserves Q and fixes z, conjugating (1) gives g T_u g^{-1}=T_{g(u)}. In particular this elementary abelian subgroup is normalized by every such g preserving the chosen parameter space.

**Local power lemma.** Suppose e,f,h are vectors with Q(e)=Q(f)=Q(h)=1,

\[
B(e,h)=0,\qquad B(e,f)=B(f,h)=\alpha.
\]

Put a=σ_e, b=σ_f, c=σ_h, A=ab and C=bc. Then

\[
(C^k A^k)^2=1 \quad\text{for every integer }k. \tag{4}
\]

**Proof.** Put z=e+h and W=span(e,f,h). Then Q(z)=0 and B(z,W)=0. The three symmetries preserve W and fix z. Equation (3) gives t=ac=T_e. Let T be the subgroup generated by T_u for u in W; equations (1)–(2) show it is elementary abelian, and the conjugation identity shows it is normalized by A. Since C=A^{-1}t, its image in the group generated by T and A, modulo T, is A^{-1}. Hence C^k A^k belongs to T for every integer k, and its square is one. ∎

This proof works in the whole ambient space, including adjacent coordinates outside W. Proving the identity only on W would be insufficient when W is degenerate.

## 2. Constant-coefficient symmetry groups

For d≥3, give F_q^d the quadratic form

\[
Q(x_0,\ldots,x_{d-1})=\sum_{i=0}^{d-1}x_i^2+
 \alpha\sum_{i=0}^{d-2}x_ix_{i+1},\qquad q=2^e.
\]

Let r_i=σ_{e_i}. Nonadjacent r_i commute, and all adjacent products have the same order as

\[
H_\alpha=\begin{pmatrix}1&\alpha\\\alpha&1+\alpha^2\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The equality of orders on the full space follows as well from the published symmetry construction; alternatively, the adjacent support is a nondegenerate two-space, so V is its orthogonal direct sum with its perp, on which both markers act trivially.

We retain the following exact input from Brooksbank, Ferrara and Leemans, *Orthogonal groups in characteristic 2 acting on polytopes of high rank*, arXiv:1709.02219v2 (2018), published in Discrete & Computational Geometry 63 (2020), 656–669:

- Lemma 5.3, Proposition 3.3, Proposition 5.5 and the proof of Theorem 1.4 give full string-C-group IP for **any** choices of adjacent coefficients having order q+1, for q≥4. In particular all coefficients can be the same α.
- Remark 5.4, with the q≥8 qualification in Remark 3.5, permits order q−1 coefficients and mixtures of the two orders. We use the constant order q−1 case only for q≥8. The exceptional q=4 order-three case is handled instead by the separate symmetric-group construction.

The full generation and parabolic-intersection argument of that paper is retained. It is not reclassified as a new result of this note. The finite-field choice of α is elementary: choose λ of exact order q+1 in the norm-one subgroup of F_(q²)^×, or of exact order q−1 in F_q^×, and take α to be the unique square root in F_q of λ+λ^{-1}. Then α≠0, and the characteristic polynomial of H_α is X²+α²X+1, with distinct eigenvalues λ,λ^{-1}; its order is exactly the chosen n.

**Corollary.** For every d≥3, finite regular d-polytopes satisfying all the Li–Zhang relations exist for

\[
n=2^e+1\ (e\ge2),\qquad n=2^e-1\ (e\ge3).
\]

Together with the separate n=3 and n=4 constructions, this includes 3,4,5,7,9,15,17,31,33,… in every rank.

**Proof.** The retained theorem supplies a finite string C-group on the r_i, with exact adjacent order n. Every consecutive triple has the equal-coefficient hypotheses of the local power lemma, which proves all additional relations for 1≤k<n. The standard string-C-group/polytope correspondence gives the finite regular polytope. ∎

No application to arbitrary divisors of q±1 is made: the retained generation and IP theorem requires full adjacent orders. In particular this argument does not cover arbitrary odd n or any even n≥6. Nor does a finite string-C-group image prove IP of the universal source presentation: relevant parabolic injectivity must be separately established before any quotient criterion can be invoked. Historical novelty of this specialization has not been checked.

## 3. Verification boundary

The written local identity is a direct quadratic-space calculation, not an experimental extrapolation. Finite computational examples, if supplied, check that calculation and the displayed matrices; they do not replace the retained all-rank IP theorem. No complete Lean proof of this corollary is presently claimed. A formal proof would need the actual quadratic-space identity and a precisely stated bridge to the retained IP construction and source polytope correspondence.


The exact companion checker has passed over F4,F8,F16,F32, all permitted q±1 labels and ranks3–8: 42 full matrix markings and 2,310 added power relators. It also checks 8,448 translation-parameter pairs modulo the singular radical in rank5, with ambient coordinates on both sides of the triple, plus144 conjugation identities. Independent finite subgroup generation gives full IP for (q,n,d)=(4,5,4),(8,7,3),(8,9,3), respectively of orders8,160;896;1,152 (208 subset pairs). These are finite diagnostics, not new uniform IP proofs.

The last example makes the quotient distinction concrete. For n=9 the source rank-three group has stated order2^9·9=4,608, while this matrix image has order1,152. Therefore this construction cannot silently be treated as a faithful realization of every source parabolic.
