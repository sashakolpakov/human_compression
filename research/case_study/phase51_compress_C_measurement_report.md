## What counts

The numerator uses fresh default pypdf 6.13.0 extraction of the exact primary
PDFs, page texts joined by one newline. The source body starts at its Abstract.
Only hash-bound bibliographic or page furniture is excluded. All mathematical
and historical prose, examples, auxiliary statements, proofs, diagram glyphs
and captions remain. In L088 the References section is followed by two
substantive figure pages: **both pages 9 and 10 count**. Bibliography,
affiliations, acknowledgments, MSC/keywords and repeated interactive navigation
do not. In N013 the body ends at References on page 10; page 11 is entirely
bibliography and affiliations. Every source page was visually inspected and
both complete fresh text extractions were read for these boundary decisions.

No source OCR, ligature repair, control-character replacement or hyphen
joining is performed. L088's legacy font encodings produce control characters;
they remain literal. Diagrams count through their extracted text and glyphs,
not through a mathematical encoding of their drawn edges. This is the same
declared literal-extraction counting convention used in the preceding PDF
measurements, not an estimate of all information conveyed by a figure.
