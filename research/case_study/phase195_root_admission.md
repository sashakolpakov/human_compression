# Root admission: parallel gluing and simultaneous matching

## Decision

Accept the corrected partial gluing contract in the frozen phase187
manuscript, together with the explicit written supplements in the
independent phase191 review. The eleven source-ledger rows are admitted
under their stated type-preservation, parallelism and shadow-separation
hypotheses. This adds an application of U.Helly; it does not revise the
frozen U-v3 manuscript or the A–D editions.

The common-lift criterion is an exact statement: local direction-action
lifts must have one common image. The geometric reconstruction supplies
the converse under property (O), or within the parallel-preserving
isomorphisms without (O). The review supplement explicitly proves both
general chamber extension and reconstruction without (O); these paragraphs
are included in the conservative proof cost. The diagonal-orbit description,
two-factor double cosets, automorphism fiber product and uniqueness
criterion then follow. No splitting of the resulting exact sequence is
assumed.

Inverting the lift sets gives the right cosets of the conjugated groups
used in U.Helly. The application checks the side of each coset and the
composition convention. Equivalence with typed Helly requires testing
all subfamilies, as the manuscript states. An ordinary subgroup
intersection property is not substituted for this hypothesis.

## Source and evidence

The source is Buekenhout–Huybrechts–Pasini, *Parallelism in diagram
geometry*, 1994, pp. 355–397. Root visually read the bound primary pages
356, 357, 358, 362, 364 and 365: definitions, the type convention,
parallelism axioms, construction, reconstruction and printed Lemma 3.4.
The independent reviewer read §§1–3 completely; this is not a complete
reading or replacement of the 43-page paper. Root also read the full
replacement manuscript, independent review, eleven-row source contract,
both mathematical programs, both drivers and complete Lean file.

The source imposes no hidden linear-space or equal-block-size condition
on Lemma 3.4. The example's twelve distinct subsets of eight points
satisfy its stated shadow assumption. The full base group is dihedral
of order 16, with direction image of order two and kernel of order eight.
Three matched copies yield lift constraints {1,a}, {1,b}, {a,b}.
All six printed pairwise conditions hold, but the two gluings have 136
and 128 objects. The independent size obstruction establishes
nonisomorphism without relying on the corrected theorem.

Root verified all 23 author-manifest bindings in the private checkpoint
and all eighteen independent exports. A fresh private reviewer replay
reproduced the frozen report byte for byte. A separate public replay
then ran the unchanged author Python calculation, unchanged independent
GAP reconstruction, fresh Lean compilation and all five negative controls.
The public adapter requires only distributed mathematical inputs; it
does not require primary PDFs or unrelated screening records.

The two finite calculations agree on objects, direction multiplicities,
flags, panels and all rank-at-least-two residues. There are respectively
1193 and 1185 connected residues. Each geometry has 2048 chambers, 1536
panels with two completions and 1280 with four. The independent diagnostic
also checks the uniqueness criterion on all 216 ordered subgroup triples
of S3, including 34 transitive cases. This last finite check supplements
the general written proof.

The twelve Lean declarations prove the common-image/coherent-family
equivalence and the finite coset obstruction. They do not formalize the
full incidence geometry, its automorphism group or source reconstruction.
The source imports only Std and compiles with Lean 4.32.2. Observed axioms
are Classical.choice and propext. Fresh canonical-source equality is
recorded with the public compiler output; no native-evaluation axiom or
unproved placeholder is admitted.

## Attribution and accounting

The geometry construction and reconstruction remain attributed to the
1994 source. The source proof itself already requires simultaneous
lifts; the error is replacing that requirement with independently
satisfiable pairwise conditions. The application corrects that step,
binds it to U.Helly and supplies the literal counterexample and scoped
certificates. It makes no historical-priority claim.

The source is an auxiliary outside the fixed 161-work registry. This
admission does not change its 57 encoded works, 42 component rederivations,
19 principal contracts or the sixteen principal contracts in A–D. The
number of explicitly reported auxiliary source comparisons becomes two,
including the earlier Loyola application. One canonical Lean file brings
the scoped-file count to 73. Conjecture-result counts are unchanged.

The author core is 31,440 bytes. With full U, independent GAP code,
the public driver and the complete written review supplement, the
conservative allocation is 123,587 bytes. The 191 driver remains a
historical artifact; the public driver replaces it in that allocation.
Logs, duplicate staged files and audit metadata are separately bound
in the export. No whole-source numerator or compression factor is assigned.
All previous A–D length measurements remain attached to their editions.

The generic argument is a concrete use of the retained-library method:
keep U's proof fixed, discharge a new application's hypotheses and charge
the entire application. No pushout universal property or complexity
improvement is inferred merely from that reuse.
