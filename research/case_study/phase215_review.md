## Primary statement

I read the primary author PDF of Li–Zhang, arXiv:2505.09505v1, physical page 11, both in its local extraction and as a fresh raster. Conjecture 3.7 is an existence assertion for every d,n >= 3, with exact type {n,...,n}, together with the two displayed sets of relators. The typed Coxeter quotient conditions do not relax the intersection property required by a regular polytope.

The first set is the involution relation at every marker, product order dividing n for every adjacent pair, and commuting distant markers. The second set is ((r_(i+1)r_(i+2))^k (r_i r_(i+1))^k)^2, for every consecutive triple and every k=1,...,n-1. The two exploratory GAP scripts and frozen certificate generator transcribe this literally at n=11, with no extra relations. The C++ verifier uses the same pair and triple words; its symmetric edges implement the involution relators explicitly.

For d=3,4,5 there are respectively 16,30,45 literal relators when the generator squares are included. A universal-group statement alone would be stronger than the source; a refutation of existence must additionally exclude every quotient with the exact type. That final quotient argument is separately required.

## Soundness of the finite upper-bound verifier

Fix the actual group U given by the literal presentation, and H generated by its first d-1 markers. Associate each table label u with a word w_u and hence an actual right coset H w_u. There is no distinctness assumption on these cosets.

Initially w_1 is the empty word, and H times any subgroup generator is H. These justify exactly the root loops installed by the program.

During breadth-first search, an edge from an introduced label u to a new label v defines w_v=w_u r_s. Installing the reverse edge is sound because r_s is an involution. The supplied table chooses the naming tree; this is not the assumption that all of its transitions hold. A freshly named coset may in fact coincide with an older one, which causes no problem for an upper bound.

Maintain the invariant that every installed transition u --s--> v has been proved to satisfy H w_u r_s=H w_v. For a literal relator word beginning at u, the program traverses the known prefix forward and the known suffix backward. The latter is sound because every letter is self-inverse. If exactly one transition is missing, the equation imposed by the relator proves that missing edge, and involutivity proves its reverse. This is the only non-tree installation rule.

The oracle is consulted to reject a proposed deduction if its target disagrees with the supplied table. It supplies no unproved target to the one-gap deduction: that target is the end of the already known reverse suffix. If a completely traversed relator would require two different labels to coincide, the program rejects rather than assuming that coincidence. The rejection is conservative, since two labels could represent the same actual coset; it cannot produce a false upper bound.

On COMPLETE, every transition from every named coset has been proved. The finite set of at most N actual cosets contains H and is closed under every group generator. Induction on word length shows that it contains every right H-coset. Therefore [U:H] <= N. Equality, distinct labels and a faithful permutation action are not required.

I checked the full C++ implementation against this invariant, including both directions of install, root self-loops, duplicate labels, inverse scans, bounds checks, breadth-first reachability, and the early completion condition. Reaching all N*d entries already proves the upper bound; a final separate scan of every relator is not necessary for that implication.

## The finite-field model and the geometric interfaces

I read the complete final phase212 manuscript, native GAP code, binary-field checker, portable driver, formal source and scope file, as well as the complete phase214 manuscript, C++ code, portable driver, table metadata, source contract and scope. This review concerns the conjecture clause, not recovery of every result in the source paper.

The field model is F₂[θ]/(θ⁵+θ²+1), with α=θ⁵ and β=θ³. GAP checks its polynomial relation and the selected α; the separate Python implementation uses the binary polynomial directly. Thus the two implementations have an explicit field-coordinate identification, rather than merely equal cardinality. The source's 45 literal rank-five relators are checked, including all thirty triple-power relations. Exact adjacent order eleven is verified, not inferred from a relation whose exponent is eleven.

The radical of the five-dimensional polar form is the line generated by z₀=(1,0,1,0,1), and Q(z₀)=1. Quotient coordinates are (x₀+x₄,x₁,x₂+x₄,x₃). The quotient interface is checked on all basis vectors. The written proof of faithfulness is complete: a projectively trivial isometry induces a scalar on the quotient; preservation of the nondegenerate alternating form gives λ²=1, so λ=1; the original transformation is x↦x+ℓ(x)z₀, and preservation of Q forces ℓ=0. The radical itself is fixed because its quadratic norm is one. This is needed to transfer subgroup distinctions between actual matrices and projective permutations.

The row-action convention is now correct. Inverse-transpose, not transpose alone, is the homomorphism from column matrices to GAP's row action. On the involutory distinguished generators and reflection witness it equals transpose. Consequently the native marked generators and witness permutations agree with the stated homomorphism. No order reversal is silently assumed for arbitrary products.

For each four-dimensional endpoint, the restricted polar form is nondegenerate. The projective singular orbit has 1025 points. The four explicit frame vectors in the manuscript are linearly independent: their successive differences supply the three nonzero pivots 5,17,31, and the initial vector supplies the fourth. The fifth vector has coefficients (14,4,4,15), each nonzero. A linear transformation fixing those five projective points is scalar; preservation of the nonzero quadratic form then forces the scalar to be one. The field checker verifies the singular norms and the fifth-vector coordinates; GAP locates the frame in the actual orbit. This proves faithfulness on the relevant isometries and makes the permutation-membership certificates sufficient for actual matrix membership.

The endpoint generators and witness also fix the orthogonal one-dimensional complement pointwise. Since the endpoint space is nondegenerate, equality there and on its complement gives equality on all five dimensions. Thus there is no unpaid bridge from four-dimensional membership to the ambient intersection.

The exact reflection is J(x)=x+B(x,u)u/Q(u), with u=e₁+βe₂. Its denominator is nonzero. In each endpoint's signed permutation group, the reflection permutation accompanied by the extra transposition is a member. The generators are formed individually from the stated marked matrices before the extra transposition is attached; their order is not inferred from an unordered generating set returned by an image constructor. The two signed membership checks therefore certify words of odd length giving precisely J in both endpoints.

The middle generators and J preserve the middle three-space and fix its singular radical e₁+e₃. Its two-dimensional quotient image is a dihedral group of order 22. The corresponding image of R₁J has exact order 33, verified independently by both group software and binary-field multiplication. It cannot lie in that order-22 group. Hence J is outside the middle subgroup. This uses an explicit witness and quotient obstruction; it does not mistake an endpoint order mismatch for a membership proof.

## Matching lower bound and the universal identification

Fresh native GAP runs give the projective rank-five image S order 1124799322521600 and report S simple. The group is noncommutative because adjacent involutions have product order eleven. The signed marked image has order 2249598645043200, exactly twice |S|. Its generators are (ε,Rᵢ), so it is a subgroup of C₂×S, and equality of finite orders makes it the full product. The induced nondegenerate alternating form and the usual symplectic-group order formula also identify S with Sp₄(32); the obstruction argument only needs the actual permutation group, its order and simplicity.

Every literal presentation relator has even word length. Thus the signed marking defines a homomorphism from U₅ to C₂×S. The independent upper certificate proves that U₅ has at most the displayed signed-image order. Surjectivity onto the signed image and matching upper and lower bounds give a marked isomorphism U₅≅C₂×S. In particular, this is a statement about the universal presented group, not only a selected quotient of it.

GAP remains a trusted finite permutation-group backend for the orders, simplicity and signed subgroup memberships. I checked that no expected-order Size option is supplied as an input. GAP's local primary StabChain documentation states that its default random parameter 1000 gives correctness probability one; no lower setting is introduced here. The present acceptance does not replace these algorithms by Lean proofs or claim an independent implementation of the large group calculations.

## Why every exact-label quotient is excluded

Let f:C₂×S→H preserve a nontrivial product of two equally signed adjacent generators. Their signs cancel, so f restricted to the S factor is nontrivial, hence injective by simplicity. If (c,s) is in its kernel, its commutator with every (1,t) has second coordinate [s,t] and also lies in the kernel. Injectivity on S makes s central. Since S is noncommutative and simple, its center is trivial. Therefore equality of two f-images implies equality of their S coordinates.

The signed witness w=(ε,J) belongs to both endpoint parabolics. Its S coordinate lies outside the projection of the middle parabolic. If f(w) belonged to that middle image, it would equal f(d) for a middle element d; equality of S coordinates would contradict the established nonmembership of J. Hence the intersection property fails after every such quotient.

An exact adjacent label eleven supplies the required nontrivial adjacent product. Thus no rank-five regular polytope satisfying the literal source relations exists. A higher-rank string C-group would have a string C-group parabolic on its first five distinguished generators, still with exact label eleven and all the required triple relators. This is impossible. The argument therefore proves the stated negative conclusion for every rank d≥5.

The standard string C-group/regular-polytope correspondence and inheritance by distinguished parabolics are retained inputs, not new results. This review does not negate the source's rank-three theorem or the separately established positive label families.
