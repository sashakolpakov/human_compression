# A uniform construction replacing several 2-group existence proofs

9 October 2026. This strengthens the [first 36-paper audit](compression_audit.md): the seed groups, their orders, their intersections, and the flag-transitivity argument are now constructed and proved, rather than imported as unexplained hypotheses. Internal mathematical reviews and exact checks are not external peer review. Priority is unclaimed for the fiber-product formulation or its elementary ingredients.

The resulting core is **a cube or an explicit square-grid quotient, followed by independent dihedral inflations on pairs of generators**. It derives the full all-parameter existence theorem of *On regular polytopes of 2-power order*, the principal construction in *Existence of regular 3-hypertopes with 2^n chambers*, and the existence half of *2-groups behaving as automorphism groups of regular 3-polytopes*. The last paper's eight-group classification is not thereby proved. The same core gives tight regular polytopes for arbitrary even types and regular hypertopes for arbitrary even diagrams.

## 1. Dihedral edge inflation

Let `(B;b_1,...,b_r)` be a finite C-group whose standard coset geometry is a regular hypertope. For a selected set E of unordered pairs e={i,j}, write `q_e=ord(b_i b_j)`. Suppose q_e is even and there is a retraction

    r_e:B -> B_e=<b_i,b_j>

fixing b_i,b_j and killing every other distinguished generator. These retractions, when they exist, are unique. Choose any multiples `m_e` of `q_e`. Let D_e be a dihedral group of order 2m_e with distinguished reflections u_e,v_e, and let

    p_e:D_e -> B_e

send those reflections to b_i,b_j. Its kernel K_e is the cyclic rotation subgroup generated by `(u_e v_e)^q_e`, of order m_e/q_e.

Define the simultaneous fiber product

    H = { (b,(d_e)) in B x product_e D_e : r_e(b)=p_e(d_e) for every e }.

Its distinguished element rho_i has B-coordinate b_i; its e-coordinate is the corresponding reflection when i belongs to e and is the identity otherwise.

**Theorem A.** These rho_i generate H, and H is the automorphism group of a regular hypertope with the same rank. The order of rho_i rho_j is m_{ij} on an inflated pair and is its original order otherwise. Moreover,

    |H| = |B| product_e (m_e/q_e),
    ker(H -> B) = product_e K_e,
    ker(H_I -> B_I) = product_(e subset I) K_e.             (1)

The standard parabolics satisfy the full intersection property. If the diagram remains a string, H is a string C-group. Each inflated pair has its own canonical retraction from H. Pair retractions not inflated also persist, so the construction can be iterated.

**Generation and orders.** The tuple lies in the fiber product and projects onto B. The word `(rho_i rho_j)^q_e` has identity B-coordinate, is a generator of K_e in coordinate e, and is the identity in every other dihedral coordinate: there at most one of rho_i,rho_j appears, and q_e is even. Thus the tuple generates every factor of the full fiber-product kernel and therefore all H. The e-coordinate gives the claimed pair order. On another coordinate a pair product is the identity or a single reflection, and its order equals the order of its image under p_e. That order divides the original pair order by the retraction, so no uninvolved coordinate increases a pair order. In particular, an odd-order uninﬂated pair cannot meet a selected edge in exactly one endpoint. Formula (1) for the total kernel and group order follows immediately.

**Parabolic kernels and IP.** If both endpoints of e belong to I, the same isolating word puts all K_e in H_I. If at least one endpoint is absent, the e-projection of H_I is trivial or generated by a single reflection; it has trivial intersection with K_e, a rotation subgroup. This proves the last formula in (1). Coordinate products have

    (product_(e subset I) K_e) intersect (product_(e subset J) K_e)
      = product_(e subset I intersect J) K_e.

The kernel-transport theorem therefore gives `H_I intersect H_J=H_(I intersect J)`. The abstract transport with this coordinate support law is formally checked in [CoordinateKernels.lean](formal/CoordinateKernels.lean); the construction of H and the geometric argument below remain written proofs.

**Flag transitivity, without an extra geometric hypothesis on H.** Project a flag of maximal-parabolic cosets to B. Because B is flag-transitive, translate by a lift of a common base point so that every projected coset is a standard B_i. Choose representatives to write the lifted flag as `H_i k_i`, with k_i in K.

For any two of these cosets, an element of their intersection projects into `B_i intersect B_j`. IP supplies an element of `H_i intersect H_j` with the same image. Multiplying by its inverse puts a common point in K. Hence these two cosets meet if and only if the corresponding cosets

    (K intersect H_i) k_i

meet inside K. Here `K intersect H_i` consists of the coordinates on edges not incident with i. Its cosets fix precisely the coordinates on edges incident with i. Pairwise intersection says that all assignments to a coordinate agree wherever two types prescribe it. Choose those prescribed values, and arbitrary values on all unprescribed coordinates. The resulting k in K belongs to every coset. Thus every flag has a common representative, proving the typed Helly condition and flag transitivity.

Since H is a C-group, the panel stabilizers have order two and all residue-generation equalities hold. Flag transitivity therefore gives a thin, residually connected geometry; its action on chambers is regular. These last assertions can also be read directly in the [torsor and partition calculus](torsor_matrix_theory.md). ∎

This is more than a recognition criterion: it produces the whole group, exact order, full kernel diagram and the missing coset Helly property from explicit data. It does not assume the desired upstairs IP or flag transitivity. The retraction condition is real and cannot be dropped indiscriminately.

**Marked symmetries.** A permutation of the marking that is an automorphism of B and preserves E, q_e and m_e lifts by permuting the dihedral coordinates and, where necessary, swapping their distinguished reflections. Conversely any such marked symmetry of H preserves the subgroup generated by the edge powers `(rho_i rho_j)^q_e` and descends to B. Thus, within these allowed diagram permutations, the symmetry stabilizer is computed exactly by the base marking and the parameter array. This supplies a uniform duality/correlation check, not merely an existence result.

## 2. The cube seed: all even diagrams

Take `B=C_2^r`, with the coordinate involutions. It is the regular hypertope of the all-commuting diagram: its maximal-parabolic cosets prescribe one binary coordinate, so their typed Helly property and all intersections are immediate. Every pair retraction is a coordinate projection and every q_e=2.

**Corollary B.** Every assignment of even pair orders m_{ij}>=2 is realized by a regular rank-r hypertope of order

    2^r product_(i<j) (m_{ij}/2).

Pairs with m_{ij}=2 may be omitted from the product. In particular, for a string with adjacent orders m_1,...,m_(r-1), the order is

    2 product_i m_i.

This proves the existence of tight regular polytopes for arbitrary even types directly, without taking an existing tight group as a seed. It rederives the established Conder result cited as Proposition 2.3 in the [2-power-order paper](https://dipot.ulb.ac.be/dspace/bitstream/2013/292444/3/HRRPR-revised.pdf). It does not assert minimality of the arbitrary-diagram construction: the product lower bound for a string cannot silently be transferred to every diagram.

For rank three and type `(2^s,2^t,2^l)`, this already attains order `2^(s+t+l)`. The next seed supplies every larger 2-power order while keeping the type adjustable independently.

## 3. An explicit seed for every excess exponent

Act on the square grid by the following affine transformations:

    a(x,y)=(-x,y),     b(x,y)=(y,x),     c(x,y)=(x,1-y).

They generate `W=Z^2 semidirect D_8`, with the dihedral point group generated by a,b. Indeed `y_0=c b a b` is translation by (0,1), and `x_0=b y_0 b` is translation by (1,0). The distinguished pair orders are 4,4,2.

The usual affine Coxeter presentation can be proved here rather than assumed. Let

    W_0=<a,b,c | a^2=b^2=c^2=(ab)^4=(bc)^4=(ac)^2=1>.

Put d=bab, y_0=cd and x_0=b y_0 b. The dihedral relations imply that a commutes with d, and hence with y_0. From c^2=1 one obtains `d y_0 d=y_0^(-1)`, so `a x_0 a=x_0^(-1)`. Conjugation by b swaps x_0,y_0. Writing `bc=x_0 ab`, the relation `(bc)^4=1` becomes `[x_0,y_0]=1`. Thus every word has a normal form `x_0^u y_0^v M`, with M in D_8. The displayed affine action distinguishes these forms, proving `W_0=W`.

Let Λ be either

    a_0 Z^2,                              or
    a_0 <(1,1),(1,-1)>,

where a_0 is a positive even integer. Both lattices are stable under D_8 and lie in 2Z^2. Put `B_Λ=(Z^2/Λ) semidirect D_8` with the three affine generators above. Its order is respectively `8a_0^2` or `16a_0^2`.

**Intersections.** The subgroup <a,b> is the point group at the origin, and <b,c> is the point group at (1/2,1/2). Every element of the latter has translation vector with coordinates in {0,1}. As Λ is contained in 2Z^2, equality modulo Λ with an element of <a,b> requires this vector to be zero. The only square symmetries fixing (1/2,1/2) are 1,b. Hence `<a,b> intersect <b,c>=<b>`. The generators are independent: c has nonzero translation, a is not in <b,c> by that intersection, and the swap b is not in the diagonal linear group <a,c>. Thus B_Λ is a string C-group of type {4,4}, and therefore its standard coset geometry is regular.

**Pair retractions.** Sending c to 1 and fixing a,b defines a homomorphism from W to <a,b>: it sends x_0 to a and y_0 to bab, two commuting involutions, so it kills 2Z^2 and therefore Λ. Interchanging a and c supplies the other adjacent-pair retraction; this interchange is induced by an affine symmetry preserving each displayed lattice. Finally, fixing a,c and killing b sends both translation generators to ac, again an involution, and also kills Λ. All three pair retractions exist.

For every integer δ>=0 choose

    Λ_δ = 2^(δ/2+1) Z^2                              if δ is even,
    Λ_δ = 2^((δ+1)/2) <(1,1),(1,-1)>                if δ is odd.

The group B_δ has exactly `2^(δ+5)` elements. This is a fully explicit three-generator seed for every δ; no list of small groups or unproved presentation-order computation is required.

## 4. Actual published main statements recovered

### Every allowed type in every rank: L032, Theorem 1.2

Let r>=3, k_1,...,k_(r-1)>=2 and `n>=1+sum k_i`. Put `δ=n-1-sum k_i`. Take

    B = C_2^(r-3) x B_δ,

with the independent C2 generators first and a,b,c last. Its diagram has adjacent orders 2 except for the final two orders 4,4, and every pair retraction exists. Inflate adjacent pair i to order `2^k_i`, leaving all nonadjacent pairs at order two. Theorem A gives

    |H| = 2^(δ+5+r-3)
          product_(i<=r-3) 2^(k_i-1)
          product_(i=r-2,r-1) 2^(k_i-2)
        = 2^(δ+1+sum k_i) = 2^n.

The result is a string C-group of the required rank and exact type. This proves the full statement of Hou–Feng–Leemans, [*On regular polytopes of 2-power order*, Theorem 1.2](https://dipot.ulb.ac.be/dspace/bitstream/2013/292444/3/HRRPR-revised.pdf), including its small n cases without a census. The theorem is the paper's principal result; its preliminary general facts are retained as standard background only where explicitly stated. The newly constructed model need not be identified with the paper's particular rank-r presentation to establish its existence theorem.

### All rank-three hypertope orders and types: L034, Theorem 3.2

Let s,t>=2, l>=1 and `n>=s+t+l`. Put `δ=n-s-t-l`. Starting with B_δ, inflate the three pair orders 4,4,2 to `2^s,2^t,2^l`. Then

    |H|=2^(δ+5) 2^(s-2) 2^(t-2) 2^(l-1)=2^n.

Theorem A proves the full C-group and regular-hypertope conclusions, including flag transitivity. Thus it recovers the all-parameter existence content of Hou–Feng–Leemans, [*Existence of regular 3-hypertopes with 2^n chambers*, Theorem 3.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1901.09034). It also works in the small admissible range below the paper's stated n>=10 cutoff; novelty is not claimed for those small instances.

The next section independently identifies the actual presented group in this theorem, rather than stopping at an alternative existence construction. Its cyclic kernel and affine quotient are obtained explicitly. The separate impossibility Conjecture 3.3 for n<s+t+l is not proved by this construction.

### Rank-three polytopes: L035, Theorem 1.2

Set r=3 in the L032 derivation, or l=1 in the L034 derivation. This is exactly the all-parameter existence statement in Hou–Feng–Leemans, [*2-groups behaving as automorphism groups of regular 3-polytopes*, Theorem 1.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1802.08947v2). The paper's Theorem 1.3 classifies eight groups in three high-type families. Neither Theorem A nor the cube/grid construction proves that completeness assertion, so this paper has not been reduced in its entirety.

## 5. Presentation recognition without a second order proof

**Lemma C.** Suppose a marked presentation P has cyclic normal subgroups A_e of orders at most h_e, and its quotient by their product is a known finite group B. Suppose a constructed marked quotient H of P has order `|B| product_e h_e`. Then `P=H` as marked groups, every A_e has its full indicated order, and their product is direct whenever its image in H is direct.

**Proof.** A product of normal subgroups is a subgroup. Successive product cardinalities give `|P|<=|B| product_e h_e`, without assuming their intersections are trivial or their product is abelian. Surjectivity onto H gives the reverse bound and a trivial kernel. ∎

Apply this to the presentation in L034, Theorem 3.2. Write its involutions as a,b,c and set

    A_01=<(ab)^4>,    A_12=<(bc)^4>,    A_02=<(ac)^2>.

Each is cyclic normal: its two defining involutions invert its generator, and the third commutes with it by a displayed defining relation. Their orders are at most `2^(s-2),2^(t-2),2^(l-1)`. Modulo their product, the presentation is the affine Coxeter group W above with one additional relation. In its affine model,

    [(ab)^2,c]       = translation by (0,-2),
    [(ab)^2,(bc)^2]  = translation by (-2,-2),

using `[x,y]=x^(-1)y^(-1)xy`. For δ even, raising the first word to `2^(δ/2)` and taking its normal closure gives exactly Λ_δ. For δ odd, raising the second to `2^((δ-1)/2)` gives exactly the other Λ_δ. The quotient is therefore B_δ, with its already proved order.

Our constructed H satisfies every defining relation. The three edge-power commutators hold because their powers lie in their isolated coordinate kernels. Each of the two displayed commutator words has trivial projection to every two-generator dihedral coordinate; the required power is trivial in B_δ. Lemma C now proves that the paper's presented group is exactly H, and

    K=C_(2^(s-2)) x C_(2^(t-2)) x C_(2^(l-1)),
    H/K = (Z^2/Λ_δ) semidirect D_8.

This reconstructs the explicit presentation, order, kernel, quotient, IP and flag-transitivity conclusions from the compact core. The source also prints a dihedral-by-dihedral description of its quotient. We use the unambiguous affine description above, and do not silently treat that differently written display as an independently checked identity.

The same argument with A_02 trivial identifies the rank-three construction used in L035. This does not identify or classify every other rank-three group of the given order.

## 6. Tests, matrix meaning and coverage accounting

A second construction now removes the unexplained lattice seeds from L012. The [cubic affine reduction](phase2_cubic_lattices.md) uses the three transformations `(y,x,z)`, `(1-x,z,y)`, `(-y,-x,z)`. Their point group has order 48 and translation kernel is 2D3. Three elementary invariant sublattices give exactly the source's families of orders 192m³,384m³,768m³. A single index-48 Reidemeister–Schreier certificate identifies the source's universal presentation, and evaluation of its actual defining words identifies all three lattices. One four-entry parabolic-overlap calculation proves IP for every m. An independent two-line central cover of the tetrahedron supplies order 96, giving the source's entire all-n Corollary 5.2. Thus the three lattice assertions that were retained as seed hypotheses in the first audit have now been independently proved with short fixed data.

The [exact GAP verifier](phase2_edge_inflation_check.g) constructs the affine seeds on translation coordinates **and an eight-state orientation coordinate**, so it does not lose the signs when the grid has side length two. It checks all three pair retractions and full IP for seed orders 32 through 512. It then checks seven rank-three inflated families through order 1024, including all three pair orders, every subgroup intersection, and Tits' exact rank-three flag-transitivity condition. Four rank-four/rank-five families through order 2048 also pass all subgroup IP and string tests. The [saved log](phase2_edge_inflation_check.log) records every case and PASS. These tests support the universal proofs; they are not substitutes for them.

In the torsor-matrix object, each edge kernel is an independent coordinate. A maximal-parabolic partition forgets exactly the coordinates on edges avoiding its type. Its Boolean intersections and typed Helly property are therefore coordinate-consistency statements. The fiber product is useful because it **solves** those constraints, rather than merely re-expressing an unknown subgroup problem as a matrix equation. The distinguished basis remains necessary.

Thus phase two genuinely upgrades the proof compression: one principal-result paper is fully recovered on this construction track, a second paper's actual principal construction is recovered with an explicitly stated display caveat, a third paper's full existence half is recovered, and a fourth paper's three-family lattice theorem and all-exponent corollary are recovered with the actual presentations identified. Together with the independently completed L066 reduction, this is substantially more than generic transport tails. It is still not evidence that dozens of complete papers have been reduced. The compact framework now explains exactly why several construction proofs belong to one family, and identifies what a further reduction would have to solve: classification completeness, sharp bounds, or different seed geometry.
