## Exact theorem and source boundary

For a finite 2-group with a C-group marking whose noncommutation graph is a
forest T, all edge commutators are independent in G′/γ₃(G). If T has d
vertices and m edges, the canonical marked quotient G/γ₃(G) is the explicit
vertex/edge cocycle group of order 2^(d+m), and it has subgroup IP.

The string case is the path m=d−1. It gives the full marked group-existence
statement in Hou–Schulte, *String C-groups of 2-power order project onto a
common string C-group*, arXiv:2605.20691v1, Theorem 1.1 (printed p.2), with
canonical kernel γ₃(G) and no nilpotency-class restriction. The usual
string-C-group/regular-polytope correspondence gives the covering statement
of Theorem 1.2. This does not rehabilitate the source's specified
Frattini-intersection kernel or its false consequences: the earlier
counterexamples to that kernel remain unchanged.

The complete source had been read in this research chain; its definitions,
nondegeneracy footnote and exact Theorems 1.1–1.2 were rechecked in this phase.

## Adversarial checks

**The descending lifting lemma is valid without normality or permutability
of A and B.** All A_j=A∩γ_j(G) and B_j=B∩γ_j(G) are subgroups, with trivial
mutual intersection. Suppose γ_(j+1)=A_(j+1)B_(j+1). Equal quotient images
of a∈A_j and b∈B_j give a b⁻¹=n_A n_B, so n_A⁻¹a=n_B b∈A∩B=1. Their
images in γ_j/γ_(j+1) are therefore disjoint and, by hypothesis, generate
that abelian quotient. The displayed index product follows. Combining it
with the already established order of γ_(j+1) gives
|γ_j|=|A_j||B_j|. Crucially, the *actual binary product* A_jB_j has precisely
that cardinality because A_j∩B_j=1; it is already contained in γ_j. Equality
of sets is therefore justified. No equality of three subgroup orders is
being mistaken for a product factorization. The nilpotent terminal term
starts the induction, and finiteness is used explicitly in this last step.

**Ordinary lower-central Lie generation is the needed one.** The graded Lie
ring of the ordinary lower-central series is generated by the images of a
generating family in degree one. With involutory generators, degree one has
exponent two; bilinearity makes every homogeneous component an F₂-vector
space. This uses neither a dimension-subgroup series nor a restricted Lie
p-power operation. Every homogeneous bracket on X alone has a representative
in G_X∩γ_j(G), and similarly for Y; sums are represented by products in those
same actual subgroups.

**The support lemma closes under all brackets.** If each cross bracket
[x,y] belongs to L_Y, ad(x) preserves L_Y by the derivation identity. Brackets
and linear combinations of such derivations retain that property, yielding
[L_X,L_Y]⊆L_Y. Thus L_X+L_Y is a graded Lie subalgebra containing every
degree-one generator. It equals the whole graded Lie algebra. Alternation
and Jacobi are valid in characteristic two; no division by two occurs.

**The forest cut works for arbitrary finite forests.** In a chosen component
containing a selected edge, the edge with deepest selected child has no
other selected edge in its descendant component X. All other selected edges,
including those in different components, lie wholly in Y. There is exactly
one crossing edge. Solving its nonzero F₂ coefficient in the hypothesized
relation puts its degree-two bracket in the pure-Y subalgebra. All other
cross brackets vanish by the original marking's commutation relations.

**All uses of IP take place in G.** IP gives G_X∩G_Y=1. Irredundancy and
Burnside give G/G′ elementary abelian of rank d and G′∩<r_v>=1. Lie support
and the lifting lemma yield G′=(G′∩G_X)(G′∩G_Y). For the boundary commutator
c=u v, the first support intersection puts u in <r_x>∩G′, hence u=1; the
second puts c in <r_y>∩G′, hence c=1. This contradicts the edge being
noncommuting. At no stage is IP assumed for G/γ_j(G). The separate known
failure of IP in a last-term quotient therefore does not obstruct this
argument.

**The target model and canonical identification are complete.** The edge
bilinear cocycle gives an associative group with independent vertex and
edge coordinates. Every marked induced subgroup has exactly its vertex
coordinates and internal-edge coordinates, proving all subset intersections.
The class-two presentation collects into at most 2^(d+m) words; the model
attains that number. The quotient has that same order by independence of the
degree-two edge images and the rank-d abelianization. The natural marked
surjection is an isomorphism. Quotient IP is concluded only here, after
identification with the model.

## Trust boundary

The written proof retains the elementary lower-central Lie construction,
Burnside's basis theorem, finite nilpotency, commutator bilinearity/Jacobi,
and the original marked IP. It uses no classification of finite groups,
subgroup catalogue, finite census, numerical sampling or earlier class-four
result as a proof input. No new computation was necessary for this review.
The forest hypothesis is substantive; this audit does not extend the result
to arbitrary commutation graphs. Existing finite diagnostics and formal
components elsewhere in the project are not a full formalization of this
argument.
