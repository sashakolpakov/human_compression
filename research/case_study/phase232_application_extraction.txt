Even subsets of a polygon and an equal-label presentation
Abstract
The halving construction of Montero and Ivić Weiss, applied to a polygon, gives a self-dual regular map
of type {𝑛, 𝑛} with 𝑛2𝑛 flags. We identify its distinguished generators in the semidirect product of the
even subsets of an 𝑛-cycle by the dihedral group. This recovers the main construction of Li and Zhang
from the previously established polygon corollary. We then prove their exact universal presentation by
a normal subgroup generated by commuting involutions. The proof works for every 𝑛 ≥ 3 , without enu-
merating groups. The existence theorem and presentation are attributed to their sources; the contribution
here is their explicit identification within the common construction and a checked algebraic part of the
presentation argument.

There is a convention issue in the source’s displayed multiplication on physical page 7. It specifies a
homomorphism 𝜑 ∶ 𝐻 → Aut(𝐸𝑛) and column vectors, but writes (𝑣, 𝑔)(𝑤, ℎ) = (𝑣 + 𝜑(𝑔) −1𝑤, 𝑔ℎ).
With ordinary left composition, inversion is an antihomomorphism and that formula is not generally
associative. For example in the equivalent even-subset action with 𝑛 = 3 , put 𝑢 = (0, 𝑎) , 𝑣 = (0, 𝑏) ,
𝑤 = (𝑒 0 + 𝑒 1, 1). Under the inverse-action formula the translation coordinate of (𝑢𝑣)𝑤 is 𝑒0 + 𝑒 1,
whereas that of 𝑢(𝑣𝑤) is 𝑒1 + 𝑒 2. Equation (5) fixes the convention. One may instead use a right
action, provided the corresponding order of composition is changed consistently. Our recovery concerns
the main group and polytope statements and the exact abstract presentation (3); it does not endorse the
inconsistent displayed product as written.

The generalized-cube and halving construction, its polygon outputs and its duality belong to [MW20] and
the antecedents attributed in [C]. The main later existence statement and presentation belong to [LZ25].

correspondence is retained from [MS02]. The explicit common marking (6), the resulting source-to-
source identification, the convention check and the formal verification are the work of this application.
