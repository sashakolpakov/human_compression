## Mathematical checks

### Endpoint operation and actual graph realization

The endpoint theorem exhausts all possible new marked parabolics. Conjugation by
the distinguished involution fixes the higher generators; the fixed maximal
overlap therefore controls the mixed pairs. For the two parabolics containing
both endpoints, old IP confines their intersection between H_K and U_K, of
index at most two. The only extra possibility is exactly the common parity
support excluded by intersection closure of the upward family. This proves both
necessity and sufficiency, including the least-support description for finite
rank. The odd-endpoint recovery word and the proper-index-two specialization
are valid. The central-twist lemma proves generation and changed order only;
it does not silently supply remaining IP or Helly conditions.

Graph halving constructs actual components of a bipartite double cover. The
simple-graph and edge-shadow assumptions ensure that compatible lifted higher
flags extend to chambers. Its vertex residues are identified with old residues;
the remaining connectedness obstruction is precisely the stated parity
compatibility condition. Necessity uses a connected truncation within the
relevant residue, without confusing a truncation with that residue itself.
Panel correspondences prove thinness, and the old/new chamber bijection proves
transitivity in the bipartite and nonbipartite cases.

The U.Action attachment has a genuine onto forgetting map, an injective
equivariant encoding by old labels and vertex subsets, and faithful reconstruction
of omitted edges from simplicity. U.Geometry is used for full-group and coset
identification only after geometry, thinness, connectedness and transitivity
have been supplied. C.Gallery then identifies all marked stabilizers and proves
IP. For lattice polytopes, joins give uniqueness of the edge determined by two
vertices, and nested higher flags give parity compatibility. Thus the source
halving theorem follows with its actual lattice hypothesis.

### Parity groups, spherical residues and toroidal labels

substantial retained inputs. With those stated inputs, the even-weight subgroup,
generation by edge differences, exact order and source dual convention are

and right chamber changes give the stated word convention. Flag extension and
