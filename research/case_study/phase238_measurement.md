# Topic E: scope and measurement proposal

The intake is the principal higher-rank classifications from L083 and L079. The actual proof structure is

\[
 U_E \longrightarrow A_1\;[L083],\qquad U_E\longrightarrow A_2\;[L079].
\]

U_E includes its complete finite table and required exact program. A1 and A2 are complete separate application papers. A2 does not use A1. The historical combined manuscript is the provenance source for the work; it is not an extra mathematical premise charged on top of the three papers.

For this topic the requested acquisition cost is the full U_E paper plus the required finite program, followed by the full local cost of each application. Retained classical inputs need an explicit, symmetric accounting convention. A theorem reused in another topic is part of that other topic's full chosen umbrella and is charged there again. No overlap subtraction across topics is permitted.

The source numerator must refer to the old principal proofs actually replaced. The proposed extraction spans are bound in `phase238_source_boundaries.json`. They include the original preliminary definitions, printed subgroup inputs and principal proof arguments, including subfield lemmas our argument bypasses. Inclusion of an old lemma's proof as a cost of obtaining the old principal theorem does not claim that we recover that lemma as a corollary. The original subgroup multiplicity formulas and unused auxiliary assertions remain separately marked.

The excluded source material is not a free compression gain: L083's separate one-Petrie-relation and chiral discussion, and L079's post-classification almost-simple census and conjecture, are outside the two contracts. The latter conjecture has a later historical answer, addressed by a separate status audit; nothing here supplies new question credit.

Before publishing any ratio, independently fix a common representation for both sides, verify the source-span endpoints against the primary pages, and declare the retained-input convention. The raw extractions contain OCR and layout artifacts. Comparing their byte lengths or original PDF pages with clean Markdown would be a mismatched statistic. The unavailable original finite programs have unknown size; an artifact-inclusive calculation must not invent a zero cost for them. Our complete required program has a known cost and is charged.

`phase238_dependency.json` gives exact native file sizes only, with their roles. These values help audit which files must be included; they are not a normalized proof-complexity measure. No compression ratio, source-to-paper count ratio, or empirical sawtooth is claimed. A later sequence of complete applications can test whether substantial local costs fall after acquisition of the common theory. Two applications establish a reusable interface, not a decades-long trend.
