# Bounded priority audit: cyclic TPP in odd-order nilpotent groups

10 October 2026. Internal literature audit, not a publication-priority determination.

The result inspected is: if a finite nilpotent group $G$ has odd order and cyclic subgroups $A,B,C$ satisfy $abc=1\Rightarrow a=b=c=1$, then $|A||B||C|\leq |G|$. ## What the proposed proof adds beyond the located statements

The candidate-specific step is a minimal counterexample reduction: pairwise generation gives Frattini index $p^2$; shrinking each cyclic factor by its $p$-th powers forces the excess ratio to equal $p$; minimality then makes those powers generate the whole Frattini subgroup. Thus $\Phi(P)=P^p$. For odd $p$, this makes $P$ powerful, and the published ordered-factorization theorem finishes the contradiction. The proof adds this reduction to the retained factorization theorem; it does not re-prove powerful-group theory.

None of the located theorem statements makes that reduction redundant. This is a mathematical comparison of statements, not evidence that nobody has used the reduction before. Nor does the result establish $\rho_0(G)=1$ for odd nilpotent groups: $\rho_0$ maximizes over **all** subgroups, whereas the proposed result restricts all three factors to be cyclic. The 2-group case and the Hou–Feng–Leemans order conjecture remain outside this result.
