# Cyclic subgroup TPP in every finite nilpotent group of odd order

10 October 2026. Private proof for review. This argument uses elementary
p-group reductions and one published ordered-factorization theorem.
It does not use the phase11 action lemmas or powerful-embedding criterion.

**Theorem.** If G is a finite nilpotent group of odd order and cyclic
subgroups A,B,C have the triple product property, then

    |A||B||C|≤|G|.

## 1. Retained primary input

For odd p, a finite p-group P is powerful when P′≤P^p. Lubotzky–Mann,
*Powerful p-groups. I. Finite groups*, Journal of Algebra 105 (1987),
484–505, [Theorem 1.11 and its forward proof, printed pages 489–490](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/265533173_Powerful_p-groups_I_Finite_groups),
prove that **any** generating list g₁,…,g_s of a powerful group gives

    P=⟨g₁⟩⋯⟨g_s⟩

as an ordered set product. This is a retained published theorem. The
exact statement and proof were read in the author's uploaded primary
text. No local PDF hash or re-proof of the powerful-group theorem is
claimed. The theorem's arbitrary-generating-list formulation is essential.

## 2. The p-group proof

Fix an odd prime p. Suppose P is a counterexample of minimum order among
all finite p-groups. Each factor A,B,C is nontrivial: if one were trivial,
TPP would make the other two intersect trivially, and their set product
would already have the required order bound. Also P is nonabelian,
because in an abelian group the multiplication map for a TPP triple is
injective.

Every pair generates P. For example, if H=⟨A,B⟩ were proper, the
triple A,B,C∩H would retain TPP. Minimality and the injection of cosets
of C∩H in C into cosets of H in P give

    |A||B||C| ≤ |H| |C:C∩H| ≤ |P|,

contrary to assumption. The other pairs follow because subgroup TPP
is invariant under permutations, by cyclic rotation and inversion of
its defining relation.

Thus P can be generated by two elements and is not cyclic. Burnside's
basis theorem gives |P:Φ(P)|=p². Put K=Φ(P), and let

    r=|A||B||C|/|P|.

The cyclic groups A^p,B^p,C^p lie in K, retain TPP, and have order
product |A||B||C|/p³. Minimality applied to the proper subgroup K gives

    |A||B||C|/p³ ≤ |K|=|P|/p²,

so r≤p. Since r>1 is a power of p, it follows that r=p. Consequently

    |A^p||B^p||C^p|=|K|.                              (1)

These three p-th-power subgroups generate K. Otherwise their generated
subgroup H<K would be a smaller counterexample, since the same triple
has TPP and its order product in (1) is larger than |H|. Thus, for
cyclic generators a,b,c,

    K=⟨a^p,b^p,c^p⟩≤P^p≤Φ(P)=K.

Hence Φ(P)=P^p, so P′≤P^p and P itself is powerful. Since P=⟨a,b⟩,
the retained theorem gives P=AB as an ordered set product. Choose a
nonidentity c₀∈C. Then c₀⁻¹=ab for some a∈A,b∈B, and abc₀=1
contradicts TPP. This proves the inequality for every finite odd-p group.

Finally, a finite nilpotent group is the direct product of its Sylow
subgroups. Each cyclic factor likewise splits into its Sylow subgroups,
and TPP restricts to every Sylow component. Applying the p-group theorem
and multiplying the inequalities proves the statement. ∎

## 3. Exact scope

The only non-elementary retained result is the published powerful-group
factorization theorem. In particular the proof does not depend on the
binary square-action lemma, the odd-prime norm lemma, a classification,
a finite catalogue or a group-order cutoff. The equality in (1) is used
to prove generation of K by contradiction with minimality. It is not
used to infer injectivity of a three-factor multiplication map.

The 2-group case remains unresolved: equality Φ(P)=P² does not imply
powerfulness there, whose definition requires P′≤P⁴. The full
Hou–Feng–Leemans 2-group order conjecture is therefore not settled by
this odd-order theorem. Nilpotence is also a hypothesis of the stated
odd-order result; no claim is made for all groups of odd order.
There is an exact small boundary witness: SmallGroup(21,1), isomorphic
to C₇⋊C₃, has a cyclic TPP triple of orders 3,3,3. Its factor-order
product is 27>21. The standalone
[GAP witness](phase11_odd_nonnilpotent_witness.g) verifies TPP and
nonnilpotence, with its saved [output](phase11_odd_nonnilpotent_witness.log).
This is a written proof with internal review, not a complete Lean
formalization, external peer review or a publication-priority determination.
