## The actual common theorem

K(c) proves equality of action kernels from two checked interfaces: an injective equivariant encoding of constructed objects by elements/subsets of retained objects, and an equivariant forgetting map onto all retained objects. It identifies that kernel with the intersection of retained stabilizer cores. A thin residually connected chamber-transitive output has the full faithful automorphism image, and the index of the kernel in a panel preimage is two.

This has three explicit uses. N008's typed-coset action is its identity-encoding specialization. The endpoint construction encodes a lifted higher object by its old label and the two vertex subsets of its double-cover component; the forgetting map is onto, including for the selected component in the bipartite case. Simplicity recovers omitted edges and proves faithfulness. For Petrials the retained objects are vertices and edges, and each new face is its actual pair of subsets. Thus its exact action kernel is **core_G(⟨a⟩)**. Since a has order two, the kernel is ⟨a⟩ exactly when a is central, and is otherwise trivial.

The new O0 lemma proves chamber-gallery connectivity, free chamber action in the thin case and the exact generation of residue stabilizers. The earlier recognition proof and endpoint connectivity argument now use it. It also supplies the Petrial's original parabolics and the thin output's free action. Its proof uses finite rank but permits infinite groups, object sets and residues.

The Petrial-specific work has not disappeared. Section 23 gives the complete geometric connectivity argument, handles **both** cases bP=P and bP≠P, proves the obstruction by the thin residue four-cycle or the dihedral rotation/reflection intersection, and then proves regularity, faithfulness and injectivity of old face shadows. Only after these checks is the new marking (a,b,ca) identified. It is never assumed to be a homomorphic image of the original marking. These distinct proof bodies remain charged.
