## Mathematical checks

- **Minimal support and its cost.** Actual marked IP identifies the unique least support. In a normal-closure step, a fresh vertex v supplies an element supported on J∪{v} but outside G_J. Its intersection with the subgroup on J and any previously added vertices is G_J by actual IP. Each such vertex therefore forces a strict subgroup increase inside U⁺, costing a factor at least p. Changes inside G_J can consume order without increasing support. Iteration reaches the normal closure because the marking generates the finite ambient group. No quotient IP or multiplication injectivity is assumed.
- **Path intervals.** A nontrivial basic third commutator has connected support of size at most three. A disconnected support within such a triple is elementary abelian on independent abelianization directions and cannot contain a nontrivial derived element. New vertices must neighbor the current support. Thus the initial at-most-k connected seed supports cannot acquire additional connected components.
- **Restriction of the kernel.** The order of the image of G_A in the canonical forest quotient equals the order of G_A/γ₃(G_A). The natural map consequently has exactly the claimed kernel. This proves the equality for every subset A without asserting IP for a lower-central quotient. The component factors of the kernel have their full component supports by minimality of J, and the normality argument uses actual support intersections.
- **Exact reconstruction.** An edge outside the closed neighborhood has no exceptional support vertex; a triple not wholly inside that neighborhood has at most one. These observations justify every imposed attachment relation. The complete core presentation embeds into the reconstruction because its composite map to the original group is the original inclusion. Its third lower-central subgroup is represented by J-words and centralized by all outside-neighborhood markers. After dividing by it, local class-two relations make every edge commutator central. The two quotient maps to and from C_d give the finite order sandwich. This proves an exact presentation, not merely a common quotient.
- **Finite acceptance.** For an arbitrary candidate core retained by the stated finite tests, the image of its γ₃ is still normal and has order at most 2^k. Its canonical forest quotient embeds in C_d and satisfies every attachment relation, so W/L=C_d and W has the stated order bound even before acceptance. Enumeration of all finite tables up to that bound terminates and includes W itself. A maximum-order quotient is W. Full order then forces the core map to be injective, and the final actual-IP test is explicitly retained. The argument establishes effectiveness without an efficiency claim.
- **Counts and sharpness.** The run-placement formula has the correct binomial factors. The finite core library depends only on k. Primary Theorem 5.1 of Cunningham–Feng–Hou–Schulte was read in its complete statement and proof on source pages 8–10, together with the preceding toroidal notation. Its prescribed order-32/order-64 triple sections give the binomial lower bound and distinguish ordered markings. For rank 5k, the selected triples beginning at 2,7,…,5k−3 have full three-vertex kernel supports, and their closed neighborhoods partition the path. All four bounds are therefore attained, using the cited construction as an explicit retained input.
- **Local excess product.** The earlier product of local kernels is invariant under right multiplication by the shared edge kernel because its last factor contains that kernel. Actual IP identifies the common support intersection. Multiplication fibers have exactly the edge-kernel cardinality, whether or not the product set is a subgroup. This proves the local budget without replacing normal closure by ordinary generation.
