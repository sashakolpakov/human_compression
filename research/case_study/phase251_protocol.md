# Topic E: measurement protocol

10 October 2026. This protocol is fixed before computing the new comparison.
The analysis is retrospective: both historical proofs and the replacement
already exist. It is not a prospective test of discovery or a measurement
of minimum possible mathematical complexity.

## Contract and ordering

Compare the exact principal contracts independently accepted in phases234
and243: L083's PSL2 classification and stated geometric properties first,
then L079's PGL2 classification. Exclude the separate chiral, one-Petrie and
semilinear census/conjecture branches. Inspect every source boundary against
the primary pages. The phase238 proposed boundaries are the starting point,
not permission to count unused auxiliary conclusions as recovered results.

Our acquisition is the complete frozen U_E manuscript and its complete
required finite program, charged once. The local costs are the two complete
frozen application papers, including their actual hypotheses, quotient and
geometric arguments. No text is removed from our manuscripts to improve a
ratio. Other topics still pay their full own umbrellas without overlap
subtraction. Optional Lean checks, independent audit/replay machinery and
rendering scripts are inventoried separately from mathematical proof costs.

## Fixed measurement

Use the existing project's comparable-PDF extraction convention: pypdf
6.13.0 default text extraction from every mathematical document, NFC and
NFKC normalization, with five recorded length proxies: whitespace tokens,
alphanumeric runs, characters without whitespace, characters with collapsed
whitespace, and UTF-8 bytes with collapsed whitespace. The designated
primary coordinate is NFC characters without whitespace. All five metrics
and both normalizations must be reported, regardless of outcome. These are
representation-dependent length proxies, not Kolmogorov complexity.

Keep the source mathematical text, including formulas and tables, unchanged.
Remove only explicitly bound running titles, author headers, printed page
numbers and extraction markers, plus excluded source branches outside the
contract. The replacement mathematical manuscripts are counted in full
apart from the same page furniture. In particular all local statements,
proofs, attribution and reference paragraphs remain in their cost.

Render the exact required GAP program as a code appendix with the project's
existing mathematical typography. Check that all non-whitespace program
characters survive PDF extraction in order and unchanged. Count that PDF
text with the same rules as prose. Report original page counts as an
inventory only; they are not a common-format page ratio. Record extraction
anomalies and check boundaries and mathematical displays visually. Do not
repair OCR or change source formulas silently.

## Earlier inputs and unknown costs

The written comparison is conditional on a named common classical library:
Dickson's subgroup types and standard embeddings, elementary finite and
projective group facts, the string C-group/polytope and covering
correspondences, and the named classical local maps. Record the source's
own invocations and reuse, including any cross-paper proof dependencies.
The comparison also conditions on source-specific cited results outside the
intake, including the earlier PSL2 rank bound. Display these separately in
the source DAG. This is a common available library, not an assertion that
every item is invoked by both sides. These are retained external inputs,
not proved for free by this project.
A claim of unconditional acquisition cost would require their proof costs.

Report (1) the written-manuscript ratio and conditional application factors,
with full U_E visible, and (2) the conservative artifact-inclusive lower
bound obtained by adding our complete required program. Historical finite
programs were used, but their actual source files and sizes are unavailable.
Their cost is unknown, not zero. Consequently the second calculation is a
lower bound on a full artifact-inclusive factor under the stated common
library convention, not its exact value. The first is a text-length ratio,
not the complete cost of the computer-assisted proofs.

For the source sequence, show historical per-paper and cumulative costs,
one full umbrella acquisition bar and the two local costs. Show the code
charge separately and in the full acquisition bar. No sawtooth beyond this
one acquisition and two applications, no decades-long trend, no scientific
worth judgment, and no new principal-source or conjecture credit follow.
