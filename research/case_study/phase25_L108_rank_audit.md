## Principal proof

The new proof uses the source's actual geometric face: a pair of vertex and edge sets with induced incidence. Equal sets are identified. It does not substitute labelled chamber-walk orbits or assume a faithful induced action.

The old regular action gives the three distinguished panel involutions and dihedral rank-two stabilizers. The identities `wv=cv` and `w^-1 e=be` put both required vertices and both required edges into a Petrie face. These identities also justify the residue-connectivity replacement paths. Every rank-two flag of the new incidence system extends to a chamber; its face residues are connected images of the defining walks. The vertex and edge residues, and then the whole geometry, are connected by the explicit path replacements. These arguments work without a finite-group assumption.

Equivariance gives a homomorphism to the Petrial's automorphism group. Its kernel is contained in the original vertex-edge stabilizer `<a>`. The proof correctly retains this kernel instead of treating the homomorphism as an embedding.

I specifically challenged the assertion that `b` fixes the Petrie face: it is not generally true. The revised principal argument covers both possibilities. If `bP` is a different face, the two faces and two edges form a four-cycle in the thin connected vertex residue. This cycle exhausts that residue, and the unchanged vertex-edge incidence forces `|ab|=2`. If `bP=P`, then `ca` fixes the new edge-face panel. Its automorphism image has square one by thinness and the trivial chamber stabilizer, so `(ca)^2` lies in `<a>`. The rotation/reflection intersection in the original dihedral subgroup is trivial, giving `|ac|=2`. Thus there is a commuting pair, the precise linear-diagram conclusion. Regularity of the Petrial is not needed as an additional assumption.

## Conditional conclusions and literal boundaries

After interchanging vertices and edges if necessary, the proof has `[a,c]=1`. Then `b` reverses the Petrie walk and the subgroup generated by `b` and `ca` is transitive on its face-residue flags. Together with the original chamber-transitive action this proves that the induced action is chamber-transitive on the Petrial. Since its full type-preserving automorphism group acts freely on chambers, the two groups coincide. Lifting the element that exchanges the two faces of the new `(v,e)` panel forces `a` to act nontrivially there, so the action kernel is trivial.

The new panel marking is exactly `(a,b,ca)`. Its second Petrie step is `cb`, whose vertex-edge orbit is the shadow of the old face. The extra injectivity argument for old face shadows is necessary and valid: equal shadows force another four-cycle in the old vertex residue, hence `[a,b]=1`; together with `[a,c]=1` this makes `a` central and invisible on retained objects, contradicting the already established faithfulness. Therefore the second Petrial recovers the old geometry canonically. This proves the conditional statements of Lemma 3.2 without using its false unconditional inclusion.

The source's unconditional Lemma 3.2(ii) fails literally for `K_(2,2,2)`: its type-preserving automorphism group has order eight, but deduplicating Petrie faces gives `K_(2,2,1)` with automorphism group of order four. The natural action loses the face-coordinate involution. The `C2 × S3` control also separates the six-step chamber-walk order from the three vertices and three edges of its geometric Petrie face. These are precisely scoped nonthin boundaries; neither undermines the principal theorem or the corrected thin-case conclusions. No assertion is made about previous recognition or correction of these source issues.

## Independent finite replay and coverage

I independently ran the unchanged checker into [phase25_L108_rank_audit.run.json](phase25_L108_rank_audit.run.json). Its output is byte-identical to the saved author report. The checker constructs the groups from explicit permutations, computes all typed cosets and incidences, applies the literal three chamber flips, and deduplicates faces as pairs of sets. The original regularity and the new action properties are checked rather than inserted as assumptions.

All seven examples pass, with 188 original chambers. Four have thin Petrials and exact bidual face shadows; two nonthin examples have an action kernel of order two. The tetrahedron and its vertex-edge swap test the two different face-stabilizer branches of the principal proof. The nonlinear star-transposition example is also rejected by actual thinness. These are focused controls, not an exhaustive census or a substitute for the uniform proof.

I recommend principal-result coverage for Theorem 1, with the statement-level record kept explicit: Lemma 3.1 is recovered as a walk-definition adapter; Lemma 3.2(i), (iii) and (iv) are rederived; Lemma 3.2(ii) is corrected, not falsely counted as proved. No cited atlas classification is imported into the proof, and no claim is made that every literal source assertion is true. The retained foundations are elementary group actions, dihedral normal forms and incidence/chamber connectivity. There is no Lean formalization or group-catalogue dependency in this package.
