# Exact small-index kernels for the third excess

10 October 2026. Research draft. The statements below prove an
exhaustive invariant-kernel method and the exact recognition criterion
used by the accompanying finite censuses. They do not give an all-rank
third-excess formula. Frozen phases twenty and earlier are unchanged.

Let the ordered marking consist of d involutions, with path
commutations, actual IP and adjacent products of order at least four.
At excess three, the group order is 2^(2d+2). The forest canonical
quotient gives |gamma3|=8, and strict lower-central descent gives
class at most five. Therefore [gamma3,gamma3]<=gamma6=1: the possible
kernel groups are C8, C4 times C2, and C2^3. Neither D8 nor Q8 can
occur as gamma3 in this class.

## 1. The universal group and its marking

Let F_d be the group presented by r_i^2=1 and [r_i,r_j]=1 whenever
|i-j|>1. Set U=F_d/gamma6(F_d), and retain the ordered images of the
original r_i. This finite nilpotent group is a two-group: its
lower-central factors are generated by multilinear commutators in
the degree-one involutions and hence are elementary abelian.
Write K=gamma3(U). It is abelian since U has class at most five.

Every map that kills an arbitrary subset of the original generators
and fixes the others respects the defining relations and the
nilpotency law. These deletion maps therefore exist on U itself.
They commute and their products are the corresponding intersection
deletions. Consequently the universal marking has actual IP: if
x belongs to U_S and U_T, both deletions fix x and so does their
product, whose image is U_(S intersect T). This argument applies
only to the universal group, not to its arbitrary quotients.

Every desired excess-three marked group is U/N for a unique normal
subgroup N of U contained in K with [K:N]=8. Indeed the universal
marked map exists by the class bound. On the class-two quotients it
induces the marked isomorphism of the two canonical path groups,
so its kernel lies in K; the order gives index eight. Conversely
each such N gives a group of the right order and marking, and
the remaining recognition test is actual IP. Adjacent noncommutation
survives in the canonical path quotient, so nondegeneracy is retained.

Distinct kernels give distinct ordered marked isomorphism classes.
The reversal r_i -> r_(d+1-i) is an automorphism of U and acts on
these kernels. Orbits of this involution are exactly the classes
up to path reversal. No quotient-group automorphism factor is
to be divided out a second time.

The universal NQ measurements, with the original-generator
epimorphism preserved, are:

| Rank d | Order of U | Order of K | Abelian type of K |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 3 | 2^18 | 2^13 | C2^2 times C4 times C8^3 |
| 4 | 2^34 | 2^27 | C2^6 times C4^3 times C8^5 |
| 5 | 2^51 | 2^42 | C2^11 times C4^5 times C8^7 |

These are computed data, not a proposed all-rank invariant-factor
formula. A pc representation keeps them compact; enumerating all
elements or all subgroups would be inappropriate.

## 2. A complete invariant index-two ladder

The following lemma applies to any finite two-group U and any normal
subgroup M, whether or not M is abelian. Let

    C_M=Phi(M)[M,U],   V_M=M/C_M.

Then V_M is an elementary abelian group with trivial U-action. The
normal subgroups L of U with L<=M and [M:L]=2 are precisely the
preimages of all hyperplanes of V_M.

For necessity, an index-two subgroup contains Phi(M). The U-action
on M/L is trivial because Aut(C2)=1, so L contains [M,U]. For
sufficiency, every preimage contains [M,U], hence is invariant under
conjugation by U, and it has index two by construction.

Moreover every U-normal N<=K of index 2^k appears in a chain of k
such steps. To see this, suppose M/N is nontrivial. The two-group
U/N acts on the nonzero F2-vector space

    Hom((M/N)/Phi(M/N), C2).

Its nonzero vectors number 2^a-1, an odd number. Orbits have
power-of-two sizes, so there is a nonzero fixed character. Its
kernel lifts to a U-normal subgroup between N and M of index two
in M. Repeat. This proves completeness of the breadth-first ladder;
no chosen presentation pattern or expected census count is a filter.

At each depth, duplicate subgroups are removed using their canonical
induced pc generating sequence relative to one fixed pc sequence
of U. The actual subgroup is retained, not just its order, type or
number of generators. The guarded implementation explicitly asserts
that every relative pc order is two before encoding an exponent
vector as the binary integer sum of its bits times 2^(i-1). This
makes the element encoding injective; the canonical induced sequence
then identifies the subgroup. Normality and the required index are
checked for every newly retained kernel. The separate coordinate
diagnostic checks the same input condition in all three actual
universal groups.

## 3. Kernel-profile recognition of actual IP

There is a useful independent optimization. Define

    K_S=K intersect U_S=gamma3(U_S).

The equality on the right follows directly from the universal
deletion map e_S: if x belongs to K intersect U_S, then e_S fixes
x and sends gamma3(U) into gamma3(U_S). The reverse containment
is immediate. This functorial argument is specific to a group
with these deletion maps. For N<=K, let q:U -> G=U/N and T=K/N. Then

    T intersect G_S = q(K_S) = (N K_S)/N.                 (1)

The first equality holds because q^(-1)(q(K))=K. The image of the
marked G_S in G/T is the corresponding standard parabolic of the
canonical path quotient, which has actual IP. It follows that G
has actual IP if and only if the finite family in (1) has exact
intersection meets:

    q(K_S) intersect q(K_T) = q(K_(S intersect T)).        (2)

Necessity follows by intersecting actual IP with T. For sufficiency,
take x in G_S intersect G_T. Its image in G/T is represented by
y in G_(S intersect T). Then xy^(-1) belongs to both kernel
intersections in (1), and (2) puts it in G_(S intersect T).
Thus x lies there as well. This is the ordinary image-plus-kernel
intersection argument; it assumes neither quotient IP in general
nor an unproved restriction formula for higher gamma_j.

For the third excess, T has only eight elements. Precompute the
K_S once in U, test (2) in T for every candidate kernel, and build
the full marked quotient only for passing candidates. Direct
full-group intersection checks on all accepted quotients can then
serve as a separate diagnostic of this optimized recognition step.

from the present rank-four agreement. No priority claim or matched
proof-length ratio is assigned here.

## 6. Reproduction and retained inputs

Use GAP 4.15.1 with NQ 2.5.11, or record and validate any replacement
versions. Work in a disposable output directory; the script writes the
specified checkpoint paths. Run the unchanged source separately for
ranks 3, 4 and 5. For example, from a directory containing the source:

```sh
gap -q -T -c 'rank21:=4;; checkpoint21:="rank4_run";; Read("phase21_kernel_census.g");' > rank4_run.log
python3 phase21_build_census_report.py --rank 4 --log rank4_run.log --checkpoint-prefix rank4_run --executed-sha256 385e863b18e9a6a876290fc05564b1529304340cfbe719690ca74c55f743d4fc --output-dir rebuilt
```

The report builder requires a terminal PASS and validates the executed
source hash, checkpoint uniqueness and sizes, accepted membership,
metadata counts and reversal parity before writing reports. Checkpoints
are deterministic gzip files; the uncompressed content hashes are also
recorded. Logs retain their measured runtimes, so a fresh successful
log need not have the same hash. Only an initial carriage return or
newline is stripped when packaging the author's logs.

The software input is the exact universal nilpotent quotient, together
with GAP's pc subgroup, homomorphism and intersection algorithms.
The official [NQ manual, section 3.1-3](https://gap-packages.github.io/nq/doc/chap3.html)
states that `NqEpimorphismNilpotentQuotient` with class bound c returns
the map to the quotient by gamma_(c+1). The implementation carries the
original free-marker images through this epimorphism and its pc
isomorphism; it never substitutes the resulting pc generators for the
marking. Canonical induced pcgs and the explicit relative-order assertion
are the retained basis for subgroup-key equality.

The mathematical retained input is the reviewed all-class forest
canonical-quotient theorem in
[phase15_graded_support.md](phase15_graded_support.md). The index-two
ladder and kernel-profile recognition are proved in this note. Future
use of the resulting rank-five alphabet in the
[finite-state theorem](phase20_finite_state.md) requires an actual
transition computation; the alphabet alone is not an all-rank formula.
No SmallGroups catalogue, source census list, empirical fitting or
proof-assistant formalization of this census is used. Internal written
reviews and reproducible exact computations are distinguished from
external peer review.
