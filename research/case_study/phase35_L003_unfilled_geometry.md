# The unfilled universal group is a regular hypertope group

This is a separate consequence of the compact affine certificate and the free-voltage certificate. It concerns the source presentation with its parameter relator omitted, not any previously unsettled odd parameter.

## 1. Presentation and result

Let \(W\) be the Coxeter group on involutions \(r_0,r_1,r_2,r_3\), with pair orders
\[
m_{01}=6,\quad m_{02}=3,\quad m_{03}=2,\quad
m_{12}=2,\quad m_{13}=3,\quad m_{23}=6.
\]
Set \(x=r_0r_1,\ y=r_0r_2,\ z=r_0r_3\). Define \(U_\infty\) to be the quotient of \(W\) by
\[
(y^{-1}x^{-2})^3=1,\qquad (zx^3)^2=1,\qquad
x^{-1}z(y^{-1}z)^2\,zx(zy)^2=1.
\tag{1}
\]
There is no imposed relation on \(T=y(zy)^2\).

**Theorem.** With its displayed marking, \(U_\infty\) is the full type-preserving automorphism group of an actual regular rank-four hypertope. Its maximal parabolics omitting \(0,2,3\) are the finite toroidal groups with parameters \((1,1),(2,0),(3,0)\) and orders \(36,48,108\). The maximal parabolic omitting 1 is the full Euclidean triangle reflection group \([6,3]\), with translation lattice \(\mathbb Z^2\).

The rotational subgroup is exactly the nine-relator group \(P_{\rm omit}\) of the free-voltage certificate, and
\[
U_\infty=P_{\rm omit}\rtimes C_2.
\]
Consequently \(U_\infty\) has a subgroup of index 1296 mapping onto \(F_2\). In particular it is large, contains a nonabelian free subgroup, and is not virtually solvable.

The compact six-dimensional affine group is a proper marked quotient of \(U_\infty\). Its virtually abelian structure must not be substituted for the universal group.

## 2. Three finite parabolics suffice for the geometry

The four compact affine involutions satisfy all defining relators of \(U_\infty\), so there are marked epimorphisms
\[
U_\infty\longrightarrow\Gamma\longrightarrow H_1.
\]
The source's three fixed local toroidal presentations give upper bounds \(36,48,108\) on the universal parabolics omitting \(0,2,3\). The corresponding parabolics in \(H_1\) have these exact orders by the standalone compact certificate. Therefore all three universal parabolics inject into \(H_1\).

The remaining maximal parabolic is deliberately unprotected. The base \(H_1\) has full marked subgroup IP and typed coset Helly, checked respectively on all 256 subgroup meets and all 5287 pairwise incident coset flags. The one-exceptional-parabolic quotient theorem applies directly to \(U_\infty\to H_1\). Hence \(U_\infty\) has both properties, without any assumption that a quotient preserves IP or that an infinite maximal parabolic injects.

Typed Helly gives an actual flag-transitive coset geometry. Marked IP gives trivial chamber stabilizer, thin panels and the standard parabolic descriptions of residues. Their incidence graphs are connected because each such parabolic is generated by its distinguished involutions. Thus the geometry is a regular hypertope. Thinness and residual connectivity also show that a type-preserving automorphism fixing one chamber fixes every chamber; the chamber-regular \(U_\infty\)-action is therefore the full type-preserving automorphism action.

## 3. The remaining parabolic is exactly Euclidean

Write \(E=[6,3]\) for the abstract group on the omitted-1 triangle. With
\[
u=r_2r_3=y^{-1}z,\qquad v=r_2r_0=y^{-1},
\]
its orientation-preserving subgroup has presentation
\[
\langle u,v\mid u^6=v^3=(u^{-1}v)^2=1\rangle.
\]
The elementary Euclidean triangle model gives \(E=\Lambda\rtimes D_{12}\), where \(\Lambda\cong\mathbb Z^2\), and the two translations
\[
T=v^{-1}u^{-2},\qquad T'=u^{-1}Tu
\tag{2}
\]
are a lattice basis. In particular the finite linear action on \(\Lambda\) is faithful, and \(C_E(\Lambda)=\Lambda\).

For clarity, one explicit integral version of this standard model uses
\[
R=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad S=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
\]
with \(u=(R,0),\ v=(R^{-2},(0,1))\), and common reflection \(r_2=(S,0)\). Then \(r_3=r_2u\), \(r_0=r_2v\), \(T\) translates by \((1,0)\), and \(T'\) by \((1,-1)\). The invariant positive definite form has Gram matrix \(\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}\); the three mirrors bound a \(30^\circ,60^\circ,90^\circ\) fundamental triangle. This is the same full triangular translation lattice underlying the source's toroidal order formula.

There is a natural epimorphism \(E\to (U_\infty)_1\), and then to the compact omitted-1 subgroup. In that compact group, (2) has squares equal to the independent translations \(-2e_1,-2e_2\). Thus the composite is injective on \(\Lambda\): a killed \(T^a(T')^b\) would have killed square with translation \(-2ae_1-2be_2\), forcing \(a=b=0\).

Let \(N\) be the kernel of the composite \(E\to\Gamma_1\). Since \(N\) is normal and \(N\cap\Lambda=1\), every commutator of \(N\) with \(\Lambda\) lies in this trivial intersection. Hence \(N\leq C_E(\Lambda)=\Lambda\), forcing \(N=1\). Both intermediate maps are therefore injective too. This proves the exact infinite local group rather than merely an infinite image or an order lower bound.

## 4. The literal rotational presentation

The sign homomorphism of \(W\), sending all four involutions to the nonidentity element of \(C_2\), splits via \(r_0\). Its kernel has the standard presentation on \(x,y,z\) with the first six source relators. Conjugation by \(r_0\) sends each of \(x,y,z\) to its inverse.

As in the separately reviewed universal-even proof, the normal closure of the three additional words (1) is invariant under this involution. The reason is local: each word imposes a full toroidal lattice, of type \((a,0)\) or \((a,a)\), on one Euclidean triangle. Such lattices are reflection-invariant. If that triangle omits 0, conjugation by \(r_0\) is a local reflection followed and preceded by an even word, which does not change its normal closure. Omitting the variable torus relator removes one invariant lattice condition and leaves this argument unchanged.

Thus taking the semidirect product by \(r_0\) adds no hidden rotational relators: the rotational subgroup is precisely \(P_{\rm omit}\), not just a quotient of it.

The frozen free-voltage certificate provides an index-648 subgroup of \(P_{\rm omit}\) that surjects onto the free group \(F_2\), by checking all nine relators in an explicit nonabelian-voltage action. Its index in \(U_\infty\) is 1296. The free-subgroup and failure-of-virtual-solvability consequences follow. Since \(\Gamma\) has translation subgroup of index 1296, it is virtually abelian; the epimorphism \(U_\infty\to\Gamma\) cannot be injective.

## 5. Dependencies and limitations

This corollary retains:

- The frozen compact construction and finite base certificate, including exact three-parabolic orders and the two integral local translations.
- The abstract one-exceptional-parabolic IP/typed-Helly theorem.
- The elementary Euclidean triangle presentation/lattice model and the source's three fixed toroidal upper bounds.
- The source-presentation/reflection-invariance adapter from the universal-even note.
- The frozen nonabelian-voltage free-quotient certificate.

No new census, rewrite calculation or unverified classification is used. The new logical combination is that three fixed local injections already prove the unfilled universal geometry; injectivity of the fourth local group is then established separately.

This does not construct a quotient with arbitrary prescribed odd \(T\)-order. Neither largeness nor existence of a regular unfilled geometry automatically preserves the necessary local groups or geometric conditions under further quotients. It is therefore not an all-parameter solution of L003 Problem 9.2. Bibliographic priority for this combination has not been established.
