# Finite marker intervals: an adapter to overlap cancellation

This additive module transports the complete frozen phase149 theorem from finite subsets of the natural numbers to finite subsets of `Fin n`. It does not change `IntervalOverlapCancellation.lean`, its hypotheses or its proof.

The exact new consumer theorem is `MathCompression.IntervalOverlapCancellation.fin_weights_eq_zero`. Given two finite families of order-convex subsets of `Fin n`, disjoint within each family, and weights in an additive commutative monoid which vanish on disjoint pairs, zero row and column sums imply that every weight is zero. Both empty intervals and `n=0` are permitted. There are no group hypotheses or additional forest assumptions.

The proof has two transport steps. First, `fin_val_ordConnected` proves that taking the image under `Fin.val` preserves order-convexity. If a natural number `z` lies between values of two elements `a,b` of the finite set, then `z ≤ b.val < n`; hence it defines an element of `Fin n` lying between `a` and `b`, and convexity places it in the original set. Second, `fin_val_disjoint_iff` proves that the images are disjoint exactly when the original sets are disjoint, using injectivity of `Fin.val`. Applying the frozen `weights_eq_zero` to these image families, with the original weights and sums, proves the adapter. This is the complete written proof of the three declarations in `formal/FinIntervalOverlapCancellation.lean`.
