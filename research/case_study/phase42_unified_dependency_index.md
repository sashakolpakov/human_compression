# Qualified dependency index for the unified manuscript

This additive index resolves the naming issue identified by the independent
integration review. The frozen intake remains unchanged. Its arrays called
`imported_background_transitive_closure` are **selected inputs**, not complete
transitive closures.

The [machine-readable index](phase42_unified_dependency_index.json) gives all
34 adapters the explicit field `selected_imported_inputs`, normalizes every
input to an identifier in the same retained-background inventory, and preserves
the original labels and their finite/infinite qualifications. It also records
the inherited inputs identified by the reviewer for D6, D7 and D8, including
the Burnside input reached through primitive saturation and the square seed.
Those additions are examples of inherited dependencies, not a claim to have
finished every closure.

For mathematical use, the full internal proofs and the global retained inventory
remain authoritative. The [independent review](phase40_unified_recent_audit.md)
explains why the metadata omission did not conceal a mathematical hypothesis.
The index does not add a theorem, change a source contract, alter a proof,
remove a retained assumption, admit another paper, or change any length factor.
A mechanically complete dependency graph remains future work.
