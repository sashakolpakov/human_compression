# Schulte–Weiss 2006: Problem 30, source date, and present scope

Source intake and bounded status check, 10 October 2026. Proposed registry ID: **N010**. This is a problem-source record, not credit for rederiving the paper.

Egon Schulte and Asia Ivić Weiss, *Problems on polytopes, their groups, and realizations*, **Periodica Mathematica Hungarica 53 (2006), 231–255**, appeared in September 2006. [Publisher record](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10998-006-0035-y).

**Problem 30**, printed p.19 under “Specific groups,” asks which groups of orders $2^k$ or $2^k p$ ($k>0$, $p$ odd prime) occur as automorphism groups of regular or chiral polytopes. No rank, nilpotency-class or excess bound is imposed. Reading covers this passage, front matter and corresponding TeX; not the complete paper. [Primary v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0608397v1).

The [arXiv history](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0608397) lists only v1, dated 15 August 2006. Its [TeX source](https://arxiv.org/src/math/0608397v1) identifies that version, uses `\maketitle`, and supplies no `\date`. The current PDF title and creation/modification metadata instead show 26 November 2024. The default LaTeX date explains this as a compilation artifact, **not evidence of a 2024 mathematical revision**. Bibliographic year remains 2006.

## What the present work covers

The [fixed-excess support theorem](phase16_fixed_excess.md) and [canonical attachment theorem](phase16_central_join.md) concern ordered nondegenerate regular markings of finite 2-groups, at every fixed excess $k$ in the order formula $2^{2d-1+k}$. They reduce the classification to finitely many bounded cores and give exact pattern generating functions. A bounded-core reduction is not an explicit listing of every core for all excesses.

The [class-three theorem](phase18_class_three.md) gives a complete classification and counting polynomial for these markings when the group has nilpotency class at most three, across all excesses. The [leading-coefficient theorem](phase18_leading_coefficient.md) compares their number with the unrestricted fixed-excess marked count. These are meaningful regular 2-group components of Problem 30. They do **not** characterize all groups in the original question: higher-class cores at arbitrary excess, chiral markings, and the $2^k p$ part remain outside these conclusions. Ordered marked classes also differ from unmarked abstract group isomorphism classes; forgetting a marking can identify many entries.

## Bounded status check

The later primary literature found in this check describes partial progress. Hou–Feng–Leemans [2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.08947) classify specified rank-three 2-group types. Conder–Feng–Hou [2019 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.03398) construct chiral families and explicitly describe a partial answer. Hou–Feng–Leemans [2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.02945) treat regular rank-three groups of order $2^n p$ with specified type/existence conclusions. These abstracts were read; this intake does not claim new complete readings of those papers.

Queries covered the exact title, the exact problem phrase, and Schulte/Weiss/Problem 30 with 2024, 2025 and 2026. A recent primary [2025 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.20654) still cites Problem 30 as context while treating a different restricted family, order $2p^m$. No complete solution of the literal Problem 30 was located in this bounded search. This is **not** proof of current openness or of priority for the workspace results. No citation census or author contact was undertaken.
