# Independent audit: the metacyclic adapter

10 October 2026. **PASS: no mathematical gap found** in
[phase11_metacyclic_adapter.md](phase11_metacyclic_adapter.md).
This is a private internal written-proof audit, not external review or
formal verification.

For H=⟨u,v⟩ with ⟨u⟩ cyclic normal, conjugation has odd exponent r.
The subgroup L=⟨u²,v²⟩ is normal: conjugation of v² by u differs from
v² by a power u^(1−r²), which lies in ⟨u²⟩. The quotient H/L has
commuting involutory generators because [u,v]=u^(r−1) is an even power.
Hence Φ(H)≤L, while the reverse inclusion follows from the two squares.

The cyclic subgroup generated by [u²,v²]=u^(2(r²−1)) is normal in L,
and the quotient makes both generators commute. It is therefore exactly
L′. Since r²−1 is divisible by eight, this subgroup lies in ⟨u⁸⟩,
which is contained in L⁴. Thus L=Φ(H) is powerful with the correct
fourth-power condition for 2-groups. Cyclic and small-order cases introduce
no exception; powers may simply become trivial.

Every subgroup of a metacyclic group remains metacyclic by intersecting
the normal cyclic subgroup and injecting the quotient into the original
cyclic quotient. Therefore this calculation verifies the precise hereditary
hypothesis of the reviewed powerful-Frattini theorem. Its cyclic-TPP
consequence applies to all finite metacyclic 2-groups. Together with the
odd-prime theorem it covers finite metacyclic p-groups for every prime;
it is not a claim for arbitrary finite metacyclic groups, which need not
be nilpotent. No metacyclic classification, new computation or new Lean
execution is used by this adapter.
