# N011: published rank-three generation for projective linear groups

Status checked 2026-10-10. **`source_theorem_retained`: no rederivation or compression credit.**

I. A. Markovskaya and Ya. N. Nuzhin, *On generation of the groups GL_n(q) and PGL_n(q) by three involutions, two of which commute*, Trudy Instituta Matematiki i Mekhaniki UrO RAN **31**(4) (2025), 247–259. [Publisher](https://journal.imm.uran.ru/2025-v.31-4-fon-03), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.21538/0134-4889-2025-31-4-fon-03), [primary PDF](https://journal.imm.uran.ru/sites/default/files/content/31_4/TrIMMUrORAN_2025_4_p247_L.pdf). The publisher records online publication on 12 May 2025.

**Theorem 2, printed page 248**, classifies the stated involution generation of $\operatorname{PGL}_n(q)$. Its three alternatives are: $n=2$; $n\ge4$ with $\gcd(n,q-1)=2$; or $n\ge5$ with $\gcd(n,q-1)=1$. The same page explains the rank-three polytope consequence using the absence of cyclic normal subgroups and the Conder–Oliveros intersection result.

For $n=4$ and odd $q$, the positive condition is precisely $q\equiv3\pmod4$. Thus rank-three existence for this full $\operatorname{PGL}_4(q)$ is **published input**, not a remaining gap or a new result here. The theorem does not classify the proper diagonal index-two subgroup when $q\equiv1\pmod4$; that case remains unresolved in this project's investigation.
