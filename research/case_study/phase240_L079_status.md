# Historical status of L079, Conjecture 11

10 October 2026. This conjecture belongs among questions answered in
later literature, not among targets still requiring an original solution.

Leemans and Schulte's *Polytopes with groups of type PGL₂(q)*,
arXiv:0909.1991v1, Conjecture 11, asks two questions for odd square prime
powers: nonexistence for a specified twisted extension of PSL₂(q), and
existence of rank-four regular polytopes for the Baer extension.

The author's later [survey, §5.3](https://arxiv.org/html/1910.08843v2#S5.SS3),
immediately before Theorem 5.6, explicitly identifies the classification
by Connor, De Saedeleer and Leemans as answering that earlier conjecture.
The displayed classification includes the required Baer extension in
rank four and excludes the twisted extension. The original answer is
attributed to their [2015 article, Theorem 1.1](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2014.10.020).

This audit reads the original question and its surrounding definitions,
the survey's full classification statement and references, and the
publisher's bibliographic record. It does not newly obtain or review the
complete 2015 proof. The retained full texts are identified by hashes in
`phase240_L079_status.json` and are not distributed here.

The common projective-line proof in phase230 recovers L079's principal
classification but does not derive this additional semilinear result.
That is a limit of our replacement, not evidence that Conjecture 11
remains open. Reworking the 2015 classification would count as recovery
of known mathematics. It would earn no new-conjecture credit.

The adjacent survey Problems 3 and 4 concern enumeration in the
semilinear families. Their current status requires a separate audit;
the survey's age alone does not establish that they are still open.
No intake, principal-source or solved-question count changes here.
