I independently read the entire frozen addendum. Its Gray map is a bijection between the two bits attached to an antipodal vertex pair and the four vertices of a square cycle. This proves the face-poset identification of the actual generalized cube with the cubical quotient by `4Z^r`; it is not inferred from an equal order. The explicit complementary-coordinate duality reverses faces, descends through the same translation lattice, and deals with N013's dual construction. Type reversal carries its final-end operation to the first-end operation being proved; no compatibility with an unspecified self-duality is assumed.

The affine generators agree exactly with the Gray map: flipping the first bit acts by `x₁ ↦ 1−x₁`, swapping pairs permutes coordinates, and interchanging the two last bits changes the final coordinate's sign. The action kernel on the quotient is exactly `4Z^r`: after testing the origin, testing each unit vector forces the signed linear part to be the identity modulo four, hence the identity. The changed reflection is `(x₁,x₂) ↦ (1−x₂,1−x₁)`. Together with the signed permutations it generates all even-sum translations, including `2e_r`; conversely every generator preserves that parity. Thus the halved group is exactly `E⋊B_r`, and the original lattice lies inside it.
