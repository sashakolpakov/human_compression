# Bounded source and status check for the odd-prime family

10 October 2026. This is a selected primary-source and current-record check,
not a proof of priority or comprehensive current openness. The phase49 theorem
was an unfrozen author draft when this check began; this note does not admit
its mathematical conclusions.

The exact retained family is the first row of Table 2, group Γ1, in
Cunningham–Pellicer, [Tight chiral polytopes](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2009.04566).
The primary text's Theorem 4.7 identifies the equal-parameter case k1=k2
with the atomic chiral family of type {2p,p^α,2p}; its table has p odd prime,
α≥2 and nonzero k modulo p. The root inspected selected extracted primary
text around the table and theorem, not the full 43-page proof or all page
images in this check. The phase49 draft openly retains this source construction,
its exact order/tightness and intersection property. It aims to construct
additional regular covers, not reprove the classification.

The [smallest-cover author manuscript](https://gabrielcunningham.com/WhichChiralPolytopes.pdf)
identifies five tight families with nonpolytopal smallest regular covers in
Example 3.5. Section 4 gives a finite example with two minimal regular
polytopal covers and discusses extending multiplicity. The root reopened its
primary text and the selected finite-construction passage. The
[publisher page](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcta.2023.105839) confirms the 2024
journal publication; full publisher-version text was not acquired in this
check. The older author-version contract remains in force.

The current [arXiv record for Cunningham–Hubard](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.19334)
lists v1 submitted 24 June 2025 and no later submission in its displayed
history. The [HTML text](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.19334v1) describes criteria
for polytopality of a specified mix and identifies its two-orbit/smallest-cover
specializations. Its introduction explicitly presents a unification of earlier
criteria. A whole-text search for “tight” had no match; a “minimal” search did
not expose an infinite tight-family multiplicity theorem. This is search-level
negative evidence, not a full new theorem-by-theorem review. The HTML displayed
an August 2026 document date while its version header and submission record
say v1 June 2025; that display is not evidence of a new submitted version.

The author [publication list](https://gabrielcunningham.com/math) and six web
queries were also checked. The queries combined chiral polytopes, minimal
regular covers, tight families, mix polytopality, and years 2025/2026. They
returned the primary papers above and related cover literature; no matching
resolution was located. Irrelevant and secondary search results were not used
as mathematical authority. Search-index dates were not treated as publication
dates.

The warranted conclusion is limited: the proposed family target is tied to an
explicit earlier discussion and a precisely identified construction. This
check found no competing all-parameter solution. It does not establish that
none exists, nor that the general multiplicity question is currently open.
Independent mathematical review of phase49 and a broader priority comparison
remain necessary before any novel-open-problem claim. No infinite value is
assigned to a manuscript-length ratio.
