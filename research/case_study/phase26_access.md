## L111: the 2000 construction paper

C. Lefèvre-Percsy, N. Percsy and D. Leemans, *New geometries for finite groups and polytopes*, Bulletin of the Belgian Mathematical Society–Simon Stevin 7(4) (2000), 583–610; [DOI](https://doi.org/10.36045/bbms/1103055618).

**The original full body was not recovered.** The author-uploaded [ResearchGate record](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/29655004_New_geometries_for_finite_groups_and_polytopes) supplies substantial indexed excerpts, but the rendered page and direct request fail; the saved response is temporary-unavailable HTML. A legacy official Euclid download returns challenge HTML. The EMIS journal archive lists the exact paper but provides no accessible link for that entry. The old file `catalogue_L111.pdf` is HTML and is not upgraded to PDF status.

The read indexed passages concern simple graph truncations, edge incidence determined by endpoint shadows, neighbourhood geometries and flag geometries. They identify Theorem 4.1 and Theorem 6.1 as construction results, but do not supply a complete reliable statement/proof ledger. The primary later paper discussed below explicitly restates Constructions 5.2 and 6.2 and attributes a bipartite theorem to Theorem 6.2. Those are evidence about the later statements, not a substitute for reading all 28 original pages. No principal-result coverage is proposed for L111.

## L081: the 2008 Petrie-like operation

M. I. Hartley and D. Leemans, *A new Petrie-like construction for abstract polytopes*, Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series A 115(6) (2008), 997–1007; [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcta.2007.11.008).

**This remains primary-author-abstract-only.** The [author abstract](https://www.math.auckland.ac.nz/~dleemans/abstracts/newpetrie.html) describes higher-rank Petrie constructions and a hypercube enumeration. The publisher request fails. The previously inspected ULB Holdings page points to a restricted `Elsevier_23599.pdf`, not an openly obtained manuscript. The ResearchGate entry offers a full-text request rather than an authored body. The author's former personal site is unavailable; the current polytope site's publications page supplies general references, not this manuscript. No request to an author was sent.

A separate, already cached primary follow-up, *On the ranks of string C-group representations for symplectic and orthogonal groups*, Theorem 4.2/Remark 4.3, attributes the replacement $(r_0,r_1,r_2,r_3,\ldots)\mapsto(r_1,r_0r_2,r_3,\ldots)$ to the Petrie-like line of work, with a later odd-order rank-reduction criterion. This operation removes a generator and differs from endpoint halving $a\mapsto aba$. It does not establish L081's exact original theorems or its hypercube count. No principal-result or full-reading upgrade is justified.

## Exact newer bridge

The final [2023 paper by Piedade](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/v30i2p20/pdf/) is already cached as `sources/hypertope_infinite2023.pdf`. This intake reads §§2.2–2.4 and the orthoplex application §3.2. Section 2.3 retains the nondegenerate, meaning face-poset lattice, condition for halving; §2.4 uses matching-diagram semidirect products. The orthoplex family is one explicit application. The remaining family tables and presentation reductions have not been rederived here.

The underlying Montero–Weiss [arXiv 2001.11082v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.11082v1), 29 January 2020, was downloaded as an 11-page primary PDF. Selected reading covers the relevant background in §2 and all of §3, including Theorem 3.1, Proposition 3.2 and Corollary 3.3. This is the preprint version; the eventual article is Art Discrete Appl. Math. 4(3) (2021), Paper 3.07. In this preprint the corollary is 3.3, whereas the 2023 paper cites a Corollary 3.2. No silent numbering transfer is made.

Most directly, the cached Piedade–Tranchida manuscript has title-page version **arXiv 2405.19050v1, 29 May 2024**, despite its historical basename `halving_hypertopes2025.pdf`. Its §§3–4 relate endpoint halving to the 2000 constructions and state the exact overlap criterion in Lemma 4.5. The [separate adapter](phase26_halving_source_adapter.md) identifies what the existing abstract subgroup theory supplies and what remains a geometric hypothesis. Only the indicated sections and proofs have been read; no complete-paper claim is made. No prior paper ID for this title was found in the current work registry; it had been stored as an unassigned source asset.
