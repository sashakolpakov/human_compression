The theorem concerns ordered marked finite two-groups with involutory
path generators, actual IP, no adjacent product of order two, and a
fixed nilpotency-class bound c. It counts marked isomorphism classes,
not just abstract groups or polytopes up to reversal.

## Checked implications

The normal closure of the last marker is generated by its iterated
marked commutators through weight c. Each nonzero commutator has connected
support and extends that support by at most one place per weight. Thus
the normal closure lies in the last c-window and is centralized by all
earlier markers. This proves the product/order formula and reconstruction
uniqueness; neither step assumes a deletion retraction.

The retained regular-polytope correspondence proves the needed coset
Helly statement for arbitrary standard parabolics of a string C-group.
Cosets prescribe partial flags; pairwise compatible prescribed faces
form a chain, and flag transitivity extends it to a common element.
Root separately checked the specified primary Schulte passages. This
argument is not asserted for general C-groups without the string axiom.

For a prefix P, the stored boundary kernel R records the actual quotient
after killing all earlier markers. The two proposed tests provide an
action P on the new marker's normal closure N and make J=N intersect B
a normal, equivariantly identified subgroup of P. In N semidirect P,
the diagonal {(j^-1,j)} is a subgroup, is normalized by P, is centralized
by N, and meets either factor trivially. Its quotient therefore embeds
P and the candidate window H with their exact shared boundary B.

Mixed marked commutators of weight c+1 vanish explicitly: an old marker
before the first new marker gives trivial action on N, while one after
it centralizes the prefix commutator already in N. The remaining words
vanish inside P or H. The constructed group is a finite two-group, so
equality of two successive lower-central terms implies they are trivial.

Every marked parabolic factors as P_S H_S. The exact factor intersection
and Helly in P then give a common representative in P_S intersect P_T
of the relevant boundary coset. Correcting that representative gives
an element of H_S intersect H_T, proving actual IP in the construction.
There is no unjustified quotient-IP implication.

The universal presentation argument for the deletion update has a
surjection from the semidirect product quotient, giving the requisite
upper bound without first assuming its factors embed. Killing the past
then leaves exactly H with R killed; killing the first boundary marker
gives the stated new boundary kernel. Hence future transitions depend
only on the finite state, with no forgotten presentation relations.

The finite alphabet bound follows from the elementary abelian graded
quotients generated by weight-j marked commutators. Windows and their
normal subgroups can consequently be enumerated by a finite algorithm.
Accepted walks reconstruct all and only the marked groups; distinct
walks have different marked windows. The order increments telescope,
and the finite weighted transition matrix proves rationality in rank
and excess. It does not prove that a practical or minimal graph has
already been constructed for every c.

## Fixed excess and limits

If the excess is k>=1, the canonical quotient gives |gamma3|=2^k.
Strict lower-central descent gives class at most k+2. Its parabolic
restriction bounds the excess of every window by k. Therefore the
required rank-(k+2) windows have order at most 2^(3k+3), and truncating
weights modulo x^(k+1) preserves every desired count. This is a bound
on local groups, not a smaller bound on the whole canonical support core.
