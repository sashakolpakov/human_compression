# Coordinate-kernel typed Helly: admitted formal scope

[SupportedKernelHelly.lean](SupportedKernelHelly.lean) has a complete
[independent statement and proof review](../phase142_supported_kernel_helly_review.md).

The marked typed-coset Helly theorem derives the common-fiber lifting
condition from base IP. Its supported-kernel specialization also derives
the coordinate profiles and multiplicative kernel equivalence. No IP
upstairs is assumed. Nonabelian factors are allowed. Full geometry
recognition, involutivity of local markers and source-specific
presentations are outside this module.

The unchanged [author scope](../phase137_supported_kernel_helly_scope.md)
lists every hypothesis, theorem and retained input. Its pending-review label
records an earlier checkpoint; the root review supplies the subsequent
admission. The [public compilation](SupportedKernelHelly.check.json) records a fresh
pinned compiler run. The compiler and cached dependencies are trusted;
two missing Mathlib modules were rebuilt in a private overlay.

Only standard axioms are reported. This component does not constitute a
complete formalization of U, Module A or an open conjecture, and adds no
known-source compression credit.
