# Formal scope of the projective matrix calculations

Six declarations prove the entry restrictions, semisimple and unipotent
centralizers, trace-zero identity and determinant assertion for actual
two-by-two matrices over fields. See the
[complete scope](../phase237_scope.md) and
[independent review](../phase241_review.md).

The canonical source is byte-identical to frozen phase237. Fresh public
execution compiles all six declarations with propext, Classical.choice
and Quot.sound only. All nine pinned dependency revisions and clean
source trees are checked; cached compiled dependencies remain trusted.

The two deliberately changed hypotheses are tests of excluded cases,
not literal logical weakenings. Independent finite-field counterexamples
show that the changed conclusions are false. Those failing test files
are not admitted theorems.

Conjugacy to these normal forms, basis changes, the PGL quotient
interface and general field descent remain written. This file does not
formalize the complete classification of regular polytopes.
