# Label-four relations: admitted formal scope

[LabelFourRelations.lean](LabelFourRelations.lean) proves a bidirectional
local relation equivalence in an arbitrary group with three involutory
markers and commuting endpoints. Adjacent fourth-power relations and
the three source power relations are equivalent to the adjacent nested
commutators and cross commutator. Identity markers are allowed; exact
orders are not asserted.

The [independent proof review](../phase151_source_relators_root_review.md)
compares the complete source with the written universal-label-four adapter.
The [frozen author scope](../phase140_source_relators_scope.md) gives the
exact declarations and hypotheses. The eight audits in the
[fresh public build](LabelFourRelations.check.json) use only `propext`.
The installed compiler and pinned compiled package objects remain trusted.

Presented-group normal closures, a universal-group isomorphism, the
retained Biss–Dasgupta presentation theorem, cardinality, and the
intersection property are outside this certificate. No full conjecture
or historical-priority claim is certified here.
