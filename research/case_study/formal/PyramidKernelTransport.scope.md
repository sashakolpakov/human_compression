# Formal scope of the pyramid kernel argument

Four declarations prove disjoint support triviality, vanishing of a 0-or-1
coordinate modulo an integer at least two, injectivity on a subgroup from
its trivial restricted kernel, and transport of a kernel intersection test.
The [complete manuscript](../phase239_odd_toroidal_pyramids.md) and
[independent review](../phase242_review.md) explain their use.

Fresh public execution compiles these four declarations and nine retained
declarations about coprime pullbacks and cover kernels. All use standard
Lean axioms only. The three deliberately weakened versions fail; their
false conclusions have the elementary counterexamples described in the
review. They are not members of the canonical verified library.

The all-parameter affine action, finite group computations, surface
homology, retained toroidal classification and complete minimal-cover
theorem remain outside this Lean file. Pinned source revisions are checked;
cached compiled dependency objects remain trusted.
