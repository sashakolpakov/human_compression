# Endpoint-doubling intersection bridge

This companion formalizes the hard mixed-subgroup case of the endpoint-change criterion in `phase24_L088_theory.md`. It is also applicable to the proposed parity-free extension. The hypotheses are purely algebraic: an automorphism φ of an arbitrary group, subgroups A,B≤P, an equality P∩φ(P)=R, and pointwise fixing of R by φ.

`mixed_intersection_of_fixed_overlap` proves

    A ∩ φ(B) = (A ∩ B) ∩ R.

`mixed_intersection_identified` further uses A∩R=A₀, B∩R=B₀ and A₀∩B₀=C to conclude A∩φ(B)=C. In the endpoint application φ is conjugation by the distinguished involution a, and R is generated by the markers commuting with a. The application-specific conjugation and generator identifications remain written mathematics.

The Lean kernel checked both proofs. Their printed axioms are only `propext` and `Quot.sound`. There is no `sorry`, `native_decide`, finite lookup or assumed computational identity. The fresh compile report records the exact source hash and pinned Lean version.

This is a partial algebraic certificate. It does not formalize generation after mutation, the other all-subset cases, the complete C-group criterion, typed coset Helly, any J1 construction, or the presentation order bound. The completed GAP checks and the written proofs retain their separate trust boundaries.
