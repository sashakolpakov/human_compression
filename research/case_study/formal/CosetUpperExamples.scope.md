# Scope of CosetUpperExamples.lean

The literal presentation has two involutions and the relation (ab)^11=1. Kernel reduction accepts an eleven-name certificate for the index of the subgroup generated by a, and a twenty-two-name certificate for the trivial subgroup. The universal index theorem proves the coset space finite; the trivial-subgroup specialization bounds the actual group order by 22.

Four kernel-checked rejection statements treat a one-name root loop, omission of relators, an empty trace, and a false tree definition. There are nine selected axiom reports, including the order-bound declaration. Ordinary Lean reduction is used; no native-evaluation axiom is introduced.

The [fresh report](../phase225_public_replay/report.json), [compiler record](CosetUpperExamples.check.json), and [independent review](../phase226_coset_review.md) bind the exact scope. These examples do not prove the higher-rank bounds or the full F014 counterexample. The concrete homomorphism from this dihedral presentation to the rank-three facet has not been formalized in this package.
