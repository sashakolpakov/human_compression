# Hermitian transport: formal scope

Thirteen declarations over an arbitrary field with an involutive field
automorphism prove the equivalence of explicit entry conditions with the
four entries of the Gram equation and determinant one, norm invariance,
unique transport between two vectors of the same nonzero norm, and the
unitarity and nonzero isotropic differences of upper and lower root moves.
The structure `Mat` has four field entries and the literal matrix action;
its predicates are explicit equations, not unspecified unitary axioms.

The transport requires a nonzero norm. The specialization to a quadratic
finite field, elementary root generation, projective residue identification
and the full PGU chamber argument remain written. The existing generic
connectivity theorem is recompiled separately. All thirteen declarations
use only standard Lean axioms. Cached dependencies and the compiler remain
trusted. This is not a full Lean proof of the geometric conjecture.
