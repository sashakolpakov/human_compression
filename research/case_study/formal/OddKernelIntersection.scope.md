# Formal scope of odd-kernel intersections

Five declarations prove the prime-index subgroup intersection criterion,
its generation corollary, two reflection-product identities, and the
square of the defective source marking. The [complete manuscript](../phase236_odd_kernel_manuscript.md)
and [independent review](../phase248_review.md) explain their roles.

Fresh public execution uses standard Lean axioms only and checks all
pinned package revisions and clean source trees. Cached compiled
Mathlib objects remain trusted. An independently checked composite-index
example shows why primality cannot be dropped.

Sylow normality, the character decomposition, cyclotomic lattice and
presentation bridge, and the retained tight-polyhedron classification
remain written proofs or cited inputs. This file does not formalize
any of the three complete principal theorems.
