# Scope of the actual supported-kernel formalization

Author certificate candidate, 10 October 2026. Independent review is pending.

The new Lean module SupportedKernelGeneration.lean proves the support induction, generation of the full simultaneous fiber product, the complete kernel profile on every generated parabolic, and the full intersection property. The key profile is a conclusion. Neither an isolated normal-generator word nor an abelian-kernel hypothesis is supplied as a premise.

The target written proof is U.SK in phase123_umbrella_manuscript_v2.md, SHA-256 d6c5d4b087c4bb4cab29f4729314dfc1dbaa640410d1d0ca9a73f33651058e2c. This formal module does not yet include U.SK's finite order formula or its four separately numbered corollaries. It therefore certifies the stated structural part, not every assertion in that written theorem package or its applications.

## Exact formal data

The formal theorem uses a finite support index type J and a finite marker type I, arbitrary groups B and E(j), a tuple marking

    r : I → B × ∏ j, E(j),

and an injective support function scope:J→Finset(I). The maps are q(j):E(j)→B and p(j):B→B. The hypotheses say exactly:

1. Outside scope(j), the j-coordinate of r(i) is one.
2. On every literal tuple generator, q(j)(r(i).second(j))=p(j)(r(i).first).
3. For i in scope(j), p(j) fixes r(i).first.
4. The j-coordinate generators indexed by scope(j) generate E(j).
5. For every proper T contained in scope(j), an element of the subgroup generated by its T-coordinate markers with q(j)-image one is itself one.
6. The first-coordinate markers generate B and satisfy the full subset intersection property.

The base and local groups may be infinite and the local kernels nonabelian. Intermediate profile and IP theorems only need finite individual supports; the final global generation theorem uses finite I to express the complete marker family as Finset.univ.

These are literal hypotheses on the constructed markers and homomorphisms. They do not assume generation of the global fiber product, its kernel profiles or any desired intersection identity upstairs. Hypothesis 5 is the kernel-trivial form of proper-parabolic injectivity; the original U.SK injectivity hypothesis implies it by comparison with the identity element of that parabolic.

For the written U.SK data, compose each map E_S→B_S with inclusion B_S→B, and similarly regard the deletion B→B_S as a map B→B. Let r(i) be the exact tuple prescribed in (2.3). Then hypotheses 1–3 are the marker/deletion equations and hypothesis 4 is local marked generation. Inclusion does not change the kernel, so hypothesis 5 is exactly supplied by U.SK's proper-parabolic assumption. Hypothesis 6 is its base marking. No extra assumption is introduced in this translation. Source-specific applications still have to prove these actual equations and local injectivity facts.

## Proved declarations and proof dependency

All names below are in MathCompression.SupportedKernelGeneration.

| Declaration | Complete conclusion |
|---|---|
| triangular_isolation | In a finite product of possibly nonabelian groups, triangular witnesses can be corrected to pure coordinate elements by rank induction. The earlier pure coordinates belong to the required larger parabolic by the supplied support order. |
| coordinate_mem_marked_inter | The local coordinate of an actual word on L lies in the subgroup generated on L intersect scope(j). This is derived from the literal markers. |
| marked_compatibility | Equality of the local quotient and deleted base on generators holds on their actual subgroup closure. |
| marked_base_fixed | A deletion fixing the retained base generators fixes the base of every actual word on that support. |
| local_kernel_witness | Local generation and proper-parabolic injectivity give the triangular witness. Its base is proved to be one, every local value is proved to lie in its kernel, and unsupported coordinates are proved trivial. |
| pure_kernel_mem | Inclusion/cardinality induction isolates the entire pure local kernel inside its actual support-generated subgroup. Distinctness of supports supplies strict descent. |
| kernel_profile | For every L and arbitrary coordinate tuple y, (1,y) belongs to the actual L-parabolic iff all its coordinates lie in the local kernels and y(j)=1 for each support not contained in L. Both directions are proved. |
| generated_eq_fiber | The full generated subgroup is exactly the simultaneous fiber product defined by all q(j)(x_j)=p(j)(b). |
| marked_intersection_property | Every pair of actual generated parabolics meets in the parabolic indexed by the label intersection. |
| supported_kernel_construction | Bundles the exact global generation, all kernel membership profiles and full IP conclusions. |

The induction uses a finite direct-product assembly lemma from Mathlib. To isolate coordinate i, it chooses the local witness y, forms z by replacing only its i-coordinate by one, proves every pure coordinate of z belongs to the relevant subgroup by induction, and assembles z there. The product y z⁻¹ is then exactly the prescribed pure coordinate. This avoids an illicit use of a commutative product: distinct coordinates can be assembled in an arbitrary group product, and no commutativity is required inside a coordinate.

The two old local modules are reused as proved library components:

* CoordinateKernels.lean supplies LeemansCoordinates.coordinate_kernel_meet, applied only after kernel_profile has been established from the actual construction.
* LiftCertificate.lean supplies LeemansLift.intersection_of_image_and_kernel, with its marked image equality proved by the map-of-closure theorem and base IP.

The old intersection_transport_with_coordinates declaration remains in the imported module, but its assumed hcoords field is not used as a substitute for the new construction proof. The new final theorem supplies the profile itself.

The pinned runner phase129_supported_kernel_check.py compiles LiftCertificate, CoordinateKernels and SupportedKernelGeneration afresh, in that order, into a new directory. It checks the exact source hashes and dependency-manifest hash, the pinned compiler version, all nine package revisions and tracked-file cleanliness, every expected axiom report, and the absence of warnings, errors and placeholders. It rejects occupied output paths, including symlinks. It checks the local source hashes again after execution.

The existing compiled Mathlib/package dependency objects and the installed Lean compiler remain trusted. The three local modules are rebuilt; the entire package dependency tree is not rebuilt. ## Remaining formal obligations

The finite order formula still needs the actual projection/kernel cardinality bridge. Antichain local isomorphisms, the derived Boolean deletions, lower-central equality and separator factorization are separate remaining declarations. Specific edge, path and fork models must still bind the exact formal hypotheses. No proof of a polytope correspondence, a finite classification table, an entire source manuscript, or all of U or Application A is inferred from this module.

This work formalizes the support-induction simplification developed and challenged in phases117/122 and printed in U. It asserts no historical priority for the underlying group-theoretic ideas, no open-conjecture solution, and no compression ratio. The next formal acquisition should reuse these exact generation/profile/IP declarations rather than repeat their proofs.
