I read every proof in the assembled manuscript, rather than accepting earlier registry coverage or checking only its corollary list.

* **F and P:** Unique tree paths identify the common refinement of forest partitions, including the empty forest. Contracting the distinguished edge gives exactly the weighted-cut model needed later.
* **O, I and D:** A cross-block conjugate of a transposition proves the asserted two-block overgroup classification, including singleton blocks and the two-point exception. Positive weights permit at most one balanced cut. All connected residues of an unweighted tree have the required minimal symmetric group; disconnected residues are joins with the stated kernels. The nonstar three-edge path supplies the residual primitivity obstruction. The maximal-clique reconstruction really is a tree, and the retained B97 uniqueness identifies the entire N001 family with these constructed geometries.
* **R and M:** The quotient-minimal subfamily substitution proves both relative-generation bounds; abelianity is used exactly to replace all conjugators by the chosen lift. The residual maximality induction recovers every parabolic. In the two-point-block obstruction, an independent module basis would force all its basis lines fixed; after equality forces the full symmetric block action, every nonconstant vector generates a module containing the even-weight space. This proves the bound of two without assuming semisimplicity.
* **E:** The omission-subgroup input is W2, not a consequence silently inferred from W1. Orbit-crossing edges are distinct and acyclic and hence form a tree; all omission orbits and generator supports follow from its cuts. The only possible alternating omission yields exactly the sign-kernel marking. For a relative independent family, a pendant cut gives a fixed point, which rules out the half-swapping coset at the possible balanced cut; full intersection then forces the relative subgroup to be trivial.
* **G:** Parity correction preserves all cut cosets. The residual primitivity argument treats both a separate nontrivial component and the case where only the distinguished component remains; its small alternating action of degree three is included. For arbitrary maximum-rank geometries, the proof does not assume RWPRI: it blocks the sole possible balanced-parabolic enlargement in every panel, then uses residual generation to recover the original parabolics. This step is necessary and is present. Cyclic panels give precisely the inversion ambiguity; the action/isomorphism convention and the optional retained inner-automorphism fact are explicit.
* **Counting adapter B3:** Multiplying the actual twisted generators by their unique odd member recovers a unique distinguished-edge tree. Inversion switches only the attachment to the distinguished pair, so the contraction argument gives the stated inverse-identified count. There is no overcount from treating the independent sign choices as additional distinguished trees.

## Dependency and compression boundary

The retained B97 classification and uniqueness are substantial. W1 and the distinct W2 omission consequence are also substantial, including the source's classification-of-finite-simple-groups dependency. Cayley's formula, the source coset criterion/residue correspondence, and the explicitly mentioned symmetric-group automorphism fact remain unexpanded background. No targeted N001/N003 conclusion is imported as a premise.

Under this boundary, the manuscript is a logically complete replacement for its declared twelve-result contract. There is actual reuse: one forest intersection calculation and one laminar flag argument serve both marking families and the N001 geometry profile; the same overgroup lemma serves primitivity, the relative-subgroup result and arbitrary-geometry recognition. However, the module obstruction, extremal marking classification and arbitrary-parabolic argument remain substantial proof text. Merely naming their conclusions as common-core theorems does not eliminate that work. The general relative-generation bounds are also proved directly inside the body.

I therefore approve mathematical/source completeness of this intake, not a claimed factor of compression or the phrase “all contents of both papers.” A ratio still needs an independently agreed, symmetric body-selection and normalization protocol, including all necessary proof appendices and the damaged N001 rendered layout. Earlier matched-proof ratios are a different measurement.
