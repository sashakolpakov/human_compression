The map is **three named manuscripts → one reviewed manuscript**, and
**four other named manuscripts → a second reviewed manuscript**. This is
the completed assembly level. The wider [paper registry](PAPER_MAP.md)

is a count of assembled or compressed whole manuscripts.

For each bundle, the measurement is the sum of its complete
source main-body lengths divided by the complete replacement plus any
required external adapters/dependency exposition. Shared material is
charged once. Background and certificate costs remain explicit. A proved
main-result replacement can still yield modest or no length savings.
