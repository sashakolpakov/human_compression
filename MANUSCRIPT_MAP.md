# Named manuscripts to reviewed replacements

The current result is two reviewed replacements covering the declared
main-result contracts of seven distinct manuscripts. Each replacement
contains its common theory, proofs and explicit source corollaries.
These are conditional internal reviews with the source qualifications and
retained background visible. No corpus-wide or seven-paper length factor
has been measured, and the two replacements have not been proved to follow
from one shorter common manuscript.

The map is **three named manuscripts → one reviewed manuscript**, and
**four other named manuscripts → a second reviewed manuscript**. This is
the completed assembly level. The wider [paper registry](PAPER_MAP.md)
contains 160 indexed works and 16 principal-result candidates; neither count
is a count of assembled or compressed whole manuscripts.

For each bundle, the requested measurement is the sum of its complete
source main-body lengths divided by the complete replacement plus any
required external adapters/dependency exposition. Shared material is
charged once. Background and certificate costs remain explicit. A proved
main-result replacement can still yield modest or no length savings.
See [MEASUREMENT.md](MEASUREMENT.md) for the accounting rules.
