# Proposed Module D: Marked actions, field orbits and geometry enumeration

Historical planning note. The [reviewed Module D](MODULE_D.md) now has
three admitted principal contracts and a qualified fourth component.
The original planning text below records the earlier stage.

This manuscript is in preparation. Its initial intake is three sources
with existing principal-result derivations; their assembly into a complete
module has not yet passed independent review.

| Source | Intended complete application |
|---|---|
| Kiefer–Leemans, commuting involutions (2013), L066 | All-degree orbit formulas and the exceptional degree-six regular-polyhedron classification |
| Kiefer–Leemans, Suzuki polyhedra enumeration (2010 journal; 2011 author edition), L074 | Exact field-orbit counts, generation, intersection property and duality |
| Leemans, J2 incidence geometries (2002), L104 | The complete geometric census, full automorphism fusion and exclusion of higher rank |

The shared object is a finite group action on admissible marked
configurations. Forgetting part of a marking leaves an extension fiber
acted on by its stabilizer. U's orbit and extension theorems organize
the enumeration. Each application must still prove its generation,
intersection and geometric recognition conditions. Field-of-definition
counting, arbitrary-Borel coset recognition and exhaustive augmentation
are substantial local arguments being developed in the manuscript.

The first expansion target is Leemans–Rowley's rank-three M22 classification
(2010), L073. Its 431-class conclusion, rank-two boundary and tabulated
geometric identifications all require a complete derivation. Reproducing
the numerical total alone will not earn source credit.

This descriptive title is provisional. The module has no admitted page
count, compression ratio or full Lean certificate. Its intake adds no
new count merely by reusing previously derived source results.
