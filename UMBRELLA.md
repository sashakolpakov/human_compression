# Reusable theory: marked groups, kernels and intersections

The reviewed U-v3 manuscript is a source of mathematical components for
the new topic umbrellas. It is not a declaration that every topic shares
one completed global umbrella or pays for it only once across topics.

Read the [manuscript PDF](research/case_study/phase138_render_U/application.pdf),
[complete Markdown proof](research/case_study/phase138_umbrella_v3.md),
[exact theorem statements](research/case_study/phase138_umbrella_v3_contracts.json)
and [attribution and proof limits](research/case_study/phase138_scope.md).
The [publication review](provenance/phase99_review.md) and
[Lean statements and limitations](research/case_study/formal/README.md) record
what was checked and what remains outside Lean.

Its proved material includes marked intersections and kernel profiles,
supported extensions, deletion maps, coset intersection transfer,
finite actions and relative generation. A topic's U_A must state the
complete collection it uses, including any additional necessary theory.
The full U_A is charged within that topic, even when another topic's
umbrella overlaps it.

counts and old accounting. For the active structure, see the
[manuscript programme](MANUSCRIPT_PROGRAM.md).
